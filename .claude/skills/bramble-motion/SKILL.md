# Motion for Bramble

**Experimental (owner, 24 Nov 2026).** The guidelines and process are drafts being tested on a pilot; they will change as we learn. The owner: "we're also not trying to lock it in as final yet. We're just trying to experiment on it." If a rule gets in the way of something that looks better on film, say so and note it; that is how the practice improves.

- **Read first:** `docs/workstreams/fight-animation-24-nov/practice/GUIDELINES.md` (the rules, each with how it is checked) and `practice/PROCESS.md` (the steps, the brief and beat-spec templates, what an agent may claim).
- **Feel is the owner's.** No agent can watch motion at speed (`docs/AGENT-BLIND-SPOTS.md`). Agents check what the per-frame log and films can show; the owner judges how it feels, on films in the comparison viewer and on a Windows build.
- **A card moves toward another card only when it attacks.** Effects, keywords and buffs get their own look, often a drawn piece. A new drawn piece gets a design pass and the owner's yes on a picture before it's built (`bramble-art`).
- **Film it:** `tools/feel/` (60 fps films) and the fight fixture `tests/screens/feel/fight_compare/` (per-frame log, speed charts, per-option clips for the viewer).
- **Codex workers can't see this skill.** Put the paths above in their brief.
