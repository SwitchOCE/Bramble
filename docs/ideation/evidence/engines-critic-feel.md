# Critic B: new keen player, feel and endings

1. **Last Blow: keep, reword.** It turns a dead tie into a result at low cost. Say "If an attack empties both boards, the attacking side wins"; "blow" is vague when both units hit at once. I never saw it fire.
2. **The Last Four: keep, change.** It fixed the foregone ending and gave my only comeback. But it ended game 1 in one fight (18 damage at 18 Health), and the game 3 duel was one fight (17 at 17). Add a last stand (below).
3. **Stand Behind Me / Bread formation: change.** A good placement puzzle. On one-tribe boards it got stronger (+5 or +6 for 2 gold, against +4 before). On mixed boards it gave +1 or 0, and neutral units can never gain. The screen still shows the old text.
4. **Hand-me-down: keep.** Clear and fair, and the half never felt mean. Nothing on screen shows it.
5. **Harvest Supper at tier 3: keep.** My tribe payoff by round 6; I cast it three times in game 3.

## Details

**Formation**
- Best moment of the package: Gideon in B2 between Bog folk, +3/+3 on two units.
- But it piles stats onto back-row units that are already big. My tier 3 Gideon reached 16/20 and carried the board, the same "cheap unit carries the board" problem.
- Aimed past a neutral Lamp Moth or Pill Bug it gave 0, which reads as a bug.
- **Counter-proposal:** "Each unit behind it gains +1/+1, plus +1/+1 if a unit of its tribe touches it (up to +2/+2)." A floor, and a cast caps near +4.

**Hand-me-down**
- Toad to Whirligig (+3/+3) and Partner to Committee (+3/+3) both felt good.
- Add a screen line: "Next unit played gets +3/+3".

**Last Blow:** never came up after round 5. Log it ("Last Blow: attacker wins, 6 damage").

**Clarity problems in play**
- None of the four rules appears on screen.
- Stand Behind Me and Bread show their old wording.
- Shifting numbers cost me a wrong buy. A stale `play 0` pointed at an Acorn and quietly cost me a turn's spells.

**More interesting, or just flatter?** Items 2 and 4 add real decisions: when to sell, and when to risk it in the final. Item 3 is more fun to place but still a generic stat engine; my change would fix that. Committee stacking (two gave all 7 units +6 Attack) and Last Orders still won my fights, and the package doesn't touch them.
