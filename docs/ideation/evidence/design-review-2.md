# Design review 2: Oozes and Birds

Two independent paper critics; costing is in `costing-2.md`. Nothing has been played. These are directions for whoever builds the tribes.

## Critic 1: play
Both answer the playtest complaint. Belly asks who's inside, in what order, and where the gaps are. Fly South asks who's home and which Nest.
- Leaks back to stacking: Granny Stir-Up should gain Attack only; Mother Goose and The Great Lump should read card stats.
- Wee Wobbly: "Belly 1. After this survives a hit, the unit inside comes out in the nearest gap; if none inside, summon a Drip."
- Birds on turn 3 are a trap (Swallow misses 2 fights in 3). Nest: "Holds a 0/2 Egg until something stands on it."
- Mother Goose: "While away, Start of Combat: each Nest strikes across for its bird's card Attack."
- Gabble Ratchet: "When a unit on a Nest dies, its Bird lands there this fight."
- Cut-and-Come-Again: copies mustn't count for triples. Figgy Duff's goldens give no Triple Reward. Belly counts everything nested. Tom Tit: "after this kills." Dishwater Nell: gold only if touching an Ooze. Mrs Stork: left on an earlier turn.
- Real builds. Oozes: Second Helpings (depth), Rude Awakening (aimed text). Birds: Summer and Winter (timetable), The Passage (strikes), Highway Robbery (barely flies).
- First 8. Oozes: Dollop, Posset, Wobbly, Aspic, Pie, Frumenty, Hasty Pudding, Granny. Birds: Swallow, Chiffchaff, Dunnock, Lump, Redbreast, Jack Daw, Stork, Goose.

## Critic 2: rules
- Swallowed unit tripled: the inside copy counts, the golden goes to hand, and any lost timer is logged. A tripled Ooze's contents go to hand.
- Full hand: one "doorstep" rule, nothing lost, overflow enters when there's room. A bird's Nest ends on its due turn. Selling an Ooze warns first.
- Flown bird: triples check after birds land, before Start of Turn text; Tern's home text fires first. A landing that completes a triple ignores the hand limit.
- Nest is only a marker: the slot stays empty for summons and keeps its Ring. Goose's strikes use the bird's own Attack, ignoring Ring and Hex; Ratchet landers get both.
- Spills: sides never compete for slots. Within a side, all first-outs land before any second-out. Mag Pye's "first of all" needs its own phase.
- Rewrites: Cut "...last non-Ooze inside. Copies don't count for triples." Stork "a Bird that has missed a fight comes home." Stone Soup "A refresh washes it out." Hasty Pudding "...or when its side has nobody else." One Goose fires per side. Bubble's copy has used its split. Shy inside is off. Frumenty doesn't borrow Fly South. Dunnock "each End of Turn, floor next turn." Old Tern: delete "sells for 0".
- Phone: chips "Belly 1/2" and "Fly South"; pips for insides, tap to open. Nest: twig ring plus countdown. Sky strip for strikes. Shorten Granny, Whistlers, Jack Daw and Bubble.
- Engine: one held-units zone (belly or away) covering triples, ghosts, elimination, tray and doorstep (large). Mid-fight entry, text transfer (medium). Swallow action, sell intercept, Nest state, Goose (medium). Granny (large).
- Bots. Oozes: swallow when full or with spare gold; wall in last; keep one gap per extra body. Birds: fly only when healthy, never opening a front hole; stand bodies on Nests. All: hold carries still.
- Runaway watch: Pie pays every round, since fights run on copies.
- First 8. Oozes: Dollop, Posset, Nell, Bubble, Pie, Frumenty, Hasty, Cut. Birds: Swallow, Chiffchaff, Dunnock, Redbreast, Daw, Stork, Goose, Whistlers.
