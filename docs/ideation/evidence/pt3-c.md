## What's best

The keyword engines are clever and easy to read in the fight log: Belly, Mire's pull, Prince's kisses, Granny's triple. Fights at tier 6 were close and full of reversals. In two games a run of cheap units snowballed into a satisfying win.

## Top 5 problems

1. **Committing to a tribe doesn't pay.** The winning boards are stat piles from several tribes: Posset, Lodger, Dozey, Belladonna, Whirligig and stacked Rings. Both my wins used Belladonna and Whirligig. The bots' "main tribe" boards are mixed too.
2. **Birds are underpowered and fiddly.** Bots bought 2–9 Bird cards per lobby, against 28–42 Hunters. The Fly South loop costs slots and tempo and gives little back.
3. **Early and mid game snowball, and Hard is a wall.** One buffed unit runs over a board (Wick 17/16, Chaser 15/20). In every losing game I lost 4–6 fights in a row with no way back. One Hard bot was still at 40 health in round 11.
4. **Late games stall.** There were 4 ties in game 3 and 2 in game 5. Gold piled up to 56 with nothing to buy, and games ran 19–22 rounds.
5. **Some rules surprise you.** A Shy unit walked out when Harvest Supper buffed it. Which back slot is "behind" which front slot isn't obvious. You can't see in advance which Acorn a Sergeant will throw.

**#1 alone would stop "very good".** A keen player would find the tribe fantasy is a garnish on whatever has the biggest stats.

**Verdict: okay.**
