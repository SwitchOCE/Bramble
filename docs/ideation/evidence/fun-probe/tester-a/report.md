# Fun probe wave 1: tester A

Seeds 501–503. Totals are board Attack+Health from `log`.

**Game 1, Birds:** 1st, round 17. Felt strongest R14 (beat the 2nd-place Fairy bot for 20). Biggest jump R10: Old Tern came home doubled (19/23 to 38/47), two Cuckoos shared a tenth with every Bird. Board 160 to 329, and 2,085 by R14.

**Game 2, Fairies:** 1st, round 20. Felt strongest R17. Biggest jump R16: golden Fairy Monarch tripled Ring size 5 to 15, plus Queen of Elfland. Board 262 to 660. Slow before: fell to 7 health.

**Game 3, Hedgerow (control):** 6th, out round 13. Felt strongest R12. Biggest jump R11: Squire Lollop and two Standing Committees with all 7 slots touching. Board 76 to 98.

**Best reward moment:** Game 2, R16, Ring size tripling after a 3-turn countdown and a Silky thank. Beat everything in the control: yes. It added 398 stats in one turn; the control's best turn added 22.

**Too strong/unfair:** Yes. Birds snowball without limit: Old Tern went 19 to 38 to 86 to 208, and every Bird passed 150/190. From R12 the fights didn't matter. Both new tribes end up ~25x the flat tribes (2,759 and 3,906 vs 117), so the control felt hopeless. Also: how to thank a Shy unit isn't shown.

**One change:** Make a Bird's homecoming add a fixed gain (for example its base stats × tier) instead of doubling all its stats. Jumps stay big but stop compounding on themselves.
