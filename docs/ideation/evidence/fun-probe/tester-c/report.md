**Game 3, control, Hunters (seed 703):** 6th, round 12.

**Best reward moment:** Game 1, R10, the golden Lump. It beat everything in the control game: one unit had more stats than my whole control board until R12, and the control game never had a moment like it.

**Too strong or unfair?** Yes, Birds. Doubling has no limit: my Lump reached 820/1082 by R18. Bot Bird boards (119/194 Old Tern, a golden Whistlers) crushed Fairies and everyone else. Fights became one hit kills one unit. Fairies started slowly: Ring sources were scarce until T6. Cluricaun only fires on a tier-up, and at my last tier-up I had 6 of 7 slots Ringed.

**One change:** Limit Bird homecoming growth so it doesn't double without end, for example by doubling base stats only or capping each trip's gain. That keeps the big moment without the runaway.
