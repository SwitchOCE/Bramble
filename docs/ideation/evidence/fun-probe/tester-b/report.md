# Fun probe wave 1: tester B

Totals are Attack + Health from `play.sh log`, without hero buffs.

**Game 1, Fairies (seed 602, Brother Bracken):** placed 1st, final round 18.
- Strongest: rounds 16–18.
- Biggest jump, round 16, **293 → 1249**: golden Queen of Elfland ate two units, then golden Fairy Monarch tripled Ring size. Belladonna went 57/65 → 189/197.

**Game 2, Birds (seed 603, Dame Farthing):** placed 2nd, final round 21.
- Strongest: round 16.
- Biggest jump, round 16, **860 → 1520**: One Swallow came home at 252/388, and The Cuckoo gave every Bird a tenth of that.
- Golden Seven Whistlers, which halves every enemy twice, carried the midgame.

**Game 3, Hunters, the control (seed 604, Old Mother Mousewife):** placed 6th, final round 12.
- Strongest: round 11.
- Biggest jump, round 11, **85 → 105**: Whirligig Beetle plus Beltane Bonfire.

**Best reward moment:** game 1, round 16, the Monarch triple. It beat everything in the control game: yes. Hunters never gained more than 20 in a round, so the board never felt any stronger.

**Too strong or unfair:**
- Late fights come down to one huge unit. A bot's Swallow reached 927/1167 and beat me.
- Flat tribes can't keep up: the control lost to a Fairy board with a 57/58 Lutra.
- Whirligig Beetle swapped enemy units onto their own bigger Rings.
- A Discover that completes a triple pulls both board copies into hand. I lost my two Queens mid-plan and lost the fight.

**One change:** Birds should leave a half-stat Egg by default (Redwing's effect, built in). Early flights emptied my board, and I lost 5 of my first 7 fights before the doubling paid off.
