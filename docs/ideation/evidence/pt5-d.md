# Playtest pt5-d: build 7 (b2c3f01), how games read

Three full games with `tools/play/play.sh` against the full-strength bots. Shots: `recruit`, `reminder`, `clarity_discover_live`, `combat_mid`, `combat_reminder` and `combat_end`, at 1688×780 and 1920×1080.

**Places:** 8th (Hoolet, Oozes then Bog folk), 6th (Mousewife, Hedgerow folk), 6th (Pipkin, Bog folk Mire wall). I was out in round 9 or 10 each game.

**Verdict: okay.** It isn't very good yet.

**What's best:** lanes matter. The Mire wall (Toad, Gideon, Natterjack, Bufo and Spitfire Sal) won three straight fights by picking who hits whom. Shy is a smart trade: Stand Behind Me on Hob gave +2 gold. Changeling stealing a 10/17 Urchin is a great moment. In a fight, the "This hit" panel (before, hit, after) and the bold "X dies to Y's hit" line answer who hit and why a unit died.

## Top 5 problems
1. **The stat race isn't legible. On its own this stops "very good".** By rounds 8 to 10 the bots had 12/16 to 19/20 carries, while my tribe payoffs added +1 or +2. No card text shows a human how to get there, and Mire, Bitter and Nightshade couldn't make up the gap.
2. **The fight log has unexplained changes and silent failures. On its own this stops "very good".** Some stat changes have no cause: "Hedge Witch now 5/6 (+1/+0)", Rainbow Chaser +2 when Jenny died, and -1 to four units when Cluricaun died. Wee Wobbly's Drip fails silently on a full board. Earwig Mother's "behind it" put a Nymph at F1. Bufo's "loses half" didn't fire when Bufo died from the return damage of its own attack.
3. **The fight board is cluttered.** Quarry lines cross cards. The TARGET banner covers names. Tags are cut off ("Rainbo", "On attac"). On PC the Ring slip covers the bottom of the log.
4. **There are too many space words.** Beside, next to, touching, behind, across, and Pass's "last touching unit to its right". "Ring: Ring a slot…" defines itself. Phone nameplates cut names to "Harassed" and "Cutter". In comma-separated board lists, "Mrs Burdock, Expecting Again" reads as two units.
5. **Losses snowball, and Mousewife's power was often wasted.** Losses cost 4–5 health early and 9–12 later. Mousewife's "no room for a Mouse" came up in most of my lost fights.

## Builds that felt different
The Bog folk lane wall was really different. Hedgerow Hex and Acorns played differently too. Birds mostly served to free a slot for 0 gold.

## Answers
**(P)** Game 1, round 8: Gideon faced an empty lane, wasted his Mire, and I lost. Game 3, round 9: after scouting I put the Belladonnas at B2/B3 to Nightshade their F2–F4. It halved Brock and Wolf, but I still lost by 10. Game 3, rounds 6–7: lanes chosen for Mire won both fights.

**(B)** Unlike the bots, I countered scouted carries with Mire, Spitfire Sal, Changeling and Nightshade. I also cashed in Shy and used Hiring Fair with five Bog folk. The bots stack one huge unit.

**(W)** Bufo, Earwig Mother and Wee Wobbly, as described above. Bitter stacks to -3 but the log only says "Bitter". The log uses jargon: "bogs your F2", "Mired kill reward", "'s ability goes off".

**Lens.** Text is readable at both sizes. Shop cards on PC show their rules; on phone they show only a tag. On PC the slip is boxed, italic and smaller. On phone it's boxed but only faintly italic, with a big gap above it. On phone Discover, the Wasp's rules appear both on the card and in the box beneath it. The fight shows who hit and who killed whom, but not what triggered an aura change.
