# Playtest pt5-d: build 7 (b2c3f01), how games read

**Places:** 8th (Hoolet, Oozes then Bog folk), 6th (Mousewife, Hedgerow folk), 6th (Pipkin, Bog folk). I was out in round 9 or 10 each game.

**Verdict: okay.** It isn't very good yet.

**What's best:** lanes matter. The Mire wall (Toad, Gideon, Natterjack, Bufo and Spitfire Sal) won three straight fights. In a fight, the "This hit" panel and the bold "dies to Y's hit" line show who hit and why a unit died.

## Top 5 problems
1. **The stat race isn't legible. On its own this stops "very good".** By rounds 8 to 10 the bots had 12/16 to 19/20 carries, while my tribe payoffs added +1 or +2.
2. **The fight log has unexplained changes and silent failures. On its own this stops "very good".** Stat changes have no cause: Hedge Witch +1 Attack, Rainbow Chaser +2 when Jenny died, and four units -1 when Cluricaun died. Wee Wobbly's Drip fails silently on a full board.
3. **The fight board is cluttered.** Quarry lines cross cards, the TARGET banner covers names, and tags are cut off ("Rainbo", "On attac").
4. **There are too many space words.** Beside, next to, touching, behind, across, and Pass's "last touching unit to its right". "Ring: Ring a slot…" defines itself. Phone nameplates cut names to "Harassed" and "Cutter".
5. **Losses snowball, and Mousewife's power was often wasted.** Losses cost 9–12 health after round 7. "No room for a Mouse" came up in most of my lost fights.

**Different builds:** the Bog folk lane wall. Hedgerow Hex and Acorns, a little. Birds mostly freed a slot.
