# Birds pass 1: triage review

8 Nov 2026, a fresh cloud reviewer, on `ideation/c-birds-pass1`. Method: `.claude/skills/triage/SKILL.md` steps 3–4. Competition is every `set: "slice"`, `pool: true` unit in `content/units/` at the same tier, not only the designer's list (which leaves out Pill Bug 1/4, Rainbow Chaser, Mrs Sorrel 2/3 and The Toll-Bard). The boards are hand estimates against `benchmark-boards.md`. I didn't run the sim, because the pass-1 cards aren't in the engine.

**Verdict: partly.** The owner's first question ("why would you play the early-game Birds") is answered: every T1 and T2 Bird is now a par body or better, and the fliers make Mr Redbreast a real buy. "The entire journey" isn't answered yet. Both routes break at or after T4, and the R12 low roll still comes 6th.

## 1. Role check, T1–T2 (R1–R4)

| Card | Buy on the turn you see it? | Why |
|---|---|---|
| One Swallow 1/2, +1 gold | **Yes, as economy** | It's the only T1 that pays unconditionally (Little Nip and Bridge Toad have conditions). The 1/2 body loses to Wee Wobbly and Pill Bug 1/4. Fine. |
| Chiffchaff 2/3 on play | **Yes** | 5 stats, level with Wobbly, Pill Bug and Young Pretender, and it grows on each trip. Fine. |
| Mrs Dunnock 2/3 | **Yes, as a body** | It's par. Its text works *against* the T2–T6 Nest payoffs (see ask 1). |
| Mr Redbreast 2/3 +1/+1 per Nest | **Yes, if a flier is away** | At 3/4 or 4/5 it's the biggest T2 body, ahead of Sable, Sorrel and Moss Ooze. Without a Nest it's a par 2/3. |
| Redwing 2/4 | **Yes** | It's a Moss Ooze body. The Egg text is worth +1/+1 at T2. Fine. |
| Mrs Jay 3/3, Acorn on sale | **Yes** | It has the most stats at T2, and each flight pays 1 gold plus an Acorn. Fine. |

**Do early cards make later Birds more attractive? Mostly yes.** Swallow and Chiffchaff flown on R2 give Redbreast his Nest by R3, and Jay and Redwing make Tom Tit worth taking at T3. The exception is Dunnock, a T1 that shortens trips. That cuts the time Nests stay up, removes the Egg she stands on, and so weakens Redbreast, Tom Tit, Goose and the Ratchet.

T3 is fine. Tom Tit is par alone and the best T3 with two Eggs. Jack Daw (3/4) is a step below The Lodger (4/4) but has a real target. Old Tern (2/4) is below par, but a free Bird every four turns pays for it.

## 2. Skips

- **T4 is a skip for both routes. This is the flagged gap, confirmed, and wider than flagged.** Of the T4 Birds, Barn Owl (4/5) and Mag Pye (3/5) are situational answers that lose on stats to Burner, Wisp, Ermeline and Syllabub (3/6 to 4/6, plus text). Mrs Stork is the only route piece, and she serves Homecoming. A Hatchery player has nothing to buy at T4. A Homecoming player has one card, and it fights the T5–T6 payoffs, because Stork brings Birds home and Goose, the Lump and the Ratchet want Nests.
- **Homecoming has no T5 or T6 payoff of its own.** Goose and the Ratchet read Nests, which means Birds *away*. Whistlers fits any Bird board. Homecoming's replays (Chiffchaff +1/+1 a trip, Tern Discovers, Swallow gold) end around a 6/7 Chiffchaff and some gold. In practice it is an economy lane into Hatchery, not a second route.
- **Hatchery can't actually "keep Birds away".** Every flier comes home in 2 turns, and its Egg vanishes. No card lengthens a trip, so each flier gives an Egg for 2 fights in 3. The player re-sells every cycle, and Tom Tit's two-Egg window needs fliers staggered by hand.

## 3. Sub-varieties

There's one real route (Nests and Eggs), plus Homecoming as its engine and thieves as a splash: Jack Daw, Mag Pye, Barn Owl and Whistlers, 4 of 16 cards with no route. pt3-a's 3rd place came from thieves, so they're a real variant, but only from T3. The plan's "two routes" doesn't hold past T4.

## 4. Benchmark boards (estimates, method as in `benchmark-boards.md`)

**R6 high** (T4 on R6, golden Redbreast): golden Redbreast 8/10 (2 Nests), Jay Egg 5/5 (Redwing beside it, Tom Tit +2/+2), Chiffchaff Egg 5/6, Redwing 2/4, Tom Tit 3/4, Stork 3/6, Jack Daw 3/4. That's about 70. **Rank 3** (Oozes 92, Fairies 79).

**R6 low** (T3 on R6, no Redwing or Tom Tit): Chiffchaff 3/4, Dunnock 2/3, Redbreast 3/4 (1 Nest), Jay Egg 2/2, Pill Bug 1/4, Sable 2/3, Moss Ooze 2/4. That's about 37. **Rank 1–2** of the low rolls (Fairies 43, Oozes 32). The early game no longer collapses. This is the real gain.

**R12 high** (golden Ratchet or a second golden): the designer's board, which I checked: 130 on view, plus Goose's 9 damage and about 40 stats from the Ratchet. **Rank 1–2.** The designer's R12 "average" already needs Goose, the Lump, the Ratchet, a golden Redbreast and Redwing all found by R12, with 2 Birds at T5 and 2 at T6. That is a high roll.

**R12 low** (T5, no Goose, no Ratchet, no Redwing, no Supper): Lump 7/9 on Jay's Nest, Redbreast 4/5 (2 Nests), Chiffchaff Egg 4/5 (with Tom Tit), Barn Owl 4/5, Burner 4/6, Tom Tit 3/4, Stork 3/6. That's about 75. **Rank 6**, below Fairies' 89. The old low roll was 81.

**Where the low roll collapses: R9–R12.** It runs into the T4 skip, then everything hangs on finding the T5–T6 trio. Without Redwing, Eggs are half-size. Without Goose, the Lump has a small Nest to stand on. Without the Ratchet, the tribe has no finisher. In the skill's terms, it's a tribe that only works on a high roll from T5.

## 5. New-player confusion

- **One away Bird, four readings.** Its Egg is half its *current* stats, or full with Redwing, plus Tom Tit's bonus. The Lump and Goose read its *base* stats. Goose's own Egg is a fixed 0/2. The Ratchet lands the Bird itself. A player can't predict a Nest's value without the rules notes.
- **Dunnock "eats" the Egg.** Standing her on a Nest removes an Egg that could be 5/5 or more with Tom Tit, and nothing on the card says so.
- **The Ratchet's landing is unstated:** whether the Bird stays on the board after the fight, and whether its trip ends.
- **Stork and the Nests:** her text reads as pure upside, but each early homecoming removes a Nest that Redbreast, Goose and the Lump were counting.

## Items

**Cut (must fix before build)**
1. T4 has no Hatchery piece, and Owl and Pye are under par for T4. Add a T4 Nest or Egg piece, ideally one that lengthens trips ("Birds whose Nests are beside this stay away a turn longer"), or turn one of the two thieves into it.
2. Homecoming has no T5–T6 payoff, so "two routes" isn't true. Give it a late payoff that rewards homecomings, or drop the claim and design the second sub-variety (thieves?) to have its own way through T4–T6.

**Ask (orchestrator's call)**
1. Dunnock works against every Nest payoff. Either let her bring home a Bird *beside* her without eating its Egg, or accept the conflict as the "shops decide the lean" split.
2. Unify the away-Bird readings. Use current stats everywhere (the Lump and Goose included), or make Egg values visible on the card.
3. Write the Ratchet's landing rules on the card or in its details.
4. Relabel the R12 board as a high roll and add a low-roll target. The T4 fix should lift the R12 low roll to about 90 or more.
5. Costing should check Swallow plus Stork: about +2 gold a turn per Swallow, more with a golden Swallow.

**Fine:** all six T1–T2 cards as buys, Tom Tit, Jack Daw, Old Tern, the 16-card 3/3/3/3/2/2 spread, and dropping Tom Tit's thief design.
