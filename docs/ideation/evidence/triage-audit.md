## 4. Triage of the top items

Verdicts: K kept, P partial, O open.

**Losses snowball; knockouts in bursts (tally 2, 7, 19).**
- *System, damage curve (K).* `damage-v05.md`: on the current rule a streak ends a hero by round 10, first out round 9, 22% comebacks. Every v0.5 arm moved first-out to 12 and comebacks to 29–36%. Caps rise with the round for everyone, so low heroes take big hits together, and Last Stand needs four or fewer alive (P4 b c): bursts.
- *Early outliers (K).* Streaks start at R3–4 against Chasers and Mousewife (P4 a; P5 a 6–0): the early winner's board snowballs too.
- *Bots (O).* Hard bots out-tempo testers early (P3 B C); untested with free-choice bots.
- *Readability (P).* "Fill the board early" is never taught (P4 b).

**Rainbow Chaser early (tally 6).**
- *Card (K).* Any unit played into an empty slot paints, so seven ordinary buys make it 8/9 by R5, plus a 12-gold sale.
- *Competition (K).* No other T1 scales unconditionally; T1 bodies are 3–5.
- *Rings (K).* Piles land on it (34/38 on +9/+9).
- *Bots (P).* Bots never cash out (P5 e), inflating bot Chasers, but testers also start 6–0 and 12–0.

**Gold-only units as traps (tally 12, 13).**
- *Damage (K).* Losses cost 4–9 from R1 (P5 a e); a 1-gold card that loses a fight costs more than it pays.
- *Costing (K).* Bodies were cut to pay for gold: Nip 2/1, Peat Cutter 1/1, Pennywort 1/2.
- *Role (K).* The gold arrives next turn or needs saving, and nothing converts it.
- *Competition (K).* Gold-plus-stats cards (Chaser, Sleeping Partner into Treasurer) win the role (P5 e).

**Ring piles late (tally 5, 3).**
- *Card (K).* No per-slot cap. Sally Spore adds +1/+1 every turn; Boundary Stone (T4 spell, 2 gold: Ring a slot +3/+3) and Spore Cloud (T2, +2/+2) stack; Monarch adds more.
- *System (K).* Rings stay on the slot, so any tribe's unit inherits them: generic piles (item 3).
- *Competition (K).* 2 gold for +3/+3 permanent beats a 3-gold unit.
- *Answers (P).* Changeling and Whirligig exist, two cards, and bots never use them.

**Process cause.** No cross-round tally, so items 2 and 3 recurred every round as round-local fixes. Damage v0.5 met four of five targets and stayed opt-in. Chaser and Rings were judged on lobby win rates (Rainbow 15.8%), which pinned, never-cashing bots can't measure. Same root as Birds: no buy-moment check on early cards.
