# Design review 1, 6 Nov 2026

Three independent reviewers on the first tribe designs, none seeing the others: a high-MMR player (A), a shipped-card-game designer (B), and the costing reviewer (C; table in `costing-1.md`). Paper review; nothing played.

**Where they agreed:**
- Too many concepts: Hedgerow about 13-14, Fairies about 10 (A, B). Folded to 3 per tribe and 4 shared.
- Old Mother Mast compounds; a 20-gold Hoard becomes 106 gold in 6 turns (A, C).
- Queen of Elfland adds the largest unit to all others, which is geometric (A, C): split, don't copy.
- The Sleeping Partner is an automatic turn-1 buy (A, C).
- Changeling and Cradle Fetch: no ceiling and bad to lose to (A, C). Next batch, rewritten.
- Name collisions: Larder, Burdock, Sorrel, Mother (A, B).
- Keep Hob against Moth: a real puzzle, and the owner's.

**Where they split, and the pick:**
- Line: B makes it one visible Pass action; A moves it to End of Turn because free moves fake the gap's cost. **Both:** Pass, at End of Turn.
- All recruit position text at End of Turn (A) against instant (owner, 02h). **Instant, except Pass.** The owner liked choosing what to buff, and that outranks A's objection.
- Standing Committee: C's flat version over B's quadratic one, which measured 408% over.
- Sleeping Partner and Rainbow numbers: C's, since A and B didn't price them.

**Loops:** none infinite as worded (C). Reynard with Market Day stays in owner's range only if it reads the unit's tier; held to the next batch with fixed wording.

**Tier 6:** base rules reach it on turn 7 with 2 units bought. The set breaks turn 8 unfixed; after fixes one line is still one unit over. Bench decides; the fallback is raising the tier 6 upgrade from 11 to 12.
