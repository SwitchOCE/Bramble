# Birds, pass 3: the Brood (carry and answers)

9 Nov 2026. A pass 3 designer for Track C, on the **carry and answers** angle. Read: owner notes 09a, 09c, 09d and 08e; `fundamentals.md` §1 and §3; `plan-scale-rework.md` (Step 1, Track C); rulebook §8–9 and the BRAMBLE ideation rules; `wording-guide.md`; `content/birds.md`; `pass2-c.md`; the Birds audit. I had no price list. Every number is a first guess, sized against the target shape at rounds 6, 9, 12 and 15. The lever sweep sets the rates.

**In one line.** Birds raise one Chick. The flock flies out and comes home, and each homecoming makes every later Feed bigger, for the rest of the game. Thieves steal from the other player's carry for the Chick, and the Chick ends up hunting the enemy's biggest unit.

---

## 1. Why this shape

**The problem (audit and pass 2).**
- Birds take first in 4.9% of lobbies.
- Their payoffs wanted Birds away, and an away Bird fought as an Egg at half stats. That is the round 7–12 slump.
- 6 of their 11 stat payoffs last one fight only.
- Only the Cuckoo kept growing, at +2/+2 a turn.
- No Bird held its stats, so a big Bird died like a small one.

**What the combat model says about carries, and what I built from it.**

| Finding | What the design does |
|---|---|
| A carry among small units loses to an even board of the same total. | The carry gets ways to hold its stats: half damage (Tom Tit), an extra attack after a kill (Mr Redbreast), and Egg walls in front of it. The Flock route spreads Feeds, so the support isn't paper. |
| Under our rules, Attack above the enemy's Health is wasted, and each body soaks one exchange. | The Chick gets more kills per turn: one chain attack from tier 2, unlimited at tier 6 (the Ratchet). Health matters more than Attack, so the Lump is built Health-heavy. |
| Holding stats is worth most: 2 less damage +24.5 points, Hunker +15, an extra attack per cycle +4. | Tom Tit's half damage is the core holding piece, at tier 3. It is proportional, so it still works at 4-digit stats. |
| Answers aimed "across" land by layout luck. | Bird answers *are* aimed across, on purpose, because placement should be a decision. They are proportional, so they reach a carry of any size. One card, the Ratchet, aims at the biggest enemy in either row. |
| Payoffs must be permanent and show in the shop. | Every Feed happens in the Recruit Phase and is permanent. Jack Daw's steal is permanent on your side. The Feed bonus is a "this game" number shown on the tribe icon. |
| Growth needs compounding text, not bigger bodies. | The Feed bonus rises with every homecoming, so each Feed source gets stronger every turn. Steals track the field's own growth. The Lump doubles Feeds. |

**The owner's Fly South point (08f): "You want something to justify the fact that you've got this unreliable amount of power."** Now every trip pays twice. The Bird comes back to replay its text, and its homecoming raises the Feed bonus for good. An Egg keeps the slot as a wall with the Bird's full Health. Being away costs the Bird's Attack and text for two fights, not half its stats.

## 2. Rules and keywords

Two new keywords (Chick, Feed), one changed rule (Egg), and one shared Track B mechanic (damage halving). **Nest is gone from card text**: no card reads Nests any more. The board still marks a flown Bird's slot.

| Term | Reminder (≤ 16 words) | Rules |
|---|---|---|
| **Chick** (new) | Your biggest Bird on the board. | Biggest means most Attack + Health. On a tie the current Chick stays; otherwise the first in reading order. In a fight, the Chick is fixed at Start of Combat. The board and the scout show a Chick badge. Eggs are not Birds. |
| **Feed X** (new) | Gives +X/+X plus your Feed bonus. With no target named, it feeds your Chick. | Recruit Phase only, so always permanent. "Feed your Birds X" feeds each Bird on the board. Each Feed adds the bonus once. With no Bird on the board, nothing happens. |
| **Feed bonus** (new, this game) | Shown on the Bird tribe icon as "Feed +N". | A player number, starting at 0. Mrs Jay and the Cuckoo raise it. It never resets. |
| **Fly South** (kept) | Sells for no gold: it flies off and returns in 2 turns. | Unchanged, rule 12.1.4: it can't fly on the turn it was played or landed, and When sold still fires. |
| **Egg** (changed) | Holds a flown Bird's slot: its Health, no Attack, no text. | Was: half Attack and half Health. Now: the Bird's current Health and 0 Attack, so it never attacks (9.4.6). A unit played onto the slot replaces the Egg, and the Bird still comes home. A starting Egg that survives still adds 1 hero damage. Ask for Kin counts Eggs as Birds. |
| **Takes half damage** (Track B) | Plain words on the card, no keyword. | Rounded up, both when attacked and when attacking. |

**Golden Feeds say "twice", not "Feed 2".** Feed 2 would add the bonus once (2 + N), so a golden would fall behind as N grows. "Feed 1 twice" adds the bonus twice (2 + 2N), so a golden stays twice as good all game. That follows the wording guide's "two for a".

## 3. The sixteen

Tier spread kept at 3/3/3/3/2/2. Every creature, name and portrait is kept. Barn Owl stays out of the pool, as a reserve (section 8).

**Typical board used for the payoff numbers.** This is the engine line with average shops: the Cuckoo bought on round 8, the Lump on round 12, Supper 1/2/3 by rounds 9/12/15.
- **Feed bonus N:** about 4 at round 9, 11 at round 12, 19 at round 15.
- **Opponent, from the target curve:**

| 9 | 18 (9/9) | 40 (20/20) |

### Tier 1

**One Swallow.** *Swallow, Seasonal Labourer.* Tier 1, **1/2** (unchanged).
- **Text:** Fly South. When played: get 1 gold.
- **Golden (2/4):** Fly South. When played: get 2 gold.
- **Condition:** played, and played again each time it comes home (every 3 turns, or 2 with Mrs Stork).
- **Payoff:** 1 gold a landing. With the Cuckoo, each landing also adds +1 Feed bonus. That is worth about +1/+1, +2/+2 and +3/+3 on the Chick for every later turn, from a landing at rounds 9, 12 and 15.
- **Permanence:** economy. The bonus it feeds is permanent.
- **Enabler:** the cheapest landing for the Cuckoo and Mrs Stork, which gets 1 gold of its 6 back. It also adds a body to Old Tern's bursts and the Whistlers' count.
- **Concentrates:** no.

**Chiffchaff.** *Warbler, Town Crier.* Tier 1, **1/2** (unchanged).
- **Text:** Fly South. When played: Feed 1.
- **Golden (2/4):** Fly South. When played: Feed 1 twice.
- **Condition:** played. On round 1 it's your only Bird, so it feeds itself and fights as a 2/3, as today. After that, every replay feeds the Chick.
- **Payoff:** each play feeds the Chick +5/+5 at round 9, +12/+12 at round 12 and +20/+20 at round 15. That's about one play every 3 turns, or every 2 with Mrs Stork. Each landing is also +1 bonus with the Cuckoo.
- **Permanence:** permanent.
- **Enabler:** the Cuckoo, Mrs Stork, Old Tern and the Great Lump (doubled).
- **Concentrates:** yes.

**Mrs Dunnock.** *Hedge Sparrow, Foster Mother.* Tier 1, **1/2** (was 2/3, because she now grows herself).
- **Text:** End of Turn: Feed 1.
- **Golden (2/4):** End of Turn: Feed 1 twice.
- **Condition:** End of Turn, with a Bird on the board. Bought alone on round 1, she is her own Chick: 2/3 for the first fight, 7/8 by round 6.
- **Payoff:** every turn the Chick gets +5/+5 at round 9, +12/+12 at round 12 and +20/+20 at round 15. Golden gives twice that. By round 12 she has fed about 60/60 in all.
- **Permanence:** permanent.
- **Enabler:** every Feed bonus card (Jay, the Cuckoo) multiplies her. The Lump doubles her. Tom Tit and Mr Redbreast make the Chick she builds hold.
- **Concentrates:** yes. This is the cuckoo's host feeding a chick far too big for her.

### Tier 2

**Mr Redbreast.** *Robin, Who Stays.* Tier 2, **2/4** (unchanged body, new text).
- **Text:** After your Chick kills: it attacks again. Once a fight.
- **Golden (4/8):** After your Chick kills: it attacks again. Twice a fight.
- **Condition:** the Chick kills something. That needs its Attack to reach the defender's Health, which is easy for a fed Chick against median units.
- **Payoff:** about one more enemy dead each fight, at once: a 9/9 at round 9, a 35/35 at round 12, a 120/120 at round 15.
- **Permanence:** combat only, every fight.
- **Enabler:** a fed Chick from any source. He is the robin who never flies, so he is often the round 2–4 Chick himself.
- **Concentrates:** yes. It turns a big Attack into kills instead of overkill.

**Mrs Jay.** *Jay, Who Plants Oaks.* Tier 2, **3/3** (unchanged body, new text).
- **Text:** Fly South. When this comes home: Feeds give +1/+1 more this game.
- **Golden (6/6):** Fly South. When this comes home: Feeds give +2/+2 more this game.
- **Condition:** a full trip home, about every 3 turns. A plain sale gives nothing, so buying and selling her at once can't farm it.
- **Payoff:** about +1 bonus by round 9, +3 by round 12 and +4 by round 15. Each +1 is +1/+1 on every later Feed. With the Cuckoo, her landing counts twice.
- **Permanence:** permanent, this game.
- **Enabler:** every Feed card, plus Redwing's and Goose's spread. She is the engine seed before the Cuckoo, so the tribe doesn't hang on one tier 4 card.
- **Concentrates:** yes, through Feeds. *She buries thousands. The flock eats from them for years.*
- **Lost:** her Acorn, and with it the Hedgerow bridge. Birds now have their own store of food.

**Redwing.** *Winter Thrush.* Tier 2, **2/4** (unchanged body, new text).
- **Text:** Fly South. When played: Feed your other Birds 1.
- **Golden (4/8):** Fly South. When played: Feed your other Birds 1, twice.
- **Condition:** played, with Birds at home on the board. You play her after the others land, so she sets up turn order.
- **Payoff:** with five other Birds on the board, +5/+5 each at round 9 (50 stats), +12/+12 each at round 12 (120), and +20/+20 each at round 15 (200).
- **Permanence:** permanent.
- **Enabler:** the Flock route, the Whistlers, and Old Tern (land everyone, then play her). She keeps the Brood's support from being paper.
- **Concentrates:** no. She spreads, on purpose.

### Tier 3

**Jack Daw.** *Jackdaw, Collector of Other People's Things.* Tier 3, **3/4** (was 3/3; he no longer flies).
- **Text:** Start of Combat: your Chick permanently steals a quarter of the stats of the enemy across this.
- **Golden (6/8):** Start of Combat: your Chick permanently steals half the stats of the enemy across this.
- **Condition:** an enemy across Jack Daw, and a Chick on your board. You place him against the scouted board.
- **Payoff:** across their carry, the Chick keeps +5/+5 a fight at round 9, +22/+22 at round 12 and +75/+75 at round 15. Across a median unit, about a third of that. The enemy loses the same for the fight.
- **Permanence:** a combat debuff, and a permanent gain for you.
- **Enabler:** the Chick, the Lump (which takes over what he stole) and the Ratchet (the target is now smaller). He is also an answer.
- **Concentrates:** yes. It grows at the field's rate, so the bigger the leader's carry, the more Birds take from it.

**Old Tern.** *Arctic Tern, Who Has Seen Both Poles.* Tier 3, **3/4** (unchanged).
- **Text:** Fly South. When this comes home: your other Birds away come home too.
- **Golden (6/8):** Fly South, for 1 turn. When this comes home: your other Birds away come home too.
- **Condition:** her own trip ends while other Birds are away.
- **Payoff:** 2–4 homecomings at once. With the Cuckoo that's +3 to +5 bonus in one turn, plus every flier's replay. At round 12 one landing is about +4 bonus, Chiffchaff +12/+12, Redwing +12/+12 on each other Bird, and a Swallow's gold.
- **Permanence:** tempo. What she sets off is permanent.
- **Enabler:** the Cuckoo, Mrs Jay, Redwing, Mother Goose and One Swallow. Mrs Stork brings her home early, and she brings everyone else.
- **Concentrates:** neutral.

**Tom Tit.** *Blue Tit, Petty Thief.* Tier 3, **3/5** (was 3/4).
- **Text:** Your Chick takes half damage.
- **Golden (6/10):** Your two biggest Birds take half damage.
- **Condition:** a Chick, and Tom Tit alive. It's an aura, so killing him ends it.
- **Payoff:** about twice the Chick's Health in a fight: +33 effective Health at round 9, +125 at round 12, +515 at round 15. Rounding up keeps it modest against the 1–3 Attack hits of the early rounds.
- **Permanence:** combat aura, every fight.
- **Enabler:** the Chick, the Ratchet (the chain lasts longer) and Mr Redbreast.
- **Concentrates:** yes. This is what lets the Chick hold its stats, and it doesn't fly: he stays home and mobs whatever comes near the nest.

## 4. The engine and its rate

**Engine: the Feed bonus.** Every Feed gives its printed +X/+X plus N. N goes up by **k** each time a Bird comes home with the Cuckoo on the board, and by k when Mrs Jay herself comes home.

Homecomings come from:
- Fly South trips, about one every 3 turns per flier;
- Mrs Stork, one per 6 gold;
- Old Tern, everyone at once.

On a committed board that is about 1.5 a turn at rounds 8–9 and 2.5–3 a turn from round 10.

**Main rate parameter: k**, the Feed bonus per homecoming. It is printed as +1/+1 on the Cuckoo and Mrs Jay; the sweep tests ½, 1 and 2. The other dials:

| Dial | Default | Range to sweep |
|---|---|---|
| f, Jack Daw's steal | ¼ | ⅒ to ⅓ |
| m, the Lump's multiplier | ×2 | ×1.5 to ×2 |
| g, Mrs Stork's gold per homecoming | 6 | 5 to 8 |
| L, the Ratchet's chain | unlimited | 1, 2, unlimited |
| Egg mode | Health only | Health only, or half/half (pass 2) |
| The Whistlers' fraction | ⅒ | ⅒ or ⅐ |

**How it compounds.**
- N grows in a straight line with homecomings, and Feeds a turn grow with the flock. So the Chick's growth a turn is about 2 × Feeds × (X + N): quadratic in time.
- The Lump adds a ×2 step at rounds 10–12. Goldens double their Feed count.
- Jack Daw's steal follows the field's own exponential curve.
- **Similar to:** Battlegrounds' Blood Gems ("+1/+1 more this game"), but aimed at one unit, with Fly South trips as the fuel.

**My paper curve (k = 1).** Chick and board totals at fight time, against the target:

| Round | Chick, typical | Board, typical | Chick share | Board, strong line | Target: median / strong |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 6 | 25 (12/13) | ~60 | 40% | ~70 | 52–62 (today's level) |
| 9 | 66 (33/33) | ~130 | 50% | ~170 | ~120 / 150 |
| 12 | 247 (120/127) | ~435 | 57% | ~775 | ~500 / 700 |
| 15 | 1,030 (515/515) | ~1,830 | 56% | ~2,900 | ~1,700 / 2,500 |
| Low roll: no Cuckoo, no Lump | R12 ~90 Chick | R12 ~260, R15 ~650 | — | — | about half the median: behind, but the same order of magnitude |

Reading:
- The engine line sits level to round 7, a little behind at round 9, near median at 12 and above it from 13.
- k = 1 probably runs 1.3–1.5× hot for a strong scripted line from round 13. I'd sweep k = ½ first if the strong line's top tail shows up as common, not rare.
- The Chick holds 50–60% of the board, against Battlegrounds' median of 33%. That is this angle's identity, and the sweep should watch it. Holding stats is what makes it work: in the round 12 hand fight below, the same board without Tom Tit loses clearly.

**Hand check, round 12.** The typical Birds board (435 stats) against an even 7 × 36/36 (504 stats):
- The Chick is in B2 behind Egg walls, takes half damage, and has Mr Redbreast's one chain.
- Rough exchange count: Birds kill about 6 of 7, so it's close, which is what we want.
- Without Tom Tit, the Chick dies in the second cycle, and Birds lose with 3–4 enemies left.
- With the Ratchet (round 14+), the Chick sweeps any board whose units are all below its Attack.

**The turn the build comes online:** round 8–9. The Cuckoo arrives by round 7–8, two or three fliers are cycling, and the Chick is about 20/20 with half damage and a chain. It's clearly ahead from rounds 11–12 (N about 10, the Lump).

**The decision it creates, every Recruit Phase.**
1. **Who flies.** Each flight costs that Bird's Attack and text for two fights (its Egg keeps the Health). In return you get +k Feed bonus forever and a replay. Tempo now, or growth later.
2. **From which slot.** Eggs are 0-Attack walls. Flying from the front walls a back-row Chick; flying from the back keeps your front attackers.
3. **Gold.** A unit or tier now, or a 6-gold block for Mrs Stork, which is a homecoming, +k, and a replay.
4. **Turn order.** Land the flock, then play Redwing. Fly what's due, but only after it has paid this turn.
5. **Where the Chick stands, and whom the thieves face**, against the scouted board (section 6).

## 5. Routes

### The Brood (the carry route)
- **Picks a carry.** The first Bird you feed becomes the Chick: Mrs Dunnock or Chiffchaff on round 1, or Mr Redbreast. You steer it with Pinches. On rounds 10–12 the Great Lump takes over, keeping every buff.
- **Grows it:**
  - Feeds: Dunnock every turn, Chiffchaff every replay, Goose's share;
  - the bonus: Jay, the Cuckoo, and landings from Swallow, Stork and Tern;
  - steals: Jack Daw;
  - the Lump's doubling.
- **Protects it:**
  - Tom Tit (half damage);
  - Mr Redbreast (it finishes exchanges on its own terms);
  - Egg walls in front;
  - Mag Pye, who takes the text off the answer aimed at it;
  - placement against what you've scouted.
- **Finishes:** the Gabble Ratchet. The Chick duels their biggest unit, in either row, and keeps killing.
- **Core cards:** mrs_dunnock, chiffchaff, mr_redbreast, mrs_jay, tom_tit, jack_daw, cuckoo, old_tern, mrs_stork, great_lump, gabble_ratchet.

### The Flock (the non-carry route)
- Redwing and Mother Goose spread every Feed over the whole board. The Chick exists, but it's only 25–30% of the board.
- Its answer is breadth: the Seven Whistlers across their carry, with Mag Pye and Jack Daw beside them.
- Same engine (Jay, the Cuckoo, Stork, Tern). The ceiling is lower, but the board is harder to break with one answer.
- **When to take it:** the lobby shows carry answers (Changeling, Lady Belladonna, Hasty Pudding, Madame Bufo), or Redwing and Goose turn up before Tom Tit and the Lump.
- **Core cards:** one_swallow, mrs_jay, redwing, old_tern, cuckoo, mrs_stork, mother_goose, seven_whistlers, mag_pye.

The two routes share eight cards, so the shop decides the lean, not a plan made on round 1 (owner, 08f: discovery over formula).

## 6. Placement is the decision

| Card | What its slot decides |
|---|---|
| The Chick | **Front (F1):** attacks first, soaks hits, is safe from back-row reach. **Back (B2):** behind Egg walls, but open to Swoop, Ballooning Spider and Whirligig. Never across a scouted Changeling, Hasty Pudding, Belladonna or Mire. |
| Fliers | Their Egg is a wall where they flew from. Front Eggs shield a back Chick. An Egg in F1 never attacks, so the order starts at F2. |
| Jack Daw, Mag Pye, the Seven Whistlers | Each wants to be across the scouted carry. They compete for the same slot: Daw across the carry, Pye across their engine, the Whistlers across the second-biggest, or the reverse. |
| Tom Tit, Mr Redbreast | Back row. Kill them and the Chick loses its half damage or chain. |
| Redwing | Not a slot question but an order question: play her when the flock is home. |

## 7. Answers to other tribes' carries

| Their carry | Bird answer |
|---|---|
| **Hedgerow:** a Pass or Dozey carry, usually F4 or B3 (Pass lands last in the zigzag) | Jack Daw or the Whistlers in that slot. The Ratchet reaches B3 regardless. Mag Pye on a Pass source cuts its feed for the fight. |
| **Fairies:** a carry on stacked Rings, or the Queen's shares | Proportional steals and cuts ignore the Ring cap's shape. Mag Pye takes the Monarch's or Reel-Mistress's text. |
| **Hunters:** Nip or Lutra (permanent), the Glutton (fight-grown) | Jack Daw steals the permanent part at Start of Combat, before Takes happen. The Ratchet kills a Glutton before it grows. |
| **Oozes:** Granny Stir-Up's Attack pile (8 Health), Stone Soup | The Ratchet sends the Chick at Granny, who is the biggest by Attack + Health, and her 8 Health dies to one hit. Jack Daw on Soup. |
| **Bog:** the Lantern Man's Attack per Mired enemy | Mag Pye takes it, so your Chick gets +3 Attack per Mired enemy. Mire still forces the Chick's target, which is Bog's answer back. |

**Weak points.**
- **One target.** Changeling across the Chick turns it on your own board. Belladonna's Nightshade halves it every hit, and Hasty Pudding swallows it. An Ichneumon Wasp or a Whirligig row swap gets at it too. The counter is placement and the Flock route.
- **Back-row reach.** Killing Tom Tit removes the half damage mid-fight.
- **Tempo at rounds 7–9,** while fliers are away: Eggs have no Attack, so the board makes fewer attacks.
- **Breakpoints.** A Chick whose Attack is below their carry's Health stalls. Above it, it sweeps. Fights swing on that line.
- **Supply.** Scaling needs Mrs Jay or the Cuckoo. Without them, Feeds stay +1/+1 and the curve is flat. A second Bird player starves both.
- **Bog's Mire** forces the Chick's target, even under the Ratchet.

## 8. Two highroll moments

1. **"Everybody home" (round 9–10).**
   - Setup: Old Tern lands with four Birds away and the Cuckoo on the board.
   - Five homecomings: the Feed bonus goes from 4 to 9 in one turn.
   - You replay Chiffchaff (+10/+10 to the Chick), the Swallow pays, and Redwing lands last: +10/+10 on every Bird at home.
   - With Dunnock's End of Turn on top, the Chick goes from 35/35 to about 65/65, and every Feed after that is five bigger.
2. **"The Lump shoves" (rounds 11–14).**
   - The Great Lump takes 90/90 off Mrs Dunnock, who drops back to 1/2.
   - Dunnock's next Feed lands doubled. Goose comes home and feeds it doubled again.
   - Two turns later the Ratchet sends a 260/260 Lump, taking half damage, at their back-row 180/180 carry. It kills the carry and chains through three more.

A third, rarer one: a golden Jack Daw across a 300/300 carry steals 150/150 for good, and their carry fights at half size.

**Reserve: Barn Owl** (tier 4, 3/4, portrait exists, out of the pool). "Swoop. Takes no damage while attacking the back row." If the sweep wants a back-row answer, or a carry that holds while attacking, it swaps in for Mrs Stork in a Flock-only test. Or its clause becomes golden Ratchet's.

## 11. Loops and runaway checks
- **Stork and Swallow:** each 6 gold returns 1, and a landed Bird can't fly that turn. No loop.
- **Tern and the Cuckoo:** one burst per Tern trip, bounded by the Birds away.
- **Jay:** only a homecoming counts, so buying and selling her at once gives nothing.
- **Lump on Lump:** buffs move, never copy.
- **The Ratchet's chain:** it ends on a failed kill or a death, at most 7 kills.
- **Goose:** she lands at most every 2 turns, because she must fight once at home.
- **The Feed bonus has no cap.** It is limited by homecomings, about 3 a turn at most on a committed board, and k is the lever. If the sweep shows the Brood's top 1% as common, cut k before adding any cap.
- **Snowball watch for the sweep:** the share of round 12–14 fights decided in the Chick's first turn, and how the Chick's Attack compares with the field's biggest Health. That breakpoint is where Birds fights swing.
