# Pass 2: buy-moment costing (9 Nov 2026)

A fresh cloud reviewer, on `ideation/main` c9ddc52. Targets: `content/pass2-a.md`, `pass2-b.md`, `pass2-c.md`. Method as `birds-pass1-costing.md`: V(t) 5/7/8/10/12/14, ±15% (±25% for scaling and gold), priced on the board at purchase, against the live slice pool. Scaling: c(t) and c_combat(t) from power-budget 2.2, against the growth caps (T2 1.3, T3 1.7, T4 2.5, T5 3.8 SP a turn). Take rate 0.55 (`costing-3.md`). Hand estimates, no sims.

**Counts: 10 cut, 11 ask; the other 14 cards and rules are in band.** Parity is met in count by the three docs together (every tribe 16, 3/3/3/3/2/2). Most excess is permanent growth above the per-tier caps, plus one wording (Mrs Stork) that reopens a loop.

**Cut items (change before build)**
1. **Squire Lollop** (T6): +24 SP a turn on a full board, six times the cap. Dominant.
2. **Mrs Mallow** (T4): 3.6 SP a turn to any tribe's board, over the T4 cap. +50–100%.
3. **Beadle Warrenby** (T4): up to 4 SP a turn; Squirrel Away makes it gold-neutral +2/+2 a turn. +80%.
4. **Stone Soup** (T2): self-growth up to 4 SP a turn, three times the T2 cap. +77%.
5. **The Sixpenny Pie** (T3): uncapped 1–2 gold a death, over the T3 recurring-gold cap. +100%.
6. **Figgy Duff** (T4): a T4 golden-and-T5-Discover engine once a turn. +54%. Take the designer's fallback.
7. **Elder Ooze** (T5): +2/+2 on 4–5 units a fight on top of a par 4/8. +90% on plan.
8. **Signor Ranocchio** (T4): +3.4 SP a fight permanent, at the combat cap on a T4 body. +61%.
9. **Mrs Stork's new text**: dropping "must have missed a fight" lets Mother Goose fly and come home every turn (+1/+1 to all Birds a turn).
10. **Young Brock 2/3** (T1): the best T1 for every tribe. +48%.
