# Playtest pt4-a: build 6 (927b0b7)

| Seed | Hero | Build | Place |
|---|---|---|---|
| 101 | Pipkin | Hunters (Quarry/Pack) | 8 (out R10) |
| 202 | Mousewife | Bog folk (Mire/Snap) | 3 |
| 303 | Jill | Birds (Fly South) | 8 (out R8) |
| 405 | Farthing | Economy, Charcoal Burner | 4 |
| 507 | Hob | Rainbow Fairies, Old Tern, One Swallow | 6 |

**Verdict: okay.**

**Best:** lane play. Scout, then put a mirer, Hasty Pudding, Changeling or Belladonna across a chosen threat: a real decision most turns from tier 3. Bog folk stands out. Changeling is a nice trap: Rings stay on slots, so swapping a ringed enemy gave them a 16/14.

**Top 5 problems**
1. **Losing streaks end games too early.** In games 1 and 3 I lost 6 or 7 in a row from round 3 or 4 and was out by round 8 to 10, at 4 to 12 a loss. No room to pivot. *Alone stops 'very good'.*
2. **One slot snowballs.** Bots stack Rings (+6/+6, +12/+12) and lone carries (Chaser 15/15, Sorrel 19/21) faster than tribe boards answer. Fairy Ring stacking looks dominant.
3. **Hero gap.** Mousewife won 5 of my first 6 fights; bot Mousewife led 3 of the 4 lobbies it was in.
4. **Small-Bird flights are free, and Rainbow fights Fly South:** a flight empties the slot and fades its band.
5. **Hunters are weak early:** tier-1 bodies lose to Wobblies. Minor: one triple Discover offered only 2 options.

**Builds that felt really different: 3** (Bog, Hunters, late Burner economy). Birds and Rainbow felt thin.

**Answers**
1. **What I did that the bots don't:** after scouting, moved mirers and Pudding to face their biggest unit. Borrowed with Hob to finish an early golden. Timed Burner banking for the final four. Bots don't aim, but they out-stack.
2. **Burner:** a real choice only late. In the final four with Farthing, banking 14 (+3/+3 to two units before the fight, plus 8 carried for +2 gold) against tier 6 was a genuine trade. Earlier it was dead: every gold went to survival.
3. **Small Birds:** free. Mrs Jay's flight even paid an Acorn. Old Tern swapped a 2/3 for an Egg and a future Discover. One Swallow gave +1 gold next to the Tern. Nothing to weigh.
4. **Last four:** tense, not drawn out (rounds 12 to 15). Uncapped losses hit for 11 to 18. Last Stand saved me in games 2 and 4, and I died the next turn both times. Game 2 gave a real recovery turn (golden Belladonna, Whirligig); game 4 only a Burner bank. Mostly a one-turn delay.

Verdict: okay.
