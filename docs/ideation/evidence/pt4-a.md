# Playtest pt4-a: build 6 (927b0b7), strategy lens

Five full games with `tools/play/play.sh` against the full-strength bots.

| Game | Seed | Hero | Build | Place |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 101 | Pipkin | Hunters: Quarry/Pack, two Harvest Suppers | 8 (out R10) |
| 2 | 202 | Mousewife | Bog folk: Mire/Snap, Prince Restored, Peat King | 3 |
| 3 | 303 | Jill | Birds: Fly South, Lump, Redbreast | 8 (out R8) |
| 4 | 405 | Farthing | Economy: Money Spider, Charcoal Burner, golden Wobbly | 4 |
| 5 | 507 | Hob | Rainbow Fairies + Old Tern/One Swallow, drifted Bog | 6 |

**Verdict: okay.** Not good yet.

**What's best.** Lane play. Scout, then put a mirer, Hasty Pudding, Changeling or Belladonna across a specific threat: that's a real decision most turns from tier 3. Bog folk is the standout tribe: Mire, Snap and Jenny's follow-up read clearly in the log, and His Highness turning into the Prince felt earned. Rings on slots made Changeling a trap I learned the hard way: the Ring stays with the slot, so swapping a ringed enemy gave them a 16/14.

**Top 5 problems, in order**
1. **Losing streaks end games too early.** In games 1 and 3 I lost 6 or 7 fights in a row from round 3 or 4 and was out by round 8 to 10. Losses cost 4 to 12 each, so builds never came online and there was no room to pivot. *This alone stops 'very good'.*
2. **One slot snowballs.** Bots stack Rings (+6/+6, +12/+12) and lone carries (Rainbow Chaser 15/15, Sorrel 19/21) faster than tribe boards can answer. Fairies' Ring stacking looks dominant.
3. **Hero gap.** Mousewife (a tier-sized Mouse per front-row death) won 5 of my first 6 fights, and the bot Mousewife led three of the four lobbies it was in. Pipkin's two-buy cap hurt badly.
4. **Small-Bird flights are free, and Rainbow fights Fly South.** A flight empties the slot and fades its band.
5. **Hunters are weak early.** Tier-1 Hunter bodies lose to Wobblies, and Quarry only pays off at tier 4 and up. Also: one triple Discover offered only 2 options, and golden Hedge Witch shows "Hex 12" next to "-2 per spell".

**Builds that felt really different: 3.** Bog (Mire/Snap), Hunters (Quarry/Pack) and late Burner economy. Birds and Rainbow felt thin.

**Answers**
1. *What I did that the bots don't:* moved mirers and Pudding to face the enemy's biggest unit after scouting; borrowed with Hob to finish an early golden; timed Burner banking for the final four. The bots still out-built me on raw stats. They don't aim, but they stack.
2. *Charcoal Burner:* a real choice only late. At the final four with Farthing, banking 14 (+3/+3 to two units, and 8 carried for +2 gold) against tier 6 was a genuine trade. Before that, every gold went to survival, so it was a dead card. Note that the burn happens before the fight, so it's tempo, not just savings.
3. *Small Birds:* free. Mrs Jay's flight even paid an Acorn. Old Tern lost a 2/3 for an Egg and would have returned with a Discover. One Swallow gave +1 gold once, next to the Tern. None of these had a cost I had to weigh.
4. *Last four:* tense, not drawn out (rounds 12 to 15). With the cap lifted, losses hit for 11 to 18. Last Stand saved me once in games 2 and 4, and I died the next turn both times. Game 2 offered a real recovery turn (golden Belladonna, Whirligig); game 4 only a Burner bank. Mostly it was one turn of delay against boards far bigger than mine.

Verdict: okay.
