# Playtest pt4-b: build 6 (927b0b7), newcomer lens

Five games with `tools/play/play.sh`, learning only from the game's text, then from its own `log`.

**Places:** 5, 7, 8, 1, 5. Builds: Hunters + Supper; Bog folk + Burner; Birds Fly South (Hob); Fairies Rings + Hex; Oozes Belly + Rings.

**Verdict: okay.** In the middle of a game, most turns give a real choice between tiering, stats, banking and where to stand. The first five rounds rarely do. As a newcomer I lost three games before I understood why.

**What's best:** fights are easy to follow and full of interplay (Hasty Pudding swallowing my Queen, then Whirligig swapping its rows). The next opponent's board makes positioning matter. The shop explains triples at the right moment ("Buying this makes a golden..."). Old Tern's homecoming and Queen's Teind counter feel good.

## Top 5 problems
1. **Losing streaks end games, and nothing teaches the basics.** From round 7 a loss costs 8 to 12 damage: G1 went from 30 to 3 Health in three rounds, and G2 lost its first six fights. I only learned "fill the board early, sit on a tier, spend everything" by reading bot boards in `log`. *Alone, this stops "very good".*
2. **Rings dominate.** Boundary Stone costs 2 gold and adds +3/+3 that stacks on a slot. With a golden Nan the slot reached +6/+6, and the Triple Reward unit stood on a +4. Spore Cloud lets any tribe do it. My only win was nine straight fights on Rings. *Alone, this stops "very good".*
3. **Rules the game never states.** Behind, beside, next to and touching are undefined: I tested to learn that B1 and B2 stand behind F2. "At the bell", "cycle", Pack's "on your turn" and `*` (golden) aren't explained. The Pass keyword says End of Turn, but Mr Crumb passes when played. Income and sell value are never shown, and Rainy Day's timing is unclear.
4. **Mutual wipes look like bugs.** Last Blow decided three of my fights. The result reads "You lost, 10 damage / Survivors:" with nothing after it, and Last Blow isn't named.
5. **The last four drag.** In G4 (rounds 12 to 16) two bots sat on 1 Health after Last Stand, plus a ghost round: four fights with no decisions for me at 26 Health. In G5 three heroes fell in one round, from six left to three, so Last Stand never applied to me at 11 Health.

**Builds that felt really different: 3.** Rings with Hex, Mire lanes, and the Fly South loop. Hunter quarry played itself, and Belly felt like storage.

## Answers
1. **What I did that bots don't:** I fielded and banked for Charcoal Burner (the logs show bots almost never field it). I stacked Stones on a Ringed slot and put a Triple Reward tier-6 unit on it. I grew Hex 1 to 8 with cheap spells. I flew Jay every other turn with Dunnock on its Nest. Bots fill the board and spend gold faster than I did early on.
2. **Burner:** a real choice once (G2 round 9). I kept 13 gold for +3/+3 on two Pretenders and carried 7 instead of tiering, and won that fight. Otherwise it's weak: from F1 or F4 it feeds one unit, and F2 and F3 were taken by my Ring slots. Last Orders and Beltane Bonfire do the same job without the position. It's useful, not automatic, and often too costly.
3. **Small Birds:** effectively free. Jay earns an Acorn every other turn, Dunnock on the Nest shortens the trip and Redbreast grows per Nest; the cost is a 1/1 Egg. Old Tern costs 3 gold, came back after 3 turns with a tier-5 Discover, then flew again: the best-value card I saw. One Swallow never came up.
4. **The end:** drawn out, not tense. The bots' Last Stands only delayed the end and gave me no decision. Mine was never ready when it would have mattered.

Verdict: okay.
