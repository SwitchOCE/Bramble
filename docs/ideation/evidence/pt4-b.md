# Playtest pt4-b: build 6 (927b0b7), newcomer

**Places:** 5, 7, 8, 1, 5 (Hunters; Bog folk + Burner; Birds; Fairies Rings + Hex; Oozes + Rings).

**Verdict: okay.** Mid-game turns hold real choices (tier, stats, bank, positioning); rounds 1 to 5 rarely do. I lost three games before I understood why.

**Best:** fights are easy to read and full of interplay. The next opponent's board makes positioning matter.

## Top 5 problems
1. **Losing streaks end games, and nothing teaches the basics.** From round 7 a loss costs 8 to 12 damage. G1 went from 30 to 3 Health in three rounds; G2 lost six fights straight. I only learned "fill the board early, sit on a tier" from `log`. *Alone, this stops "very good".*
2. **Rings dominate.** Boundary Stone: 2 gold for a stacking +3/+3 slot; golden Nan took one to +6/+6. Spore Cloud lets any tribe do it. My only win was nine straight fights on Rings. *Alone, this stops "very good".*
3. **Unstated rules.** Behind, beside and touching are undefined (I tested to learn that B1 and B2 stand behind F2). "Bell", "cycle" and `*` aren't explained. The Pass keyword says End of Turn, but Mr Crumb passes when played. Income and sell value are never shown.
4. **Mutual wipes look like bugs.** Last Blow decided three of my fights; the result reads "You lost / Survivors:" with nothing after it.
5. **The last four drag.** In G4, two bots sat on 1 Health after Last Stand, plus a ghost round: four fights with no decisions for me. In G5 three heroes fell in one round, so Last Stand never applied.

**Really different builds: 3** (Rings with Hex, Mire, the Fly South loop). Quarry played itself, and Belly felt like storage.

## Answers
1. I banked for Charcoal Burner (bots almost never field it), stacked Stones and parked a tier-6 unit on the Ring, grew Hex to 8 with cheap spells, and cycled Jay with Dunnock on its Nest. Bots out-tempo me early.
2. **Burner:** a real choice once (kept 13 gold for +3/+3 on two units and carried 7; won). Otherwise weak: F1 or F4 feeds one unit, Rings held F2 and F3, and Last Orders and Beltane Bonfire need no position. Useful, often too costly.
3. **Small Birds:** effectively free. Jay earns an Acorn every other turn, Dunnock shortens the trip and Redbreast grows; the cost is a 1/1 Egg. Old Tern (3 gold) came home with a tier-5 Discover and flew again: the best value I saw.
4. **The end:** drawn out, not tense. The bots' Last Stands only delayed things; mine was never ready when it mattered.

Verdict: okay.
