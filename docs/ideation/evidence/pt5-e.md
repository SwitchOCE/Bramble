# Playtest pt5-e: economy player, build 7 (b2c3f01)

Five games against the bots, seeds 501 to 505.

**Places:** 8th (Hob borrowing), **1st** (Partners and Stashes into two Treasurers, then Bonfire), 4th (Pipkin, Bog Mire with a Burner-fed back row), 7th (Hunters gold), 4th (Rainbow Chaser bank). Average 4.8.

**Verdict: okay.** Two games were genuinely good. Three were bleed-outs in which my gold never became a board.

**Best:** cash-outs. 22 gold of Acorns became +5/+5 on my front row with Bonfire. A 7-band Chaser sold for 13 took me from tier 2 to 4 in round 6. Treasurer wants Acorns held while Burner wants gold spent.

## Top 5 problems
1. **Gold-only units are a trap.** Busker, Nip, Toll-Bard, Money Spider, Bridge Toad and Hob's loan pay 1 or 2 gold on a 1/2 body. Losses cost 4 to 9 Health, so I was under 15 by round 8 in three games. *This alone stops 'very good'.*
2. **Knockouts come in bursts.** Three heroes went out in one round twice. The lobby went from 7 to 4 and the midgame vanished.
3. **Rainbow Chaser looks overtuned.** It was an 8/9 tier 1 unit by round 5 that also banks 13 gold. A bot's Chaser reached 22/29.
4. **Burner alone isn't a real choice.** At 2 gold for +1/+1 on two units it's just where leftover gold goes. It also burns odd gold: from 8 gold it burned 3 for +1/+1.
5. **Shy is invisible.** It's on one card, buffing it repays 2 of the 3 gold, and I never had a reason to use it.

## Builds that felt different
Acorns, Treasurer and Bonfire. Chaser banking with repainted bands. A Bog back row grown by the Burner.

## Lens
- **Economy:** It's a real path only through cards that are both gold and stats (Chaser, Partners feeding a Treasurer). Pure gold makers are a trap.
- **Bank versus buy:** It's real with Treasurer against Burner or Bonfire, and with when to sell the Chaser.
- **Losing streaks:** Still too fast. A hero on 40 Health was out in round 8.
- **Last Stand:** It fired four times, and each time bought exactly one round with no comeback. Game 2's final rounds were tense.

**(P)** In game 2, round 12, Hoolet's Bridge Toad moved from F2 to F4. My Belladonna at B2 Nightshaded the wrong pair, and I lost by 15. In round 15 I moved her to B1 and added a second at B3, which covers all four front slots, and won by 22.

**(B)** I held Stashes and then cashed them with Bonfire, and I sold and rebought the Chaser. Bots never cash their Chaser.
