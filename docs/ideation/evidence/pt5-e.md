# Playtest pt5-e: economy player, build 7 (b2c3f01)

Five full games against the bot lobby, with `tools/play/play.sh`, seeds 501 to 505.

**Places:** 8th (Hob, borrowing and Snail/Money Spider), **1st** (Mousewife, Sleeping Partners and Stashes into two Treasurers, then Beltane Bonfire), 4th (Pipkin, Bog Mire with a Burner feeding the back row), 7th (Farthing, Hunters gold: Nip, Toll-Bard, Busker), 4th (Wren, Rainbow Chaser bank). Average 4.8.

**Verdict: okay.** Two games were genuinely good. Three were bleed-outs in which my gold never turned into a board.

**What's best:** cash-out moments. These were: 22 gold of held Acorns turned into +5/+5 on the front row with Beltane Bonfire; a 7-band Chaser sold for 13, which took me from tier 2 to tier 4 in round 6; and a Treasurer that wants Acorns held while the Burner wants gold spent. Whirligig moving enemies onto and off Rings was a delightful surprise.

## Top 5 problems
1. **Gold-only units are a trap.** Busker, Nip, Toll-Bard, Money Spider, Bridge Toad and Hob's loan pay 1 or 2 gold on a 1/2 body. Each loss costs 4 to 9 Health, so I was under 15 Health by round 8 in games 1, 3 and 4. Gold engines only worked when the engine was also a body: the Chaser, or Partners feeding a Treasurer. *This alone stops 'very good'.*
2. **Knockouts come in bursts.** Three heroes went out in one round twice (game 2, round 11; game 4, round 10). The lobby jumped from 7 to 4 heroes, and the middle of the game vanished.
3. **Rainbow Chaser looks overtuned.** By round 5 it was an 8/9 tier 1 unit that also banks 13 gold. A bot's Chaser reached 22/29 and beat me.
4. **Burner's bank-versus-buy choice is weak on its own.** At 2 gold for +1/+1 on two units, it trades about the same as a Boundary Stone or Bonfire, so it was just where leftover gold went. It also burns odd gold (from 8 gold it burned 3 for +1/+1).
5. **Shy is invisible.** It's on one card, and buffing that card pays back 2 of the 3 gold it cost. I never had a reason to use it.

## Builds that felt really different
Acorns, Treasurer and Bonfire. The Chaser bank with repainting. A Bog back row fed by the Burner, which Pipkin's two-buy limit made natural. Hunters gold felt like a weaker stats build.

## Lens
- **Economy, real path or trap?** It's real only through dual-purpose cards. Pure gold makers are a trap.
- **Bank versus buy:** It's real with Treasurer against Burner or Bonfire, and with when to sell the Chaser. With the Burner alone it's automatic.
- **Losing streaks:** Still too fast. A 40-Health hero went out in round 8 (game 1), and the burst knockouts make it worse.
- **Last Stand:** It fired four times that I saw, for the March Hare, Hoolet, me and Wren. Each time it bought exactly one round, and nobody came back. Game 2, rounds 12 to 15, was tense. My 1-Health recovery turn in game 3 was a real decision, but it was hopeless against a 15-damage loss.

**(P)** In game 2, round 12, Hoolet moved Bridge Toad from F2 to F4. My Belladonna at B2 Nightshaded the wrong pair, and I lost by 15. In round 15 I moved Belladonna to B1 and added a second one at B3, which covers all four front slots, and won by 22. In game 4, a Hedge Witch's Hex of -14 on my B1 zeroed my Lodger there. I hadn't checked which of my slots it was across from.

**(B)** I held Stashes for the Treasurer and then cashed them with Bonfire, and I sold and rebought the Chaser. Bots keep their Chaser and never cash it.
