# Playtest pt5-c: one game per tribe (build 7, b2c3f01)

Fresh tester, `play.sh`, full-strength bots, seeds 101 and 202–206.

**Places:** Hedgerow 6 · Bog folk **1** · Oozes 7 · Fairies **2** · Birds 7 · Hunters 7.

**Verdict: okay.** Two tribes carry the game; two feel like traps.

## Best
- **Rainbow (Fairies):** painting turns the board into a puzzle. To swap a unit, sell it and replay into the same slot. 12–0 start.
- **Bog folk:** three kisses turned golden His Highness into a 12/14 Prince Restored. I won from 1 Health.
- **Changeling:** swaps with the enemy across; Rings stay with the slot. A readable answer to one huge unit.

## Top 5 problems
1. **Uneven tribe power.** Hedgerow, Oozes and Hunters units are 1–3 Attack bodies that don't grow before tier 5. Committing lost 4–6 mid-game fights a game. Rainbow Chaser topped every lobby (34/38 on a bot). *Alone stops very good.*
2. **Birds are a trap.** One Bird in five shops, rounds 4–6. Most are vanilla; a Fly South return gives little. *Alone stops very good.*
3. **Last-four swings:** 40 Health to out in four fights (14, 19, 22, 18) after a 12–0 start.
4. **Text wrong or hidden** (W).
5. **Silent no-room failures:** the Mousewife hero and Wee Wobbly do nothing when the slot is taken, which full boards make common.

## Builds that felt different
Rainbow painting · Mire with Snap · Changeling and Hasty Pudding as lane answers · banking for Charcoal Burner, then Bonfire.

## Lens
- **Hedgerow:** a grab-bag (Pass, Spill, Acorns, Bristle). Its payoff is late and real for a bot: stacked Committees, a 46/52 Dozey.
- **Bog folk:** clearest identity and payoff, but before tier 3 only Bridge Toad has Mire.
- **Oozes:** Belly is fiddly and Aunt Aspic slow; Hasty Pudding is great.
- **Fairies:** strongest, probably too strong.
- **Hunters:** readable quarry lines, Take gold, small bodies.
- **Thinnest:** Birds. **Trap:** Birds, then Hunters.

**(P)** Placement won three fights. Changeling at B1 took a 34/38 Chaser off its +9/+9 Ring in the G2 final. Changeling faced a 46/52 Dozey in G4. Pudding swallowed a 9/3 Burdock in G3. It lost two. Sable's quarry was a Hex-8 Witch, which cut it to 0 Attack (G6). Ichneumon Wasp across a Chaser was wasted, because normal attacks pick a random target.

**(B)** Repainting by sell and replay, moving Changeling to face the biggest Ringed unit, Dunnock on the Nest, and saving 30+ gold for Bonfire or Last Orders.

**(W)**
- *Queen of Elfland:* "split their stats" is wrong; Fairies share the *destroyed* units' stats. Her Teind also killed my new Monarch.
- *Hasty Pudding:* that swallowed units go back to their side is only in DETAILS.
- *The Mountain Glutton:* is its +2/+2 permanent?
- *Fight log:* says "bogs" where the cards say Mire.
- *Hedge Witch:* Hex 8 from tier 1 surprised me.
