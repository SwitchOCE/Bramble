# Playtest 3-b: a newcomer's five games (d27abb7)

Learned only from How to play (guide pages 1-9), card text and the keyword lines, then PLAYTEST.md after playing. `tools/play/play.sh`, phone renders at 1688x780 (recruit, scout, combat mid and end).

| Game | Level | Hero | Place |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Easy | Brother Bracken | 7 (rounds 7-12 played by my own scripted buy-and-tier policy, which never stacked stats) |
| 2 | Easy | Pipkin | 5 (lost 10 fights in a row, then won 3) |
| 3 | Normal | Hob Mumblecrust | 6 |

**Verdict: okay.**

**Best:** the brick-wall board. Rows, lanes, Mire and Scout make where units stand matter. Fight logs explain every hit. Big turns pay off: seven Rainbow bands, a golden Old Mother Mast with Last Orders, a golden Charcoal Burner burning gold. Game 4's duel was tense, and I wanted another game.

**Top 5 problems, worst first:**

1. **The difficulty ladder isn't real to a newcomer.** By round 10 Easy bots had Wick 14/20 and Young Pretender 17/25. I lost 10 straight on Easy, then finished 2nd on Normal. My results followed what I'd learned, not the level. *On its own, this stops "very good".*
2. **The guide skips how the game works.** Nine pages cover the board, rows and tribes, but not gold per turn, tiering up, refresh or freeze, spells, hero powers, or above all how units grow, which is what wins. Then come 20-odd keywords. Shy is a trap: my Stand Behind Me made a Hob-in-the-Hearth I'd just bought walk out. *On its own, this stops "very good".*
3. **Power curve.** Tier-6 offers (Seven Whistlers 3/3, Figgy Duff 3/4, Cut-and-Come-Again 4/4) are weaker than a Ringed tier-1 unit. A 6-gold Hiring Fair felt like a loss. Late fights go to whoever stacked one unit (Queen of Elfland 38/42 on a +15/+15 Ring).
4. **It doesn't look like one finished game yet.** Shop cards with long text drop their portrait (Hedge Witch, Sexton Beetle, Natterjack Chorus). Natterjack's name overprints its text on phone. In combat, the This hit panel is an empty purple box at the start, and Mire/Hex tags cover names and "EMPTY". Guide page 8's diagram overlaps its own labels.
5. **Small wrongnesses.** I fought my own hero in three lobbies (Pipkin, Hob, Mousewife). The Whirligig log says "enemy X moves to the enemy's B1". A spell in hand shows its base cost after a discount (Harvest Supper paid 3, shows 4). Pipistrelle Echo echoes Last Orders for 0 gold: correct by the rules, but it reads as a bug.

No crashes or rule errors. The render script failed once because it raced the first import; the retry worked.

Verdict: okay.
