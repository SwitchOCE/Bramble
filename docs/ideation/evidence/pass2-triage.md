# Pass 2: triage review

8 Nov 2026, a fresh cloud reviewer, on `ideation/c-pass2-triage` from `ideation/main`. Targets: `content/pass2-a.md` (Hunters, Hedgerow folk), `pass2-b.md` (Oozes, Bog folk), `pass2-c.md` (Fairies, Birds T3, early rounds, Lamp Moth), against `briefs/pass2-design.md`. Method: `.claude/skills/triage/SKILL.md` steps 3–5. Competition is every `set: "slice"`, `pool` not false unit in `content/units/` with the three docs' changes applied, not each designer's own list. I checked rules against `docs/design/rulebook.md` (9.4.5 attack pointer, 12.1.4 Eggs, 4.4 refresh, 6.1.1 shop size) and the tribe docs (`hunters.md` Pack, `hedgerow.md` Acorns). I didn't run the sim: none of these cards are in the engine. Board numbers are hand estimates.

**Verdict.** The brief's problems mostly move. Every tribe is at 16, 3/3/3/3/2/2 (I counted the pool plus the docs). Hunters' T1s and Russet are now par bodies, Hedgerow has a grower at T2, T4, T5 and T6, Oozes' T6 pile-up is gone, Birds' T3 has a card per route, Shy is no longer an accident, and Pass can't land on itself. No new card is a trap in its window. **3 cut, 11 ask.**

**Cut**
1. Figgy Duff hides its "bought copies only" rule in Details: the Stork mistake again.
2. Mrs Mallow's "two smallest units" picks Shy units, whose buff then silently vanishes (A and C conflict).
3. The Mire wait doesn't say the waiting mirer loses its attack for the cycle. Under the other reading the fix doesn't work, and the reminder promises "That enemy swings first".

**Ask**: listed in §7 with my recommendation for each.

**Stork (ask 2).** "Every 6 gold you spend: the Bird away longest comes home." With one Bird away, the Bird flown this turn comes home the same turn. 12.1.4 stops it flying again that turn, so there's no loop. But a Swallow or Chiffchaff now cycles *every* turn (fly, spend 6, land, replay), not every other turn, and never spends a fight as an Egg. That doubles the pass 1 costing's "≤1 gold a turn per Swallow" and makes Chiffchaff +1/+1 a turn. It also takes the cost out of Homecoming, and that is part of why the slump eases. Costing should price it. It's an ask, not a cut, because the text is honest.

**Old Tern (ask 3).** "Fly South. When this comes home: your other Birds away come home too." Trips are a fixed 2 turns, so Birds flown on the same turn as the Tern come home together anyway. The doc's own example (fly Tern, Swallow and Chiffchaff on round 6) gains nothing from the text. It works only with Dunnock beside its Nest, a Stork pull, or Birds flown a turn after it. It isn't a trap, because the 3/4 body is par, but its primary job is often blank.

## 4. Conflicts between the docs and with other rules

- **Mallow and Shy (cut 2).** Mallow's "two smallest units" will often choose a Shy unit (Hob 1/4 is small). C's Shy rule says other units' text gives Shy units nothing, so the buff fizzles silently, and silent fizzles are part of tally item 1 (14 reports). Fix: Mallow's details say "Units that can't gain stats (Shy) are skipped." Lollop and Agnes don't select units, so they don't need this.
- **The Mire wait and 9.4.5 (cut 3).** The doc says "it passes, and the next unit in its side's order attacks instead. Once a fight." It should say: "It skips its attack this cycle (9.4.5 flags it)." The new reminder, "That enemy swings first," promises more than the rule gives: a Stalled or 0-Attack target, or a mirer that is the last unit able to attack, breaks it. Better: "Start of Combat: the enemy across must attack this while this lives. This waits for it, once." That's 17 words, and it drops the Details line.
- **Two orders on one board.** Pass reads the Hedgerow zigzag (F1-B1-F2-B2…), while attacks and triggers use reading order (1.2.3: F1–F4, then B1–B3). This existed before, but "your last unit" now carries all of the tribe's Passes. The permanent Pass mark is what makes it safe, so build the mark with the rule, not later.
- **"Twice a turn."** pass2-a defines a turn as "one Recruit Phase plus the fight after it". pass2-b's Stone Soup uses the phrase without a definition. Use one definition, in the rulebook.
- **Stale competition lists.** pass2-c lists Little Nip as 2/2, and its Lamp Moth decision names Field Vole, which isn't in the slice pool. pass2-a's T3 list omits the Lodger. None of these changes a verdict.
- **Elder Ooze and Hasty Pudding.** "Units coming out of your Oozes gain +2/+2" would buff an enemy that comes out of a dead Pudding. The details need one line on which side it lands and whether it's buffed.
- **Early Spoils and the snowball (tally 2, top problem).** "Win, get 1 gold" pays the early winner, and audit §4 found streaks start at R3–4. It's capped at 4 gold and Rainy Day pays losers, so this isn't a conflict, but the damage sim should check first-out and comeback rates with it on.

## 5. Wording

The new text is short and mostly reads well: Isegrim, Pym, Hayward, Silky, Tom Tit, Jack Daw, the Nest reminder (16 words) and the Shy reminder (15). Beyond the cuts:
- **Golden Shy units** pay 4, but the Shy reminder says 2. Put "Shy pays 4 gold" on the golden card, not only in golden details.
- **The Pass reminder** spells out the zigzag. On the card, "Pass X: your last unit, other than this, gains X" is enough, with the zigzag and the mark carried in the UI and Details. "If it's implied, let it be implied."
- **Ask for Kin:** "Neutral units don't count as a tribe" repeats what "share a tribe" already says. Cut it.
- **Agnes:** if the dead half of the grant stays, players will try F2 and wonder why F3 doesn't join. "The unit after this in the row has Pack and +1 Attack" says what happens (ask 4).

## 6. Process cause

Three designers worked in parallel, each ranking against the pre-pass benchmark and each with a competition list taken before the others' changes. No step merges the three passes into one pool before review. That's why the ranks disagree (§2), the lists are stale, and Mallow and Shy collided unseen. Fix: after any parallel design pass, one merge step that (1) rebuilds the competition list per tier from all docs, (2) puts every tribe's boards on one sheet, and (3) greps each new selector and keyword against every other doc's rule changes. The same cause will apply to the costing pass if it is also split by group.
