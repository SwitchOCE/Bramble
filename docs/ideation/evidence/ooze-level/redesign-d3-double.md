**Diagnosis: mechanic first, progression second.** Eating mostly transfers purchased stats; reliable compounding depends on assembling Soup, an eater, Nell and later payoffs. That assembly is the failure point. The [Soup triage](/home/alex/code/Bramble-ideation/docs/ideation/evidence/triage-stone-soup.md) found the paper engine assembled on **0/32 seats**, early Soup yielded +0 in 17/24 observations, and supplying Soup everywhere still suggested only ~330 at R12. Moving Soup earlier fixes access, not sufficient growth.

The [dial check](/home/alex/code/Bramble-ideation/docs/ideation/content/pass3/a35-dial-check.md) likewise found existing dials insufficient. The brief’s post-fix 398 and 0.275 realization confirm the remaining gap. Measurement is a secondary cause: paper counted Belly reserves and guaranteed finds; reported medians excluded eliminated seats. Neither justifies changing the active-board target.

**Route: top-down mechanical.** Structure the entire tribe around independently growing stomachs, then provide ways to supply, distribute and spend that growth.

**Core rework.** Keep Belly and Eat. Replace Digest’s reminder with:

*Start of Turn: eat what’s inside, then double your stats if you ate.*

Digest consumes all contents before multiplying once. It doubles base plus permanent stats, excluding tribe bonuses, auras and combat changes. Eating remains destruction without sale gold. Empty stomachs gain nothing. Preserve no nesting, release placement, and the existing borrowing exclusions.

This makes every fed stomach a growth engine. Soup and Nell accelerate one rather than collectively permitting it to exist. Compounding remains finite: food is consumed, and Digest occurs at Start of Turn.

Reuse **Bud** from the mechanics libraries for splitting and **Ride**’s reserve-body structure for Belly support. Figgy adds the missing outlet: moving accumulated stats onto useful text.

**Details:** Soup rounds up, excludes Shy, and uses only the strongest Soup. Figgy swaps base-plus-permanent stat pairs; identities, text, contents and other layers stay put. Shy cannot receive its swap. Aspic modifies meal gains, not Digest’s multiplication; only the strongest applies. Elder snapshots the digester after multiplication, before sharing; sharing never triggers Digest. Granny leaves the family on the board. Other unchanged abilities retain their current rulings.

Drip: T1, 1/1, Ooze, no text. Slice: T1, tribeless, no text, with its generated stats. Both are Created tokens.

**Centrepiece pitch: Figgy Duff — T3, 3/5.**

> Belly 1. Digest. When played: swap two other friendly units’ stats.

Figgy lets players choose where their investment fights. Swap a grown Dollop with Bubble to obtain splitting immediately, avoiding the old carry-inside-carry transition. Swap it with Worker Bee to give a Swarm attacker substantial stats, or Garden Snail to put Health above Shell’s threshold. Figgy becomes the next stomach.

Run it to convert growth into relevant combat text without destroying either recipient. At R9, swapping a 227/356 Dollop with a 2/2 Bee preserves active stats, strengthens the Bee, and leaves two Digest bodies available for rebuilding. The cost is surrendering the grown stomach’s next large doubling.

Variations:

- Swap only Attack: retain the stomach’s Health investment.
- Swap only permanent gains: preserve each recipient’s base shape.
- Transfer half one unit’s stats to another: a partial withdrawal instead of exchanging bodies.

The feeding resembles Battlegrounds Demons; the distinctive decision is cashing accumulated growth into another tribe’s combat machinery.

Without later payoff gains, the average cohort’s R8–R14 active-board floor is approximately **313 → 620 → 1,224 → 2,432 → 4,850 → 9,680 → 19,344**: comfortably above ×1.3 each round.

Applying the old 0.275 realization mechanically would reduce average R12 to ~1,334. That is a stress haircut, not a forecast; this design removes several causes of that loss.

**Skill checks.** At T3, Figgy competes with **Mrs Mallow**, whose two smallest Hedgerow units double without purchased meals, and existing **Bubble and Squeak**, which provides defensive splitting. Figgy offers immediate redistribution and unrestricted recipients; it sacrifices those combat benefits.

The journey starts with independently useful bodies and a cheap stomach, adds optional feeding support, then offers combat conversion before late sharing. Players choose between another body, food, reserves and exporting growth. Figgy, Frumenty, Syllabub, Moss, Pie and Posset support mixed boards; Aspic, Elder and Granny reward retaining an Ooze nucleus.

**Most likely failure:** acquisition variance becomes overwhelming. Early Soup plus Nell produces roughly seventeen times the average R12 total. The generous first test should measure that divergence and tune growth rates before adding size caps.
