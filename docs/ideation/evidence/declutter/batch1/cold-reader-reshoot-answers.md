HARD-TO-READ MOMENTS

1. Attacker identity without a lunge: rival 025 (all speeds), 045 at ×1 and ×4, 069 at ×4, and every reduce-motion strip. The only cue is the gold frame and triangle, and the packet never explains it. I had to learn it from 036 and 069 at ×1. In reduce motion, attacker and target are linked only by damage numbers appearing at the same time, except where a red aim line stays up (075).
2. The orange tint and ring mean different things. In rival 025 reduce, the toad being hit is tinted orange and could pass for the attacker. In 036 reduce, the attacker wolf gets the ring. Effect sources also get it.
3. Rival 025, frames 6–8: the gold line from bottom F2 badger to top F2 toad has no number change and the gold frame stays on F1, so I can't tell whether it is the next attack or an effect. The dotted rims on both units suggest a pairing, but nothing explains it.
4. Rival 025: bottom B2 stoat's green self-loop leaves its number unchanged. At ×4, frame 1 already shows the stoat highlighted, so the change, if any, happened before the strip.
5. Rival 025: the unit leaving the dead ooze is shown only as tiny 1/2 stats inside the fading card. In reduce motion the swap is instant.
6. 036: in the joint attack, two damage numbers stack on one fox and only one attacker takes a counter-hit. Nothing shows why the wolf took none.
7. 036 and 045 acorn route: the arrowhead points into the dead unit's slot while the acorn travels the other way. At ×1 and ×4 the source reads as the bottom fox. Only reduce motion shows the acorn starting at the dead unit. The acorn lands below the bottom hero and no counter changes, so its meaning isn't shown.
8. 036: the green arcs spread toward bottom F3 fox's neighbours before landing on its own stats, so it can read as a buff to neighbours.
9. Paw badges and claw marks move between units (wolf, bottom F3 fox) at deaths, with no source line.
10. 045: the +1/+1 kept-gain slip sits off the board, left of and above the bottom hero. It is easy to miss; I missed it with a board-only crop. It doesn't show when or where the gain applies.
11. 045, frame 7: the no-entry sign on top B1 toad has no source, no landing and no number change.
12. Leftover cues from earlier events at the start of a strip: arcs on bottom F3 fox at the start of 045 (×4 and reduce), and the acorn below the hero at the start of 069 (×4). They can be read as part of the current attack.
13. 075: the toad's red self-loop from 4 to 2 shows no cause.
14. The ×4 strips span about the same time as ×1: 075 ends at +1.43s at ×4 against +1.44s at ×1. So ×4 gives no speed difference to judge.
