WHERE THE ANIMATION WAS HARD TO READ

1. The attack order breaks the stated rules, and nothing on screen says why.
   - Rival: the bottom side attacked three times in a row (025, 033, 039) before the top side attacked once. One of those was the back-row weasel, while the bottom F3 and F4 front units had not attacked.
   - combat_mid: the first bottom attack was the back-row frog, ahead of bottom F1.
   - combat_mid: top B1 (sleepy toad) was skipped before B2, and the top attacked twice in a row at 075 and 080 while the bottom weasel never attacked.
   - The packet says "sides take turns" and "front row left to right, then the back row". The only hints are leftover lines from the opening (rival: witch and toad lines; combat_mid: the red leprechaun line).

2. Back-row targets are hit while the front row stands.
   - combat_mid 021: the frog hits the top B2 leprechaun while the top front row is full. This contradicts "the back row is safe until the front is empty", and the reason is not visible.

3. Forced targeting is not explained.
   - Rival: every bottom attacker hit the toad while it lived. The T-bar line from the toad in the opening is the only hint.

4. Joint attacks are ambiguous (combat_mid 026, 036).
   - Two cards move, the target shows stacked -1/-4 pops, and only one attacker takes the return hit.
   - In 036 the wolf is the one that visibly lunges, but the badger, which stays put, takes the -5 and dies. It reads as if the badger was hit by something unseen.
   - Under reduced motion there are only two small tabs and three pops, with nothing connecting them.

5. Some lunges are missing or short, so the target is known only from the damage pop.
   - Rival 047: the top-hat shows no lunge or line.
   - Rival 058, combat_mid 054: the lunge stops at the attacker's own front row.
   - combat_mid 045, 062: no lunge.
   - At ×1 the frog (021) and the mouse (031) barely leave their slots.
   - Under reduced motion there are no lunges at all, and the ▲/▼ tab is small.

6. Effects have no visible source, or no visible landing.
   - combat_mid 045: the +1/+1 on the dead fox's slot lands nowhere visible.
   - combat_mid 045: the red crossed-out icon on top B1 has no source.
   - combat_mid 021 and 031: the wolf's golden glow and ring have no source.

   - The acorn arcs (rival 047, combat_mid 036, 045, 062) show a source, but the packet only links acorns to gold, never to fight effects.
