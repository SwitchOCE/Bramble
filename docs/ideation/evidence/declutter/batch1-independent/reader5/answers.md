Run 1: bog (bog-x4, bog-x1, bog-x1-reduce), attacks 017 and 025

Board at the start of 017. Top hero: mouse with wheat, 30. Top front: F1 mouse in red cap 6/2, F2 the same mouse 2/3, F3 the same mouse 1/3, F4 empty. Top back row is empty. Bottom hero: badger in a robe, 30. Bottom front: F1 toad 8/18, F2 frog king 3/20, F3 and F4 empty. Bottom back row is empty.

attack-017 (x4, x1 and reduce agree unless noted)

(a) Who hit: bottom F1, the toad (8/18). At x4 and x1 it visibly lunges up to top F3 (frame 2). At reduce motion nothing moves. The toad is only identifiable by the gold frame with a triangle on its slot, and by the -1 on it.

(b) Who was hit:
- Top F3 mouse (1/3) shows -8.
- The toad shows -1 and goes to 8/17.
- Then a trigger:
  - The frog king (bottom F2) gets a gold ring, and its portrait and attack badge turn yellow.
  - A red yarn runs between the frog and top F2 mouse. A red star travels from the frog to the mouse's attack badge. At x1 frame 6 the arrowhead is at the mouse end.
  - The mouse's attack badge pulses and drops from 2 to 1 (top F2 is now 1/3).
- Then a green yarn with a green star runs from the frog to the bottom hero (badger). The arrowhead is at the hero, and the hero portrait pulses (enlarges at x4 and x1; barely at reduce).
- The hero stays at 30, and nothing else visibly changes. I can't tell what the green effect gave.
- The green yarn is still on screen at the start of attack 025.

(c) Deaths: top F3 mouse, killed by the toad's 8. It greys with a dark green blot over its face (no blot at reduce, it just fades), then the slot empties. It keeps no visible gain. The frog's trigger starts right as the mouse dies, but I can't tell whether the death caused it.

(d) Marks:
- Gold frame with an upward triangle on the toad's slot. It stays at the slot while the toad lunges, so it seems to mark the current attacker.
- White note on the toad with an hourglass and two dots (one pale orange, one red). Meaning unknown.
- A similar white note with two dots appears on the frog from x4 frame 5 / x1 frame 6. Its centre glyph is covered by the green star. In 025 it shows an hourglass.
- Gold ring on the frog appears at the trigger and stays into 025.
- Green reed strokes beside the attack badges of the toad and the frog. The Rules page lists reeds as the Mire mark. They don't change.
- Dark bars under the top F1 and F2 slots, but not under F3. They don't change, and the meaning is unknown.
- At x4, frame 1 still shows fading -8 on top F1 and -6 on the toad from the previous attack. They are not part of 017.
