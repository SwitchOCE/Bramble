## Verdicts
1. **Pure tribe text beats stat piles?** Unclear; the bots probably undervalue it. My Ooze game came 7th, but it lost on early tempo: weak T1 bodies, and Belly holds idle units. In every lobby the biggest stats came from text: Dozey 28/34, Grizel 44/26, Treasurer 11/15, and two Standing Committees giving +3 Attack to all 7 units. Text makes the piles.
2. **Counters beat piles?** Only for one fight (game fact). My Changeling stole Sable 10/11 and won a round. My Hasty Pudding ate a 20/21 Dollop. Bot Changelings stole my 17/19 Wick and won a round. But counters only delay: Pudding gives its catch back when it dies, and Changeling's swap ends after one attack.
3. **Keeping cheap carriers correct?** No (bot weakness). I sold buffed T1–T3 units (Partner 3/4, Wolf 8/4) for Committee, Grizel, Whirligig and Peat King. Result: 1st with 30 health.
4. **Stay low and buff strong?** Yes (game fact). 1st place at T2–T3, with a golden Wick in round 6. Stand Behind Me and Bread gave +2/+2 because my units happened to share the Hunters tribe. **How it loses:** Changeling steals the pile, Wren's power lends my top Attack to the enemy, Belladonna Nightshade, and Dozey scaling (−11). Ties cost up to 8 round toll each.
