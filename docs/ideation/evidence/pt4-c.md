# Playtest pt4-c: tribes lens (build 6, 927b0b7)

Places: Hunters 4th (Pipkin), Birds 8th (Mousewife), Bog folk 5th (Hob), Oozes 4th (Bracken), Fairies 2nd (Wren).

**Verdict: okay**, close to good.

**What's best:** Oozes, Fairies and Bog folk play like different games. Frumenty holding a Belladonna, which Syllabub fires again when it comes out. A Hasty Pudding across the enemy's carry. Mire neighbours that turned His Highness into the Prince Restored. In game 5, Scout showed a +18 Ring under the bot's Belladonna, and Whirligig's row swap pulled it off that slot.

**Top 5 problems, worst first:**
1. **You can't count on committing to a tribe.** My tribe was often absent from shops for several turns; two tier-5 Discovers offered no Bog folk. *This alone stops "very good".*
2. **Birds are clunky and weak.** Nests take slots, so I had no room to buy. Eggs are tiny, and the return payoff (Chiffchaff +1 Attack) is small.
3. **Hunters play themselves.** Quarry marks automatically, and Takes pay small change. My only Hunter decision was putting Pack beside a leader.
4. **Damage comes in bunches.** I was at 9–16 Health by round 9 in three games. One 10–11 hit then knocked me out with five heroes alive, so Last Stand never applied.
5. **Traps and outliers.** Hob-in-the-Hearth, a Shy Fairy, walks out when Harvest Supper or Stand Behind Me buffs it, and I lost two units that way. Rings stack without limit on one slot.

**Builds that felt really different: 3** (Oozes, Fairies, Bog folk).

Most turns held real decisions (placement, Bellies, Rings, tier or bank). Hunter and Bird turns were thin. Nothing dominated, though stacked Rings came closest.

**Answers**
1. **What I did that the bots don't:** I scouted and countered. Whirligig stripped a slot Ring, and a Pudding went across the biggest enemy. I moved Queen of Elfland so she ate a cheap unit rather than my golden Monarch. I hid tier-5 units in Bellies.
2. **Burner:** it was a real choice with Pipkin's two-buy cap: I banked 11 for +3/+3 on two carries rather than tiering. Otherwise it was an automatic sink for 2–4 leftover gold, partly wasted on Dozey, who was near his cap.
3. **Small Birds:** One Swallow flies for free: its Egg nearly equals its body, and it pays 1 gold when replayed. The real cost is the slot its Nest takes.
4. **The last four:** game 5 was very tense. Four heroes sat at 1–8 Health, three Last Stands were spent, and every fight decided someone's game. At 1 Health I made bold calls (Beltane Bonfire, the Teind target). In game 1, Last Stand only put off the end by a turn. In games 3 and 4 it never applied.

**Verdict: okay.**
