# Pass2 follow-up R9 boards

R9 after recruiting/EOT, before Start of Combat. NEW follow-up fixtures; historical pass2-boards.json and pass2-bog/corrected-boards.json remain untouched. All boards have an itemized 62 +/-3 net-gold spending ledger (buys+upgrades+spells+refreshes minus bought-unit sale receipts), identical Supper counts per roll (high 2, low 0), and a non-Supper permanent growth layer. Effective recruit stats are independently itemized in stat_receipts; combat auras are reapplied by the engine. These are constructed static benchmark assumptions, not captured shop/action traces or estimates of shop probability. Extra income is itemized (Ooze high three Early Spoils, Fairy high six Shy gold, Hedgerow low one cashed Stash), never converted into unexplained stats or counted as spend. No T6. Heroes ignored.

- Ooze high assumes three early wins (R1-R3), each paying one Early Spoils next turn:62+3 income covers65 net spend. Fairy high has 6 Shy income and banks 5; Hedgerow low cashes 5 and banks 5. These income triggers are assumptions, not claimed observed results.
- Common net spend means actual listed gold outlays minus listed unit-sale receipts; it does not mean income, banked/discarded gold, or a claim that all refreshes found a useful unit.
- Each named temporary copy is separate from any retained body of the same ID, bought at 3 then sold at 1; these cycling replacements were usable earlier bodies, and do not grant permanent surviving stats.
- High climb costs 4+5+6+9=24; low 3+4+6=13. Refresh counts are totals across R1-R9, never more than 15 in one turn. A matching global spending envelope is not a recorded per-round solvency or pool trace.
- Highs have 2 Suppers each; lows 0 Suppers and 8 paid Pinches each. All boards additionally have named card-based growth, using named card triggers. Low Fairy uses exact R9 support gifts instead of the old unspecified placement history; Bog uses the accepted capped Toad and old Tiddy corrections.
- Ooze high removes the optional held Young Brock to fit 65 without unsupported Dollop discounts. Ooze low also claims no discount; five paid refreshes make its 62.
- Paid Pinches are in the fixture totals. The separate +1/+1 sensitivity adds only combat fields to starting outer and recursively held fighters, including Eggs; it leaves away Bird source stats untouched and adds no direct gift to later summons (normal effects can still respond to the larger starting bodies).

## Reproduce

Static audit: `python3 tools/sim/audit_pass2_followup.py` checks gold, inventory and every stat receipt; it is not an action replay.

`godot --headless -s res://tools/sim/pass2_boards.gd -- input=res://docs/ideation/evidence/pass2-followup-boards.json repetitions=100 seed=92100 sensitivity=1 out=build/pass2-followup-boards`

Baseline:66 unordered pairs ×100 seeds ×2 seats =13,200 fights. Each same-roll board rate uses1,000 fights against its five peers. Sensitivity: each board alone receives combat-only +1/+1 on each starting fighter, recursively including held bodies and Eggs; away Bird sources stay fixed and no direct gift is added to later summons. Normal effects, including copying or splitting a larger source, still respond naturally. It plays the five unchanged same-roll peers with identical seed pairs and swapped seats, for1,000 fights per board and12,000 total. No fixture spend or recruiting Shy growth is claimed for this diagnostic. Results preserve wins/ties/losses and report strict win percentage with the percentage-point change. Fixed boards are threshold tests, not a tribe ranking; compare them with the separately measured plain free-choice lobby rate.

The historical default command and its JSON remain reproducible: omit input= and sensitivity=.
