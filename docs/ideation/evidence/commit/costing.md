## Kin spells (2 gold, realised)

| | Committed | Mixed | Off-tribe |
|---|---|---|---|
| Kin +2/+3 | 9.0 (4.5/gold) | 6.3 | 3.5 (1.75/gold) |

| Bread, kin +2/+2 | 8.0 (4.0/gold) | 6.0 | 4.0 |

Today's +1/+2 is 5.5 (2.75 a gold); Pinch gives 2 a gold, Elderflower 3. Kin rates are under S(3) = 5, but 2.75 already makes 15-21% of stats: frequency is the problem. At 4.7 casts, +2/+3 adds **+16 SP** committed and -9 mixed. +2/+2 gives +7 / -9, mirrors Bread.

## Harvest Supper

Committed: 11.5 realised now, plus about 3 replacements that keep it [g] (+6): **17.5, 5.8 a gold, +17%** at cost 3. Mixed: 7.8 (-48%). Cost 4 puts committed at -12%, so keep 3.

- **It becomes the sink.** 5.8 a gold beats every spell (sink 2.0), so with 56 gold players dig for it. 4.5 casts: +54 SP committed, +27 mixed; 7 casts: +84, half a 175-SP board. **Once a turn** stops the digging.
- **Tokens.** "Units you get later" includes combat summons (Shrewlets, Kits, Hoglets, Wisps, Peatlings, Drips). At +4/+4 Mrs Sorrel alone adds ~7 SP a fight [g], all of V(2), on Hedgerow, the pick leader.

## Neutral scalers

- **Queen.** Fairies-only split adds no stats. Enough.
- **Burner.** 6 burned = 10.5 SP a turn on 2 back units, 1.75 a gold; vs ~1.4 marginal gold nets 2.1 × c(5) 2.6: 17.5 (+46%). Golden "+2/+2 per 2" is 3.5 a gold. Cap 4 on a 4/6: 13.6 (+14%); golden doubling the cap: 27 vs 24.

## Tier 6 and 2

- **Monarch.** Rings are ~21 SP a board, ~35 on Fairy boards [g]; doubling at 0.8 uptime adds 13-22: 25-34 vs 14. "+1/+1 more": 16.4 committed (+17%), 14.0 mixed.
- **Elder Ooze.** +k/+k for the k-th out is quadratic: at 0.4 realisation [g], 8 SP with 4 out, 17 with 6, on a body already 17 with Belly 2. Proposed: 17.2 committed (+23%), 12.4 mixed. "Your units" excludes Hasty Pudding's returning enemy.
- **Moss Ooze.** ~0.75 spells a turn on a filled Belly × 4 SP × 0.35: 7.9. Counting the tribe bonus would pay every Supper twice.
- **Laird.** 3 Takes × 3 Hunters × 2 × 0.5 [g] = 9: 19. At 3/5, 17 (+21%), 14 mixed.
- **Whistlers.** Drafted: 6 Birds, -6/-6 on 6 enemies, ~65 SP at the bell [g]. Linear: 10.8; flock and placement still decide the hit.

## Egg rule

Flying cost the body and buffs for 2 fights (~25 SP at round 12); now it costs only text. Land, replay and re-fly in one turn and a Bird is away forever at full stats, paying +1 sale gold and its entry text every 2 turns: 0.5 gold a turn, recurring gold tiers 1-2 can't have (One Swallow, Mrs Jay ~1 a turn). Old Tern is 10.8 (+35%). Mother Goose works only away, and would now also fight at 4/7 plus buffs. With the fix, Old Tern is 7.8 (-2%), Chiffchaff ~5.4, and flying costs text for 2 fights in 3.

## Whole package

**Yes, as drafted.** Round 15 against today: committed -19 (old Supper) + 54 + 16 = **+51 SP (+29%)**; mixed -19 + 27 - 9 = **about -1**. One unit (~14% of a board) is worth 21-23 points of win rate, so a 30% gap makes commitment mandatory, the reverse of today's -0.5 place. Hedgerow and Fairies gain most. Proposed numbers: about +24 (+14%) committed, -10 mixed, still a ~20% gap [g]. Bench items 1-3 with the bot fix (15) before the tier-6 cards; Oozes and Birds can take theirs early.
