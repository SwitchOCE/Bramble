# Critic: play and strategic depth

Checked against `measure.md` and `cards-now.md`. The drafts' claims about current cards hold: every "vanilla" card they rewrite is vanilla, and B's figures for today's Stand Behind Me and Harvest Supper (6 and 20 stats) are right.

## Verdicts

- **Designer A (tribe counts): workable.** It is the right tool for the 5th-7th unit, and the counts are positional, so a splash can't take them. But it leaves the generic 40% alone (A says so), spans about 15 cards, and its numbers that grow with the square of the count decide fights at the bell.
- **Designer B (tribal generic gold): strong on causes, thin on decisions.** It is the only draft that goes after spells (about 30%). Its standing tribe bonus also fixes small late bodies and the 5th-7th unit. Its Ring-doubling rule feeds the two leading tribes and the Ringed-Fairy giant.
- **Designer C (capstones): weak.** Its capstones tax the enemy's biggest unit instead of paying commitment. That punishes the "one or two really big units" comp the owner named and leaves the 40% untouched. Peg Powler and the Bottomless Pot are the heaviest engine asks in any draft, and Reynard is a new snowball. Its **Egg rule is the best Birds fix offered**.

## Recommended set

Core:
1. **Stand Behind Me and Bread from the Back get kin clauses (B).** Answers the spells. Change both, or the gold moves to the mirror card.
2. **Harvest Supper becomes a standing tribe bonus (B).** The one new rule. Answers spells, small tier-6 bodies and the 5th-7th unit.
3. **Hiring Fair finds your tribe** (C's wording). Answers "6 gold wasted".
4. **One count card per tribe from A, with linear growth:** Standing Committee, Fairy Monarch (first clause only), Elder Ooze, Seven Whistlers, The Peat King, The Laird of the Sett.
5. **Birds:** C's Egg rule and A's homecoming onto the Bird's own Nest, with the Whistlers.
6. **Bounds on the neutral scalers**, which no draft covers: the Queen of Elfland's split goes to Fairies only, and Charcoal Burner gets a cap.
7. **Not a card change:** give the bots `power` and count weights for Oozes, Birds and the count cards before any bench run. Otherwise the bench re-measures the bots' priors.

Optional: B's Moss Ooze and Fieldfare, and A's Figgy Duff with a cap, if Oozes or Birds still lag.

**Cut:**
- B's Ring-doubling rule: the Monarch does the same at tier 6.
- B's "a Nest-sitter is a Bird": it clashes with the Egg rule.
- All of C's capstones. Keep the Elf-Shooter as an alternative Fairy tier 6.
- A's Moth Fairy, Bog Ooze, Crested Newt, Stoat Captain, Buzzard, Old Crane and Old Mother Mast rewrites. Crane is also permanent growth that can make a giant.
- B's Hedgerow Elder, Great Ooze, Newt and Stoat. Hold them as later users of the standing bonus.

New rules come to three: the standing bonus, the Egg rule (replacing a current line) and the homecoming swap. Add one defined term, the Hedgerow "run". No new keywords.

**Tribes per lobby: five.** Supply alone doesn't fix commitment (measure section 8). But this set makes the 5th-7th unit matter, and 0.7-0.8 of your tribe per shop view is too thin to reach a run of 6. Five tribes give about 20% more supply, with 1.6 players per tribe, so the contest doesn't starve shared shops. Go to four only if a rerun at five still can't assemble committed boards.

## Per-card notes

**Stand Behind Me** (B: kin behind get +2/+3, others +1/+1). The off-tribe rate falls to 2 stats a gold, the same as Pinch of Dust, which removes the measured pile. "Who stands behind whom" is a real Recruit Phase decision. A two-tribe board can still build one kin column, which is acceptable because commitment is paid by item 2. **Bread from the Back** mirrors it.

**Harvest Supper** (B: "your units of that tribe have +1/+1 for the rest of the game, including ones you get later"). The keystone. The bonus is spread evenly, so it works against stacking one unit, a late tier 6 arrives carrying it, and the 7th unit gets all of it. Price it per unit-round. Watch Beltane Bonfire and Last Orders: they stay generic with gold reaching 56, so they are the next sink, and surplus gold may be the deeper root.

**Hiring Fair.** Use "one of three is of that tribe" at 4+, not A's "all three". Bots already sit at 4-5 of a tribe, so A's version is a tutor for mixed boards too.

**Standing Committee** (A's run, made linear): "+1 Attack for each Hedgerow folk in the run beyond the third". That is +2 each at 5 and +4 each at 7. It is the best decision-maker in any draft, because a splash unit's slot keeps or breaks the run. A's version (35 Attack at 7) is too swingy.

**Fairy Monarch.** Keep "Rings under your Fairies give double their stats" and drop the random permanent Ring growth. A splash keeps face-value Rings but not the payoff. The risk is a Wisp or the Queen on a doubled Ring, so pair it with:

**Queen of Elfland:** "Your other Fairies split their stats evenly." Today 62% of her split goes to non-Fairies.

**Charcoal Burner:** add "up to 6 burned", which bounds it at +3/+3 per unit behind it per turn. People find the uncapped line (36/37) and bots don't, so it needs a design bound.

**Elder Ooze** (A). Each body that comes out is +1/+1 bigger than the last. It is combat-only, so no giant, and a splash Dollop gets +1/+1 once. It adds an ordering decision: who dies first, and what is inside.

**Seven Whistlers** (A, capped at -5/-5 per enemy). Flock size sets how much, and Bird and Nest positions set who is hit. Nests become a targeting choice made two turns ahead. That beats C's Parliament, which rewards a count and singles out tall boards.

**Egg rule** (C): "While a Bird is away, the Egg on its Nest fights with that Bird's Attack and Health, buffs included, and no text." Flying then costs only text, buffs on fliers keep paying, and the Nest stays on the board. Fall back to card stats only if Fly South becomes free. **Homecoming** (A): a returning Bird may go onto its own Nest, and the unit there goes to hand. Together these answer "slots and tempo", "Nests vanish" and "need free slots".

**The Peat King** (A, capped): each Mired enemy loses 1 Attack per Mired enemy beyond the first, up to -4, to no less than 1. Scouting and placement set the count. Uncapped, it is -49 at the bell.

**Laird of the Sett** (A): after each Take, your Hunters gain +1/+1 this combat. A small rewrite of today's text. Later Takes are worth more, and it can't snowball. Take it over Reynard.

**Figgy Duff** (optional). Hiring Fair will now offer it to Ooze players. If A's text is used, cap it at +3/+3 a turn, because stats hidden in a Belly are hard to scout.
