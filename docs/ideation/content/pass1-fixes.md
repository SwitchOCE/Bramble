## 1. Rainbow Chaser (T1 Fairy)

- **Role:** economy with a growing body, R1–6; the Rainbow sub-variety's opener.
- **Problem:** painting is free (any play into an empty slot), so +1/+1 a slot makes it 8/9 by R5 and the biggest unit at R6–R9 when golden (18/20, 25/28), *and* a 13-gold sale. Ring piles land on it (34/38).
- **Why buy it now:** R1–3 it's a 2/3 or 3/4 that out-stats Wobbly and Pill Bug once two or three slots are painted. From R4 it's a bank: its body stops growing, so the sale becomes the question. The good decision players praised (pt5 a, e: when to cash out) stays; the free late carry goes.
- **Text:** Rainbow. Has +1/+1 per 2 painted slots. When sold: wipe the paint and get 1 gold a slot, +5 for all seven.
- **Stats:** 1/2. Top: 4/5 at seven slots.
- **Golden:** Rainbow. Has +2/+2 per 2 painted slots. When sold: wipe the paint and get 2 gold a slot, +10 for all seven. Top: 8/9 golden.
- **Rules note:** round down (seven slots give +3/+3). Sale unchanged.

## 2. Ring cap: a rule, not a card

- **Problem:** Rings stack without limit on one slot (+6 to +18; pt5 a, b, c), from Sally Spore every turn, Spore Cloud, Boundary Stone and Nan Toadstool. Any tribe inherits the pile (tally 3).
- **Fix (rulebook 5.x, BRAMBLE ideation):** **a slot's Ring holds at most +4/+4.** It lives in the rule because six cards make Rings and any future one should obey it without its own text. Fairy Monarch's "+1/+1 more" sits on top of the cap: the Fairy payoff, not the generic slot, goes past it.
- **Reminder:** "Ring a slot: whoever stands there gets the bonus, up to +4/+4." (12 words)
- **What it asks:** spread Rings instead of piling one. Seven slots still hold +28/+28: a wide Fairy board, not one carry.
- **Rules notes:**
  - A full Ring can't be the aim of a Ring spell (the slot greys out); partial overflow is lost (a +3/+3 Ring takes Spore Cloud to +4/+4).
  - Sally Spore rings the nearest empty slot whose Ring isn't full. Her text doesn't change.
  - Reel-Mistress moves a Ring whole during a fight; the cap applies to what a slot holds while recruiting.
  - Horseshoe halves the capped value.

## 3. Hedge Witch (T1 Fairy)

- **Role:** spell enabler and lane answer, R1–3, with a late niche in spell boards.
- **Problem:** Hex 8 while a tier-1 unit (pt5 c; P1 A–C): it zeroes a carry's Attack for 1 gold.
- **Competition:** Wobbly, Pill Bug, and Nan Toadstool 2/1 "When played: Ring this slot +1/+1."
- **Why buy it now:** R1–3 Hex 1–2 blunts the enemy's best early attacker, and every Pinch of Dust or Cordial you'd cast anyway feeds it.
- **Text:** Hex 1. Each spell you cast adds 1, up to your tier.
- **Stats:** 1/2.
- **Golden:** Hex 2. Each spell you cast adds 2, up to twice your tier.
- **Rules note:** spells keep counting past the cap; the cap is read at Start of Combat, so tiering up releases stored Hex. Details: "Spells past the cap still count."

## 4. Gold-only units

### Little Nip (T1 Hunter)
- **Role:** early tempo and economy for the Take sub-variety, R1–3.
- **Why buy it now:** a 2/2 that grows each Take is a T1 body that stays worth its slot to R5, and the gold is extra. Hunters are flat early (tally 8); this is their first grower.
- **Text:** Quarry. Take: gain +1/+1 permanently and get 1 gold next turn.
- **Stats:** 2/2 (was 2/1).
- **Golden:** Quarry. Take: gain +2/+2 permanently and get 2 gold next turn.

### The Toll-Bard (T2 Hunter)
- **Role:** tempo with gold, R2–5.
- **Competition:** Jay, Moss Ooze, Bufo; Sable 2/3 "Quarry. Start of Combat: if its quarry has more Attack, gain +3 Attack."
- **Why buy it now:** its quarry pays the toll in Attack, so the Bard shrinks the enemy's strongest early hitter this fight, then pays you for it.
- **Text:** Quarry. After its quarry attacks: it loses 1 Attack and you get 1 gold next turn. Twice a fight.
- **Stats:** 2/4 (was 2/3).
- **Golden:** Quarry. After its quarry attacks: it loses 2 Attack and you get 2 gold next turn. Twice a fight.

### Old Lutra (T3 Hunter)
- **Role:** back-row Taker, R4–6.
- **Why buy it now:** a 3/4 that grows on each Take, from either row, matches the Lodger's body and keeps growing; the back-row gold rewards placing it safely.
- **Text:** Quarry, wherever it stands. Take: gain +1/+1 permanently. Back-row Take: also get 1 gold next turn.
- **Stats:** 3/4 (was 2/4).
- **Golden:** Quarry, wherever it stands. Take: gain +2/+2 permanently. Back-row Take: also get 2 gold next turn.

### The Peat Cutter (T2 Bog folk)
- **Role:** early Mire with gold, R2–5. Bog folk "barely have" Mire at T1–2 (audit).
- **Why buy it now:** a 2/4 wall that Mires the enemy across, then cuts peat when that enemy dies, so it doesn't need another Mirer first.
- **Text:** Mire. After a Mired enemy dies: get 1 gold next turn. Once a fight.
- **Stats:** 2/4 (was 1/1).
- **Golden:** Mire. After a Mired enemy dies: get 2 gold next turn. Once a fight.
- **Rules note:** pass 2's "earlier Mire" counts it.

### Cricket Busker (T2 neutral)
- **Role:** spell economy, R2–6.
- **Why buy it now:** the gold comes back the same turn, so your first spell each turn is 1 gold cheaper. Pinch of Dust becomes free +1/+1.
- **Text:** After you cast a spell: get 1 gold. Once a turn.
- **Stats:** 2/3 (was 2/2).
- **Golden:** After you cast a spell: get 2 gold. Once a turn.

### Money Spider (T2 neutral)
- **Role:** steady income, R2–6.
- **Problem:** at 0 gold carried over it paid nothing, so it asked a losing player to save.
- **Why buy it now:** 1 gold every Start of Turn, spendable that turn, from the turn after you buy it. Saving 3 adds a second gold, but is never required.
- **Text:** Start of Turn: get 1 gold, or 2 if you carried over 3 or more.
- **Stats:** 1/3.
- **Golden:** Start of Turn: get 2 gold, or 4 if you carried over 3 or more.

### Pennywort Snail (T1 neutral)
- **Role:** a wall that banks, R1–4.
- **Why buy it now:** it grows a shell while it waits: 1/5 after three turns, a front-row wall, and then sells for 4.
- **Text:** Each turn on your board: gain +1 Health and sell for 1 more gold, up to 3 turns.
- **Stats:** 1/2.
- **Golden:** Each turn on your board: gain +2 Health and sell for 2 more gold, up to 3 turns.

## 5. Heroes

### Hob Mumblecrust
- **Role:** tempo loan for a spike turn (tier up a turn early, or a key triple).
- **Problem:** Health loss compounds the damage curve; repeat use cost ~6 Health (pt5 b).
- **Text:** Power: borrow 3 gold. Your next 2 turns, get 1 less gold.
- **Rules note:** no Health cost and no way to default, since income covers it. You can't borrow while repaying. Net +1 gold a loan, so the value is timing: +3 now. Details: "You can't borrow again until it's repaid."

### Brother Bracken
- **Role:** the few-units hermit: fewer, stronger units in exchange for slots.
- **Problem:** "beside them" is zero on the full board every late player has (pt5 b).
- **Text:** Start of Combat: your units gain +1/+1 per empty slot you have, up to +2/+2.
- **Why it's a choice:** six units at +1/+1 each roughly match a seventh unit; five at +2/+2 beat it.

### Old Mother Mousewife
- **Role:** front-row defence.
- **Problem:** unlimited Mice made her too strong early (pt4 a), and "behind" failed on a full back row (pt5 c, d).
- **Text:** After a front-row unit dies: summon a Mouse in its place, with stats equal to your tier. Twice a fight.
- **Rules notes:** the dead unit's slot is always free, so she never fails for room. A Mouse dying doesn't summon another (Details). Mice keep the slot's Ring.
