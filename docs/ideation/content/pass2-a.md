# Hunters

**The problem.** Flat early (tally 8, six reports): 4-stat T1s lose to Wobblies and Pill Bugs (P4 a), early Hunters lost six fights in a row, and "my only Hunter decision was putting Pack beside a leader" (P4 c). One T6. Benchmarks: Hunters are steady from R6 (rank 1–3) and behind only on the R3 high roll.

**The fix.** Every T1 and T2 Hunter is at least a par body on an empty board, as the Birds pass did. The Pack route gets a real decision at T4 (who stands beside whom) and its own T6 finisher. The Take route is untouched above T2: it measures well.

**Two routes.**
- **Open Season (Quarry and Takes):** Little Nip, Mistress Russet, the Toll-Bard, Sable, Old Lutra, Madame Ermeline, the Mountain Glutton, the Laird.
- **The Pack (Pack and Pounce):** Wick, Young Brock, Sable as a leader, Brother Wolf, the Lodger, Old Brock, Grey Agnes, Grizel, Old Isegrim.
- **Where they meet:** Sable and Old Brock serve both: a quarry is the best thing for a pack to hit. The Laird gives every pack leader a quarry; Isegrim makes every joiner hit hard. The shop decides the lean.

## Changed cards

### Wick (T1, 2/2 → 2/3)

Text unchanged: "Pack. After this joins an attack: gain +1 Attack." *G* 4/6: "…+2 Attack."
- **Role and window:** tempo body, R1–3; Pack's first piece from R2.
- **Competitors:** Dunnock 2/3, Chiffchaff, Wobbly 1/4. At 2/3 it trades evenly with Dunnock and beats Wobbly's 1 Attack; from R2, beside any front unit, it hits for 2 more without taking the counter-hit.

## New cards

### Grey Agnes (T4, Hunters, 2/5, new; amended after review)

*She-wolf, Mother of the Pack.* "Pack. The unit after this in the row has Pack and +1 Attack." *G* 4/10: "…Pack and +2 Attack."
- **Rules:** Pack's rules (`hunters.md`) are unchanged: joiners hit the leader's target, only the leader takes the counter, one join a cycle, no chains. Only the next same-row unit receives Pack; a row does not automatically become two gangs.
- **Role and window:** the Pack route's T4 payoff, R6–10. It adds one Pack joiner through placement. Existing Pack units may still join a leader; only the leader takes the counter.
- **Competitors:** Charcoal Burner 4/6, the Cuckoo, Will-o'-the-Wisp. Burner's growth costs gold; Agnes trades a smaller body for Pack on the next unit and +1 Attack there.
- **Journey:** fills the Pack route's T4, which had only Old Brock. Makes you want Grizel (more Attack per kill) and Isegrim.
- **The decision it adds:** who leads and who joins; a joiner gives up its own quarry for the cycle. That answers "play themselves".
- **Great buy:** R7 with Wick, Brother Wolf and a quarry leader in front. **Bad buy:** a back-row-heavy board, or against a wide Bristle wall, where every leader eats the pricks.
- **Variations:** (1) "Pack. Units beside this have Pack." 4/6, no Attack. (2) "Pack. After a pack attack you join: the leader gains +1/+1 permanently." (riskier: one grows).
- **New engine piece:** a live granted keyword on the immediately next slot in the same row, read at each attack. An empty next slot grants nothing.

### Old Isegrim (T6, Hunters, 6/8, new)

*Wolf, Constable of the Wold.* The wolf of the Reynard fables. "Pack. Your units have +3 Attack while joining an attack." *G* 12/16: "…+6 Attack…"
- **Rules:** the bonus applies to each joiner's hit, not the leader's. It lasts only for that damage step.
- **Role and window:** the Pack route's finisher, R10+, the tribe's second T6.
- **Competitors:** Whirligig Beetle 6/8, the Peat King 5/9, the Laird. Two or three joins a cycle at +3 each is +6 to +9 damage into a chosen target, with no counter-hits. Whirligig scrambles once; Isegrim keeps hitting.
- **Journey:** Pack route, T6. With the Laird every unit has a quarry, so every gang is aimed: the two T6s are the routes meeting.
- **Great buy:** R10 with Agnes and Grizel in a full front row. **Bad buy:** an Open Season board with no Pack neighbours, where he's a 6/8 that joins once.
- **Variations:** (1) "Pack. Pack attacks count as kills by each attacker" (feeds Grizel and Takes). (2) "Pack. Your pack attacks hit twice." (Shell-breaker; probably too strong.)

# Hedgerow folk

**The problem.** Thinnest supply (11 cards, tally 14). R4–9 payoffs are late and thin; "bodies don't grow" (P5 c); Beadle Warrenby fails at T4 (audit §2). One T5 and one T6, so missing either drops the tribe (benchmark R12 low roll, rank 5). Mr Crumb's Pass "landed on itself whenever the zigzag broke" (birds player check), and the old keyword reminder had already failed review (review-feedback5).

**The fix.** Five new cards and one redesign, split across two routes, all of which grow Hedgerow bodies. Pass gets one simpler rule.

**Two routes.**
- **The Hoard (hold Acorns, spend them well):** the Sleeping Partner, Mrs Burdock in back, Mrs Sorrel in back, Mr Pym, Sergeant-at-Conkers, Alderman Dozey, the Honorary Treasurer, Beadle Warrenby, Goodman Hayward, Old Mother Mast.
- **The Parish (wide, many small hands):** Mrs Burdock in front, Mr Crumb, Mrs Clover, Mrs Sorrel in front, Dr Urchin, Mrs Mallow, the Standing Committee, Squire Lollop.
- **Where they meet:** Pass cards (Crumb, Pym, Urchin, Beadle) feed one carry, often Dozey, on either route. Goodman Hayward cashes the Hoard into the whole Parish. Holding for the Treasurer against cashing for Hayward is the tribe's late decision.

## The Pass rule (fixes Mr Crumb)

**Today:** Pass travels rightward along touching units in the zigzag; a gap stops it; if nothing touches on the right, the source keeps it (`BoardGeometry.pass_landing`). So Crumb played before a gap, or at the right end, buffs himself, and the reminder doesn't say so clearly.

**New rule, exactly:**
> **Pass X:** your last unit in the zigzag F1-B1-F2-B2-F3-B3-F4, other than this, gains X. Recruiting: at End of Turn.

- Gaps don't matter. The source's slot doesn't matter. The landing unit is always the same visible unit: the last one in the zigzag, the "end of the row".
- It never lands on its source. With no other unit, nothing happens; the UI says "no one to pass to".
- A sold unit's Pass reads the board without it. In combat, dead units have left, so it's the last living unit.
- In combat it lands at once; in the Recruit Phase at End of Turn, as now.
- Card text drops "right": "Pass +1/+1".
- **UI:** the last unit wears a small Pass mark all the time, not only while dragging, so a player always knows where Passes go.

**What it costs:** cutting the Line to aim a Pass is gone. The decision is which unit stands last: the carry at F4 (exposed) or at B3 with F4 empty (safe, costs a front body). Gaps still cost the Committee and Lollop. **Alternative, if the owner wants the old puzzle:** keep today's rule and change only the reminder to "Goes along the unbroken chain to the right, to its last unit. If nothing touches on the right, this keeps it," plus the permanent mark. I recommend the new rule: the owner's "clear and concise".

**Mr Crumb (T1, 1/2), text:** "When played or sold: Pass +1/+1." *G*: "…Pass +2/+2." Role, body and window unchanged (R1–3, aimed buffs). Under the new rule it can't land on itself, so its one confusing case is gone. **Dr Urchin** likewise reads "After this pricks: Pass +1 Attack."

## Changed card

### Beadle Warrenby (T4, 2/4 → 3/6)

"Your summons Spill. After you get an Acorn: Pass +1/+1. Twice a turn." *G* 6/12: "…Pass +2/+2. Twice a turn."
- **Rules:** any Acorn counts: Spill, the Partner, Mrs Jay, Mast, spells. Acorns won in a fight arrive at the start of the next Recruit Phase, so they count for that turn. Three Acorns combining into a Stash is not a new Acorn. "A turn" is one Recruit Phase plus the fight after it.
- **Role and window:** the Hoard route's T4 payoff, R6–10: Acorns become permanent stats on your end unit, and you keep the Acorns.
- **Competitors:** Charcoal Burner 4/6 turns gold above 5 into +1/+1 per 2; the Beadle turns each Acorn into +1/+1 and leaves the Acorn in hand. The Cuckoo gives +2/+2 a turn on two Eggs; the Beadle gives +2/+2 a turn on one carry, plus Spill on any tribe's full board.
- **Why it failed before:** +2/+2 a fight, only from Spills, on a 6-stat T4. Now +2/+2 a turn from any Acorn, on a T4 body.
- **Journey:** the Hoard route's T4; the bridge to Dozey and the Treasurer.
- **Great buy:** R7 with the Partner and a back-row Sorrel, Dozey last in the zigzag. **Bad buy:** no Acorn sources, or a board where the last unit is a 1/2.
- **Variations:** (1) "Once a turn" at 3/6, +2/+2. (2) "After you get an Acorn: your end unit gains +1 Attack; a Stash, +3/+3."

## New cards

### Mr Pym (T2, Hedgerow, 2/4, new)

*Vole, Clerk to the Council.* "After you get a Stash: Pass +2/+2." *G* 4/8: "…Pass +4/+4."
- **Rules:** fires when three Acorns combine, and when a Stash arrives whole (the Partner's third payout, a spell). Not on a Great Hoard.
- **Role and window:** the Hoard route's first payoff, R3–9. It is the reason to hold the third Acorn rather than cash the second.
- **Competitors:** Moss Ooze 2/4, Redbreast 2/4, Mrs Jay 3/3. On an empty board he is a Moss Ooze. With the Partner (2 Acorns on turn 3) and one Spill, the first Stash comes around R4: +2/+2 on the end unit, and you still hold 5 gold.
- **Journey:** Hoard, T2, filling the tribe's empty T2. Makes you want Dozey (the end unit) and the Beadle.
- **Great buy:** R3 holding 2 Acorns. **Bad buy:** an Acorn-free board, or when you must cash every Acorn to keep up.
- **Variations:** (1) "After you get an Acorn: Pass +1 Health" (steadier). (2) "After you get a Stash: your Hedgerow folk gain +1 Health."

### Mrs Clover, Midwife (T2, Hedgerow, 2/3, new)

*Rabbit, Parish Midwife.* "After you summon a unit: give it +1/+1." *G* 4/6: "…+2/+2."
- **Rules:** any of your summons, in either phase, any tribe: Kits, Shrewlets, Earwig nymphs, Wisps, Peatlings, Drips. A Spilled summon isn't summoned, so it isn't buffed.
- **Role and window:** the Parish route's T2 enabler, R2–6: it makes tokens real bodies.
- **Competitors:** Moss Ooze 2/4, Redbreast 2/4. She's a stat behind alone, but with Mrs Burdock in front the Kit lands 2/2 (permanent), and with Sorrel in front each Shrewlet fights as a 2/2. Burdock plus Clover is 9 stats from two buys at R2–3, against 10 for two par T2s, plus a third body.
- **Journey:** Parish, T2. Makes you want Mrs Mallow (who grows the smallest) and the Committee (who rewards seven bodies).
- **Great buy:** R2 with Burdock in hand. **Bad buy:** a Hoard board where every summon Spills.
- **Variations:** (1) 2/4, Hedgerow summons only. (2) "After you summon a unit: give it +1/+1 and Pass +1 Health."

### Mrs Mallow, Feeding Them Up (T4, Hedgerow, 3/6, new)

*Dormouse, Keeper of the Parish Larder.* "End of Turn: your two smallest units gain +1/+1." *G* 6/12: "…+2/+2."
- **Rules:** smallest by Attack plus Health; ties go to the earlier slot in the zigzag. She can pick herself only if she is among the two smallest.
- **Role and window:** the Parish route's T4 grower, R6–12. It is the "bodies grow" fix: Kits, Crumb and the Partner climb every turn, and it never piles on one unit, because a grown unit stops being smallest.
- **Competitors:** Charcoal Burner 4/6 (needs gold above 5), the Cuckoo 3/6 (+2/+2 a turn on Eggs), Will-o'-the-Wisp. Mallow's +2/+2 a turn is free and spread, and it brings T1 bodies up to fight-worthy by R9.
- **Journey:** Parish, T4. Makes you want the Committee and Squire Lollop, who pay for many mid-sized bodies.
- **Great buy:** R7 with Kits and T1s on the board. **Bad buy:** a board of even T3s, where +1/+1 on two of seven is small.
- **Variations:** (1) "your smallest unit gains +2/+2". (2) "End of Turn: units smaller than this gain +1 Health."

### Goodman Hayward (T5, Hedgerow, 4/7, new)

*Hedgehog, Keeper of the Hedges.* (A hayward was the parish officer who kept the hedges.) "After you play a Stash or Great Hoard: your other Hedgerow folk gain +1/+1." *G* 8/14: "…+2/+2."
- **Rules:** "play" means cash it or feed it to Alderman Dozey. Permanent.
- **Role and window:** the tribe's second T5, R8–12: the Hoard paying the Parish.
- **Competitors:** Grizel, Lady Belladonna 4/8, Hasty Pudding. With five Hedgerow folk each Stash played is +5/+5 permanent, spread, and you keep the 5 gold.
- **The decision it adds:** hold the Stash for the Treasurer's stats, the Sergeant's throw or Mast's interest, or cash it now for the board.
- **Journey:** the bridge from Hoard to Parish at T5. Makes you want Mast (more Stashes) or Lollop (wide).
- **Great buy:** R9 with the Partner's third payout due and Pym on board. **Bad buy:** a board with two Hedgerow folk.
- **Variations:** (1) "After you play an Acorn: your other Hedgerow folk gain +1 Health." (2) +2/+2 per Stash, Hedgerow folk touching this only.

### Squire Lollop, Lord of the Manor (T6, Hedgerow, 6/8, new)

*Hare, Patron of the Living.* "End of Turn: units touching this gain +1/+1, or +2/+2 if all 7 touch." *G* 12/16: "…+2/+2, or +4/+4…"
- **Rules:** "touching" as the Committee: the unbroken Line through this. Permanent.
- **Role and window:** the Parish finisher, R10+: the T6 that grows the bodies.
- **Competitors:** Whirligig 6/8, the Peat King 5/9, Queen of Elfland 8/8, Old Mother Mast 6/8. On a full board it's +12/+12 a turn spread over six units, and the Committee adds +3 Attack each at the bell. Queen grows by eating your units; Lollop by keeping them.
- **Journey:** Parish, T6, beside Mast for the Hoard. Each route now has its own finisher.
- **Great buy:** R10 on a full board with the Committee. **Bad buy:** a Treasury board with gaps on purpose, where it touches two units.
- **Variations:** (1) "Start of Combat: units touching this gain +2/+2 this fight, +4/+4 if all 7 touch." (2) +1/+1 to touching Hedgerow folk only, but twice a turn.
