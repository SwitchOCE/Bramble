# Birds, pass 1 (plan step 3)

8 Nov 2026, a fresh cloud designer. Written without the power budget; every number is a first guess for the costing reviewer.

**What changes:**
- 16 shop Birds at 3/3/3/3/2/2.
- Tom Tit (T1) is redesigned as a T3; the Great Lump moves from T2 to T5.
- Every T1 and T2 Bird is a par body or better on an empty board, and leaves or uses a Nest.
- Fly South, Nests, Eggs and the Ratchet's payoff are unchanged.

**Engine:** one new piece, Tom Tit's aura (Redwing's, without "beside"). The rest is stats, tiers, a dropped condition, a self target and Discover's `tribe` filter.

Target: the owner's "synergy that gets you there along the entire journey."

## Two routes, and the thieves

- **Homecoming:** fly often and replay text. Swallow, Chiffchaff, Dunnock, Jay, Old Tern, Mrs Stork, Mother Goose.
- **Hatchery:** keep Birds away and make their Nests fight. Redbreast, Redwing, Tom Tit, Great Lump, Gabble Ratchet, Mother Goose.
- **They pull apart:** Dunnock and Stork bring Birds home, emptying the Eggs Tom Tit and the Ratchet want. Shops decide the lean.
- **Thieves** (Jack Daw, Mag Pye, Barn Owl) are answers that fit either route.

## The competition, by tier

- **T1:** Young Pretender 3/2, "Snap. After this kills a Mired enemy: get 1 gold next turn." Wee Wobbly 1/4, "Belly 1. After this survives a hit: the unit inside comes out, or a 1/1 Drip if empty. Twice a fight." Little Nip 2/1, "Quarry. Take: get 1 gold next turn."
- **T2:** Moss Ooze 2/4, "When a spell gives your Ooze stats: what's inside gains them." Sable 2/3, "Quarry. Start of Combat: if its quarry has more Attack, gain +3 Attack."
- **T3:** The Lodger 4/4, "Pounce. After this pounces: gain +2/+1." Brother Wolf 3/4, "Pack. Units beside this attack first; this joins each once a cycle." The Natterjack Chorus 3/4, "Your units beside this have Mire and Bitter 1."
- **T4:** Charcoal Burner 4/6, "End of Turn: burn up to 6 of your gold above 5. Units behind this gain +1/+1 per 2 burned." Will-o'-the-Wisp 3/6, "Start of Combat: summon a 1/1 Wisp into each empty Ring."
- **T5:** Grizel 5/6, "Pack. After your pack attack kills: its attackers gain +1 Attack permanently." Hasty Pudding 2/8, "Belly 1. Start of Combat: if this has room, swallow the enemy across."
- **T6:** Whirligig Beetle 6/8, "Start of Combat: swap the enemy's rows. Each enemy moved takes 2 damage." The Peat King 5/9, "Mire. After a Mired enemy dies: summon a 4/4 Peatling with Bitter 2. Twice a fight."

## The 16 cards

*G* is the golden version and *R* the rules note.

### Tier 1

**One Swallow** 1/2, changed. "Fly South. When played: get 1 gold." *G* 2/4: "…get 2 gold." *R:* pays again on every return; "next to a Bird" is gone.
- **Role:** economy, and your first Nest. **Window:** R1–R3, on an empty board.
- **Vs:** Little Nip, Young Pretender.
- **Why:** it's the only T1 that pays on turn 1, now, with nothing beside it. R3 then buys two units, and flown on R2 it's the Nest R3's Redbreast counts.

**Chiffchaff** 1/2, changed. "Fly South. When played: gain +1/+1." *G* 2/4: "…+2/+2." *R:* kept while away; its Egg is half its current stats.
- **Role:** a tempo body that grows by flying. **Window:** R1–R4 as a 2/3; later, Stork's and Dunnock's target.
- **Vs:** Young Pretender, Wee Wobbly.
- **Why:** it lands as a 2/3, level with the best turn-1 bodies, and comes back bigger after every trip.

**Mrs Dunnock** 2/3 (was 1/3), text kept. "End of Turn: if this is on a Nest, its Bird comes home a turn sooner." *G* 4/6: "…two turns sooner." *R:* never sooner than next turn; a Nest she stands on has no Egg.
- **Role:** a body, then the trip-shortener. **Window:** R1–R3 as a plain 2/3; from R3, standing on the Nest you want back.
- **Vs:** Wee Wobbly, Young Pretender.
- **Why:** on turn 1 she's tied for the most stats in the tier. Then she turns two-fight trips into one, at the price of the Egg.

### Tier 2

**Mr Redbreast** 2/3 (was 1/2), text kept. "Has +1/+1 for each Nest you have." *G* 4/6: "…+2/+2…" *R:* counts Nests whether or not something stands on them.
- **Role:** Nest payoff and front wall. **Window:** R3–R6, at 3/4 to 4/5 with one or two Nests from T1 fliers.
- **Vs:** Moss Ooze, Sable.
- **Why:** alone he's a par 2/3. One flown T1 makes him the biggest T2 body.

**Redwing** 2/4 (was 2/2), text kept. "Fly South. Eggs beside this have their Bird's full stats." *G*: "…full stats, +2/+2." *R:* as now.
- **Role:** Hatchery enabler. **Window:** R3–R7, beside one or two Eggs.
- **Vs:** Moss Ooze, Sable.
- **Why:** alone it matches Moss Ooze; beside a flown 3/4 Chiffchaff it refunds the 1/2 flying cost.

**Mrs Jay** 3/3 (was 2/2), text kept. "Fly South. When sold: get an Acorn." *G* 6/6: "…two Acorns." *R:* flying is selling, so it fires.
- **Role:** the flier that leaves for free; a bridge to Hedgerow. **Window:** R2–R6.
- **Vs:** Sable, Moss Ooze.
- **Why:** tied for most stats in the tier, most Attack, and her Acorn makes every trip a free Nest.

### Tier 3

**Tom Tit** 3/4, redesigned and moved up from T1. "Your Eggs have +2/+2." *G* 6/8: "…+4/+4." *R:* after Redwing; only the strongest Tom Tit counts.
- **Role:** Hatchery payoff, the reason to leave Birds away. **Window:** R5–R9, with two Eggs out.
- **Vs:** Brother Wolf, Natterjack Chorus, The Lodger.
- **Why:** with no Eggs, a par 3/4. With two Eggs, +4/+4, more than any other T3 text adds by R6.

**Jack Daw** 3/4 (was 1/2), text kept. "Fly South. Start of Combat: steal a quarter of the buffs on the enemy across. If you do, get 1 gold next turn." *G*: "…2 gold…" *R:* at most +4/+4.
- **Role:** a flier that answers buffed carries. **Window:** R5–R10, facing Ring piles, Chasers and Dozey.
- **Vs:** The Lodger, Brother Wolf.
- **Why:** a par body now, and the two strongest R6 benchmark boards are buff piles to steal from.

**Old Tern** 2/4 (was 1/3). "Fly South, for 3 turns. When this comes home: Discover a Bird of your tier." *G*: "…two Birds…" *R:* offers fewer if the tier has fewer than three Birds.
- **Role:** card flow for Homecoming; it finds the T5 and T6 pieces. **Window:** R5–R10, flown the turn after you buy it.
- **Vs:** Brother Wolf, Natterjack Chorus.
- **Why:** a free Bird each trip. Birds only, because finding payoffs is where the old journey broke.

### Tier 4

**Mrs Stork** 3/6 (was 2/5), text kept. "Every 6 gold you spend: a Bird away comes home." *G*: "…two Birds…" *R:* as now.
- **Role:** the Homecoming engine. **Window:** R7–R12, with two or more Birds away.
- **Vs:** Charcoal Burner, Will-o'-the-Wisp.
- **Why:** Burner turns late gold into stats; Stork turns it into replays (Tern Discovers, Chiffchaff growth, Swallow gold).

**Barn Owl** 4/5 (was 3/4), text kept. "Swoop. Takes no damage while attacking the back row." *G:* stats only. *R:* as now.
- **Role:** a raider body. **Window:** R7–R12, facing back-row scalers.
- **Vs:** Charcoal Burner, Will-o'-the-Wisp.
- **Why:** kills the back-row scalers every benchmark board protects, undamaged.

**Mag Pye** 3/5 (was 2/3), text kept. "Start of Combat: steal the text of the enemy across." *G*: "…two enemies: the one across and the next along." *R:* as now.
- **Role:** an answer. **Window:** R7–R12, facing an engine card.
- **Vs:** Will-o'-the-Wisp, Charcoal Burner.
- **Why:** the only T4 that removes Nightshade, Hex or a Mire wall.

### Tier 5

**Mother Goose** 4/7, kept. "Fly South. While away, Start of Combat: each Nest strikes the enemy across for its Bird's base Attack." *G*: "…strikes… twice…" *R:* her own Egg stays 0/2.
- **Role:** the shared Nest payoff. **Window:** R10–R12, with three or more Nests.
- **Vs:** Grizel, Hasty Pudding.
- **Why:** 6–10 damage before anyone attacks; her Nest seats the Lump.

**The Great Lump** 4/6 (was T2, 1/3), text kept. "On a Nest, this also has its Bird's base stats." *G*: "…twice its Bird's base stats." *R:* base stats, so buffs aren't doubled; it shrinks the turn the Bird lands.
- **Role:** the Hatchery's big body. **Window:** R10–R12, on Goose's Nest (8/13) or Jack Daw's (7/10).
- **Vs:** Grizel, Hasty Pudding.
- **Why:** on a Nest from T3 up, it's the biggest T5 body.

### Tier 6

**The Gabble Ratchet** 4/8 (was 3/7), text kept. "When a unit on a Nest dies: its Bird lands there." *G*: "…with twice its Attack." *R:* as now: Eggs count, once per Nest each fight.
- **Role:** the capstone, where both halves of each Bird fight. **Window:** R12+, with three or more Nests.
- **Vs:** The Peat King, Whirligig Beetle.
- **Why:** with three Nests, 20–40 stats arrive mid-fight.

**The Seven Whistlers** 5/7 (was 3/3), text kept. "Start of Combat: give each enemy across from your Birds and Eggs -1/-1." *G*: "…-2/-2." *R:* as now.
- **Role:** an answer to wide boards, for either route. **Window:** R12+.
- **Vs:** Whirligig Beetle, The Peat King.
- **Why:** kills Wisps, Kits and Drips before they act.

## The journey, checked

| Rounds | Homecoming | Hatchery | Makes you want |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1–3 | Swallow, Chiffchaff, Dunnock | Same | A Nest by R2–R3 |
| 4–6 | Jay, Old Tern, Jack Daw | Redbreast, Redwing, Tom Tit | Birds away: Stork, or Eggs worth keeping |
| 7–9 | Mrs Stork | Barn Owl, Mag Pye | Goose; the Lump |
| 10+ | Goose, Whistlers | Lump, Goose, Ratchet | — |

**Skip risks:**
- **Hatchery at T4** has bodies but no route piece. If players hold T3 and rush the T5s, add a T4 Egg piece.
- **Homecoming at T2** has only Jay; Redbreast pays for its Nests.

## Benchmark boards, average roll

Assumptions as in `benchmark-boards.md`; Eggs at fight values.

**R3, tier 2, 13 gold.** R1 Swallow (+1 gold). R2 Tier up, then fly the Swallow. R3 Redbreast and Chiffchaff.
- Board: F1 Redbreast 3/4, F2 Chiffchaff 2/3, F3 the Swallow's Egg 1/1.
- **14 stats. Expected rank 2–3** against 12–14; it was 6th.

**R6, tier 3, 36 gold** (with the Swallow's gold and an Acorn).
- R4 Jay, Redwing; R5 Tom Tit, fly Chiffchaff and Jay; R6 Jack Daw, Supper, Cordial.
- Board: F1 Redbreast 6/8 (2 Nests, Supper, Cordial), F2 Chiffchaff Egg 4/5, F3 Redwing 3/5, F4 Jay Egg 5/5; B1 Jack Daw 4/5, B2 Tom Tit 4/5, B3 Swallow 2/3.
- **64 stats. Expected rank 2–3**, between Oozes (65) and Fairies (62); it was 6th.

**R9, tier 4, one Supper.**
- Adds Stork, Barn Owl, Old Tern and Stand Behind Me; Chiffchaff and the Swallow benched.
- Board: Redbreast 5/6, Tom Tit 4/5, Redwing 3/5, Stork 4/7, Owl 5/6, Jay Egg 6/6 (beside Redwing), Tern Egg 4/5, plus +2/+2 on two units.
- **About 79. Expected rank 3–4**, level with Hedgerow folk and Hunters, behind Oozes (97) and Fairies (86); it was 6th.

**R12, tier 6, Supper +3/+3, one golden.**
- Adds Goose (away), the Lump and the Ratchet.
- Board: F1 Lump 11/16 on Goose's Nest, F2 Jay Egg 8/8, F3 Redwing 5/7, F4 Tern Egg 7/9; B1 golden Redbreast 13/15, B2 Ratchet 7/11, B3 Tom Tit 6/7.
- **About 130 on view**, plus Goose's 9 damage and about 40 stats the Ratchet lands as the Lump and Eggs die.
- **Expected rank 1–2**, against Oozes (155) and Fairies (151).

**Reading:**
- Overshoot risk is R12: first knob Tom Tit (+1/+1), then the Ratchet's body.
- Likeliest low-roll collapse: Hatchery without Redwing.

## Calls I made

Tom Tit's thief design is dropped; Old Tern Discovers only Birds; the Lump keeps base stats; Mother Goose is unchanged.
