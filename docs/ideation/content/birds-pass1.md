# Birds, pass 1b (plan step 3)

8 Nov 2026, a cloud designer, revising pass 1 after `evidence/birds-pass1-triage.md` and `evidence/birds-pass1-costing.md`. No power budget; numbers are guesses for costing.

16 shop Birds at 3/3/3/3/2/2; every T1 and T2 Bird is a par body on an empty board. Fly South, Nests and Eggs are unchanged. **New engine pieces:** Tom Tit's aura, the Cuckoo, Goose's homecoming buff, the Whistlers' homecoming count. Target: the owner's "synergy that gets you there along the entire journey", with "multiple different sub-varieties".

## Two routes that mix, and the thieves

- **Homecoming:** fly often, bring Birds home, replay text. Swallow, Chiffchaff, Dunnock, Jay, Old Tern, Mrs Stork, Mother Goose, the Seven Whistlers.
- **Hatchery:** keep Birds away and make their Nests fight. Redbreast, Redwing, Tom Tit, the Cuckoo, Mother Goose, the Great Lump, the Gabble Ratchet.
- **Where they meet:** Goose is in both: away, her Nests strike; home, she buffs every Bird. The Cuckoo grows Birds while they're away, and they come home big. Dunnock and the Cuckoo pull the same Nest opposite ways, so slots decide. Shops decide the lean.
- **Thieves** (Jack Daw, Mag Pye) are a splash for either route, not a route.

## The competition, by tier

Every slice-pool unit at the tier, as the reviewers listed them (full text in their card files).
- **T1:** Young Pretender 3/2 (Snap, gold), Wee Wobbly 1/4 (Belly, Drips), Little Nip 2/1 (Quarry, gold), Pill Bug 1/4 (feeds the back row), Warty Nan 1/3 (Bitter beside).
- **T2:** Moss Ooze 2/4, Sable 2/3 (Quarry, +3 Attack), Madame Bufo 1/4 (Mire, Bitter 2), Mrs Sorrel 2/3 (Shrewlets, Spill), the Toll-Bard 2/3 (up to 3 gold a fight), Turnip Scarecrow 0/4 (skips an attack).
- **T3:** The Lodger 4/4 (Pounce, grows), Brother Wolf 3/4 (Pack), the Natterjack Chorus 3/4 (Mire, Bitter beside), Sexton Beetle 2/4 (gold on friendly deaths, twice a fight), Ballooning Spider 3/2 (back-row reach).
- **T4:** Charcoal Burner 4/6 (burns gold above 5 into +1/+1s behind it), Will-o'-the-Wisp 3/6 (Wisps into empty Rings), Madame Ermeline 3/6 (cheaper upgrades per Take), Mr Syllabub 3/6 (Start of Combat text fires again out of Oozes).
- **T5:** Grizel 5/6 (Pack, permanent Attack), Hasty Pudding 2/8 (swallows the enemy across).
- **T6:** Whirligig Beetle 6/8 (swaps the enemy rows, 2 damage each), The Peat King 5/9 (Mire, 4/4 Peatlings).

## How an away Bird is read

Two readings only:
1. **The Egg:** half its Bird's current stats, full beside Redwing, plus Tom Tit's +1/+1. Mother Goose's Egg follows the same rule (her 0/2 exception is gone).
2. **The Bird's current stats:** what comes home, what the Ratchet lands, and (halved) what the Lump borrows.

Goose's strike is a flat 2, so base stats are read nowhere.

**The card UI must show on an Egg:** its own stats; its Bird's current stats; and turns until it comes home, since Dunnock, the Cuckoo and Stork change that. Dragging the Lump onto a Nest previews its total.

## The 16 cards

*G* is the golden version and *R* the rules note.

### Tier 1

**One Swallow** 1/2. "Fly South. When played: get 1 gold." *G* 2/4: "…2 gold." *R:* pays again on every return.
- **Role:** economy, and your first Nest. **Window:** R1–R3 on an empty board; later, Stork's gold loop.
- **Vs:** the whole T1 field; sharpest Young Pretender.
- **Why:** the only T1 that pays on turn 1; flown on R2, it's Redbreast's Nest.

**Chiffchaff** 1/2. "Fly South. When played: gain +1/+1." *G* 2/4: "…+2/+2." *R:* kept while away.
- **Role:** a tempo body that grows by flying. **Window:** R1–R4 as a 2/3; later, Stork's and the Cuckoo's target.
- **Vs:** the whole T1 field; sharpest Wee Wobbly and Pill Bug.
- **Why:** lands as a 2/3, level with the best turn-1 bodies, and returns bigger.

**Mrs Dunnock** 2/3, changed. "End of Turn: a Bird whose Nest is beside this comes home a turn sooner." *G* 4/6: "…two turns sooner." *R:* never sooner than next turn; she doesn't stand on the Nest, so its Egg stays and fights.
- **Role:** a body, then Homecoming's trip-shortener. **Window:** R1–R3 as a plain 2/3; from R3, beside the Nest you want back.
- **Vs:** the whole T1 field; sharpest Wee Wobbly.
- **Why:** tied for most stats in the tier; Hatchery keeps her off its Nests.

### Tier 2

**Mr Redbreast** 2/4. "Has +1/+1 for each Nest you have." *G* 4/8: "…+2/+2…" *R:* counts Nests whether or not something stands on them.
- **Role:** Nest payoff and front wall. **Window:** R3–R9, at 3/5 to 4/6 with one or two Nests.
- **Vs:** the whole T2 field; sharpest Moss Ooze and Madame Bufo.
- **Why:** alone he matches Moss Ooze. One flown T1 makes him the biggest T2 body.

**Redwing** 2/4. "Fly South. Eggs beside this have their Bird's full stats." *G*: "…full stats, +2/+2."
- **Role:** Hatchery enabler. **Window:** R3–R12, beside one or two Eggs.
- **Vs:** the whole T2 field; sharpest Moss Ooze.
- **Why:** a Moss Ooze body that makes a flown Bird whole.

**Mrs Jay** 3/3. "Fly South. When sold: get an Acorn." *G* 6/6: "…two Acorns." *R:* flying is selling, so it fires.
- **Role:** the flier that leaves for free; a bridge to Hedgerow. **Window:** R2–R6, then the Cuckoo's best Nest.
- **Vs:** the whole T2 field; sharpest Sable and the Toll-Bard.
- **Why:** most Attack in the tier, and each trip is a free Nest.

### Tier 3

**Tom Tit** 2/4, changed. "Your Eggs have +1/+1." *G* 4/8: "…+2/+2." *R:* after Redwing; only the strongest Tom Tit counts.
- **Role:** Hatchery payoff. **Window:** R5–R9, with two Eggs out.
- **Vs:** the whole T3 field; sharpest the Lodger.
- **Why:** with two Eggs, 10 stats against the Lodger's 8 and climbing; with none, a Moss Ooze body.

**Jack Daw** 2/4, changed. "Fly South. Start of Combat: steal a quarter of the buffs on the enemy across. If you do, get 1 gold next turn." *G*: "…2 gold…" *R:* at most +4/+4.
- **Role:** a thief splash, an answer to buffed carries. **Window:** R5–R10, facing buff piles.
- **Vs:** the whole T3 field; Sexton Beetle sets its bar.
- **Why:** level with Sexton Beetle on gold, with a stat swing on top.

**Old Tern** 2/4. "Fly South, for 3 turns. When this comes home: Discover a Bird of your tier." *G*: "…two Birds…" *R:* offers fewer if the tier has fewer than three Birds.
- **Role:** card flow for Homecoming; finds the T5s and T6s. **Window:** R5–R10, flown the turn after buying.
- **Vs:** the whole T3 field; sharpest Brother Wolf.
- **Why:** a free Bird per trip.

### Tier 4

**Mrs Stork** 3/6. "Every 6 gold you spend: a Bird away comes home." *G*: "…two Birds…" *R:* a Bird must have missed a fight.
- **Role:** the Homecoming engine. **Window:** R7–R12, with two or more Birds away.
- **Vs:** all four T4s; sharpest Charcoal Burner.
- **Why:** Burner turns late gold into stats; Stork turns it into replays and Goose buffs.

**The Cuckoo** 3/6, new (replaces Barn Owl). "A Bird that flies from beside this stays away a turn longer. End of Turn: Birds away whose Nests are beside this gain +2/+2." *G* 6/12: "…two turns longer… +4/+4." *R:* the longer trip is set when the Bird flies, once; the growth is permanent and comes home with the Bird.
- **Role:** Hatchery's T4 piece: it keeps Birds away and makes their Eggs grow. **Window:** R7–R12, between two Nests.
- **Vs:** all four T4s: Burner, Wisp, Ermeline, Syllabub.
- **Why:** Burner's growth costs gold; hers is free, on two Nests, visible on the Eggs (+1/+1 a turn each). Birds she grew come home big, so she pays Homecoming too.

**Mag Pye** 3/6 (was 3/5). "Start of Combat: steal the text of the enemy across." *G*: "…the one across and the next along."
- **Role:** a thief splash, an answer. **Window:** R7–R12, facing an engine card.
- **Vs:** all four T4s; sharpest Will-o'-the-Wisp.
- **Why:** the only T4 that removes Nightshade, Hex or a Mire wall; now a T4 body.

### Tier 5

**Mother Goose** 4/7, changed. "Fly South. While away, Start of Combat: each Nest strikes the enemy across for 2. When this comes home: your Birds gain +1/+1." *G*: "…for 4… +2/+2." *R:* the buff is permanent and reaches Birds away.
- **Role:** the shared payoff: Hatchery's while away, Homecoming's when Stork brings her back. **Window:** R10–R12.
- **Vs:** Grizel, Hasty Pudding.
- **Why:** 6–10 damage before anyone attacks, or a tribe buff every other turn.

**The Great Lump** 3/5, changed. "On a Nest, this also has half its Bird's current stats." *G*: "…its Bird's full current stats." *R:* that's the Egg before auras; it shrinks the turn the Bird lands.
- **Role:** the Hatchery's big body. **Window:** R10–R12, on a grown Bird's Nest.
- **Vs:** Grizel, Hasty Pudding.
- **Why:** on a Cuckoo-grown Jay's Nest it's 7/9 before Supper, the biggest T5 body.

### Tier 6

**The Gabble Ratchet** 3/7. "When a unit on a Nest dies: its Bird lands there." *G*: "…with twice its Attack." *R:* Eggs count; once per Nest each fight. The Bird lands with its current stats and fights the rest of the fight. After it, the Bird is away again: its trip isn't ended or changed, and its Nest and Egg are back.
- **Role:** Hatchery's capstone, both halves of each Bird. **Window:** R12+, with three or more Nests.
- **Vs:** The Peat King, Whirligig Beetle.
- **Why:** with three grown Birds, 25–35 stats arrive mid-fight.

**The Seven Whistlers** 5/7, changed. "Start of Combat: for each Bird that came home this turn, give each enemy across from your Birds -1/-1 (up to -3/-3)." *G*: "…-2/-2 each (up to -6/-6)."
- **Role:** Homecoming's capstone, still an answer to wide boards. **Window:** R12+, with Stork.
- **Vs:** Whirligig Beetle, The Peat King.
- **Why:** two homecomings a turn kill Wisps, Kits and Drips before they act.

## The journey, checked

| Rounds | Homecoming | Hatchery | Makes you want |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1–3 | Swallow, Chiffchaff, Dunnock | Swallow, Chiffchaff | A Nest by R2–R3 |
| 4–6 | Jay, Old Tern | Redbreast, Redwing, Tom Tit | Stork or the Cuckoo |
| 7–9 | Stork | The Cuckoo | Goose; the Lump |
| 10+ | Goose (home), Whistlers | Goose (away), Lump, Ratchet | — |

Thieves splash in at R5 and R7. No route skips a tier; Homecoming's T5 is Goose alone, the thinnest step.

**Swallow with Stork:** not runaway. A Bird must miss a fight, so a Swallow cycles every 2 turns at most: 1 gold home plus 1 flying, ≤1 a turn (golden ≤1.5). Each Stork trigger needs 6 gold spent and refunds ≤2, so gold can't feed itself. Watch two Swallows in bots.

## Revision

- **Costing 1–5:** Tom Tit 2/4, Eggs +1/+1; Jack Daw 2/4; Lump 3/5; Ratchet 3/7; Redbreast 2/4. They fix the R12 overshoot.
- **Cut 1:** Barn Owl becomes the Cuckoo, Hatchery's T4 buy. Spread stays 3/3/3/3/2/2.
- **Cut 2:** Homecoming runs through T4–T6 (Stork, Goose home, Whistlers); Goose and the Cuckoo serve both routes.
- **Dunnock:** a Nest beside her; the Egg stays.
- **Ratchet:** landing rules stated.
- **Readings:** four down to two; Egg UI written out.
- **Mag Pye 3/6:** under the T4 bodies.
- **Boards:** R12 high relabelled; average (both routes) and low added; R6, R9 recosted.
- **Swallow with Stork:** bounded.

## Calls I made

The Cuckoo replaces Barn Owl, not Mag Pye, the only text-removal answer. Homecoming, not thieves, is the second route: it already had four early cards. The Whistlers keep -1/-1 but count homecomings.
