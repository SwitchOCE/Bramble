# Layout options

- **Woodland Table** — The Bazaar's stable central stack translated to Bramble: shop, two ranks and hand share one woodland table, with a compact action dock and a quiet lobby rail.
- **Sidecar Market** — A compact merchant display sits beside the player's two ranks, keeping shopping and formation in separate, stable places and turning the merchant side into the opponent's board during a fight.
- **Rootbridge Diorama** — A steeply viewed woodland platform carries upright Ink-B pieces at different depths, with the merchant beyond the formation and a mirrored opponent beyond the fight's centre line.
