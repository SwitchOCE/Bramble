# Mechanics library (archived 16 Nov 2026)

Unit and mechanic ideas written on 2 Nov 2026, before the design skills existed: 65 first-round mechanics from four designers, then a library of 75 mechanics with example units, play scenes and variations. Idea material only. Nothing here is decided or adopted, and it is not a roster.

## Why it was archived

The owner's words, 16 Nov 2026:

> "I am worried that it's just lower quality and we should probably just archive it."

and earlier, that it "was pre-design skill".

## What is here

- [mechanics-ideas.md](mechanics-ideas.md) and [mechanics-ideas/](mechanics-ideas/): the first-round list, merged from four designers' files, with a rough budget review.
- [mechanics-library.md](mechanics-library.md) and [mechanics-library.html](mechanics-library.html): the 75-entry library, built from the files in [mechanics-library-src/](mechanics-library-src/).
- [build_mechanics_library.py](build_mechanics_library.py): the script that built the library. It was `tools/research/build_mechanics_library.py` and still reads and writes the old `docs/design/` paths, so it won't run from here as is.
- [PLAN.md](PLAN.md): the paused workstream plan. Was `docs/workstreams/mechanics-library.md`.

The proposed effect classes E89–E98 and R32–R34 in [effect-taxonomy.md](../../design/effect-taxonomy.md) came from this budget review. They are proposals there too.

Files were moved with `git mv`, so `git log --follow <file>` shows their earlier history.
