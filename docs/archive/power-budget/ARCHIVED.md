# Power budget (archived 16 Nov 2026)

The costing method from 2 Nov 2026 and its in-depth review. The owner never reviewed either. Both are built on Battlegrounds-scale stats and the economy from before rulebook v0.7. Nothing here is decided, and it is not how content is costed.

## Why it is archived

The [tier guide](../../design/tier-guide.md) replaced strict per-card budgets on 12 Nov 2026. The owner, 12 Nov: "more important than the budget is just having it be like, instead of being really strict, have it be like pretty simplistic."

On 16 Nov 2026 the owner was asked whether this file should move to the archive, taking review points B1–B7 and "a simulation validates the power budget" with it, and agreed.

## What is here

- [power-budget.md](power-budget.md): the method (v1 draft, revision 2).
- [power-budget-review.md](power-budget-review.md): how long a scaling card takes to catch up, with decision points B1–B7. None was answered.
