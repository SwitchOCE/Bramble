# Ogres

## 1. Identity

**Chieftain (rule, not a keyword).** Your Chieftain is your minion with the highest Attack. If two are tied, the leftmost one is Chieftain. The Chieftain is rechecked at once whenever Attack changes or a minion arrives or dies, in the Recruit Phase and in combat, and a crown icon marks it. A minion of any type can be Chieftain.

**Usurp (new keyword).** Triggers when this minion becomes your Chieftain. It triggers at most once per turn in the Recruit Phase, where its effect is permanent, and once per combat. When a Chieftain dies in combat, the next-highest minion takes the crown, and that counts as becoming Chieftain.

**Bicker (new keyword).** At the end of your turn, swap this minion's Attack and Health. The two heads take turns to be in charge.

**Gordok Green Grog (token spell).** Ogres brew it, and it gets stronger the longer you keep it.

**How the tribe plays.** Might makes right: the biggest ogre on your board wears the crown, and the rest either feed it or try to take it. You have three ways to move Attack around. Grog adds Attack only. Bicker and swap effects turn Health into Attack and back. Tribute effects pour stats into whoever wears the crown. Every time the crown changes hands, Usurp effects and King Gordok pay out. Every stat you hand out therefore also decides who leads. A board you feed one tall king works against a board where the crown moves several times a turn, and the best Ogre players hold both in mind. Bicker turns Grog into Health, Mog'dorg flips the crown to the weakest minion, and a Fleshshaper can crown another tribe's carry. The tribe is built so that its pieces can be used against their obvious purpose.

**Tokens, spell and trinkets**

- **Gordok Green Grog** (token spell, 0 Gold): Give a minion +1 Attack. *Ferments:* gains +1 Attack at the start of each of your turns while it is in your hand.
- **Pol** (token, Ogre): created by Phemos. It has Phemos's current stats swapped.
- **Altar of Storms** (Tavern spell, Tier 2, 2 Gold): Give a minion Bicker and +2/+2.
- **Grog Barrel** (Lesser trinket, 4 Gold): Your Grogs ferment twice as fast. Add a Grog to your hand now.
- **Tribute Chest** (Greater trinket, 5 Gold): At the end of your turn, give your Chieftain +2/+2 for each Gold you didn't spend.

## 3. Builds

**Revolving Crown.** Squabbling Two-Heads, Gordok Tapster, Crushridge Mercenary, Grog, Maulgar, Ko'ragh and King Gordok. The aim is to move the crown several times every turn. A Bicker minion swaps into its tall form at end of turn and takes the crown. Tapster's Usurp hands you a Grog. You cast it on someone else, the crown moves again, and King Gordok buffs all your Ogres each time. The Mercenary is a crown you can buy back for 1 Gold. In combat, every dead king passes the crown on. Ko'ragh gets a shield when his turn comes, and Maulgar pumps the rest of the court. Skill lies in ordering the changes so each Usurp minion fires once and King Gordok fires as often as possible.

**One True King.** Tribute-Bearer, Kromcrush, The Butcher, Pit Fighter, Doomhammer's Guard, Twilight Zealot, Gruul and Mar'gok. Pick one carry and feed it. The carry need not be an Ogre: crown a Divine Shield Mech, a Reborn Undead, or a Dragon you have been stacking. Kromcrush plus Ogre Magi is the trick here. Swap a 2/10 into 10/2, sell it, and the king gains +10 Attack. The Butcher can eat the king itself, which moves its stats to the heir and counts as a crown change for King Gordok. Mar'gok sends the king to duel their best minion, and Doomhammer's Guard punishes anyone who touches the king.

**Two Heads.** Ogre Magi, Bicker minions, Altar of Storms, Cho'Rush, Phemos and Cho'gall. Here the swap is the resource. Cho'Rush adds +2/+2 to every swap. Cho'gall swaps your whole board each turn. The key Johnny trick: a Bicker minion under Cho'gall swaps twice, so it ends where it started, and Cho'Rush still pays out twice. Grog cast while a Bicker minion is in its short form becomes Health after the swap. Altar of Storms on Phemos makes the twins swap places each turn.

**The Wizened Wall.** Mog'dorg, Gog and Mar'gok, with Gruul or King Gordok. Under Mog'dorg the weakest minion is king and has Taunt. Gog is built with low Attack on purpose, so he takes the crown and the Taunt and kills the first big minion that hits him. Mar'gok then sends him at their highest-Attack minion at Start of Combat. Gruul under Mog'dorg spreads tribute around: each +2/+2 lifts the current weakest minion, and someone else becomes king. Add King Gordok and one Gruul turn can move the crown six times.

**In a five-tribe lobby**

- **Pirates:** Gold feeds Mercenary activations, Tribute Chest and sell loops with Kromcrush.
- **Beasts:** Low-Attack tokens take the crown under Mog'dorg. Deathrattle swarms give you a long line of heirs.
- **Mechs:** A Divine Shield king, Magnetic upgrades on Gog, and a Ko'ragh heir.
- **Undead:** A Reborn king dies, crowns an heir, then comes back.
- **Aberrations:** The Zealot counts toward the Deity. Discard effects can throw away a Grog, at the cost of its fermented value.
- **Murlocs:** Battlecry repeats make Ogre Magi swap twice. That cancels the swap but keeps both +1/+1 buffs, so it is a clean buff.
- **Demons, Dragons, Quilboar:** Each grows a big minion that the tribute can crown.
- **Elementals:** These connect least. Their Tavern buffs don't touch the crown.

Fleshshaper is the universal bridge. An Ogre-Murloc gets both tribes' buffs and counts for Gruul.

## 4. Risks and holes

- **Readability.** The crown moves often, especially in combat. It needs a clear crown icon and a short animation whenever it moves. Mog'dorg changing the rule mid-game will confuse opponents watching the replay.
- **King Gordok churn.** Loops can't run forever: Usurp is once per turn per minion, and Grog, sells and Activates run out. But Gruul plus Mog'dorg plus King Gordok can buff the whole board many times a turn. If it is too strong, cap "Chieftain changes" at 5 triggers per turn.
- **Grog hoarding.** Ferment has no cap. Ten old Grogs clog the hand but hit hard late. A cap of +6 is the fallback.
- **Bicker feel.** On alternate turns a Bicker minion fights as a glass cannon or as a wall, which can feel like a coin flip. Spikes will want to play around it, Timmys may not. Cho'gall makes a whole board unstable if you didn't plan for it.
- **Gog** with Divine Shield or Reborn is a strong answer to single-carry boards. The once-per-combat limit is the safety valve.
- **Mar'gok's duel** strongly favours whoever has the bigger king. It snowballs in mirrors.
- **Fleshshaper** gives minions an extra type. It needs a check against every "your [Type]" effect in the game.
- **Few high-tier Usurp minions** (Maulgar, Ko'ragh). Revolving builds lean on cheap Usurp minions plus King Gordok, and may run out of payoffs late.
- **Elementals** barely interact with Ogres.
- **Duos:** passing fully fermented Grogs to a teammate may need a rule, for example that passing resets ferment.
- **Tone:** the lore's slave fights are framed here as pits and gladiators.

## 5. Design rationale

1. Started from Gordok Green Grog: beer as a resource that you stockpile and that ferments in hand. Holding it is a choice no other tribe has.
2. Took King Gordok's tribute run, where anyone may take the throne, and made the crown go to the highest Attack, because "might makes right" is then visible on the board.
3. Saw that Grog adds only Attack, so it moves the crown. That was the first interlock, and Attack became the stat the tribe cares about.
4. Took "kill the king, become king" and made Usurp trigger on any succession, including in combat, so the mechanic matters in both phases.
5. Took the bickering two heads and made Bicker swap Attack and Health. A swap moves the crown without adding stats, and it turns Grog into Health.
6. The shared mechanic came out of those cards: who holds the most Attack. The Chieftain may be of any type, so Ogres can crown other tribes' carries.
7. Tribute chests and The Butcher's cauldron gave the "feed the king" cards, which work against the revolving crown. Two builds came from that tension.
8. Mog'dorg seeking peace became the rule-inverter: one card that rewrites the system, for Johnny.
9. Gog slaying gronn became a minion that kills bigger enemies. His low Attack is deliberate so he pairs with Mog'dorg, and his ability is once per combat for Spike.
10. The Highmaul arena gave Mar'gok's duel. It gives the crown a job in combat and gives Spike an answer to single carries.
11. Kelgrok breeding the Mok'nathal became Fleshshaper, the bridge to other tribes.
12. Mercenary, Guard, Pit Fighter and Tapster were added last to fill gaps by role. Only two tokens, one spell and two trinkets were added, to keep the extras few.
