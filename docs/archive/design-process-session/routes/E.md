# Gemspinner Mystic

## 1. The card
- **Tier 4 | Quilboar | 3/5**
- **Text:** After you play a Blood Gem on this, get a copy of the last other Tavern spell you cast this turn. *(Once per turn.)*
- **Golden (6/10):** Twice per turn.

"Other" means not a Blood Gem. Copies of temporary spells stay temporary. The card shows which spell it will copy.

## 2. Its place in a game
Bought on turns 6-9, once you buy a strong Tavern spell each turn or your hero makes spells. It goes into Quilboar boards that buy spells, with gem banks such as Sun-Bacon Relaxer, or into Quilboar/Naga boards. Early on it's a 3/5 that grows from its own gems. Late on it's the engine.

## 3. Interactions outside Quilboar
- **Naga:** a copied Spellcraft spell is a second Spellcraft cast. "After you cast a spell" payoffs trigger three times: the gem, the original and the copy.
- **Shop spells:** doubles what you bought, whether Discover, stat or tribe-buff spells.
- **Charlga:** her end-of-turn gem still triggers it. The copy goes into hand and carries over to the next turn as a stored spell.
- **Hero powers that give spells:** these feed it with no shop luck needed.

## 4. Risks and failure cases
- **Gold spells:** copying a spell that gives Gold adds economy. Test it against the strongest Gold spell, and exclude any spell that nets about 1 Gold or more.
- **Gem-making spells:** a spell that gives Blood Gems makes a loop. The once-per-turn limit ends it, but check the golden version.
- **Dead turns:** with no spell cast, it's a plain 3/5.
- **Memory load:** players lose track of "last spell this turn", so the on-card display is required.
- **Golden ceiling:** two copies of Tier 6 spells per turn may be too strong. If so, the golden version gets 2 copies once per turn instead.

## 5. Design rationale
1. Listed what Blood Gems need: generate, scale, target, pay off, convert. Generate and scale have many cards. Convert has almost none.
2. Chose convert: turning gems into another resource takes the system outside Quilboar.
3. Converted into Tavern spells: gems already count as spells, so spell payoffs from other tribes join in.
4. Copies the spell you chose, not a random one: the player sets up the result, which suits Johnny.
5. The gem keeps its normal +1/+1, so the card never costs stats.
6. Once per turn limits loops, which matters for Spike.
7. Tier 4 at 3/5: you need gold left over for spells, and it shouldn't win fights on its own.
8. Named it last.
