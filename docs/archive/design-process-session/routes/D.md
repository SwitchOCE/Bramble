# Truffle Snuffler

**Tier 3 · Quilboar · 3/4**

**Text:** At the start of your turn, bury a Blood Gem in the leftmost minion in the Tavern. When you play a minion, play its buried Blood Gems on it.

**Golden (6/8):** Same, but bury 2 Blood Gems.

Buried gems stay while the minion stays in the Tavern. Refreshing or leveling up loses them. Freezing keeps them, and next turn's gem lands on the same minion, so the truffle ripens.

## Its place in a game

You buy it on turns 5–7 as a mid-game Quilboar engine, or as a flexible pickup for any player who freezes often. The leftmost shop slot becomes each turn's bonus buy. Late on, with permanently scaled gems (Moon-Bacon Jazzer and the like), a truffle you leave three turns is a large one-shot buff for a carry.

## Interactions outside Quilboar

- **Any tribe can host it.** A Demon, Mech or Naga carry in the leftmost slot takes Quilboar stats.
- **Elemental shop buffs (Nomi-style)** stack on the same frozen Tavern minion. Both keep growing.
- **Holding the host in hand** delays its gems. Play it on the turn Bristleback Brute is down (double gems), or with "after you play a Blood Gem" triggers on board, and each buried gem fires them.
- **Triples:** bury into a copy you need, and the golden keeps the gems.
- **Freeze-centred heroes** let truffles ripen longer.

## Risks and failure cases

- **Low floor:** a bad leftmost minion means you waste either the gem or the buy.
- **Free freeze:** ripening costs only a fresh shop. With heavily scaled gems over 4+ frozen turns it could overshoot. Cap it at 3 buried if needed.
- **Tracking:** buried gems need a clear marker on Tavern and hand cards.
- **Several Snufflers** all bury into one minion. Check Golden + Brute + gem scaling together.

## Design rationale

1. Started from truffle hunting: pigs rooting for buried treasure.
2. A truffle is hidden, buried and ripens, so it became Blood Gems stored off your board.
3. It is buried in the Tavern because the shop is the ground you dig in, and that ties the card to freeze and refresh choices.
4. Leftmost rather than random, so Johnny plans the dig rather than hoping for it.
5. Start of turn, so the truffle is usable at once and ripening is an opt-in freeze.
6. Gems fire on play, not on buy, so holding the host in hand becomes a timing tool.
7. Tier 3 at 3/4, because one gem a turn locked to a fixed slot is about Tier 3 value.
