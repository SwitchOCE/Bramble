# Bramblewound Seer

## 1. The card
- **Tier:** 4. **Tribe:** Quilboar. **Stats:** 2/6
- **Text:** Whenever a friendly minion takes damage and survives, play a Blood Gem on it. (5 times per combat.)
- **Golden (4/12):** Same, but play 2 Blood Gems.

Survival is checked after damage resolves, so a simultaneous hit resolves fully first. Combat gems last that combat only.

## 2. Its place in a game
Bought on turns 7–9, once your minions have enough Health to take a hit. It fits:
- **Quilboar gem boards:** gem-Health buffs (Moon-Bacon Jazzer) mean more survivals, and each survival brings another gem.
- **Taunt or high-Health boards:** front minions take several hits before dying.
- **"Whenever this takes damage" boards** in other tribes, where the Seer is the glue, not the carry.

## 3. Interactions outside Quilboar
- **Imp Mama / Imp Gang Boss (Demon), Security Rover (Mech):** each gem buys one more hit, and each hit summons another body.
- **Tunnel Blaster:** your own Deathrattle hits all minions, and every survivor gets a gem. Self-harm becomes a buff.
- **Aggem Thorncurse:** combat gems trigger it, so one hit on Aggem spreads gems across your other tribes mid-fight.
- **Enemy cleave (e.g. Foe Reaper):** a splash that doesn't kill feeds you.

## 4. Risks and failure cases
- **Venomous, Poisonous and one-shots** skip the survival, so nothing triggers.
- **Divine Shield blocks the damage, so nothing triggers.** Shield-heavy gem boards (Tough Tusk, Gemsplitter) work against it, a tension the player must manage.
- **Invisible payoff:** it does nothing in recruit, so it is easy to undervalue and losses can feel random.
- **Late-game scaling:** heavily buffed gems make 5 (golden 10) a large swing. The cap is the balance dial: lower it to 4 before touching stats.
- **Animation clutter:** many small triggers. Batch them per minion.

## 5. Design rationale
1. Quilboar come from Agamaggan's spilled blood, which became thorns. The fantasy: the herd grows stronger as it bleeds.
2. So the card rewards damage taken, not dealt, and Blood Gems are the visible blood.
3. It triggers on surviving, not dying: enduring the wound is the thorn fantasy, and death effects can't feed it.
4. Any damage counts, including your own, which opens off-label uses for Johnny.
5. Gems are played in combat, not given to hand, so existing "Blood Gem played" effects become new combos.
6. A per-combat cap keeps late gem scaling balanced for Spike.
7. 2/6 body: sturdy enough to trigger itself, too weak to carry alone.
8. Tier 4 is when high-Health and damage-trigger boards take shape.
