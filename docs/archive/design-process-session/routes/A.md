# Reliquary Razorback

## 1. The card
- **Tier 3 · Quilboar · 3/3**
- **Text:** *Deathrattle:* Play a Blood Gem on adjacent minions for every 2 Blood Gems played on this this game. (Shows a counter.)
- **Golden (6/6):** *Deathrattle:* Play a Blood Gem on adjacent minions for every Blood Gem played on this this game.

## 2. Its place in a game
Bought on turns 5–7, when gems from Geomancer and Relaxer have no good home. It goes in slot 2 or 3, between two carries. When it dies early in combat, its stored gems pass on at their current value, so Jazzer and Agamaggan upgrades still apply. Late game, every end-of-turn gem from Charlga or Gem Smuggler adds to its count.

## 3. Interactions outside Quilboar
- **Baron or Titus Rivendare:** repeats the deathrattle, so early gems come back two or three times late.
- **Gem splash:** Sun-Bacon Relaxer works in any board. A Beast or Undead board sells Relaxers, banks gems here, and hands them to its carries mid-fight.
- **Taunt and attack order:** giving it Taunt, or moving it to slot 1, times the gems around enemy Cleave and Venomous.
- **Buff-triggered minions:** each gem it plays counts as a separate buff during combat, so it feeds minions that react to friendly buffs.

## 4. Risks and failure cases
- **Reborn:** the Reborn copy comes back with its counter at 0.
- **Board edge:** one neighbour loses half the payout; no neighbours loses all of it.
- **Survives combat:** if it lives, it pays nothing that fight.
- **Animation time:** 20+ gems played in combat is slow, so it needs a batched visual.
- **Balance:** golden plus Titus is a lot of late-game value. Tune the ratio, not the body.

## 5. Design rationale
- **Gap:** Quilboar have gem sources at tiers 1–2 and gem broadcasters and upgrades at 4–6, but almost nothing at tier 3. Gems die with the minion they're on, which pushes every game into "dump everything on one holder".
- **Tier 3:** fills the empty middle of the curve, where the player has gems but no plan.
- **Deathrattle:** the one lever that makes gems outlast their minion, and it plugs into Undead deathrattle support.
- **Counts gems played on it, not its stats:** pulls in Charlga, Smuggler and Aggem without extra wording.
- **Adjacent, not random:** positioning becomes a skill test for Spike and stays readable.
- **Every 2:** total payout equals the investment: it moves value, not creates it.
- **3/3 body:** it wants to die, so it must not double as a stat holder.
- **Name last:** a reliquary keeps relics after their owner is gone.
