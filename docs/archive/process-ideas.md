# Process ideas to try later

Ways of getting better results from agents that the owner has tried or thought of and isn't using now. Nothing here is a rule, and nothing here is in use. It's a list to pick from when the owner next works on the process. The owner, 16 Nov 2026: "it can be another avenue I try when I next go on an improvement journey."

Each entry says what the idea is, what happened when it was tried, and where the record is. Newest at the bottom.

---

## 1. Goldfish designers (in use 2–12 Nov 2026)

**The idea.** Design agents are briefed without the power budget, so it doesn't shape their ideas. A separate review agent then costs the designs, flags any that are well outside the budget, and proposes changes that keep the card's creative core.

**The owner, 2 Nov:**

> Maybe design agents should be given cards to design without any concept of power budgets. They're not even informed; they're goldfish. They don't know that power budgets even exist. Then review agents can look at them against the power budgets, and if they're significantly outside them, flag it, and maybe propose some changes that keep the core of the creative card.

**What happened.** It was a working rule in `CLAUDE.md` from 2 Nov. On 12 Nov the owner replaced it after the Ogre test, where designers who were given the tier guide made tribes the owner preferred: "The previous rule says no power budgets, and whatever gave me those results I want to be the new standard." The method in use now is the [tier guide](../design/tier-guide.md), given to designers in full.

**The owner, 16 Nov:**

> I don't know if the goldfish idea was the best. It was one of the things I tried, but we've got a method that's working at the moment, the best we've seen any method work.
