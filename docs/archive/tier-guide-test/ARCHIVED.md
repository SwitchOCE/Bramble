# Tier guide test (archived 16 Nov 2026)

The 12 Nov 2026 test behind the [tier guide](../../design/tier-guide.md): five Ogre tribes for Battlegrounds, designed with and without the guide and judged blind. It is the guide's evidence. The Ogre designs are test material for another game's tribe, not Bramble content, and nothing in them is decided.

## Why it is kept

The owner's words, 12 Nov 2026, after reading it:

> "I like these designs better. They're more creative. They feel more powerful. I can balance them afterwards."

The full note is [2026-11-12a-tier-guide-designs-and-less-review.md](../../design/sources/2026-11-12a-tier-guide-designs-and-less-review.md).

- [README.md](README.md): what ran and the results.
- `old/` and `new/`: the designs and briefs, `prompts/`: the exact prompts, `judge/`: the judge brief, verdicts and A/B key.
