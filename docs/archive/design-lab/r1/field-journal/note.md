# Field Journal (#2)

- **Idea:** the game as a naturalist's notebook: graph-paper pages, units as taped specimen cards with typed labels and a Latin-ish binomial, pencil arithmetic and red-pencil marks for what matters right now.
- **For:** players who like dense, readable information and a calm, crafted table (Battlegrounds regulars, tabletop and Wingspan-style players). Medium ornament, high density.
- **Distinct:** the UI explains itself in the journal's own voice: the Nightshade halving is worked out in pencil (26 → ½ → 13 → −1 → 12), the next opponent is circled, B3 is annotated "behind F3 & F4", tier-up shows "7 − 6 = 1 left". Tribe is the washi-tape colour on each card; frozen is a strip of blue tape; golden is a gilt double edge. The board is a ruled specimen tray with typed slot tags (F1–F4, B1–B3), the back row visibly in the gaps, empty slots as dashed outlines.
- **Godot 4 cost: low to medium.** One Theme: StyleBoxFlat for cards, buttons and trays; a tiled graph-paper texture; nine-patch only for tape strips (torn ends) and the cloth cover. Five fonts (could cut to three: Courier Prime, Crimson Pro, Caveat). Stat badges are two small SVGs. Pencil annotations and the attack arrow are Labels and a Line2D/Curve2D; the slight card tilts are a rotation per Control. No shaders. Animation: Tweens for lift, splat pop and a pencil "write-on" for numbers.
- **Risks:** high density costs readability on a 390 px-tall phone: rules text on shop cards drops to about 7 CSS px (it is clamped there; needs tap-to-inspect), names at board size are small; attack and health stay at 15-18 CSS px. Five typefaces is a lot. Ochre attack, brass gold and gilt sit in one family (shape separates them; needs a colour-blind check). Handwritten text has to be localised per language. The Ink B portraits sit well as "pasted plates", but only six exist, so units borrow the nearest one (birds use the squirrel, the boar the marmot). The Latin names are flavour I added, not content.
