# Woodland Nouveau (#8): note

- **Idea:** Bramble as an Alphonse Mucha poster: arched gilt card frames, whiplash-vine ornament, a gold halo behind every portrait, stats set as jewels (sapphire lozenge for Attack, garnet cabochon for Health), on deep malachite green.
- **For:** players who want a premium, collectible "illuminated" feel (Gwent or Legends of Runeterra collectors), rather than casual-mobile or esports players.
- **Distinct:** the board is a gilt brick: vertical gold plaques mark Market / Front / Back, the back row sits half a slot in, and empty slots are dashed arches labelled F4 / B3. Nightshade is one violet (aura, berry badge, the "½ 26 → 13" ribbon); Freeze is a frosted glass pane over the shop card; golden is a gold-leaf sunburst behind the art.
- **Godot 4 cost: medium to high.** A fixed 660-high stage maps to `canvas_items` stretch with `expand`. Frames, plaques and buttons are nine-patch textures (the arch needs one nine-patch per card size, since the arch's curve can't stretch); panels are StyleBoxTexture. Gems are two small textures and an outlined Label. The gold-leaf sheen, golden sunburst, frost pane and Nightshade glow are each one CanvasItem shader. The attack arrow is a Line2D or a Curve2D path with a texture. Animation is mostly Tweens (lift, glow pulse).
- **Art cost:** high. 3 Codex images (a 3×3 sheet and a 2×2 sheet cropped into 13 portraits, plus the menu glade). It only works with ornate, gold-haloed portraits; Ink + flat B portraits would look flat and cheap in these frames unless the frame ornament were simplified a lot.
- **Risks:** ornament competes with information (rules text on shop cards is about 7 CSS px on a phone, so it needs a long-press inspector; names on board cards are small, though stat numerals stay at about 13–16 CSS px). Gold on gold can blur hierarchy, so golden units need their sunburst and star. Roman tier numerals are slower to read than digits. Federant and Cinzel need licensing checks (both are OFL on Google Fonts). Heavy texture memory for nine-patch gilt frames at three sizes on low-end Android.
