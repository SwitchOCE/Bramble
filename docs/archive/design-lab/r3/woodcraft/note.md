# Hedgerow Woodcraft, round 3 (combat and settings)

- **Breakdown:** a fixed purple "This hit" plaque at the top left, in the same place for every hit. It names the attacker (acorn chip) and target (red chip) with the red yarn between them, then shows three numbered rosehip beads: ① 26 before, ½ Nightshade berry, ② 13 halved, −1 starburst, ③ 12 after. The −1 starburst on the boar is the same shape as the plaque's, so the two connect.
- **Round 1 fix, attacker label:** "Attacking" is an acorn tag hanging from the Hornetling in B1, and the red yarn is tied to that tag. The Hornetling is lifted out of its pocket with an acorn ring. The target has a red ring and a red "Target · Tusker Boar" tag. The yarn now runs round your tray and along the gap between the trays, then up into the boar, so it crosses no pocket, empty or not.
- **Round 1 fix, Hornetling chip:** ability chips are birch with a verb: "Hunts Nightshade" (Hornetling) and "Gives Nightshade" (Sprite). A status on a unit is a purple tag hanging from its top edge, like Frozen: only the boar wears "Nightshade". There are no purple-filled chips on the board any more.
- **Round 2 match:** limewood table, oak trays cut to the 4–3 brick on both sides, and dotted pokerwork grooves from each back pocket to its two front pockets (they point up on the enemy tray). Slot chips (F1, B2) are burned on each pocket's bottom edge. To fit two trays in the height, combat drops round 2's name strips.
- **Borrowed:** Folk Print's numbered steps, Clean Arena's fixed panel with the before/halved/after labels, and Field Journal's plain words ("to the result", "more later", "when you watch them").
- **Settings:** volume is a burned groove with a wooden peg. Speed uses the combat screen's ×1/×2/×4 tokens, and the toggles are latch pegs that say ON/OFF as well as changing colour. Text size shows the Bear Cub board tile at Small, Medium and Large: the chip, the slot strip and the bead numbers grow, and the tile keeps its size. The colour-blind aid turns the Attack nut pale straw and the Health rosehip deep red with a cream ring, and adds a burned tribe brand (a feather for Birds). Language is a dashed placeholder.
- **Phone shortfalls:** the side columns and the whole settings page are at 18 design px or more (11 CSS px), and the settings controls are 72 px (44 CSS px). On the combat trays, the keyword chips, slot chips and Attacking/Target tags are 13–15 px, about 8–9 CSS px. The trays can't grow without dropping a row of the brick.
- **Flag:** the tribe brand exists only on the settings page so far. If it's adopted, the board tiles need a corner for it, and the golden star sits in that corner now. On Large text, "Shoulder" runs under the tier disc.
