# Design lab directions, round 1

Each direction makes the same set of screens from `fixture.json`. Axes: material, palette temperature, ornament, density. Directions 1–5 sit around the decided illustration style, Ink + flat B (`tools/art/prompts/trials-ink-b.txt`, portraits in `docs/folio/art/web/trials/ink-b-*.jpg`). Directions 6–8 deliberately don't, to give a range of tastes.

| # | Id | Name | The idea | Axes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `storybook-ink` | Storybook Ink | The decided style straight: cream paper, bold even ink lines, flat fills, warm and friendly. The baseline. | paper · warm · low ornament · medium density |
| 2 | `field-journal` | Field Journal | A naturalist's notebook and herbarium: graph paper, tape, specimen labels with Latin-ish names, pencil annotations, pressed leaves. | paper · muted greens · medium ornament · high density |
| 3 | `woodcraft` | Hedgerow Woodcraft | A physical tabletop diorama: the board is a carved wooden tray with seven slots, cards are wooden tiles or tokens, acorns for gold, leaves for buttons. | wood · warm browns · medium ornament · low density |
| 4 | `night-wood` | Night Wood | Dark mode: a moonlit forest at night, pale ink lines on deep blue-green, glowing fungi and firefly accents for highlights and statuses. | dark · cool with warm glows · low ornament · medium density |
| 5 | `folk-print` | Folk Print | European folk art: linocut and woodcut in two or three inks, papercut borders (think Polish wycinanki), bold symmetrical ornament. | print · limited palette · high ornament · medium density |
| 6 | `clean-arena` | Clean Arena | Off-style on purpose: modern competitive mobile, flat minimal UI, high contrast, sans-serif, numbers first; the woodland comes only from colour and icons. | digital · neutral · no ornament · high density |
| 7 | `chunky-cozy` | Chunky Cozy | Off-style on purpose: bright casual mobile, rounded chunky shapes, soft shading, bouncy big buttons, candy-ish woodland colours. | soft plastic · bright · low ornament · low density |
| 8 | `nouveau` | Woodland Nouveau | Off-style on purpose: Art Nouveau, whiplash vines and arched card frames, jewel tones and gold leaf, ornate typography. | gilt · jewel tones · very high ornament · low density |

Wave 1: 1, 4, 6, 7 (one from each corner of the range). Wave 2: 2, 3, 5, 8.
