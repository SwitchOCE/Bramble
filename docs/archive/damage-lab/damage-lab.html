Damage Lab

The rulebook's system: heroes start on 40 health. A loss costs the round number + 1, up to 10. It's a small loss when you killed at least half of the winner's starting units; otherwise it's a big loss and costs 3 more. A draw costs both players nothing. The numbers behind the charts

What this model assumes

  • 8 players in a live lobby, paired at random each round. With an odd number left, one player fights a ghost, which deals damage but takes none. Bramble's async model is still open, so treat the results as shapes, not final values.
  • Every player has a fixed skill plus a board strength that drifts each round. The fight's winner is decided by the strength gap plus luck.
  • Boards start with 2 units on round 1 and grow by one a round to 7 on round 6. The winner's survivors grow with how lopsided the fight was.
  • Only starting units count, so units summoned during combat never add to a loss. Tokens played before combat count like any unit, but the rule only asks "did you kill half or not", so a wide board can't stack extra damage.
  • With 7 starting units you need 4 kills for a small loss; with 2 units, 1 kill. Draws are the closest fights by margin; the slider sets roughly how many.
  • Small losses are rounded to whole numbers: the starting value plus the growth per round, up to the highest small loss.
  • "Close fight" means the strength gap before the fight was small; "outclassed" means it was large. The same thresholds are used for every curve.
