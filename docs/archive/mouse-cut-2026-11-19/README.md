# Mouse cut 2026 11 19

These six Mice left the roster when the Kin replaced the siege and slot-buff plans. Nothing here is decided. The card data and text are from commit 8da8a17d.

- **Kettle-drummer** — Your back-row units attack twice.
- **Master of Engines** — Start of fight: Your back-row units each Hit the enemy with the least Health.
- **Spoon Catapult** — Instead of attacking, Hit the enemy with the least Health.
- **Edgewright** — Start of fight: Your slots give double Attack this fight.
- **Whetstone Vole** — At the end of your turn, give this slot +3 Attack.
- **Grindstone Miller** — At the end of your turn, give this slot and its neighbours +2 Attack.
