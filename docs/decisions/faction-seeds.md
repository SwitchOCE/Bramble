Before we settled on a Battlegrounds-style game, the Woodland Folio gathered 36 faction seeds: one-line ideas for groups of woodland folk, each with a twist. The Marmot Cantons are a fiercely neutral mountain confederation that sells to every side and sleeps through half the year. The Rat King is dozens of rats whose tails have knotted into one gossiping hive-mind. The Parish Council is a hedgerow village run by a committee in permanent emergency session.

The art work is paused, waiting for you to star the seeds you like. The next step would be to draw each starred group as a lineup of characters, in the style you picked: bold ink lines with flat colour.

{image:1}

Since then, the game's groups have moved on. They're now tribes with their own ways of playing, like the Fairies, Oozes, Hedgerow folk, Hunters and Birds, and you've said you won't know the top tribes until the best mechanics are clear.

**What we need from you:** do you want to go through the seeds now and star the ones to draw? Or should the art wait, and draw lineups for the tribes once they're picked?

**Why it matters:** the lineups would be the first character art drawn for the game's own groups, and they'd set what each group of folk looks like.

We'd suggest waiting. The seeds came before the tribes, so drawing them now could mean drawing groups the game won't have. They're still good idea material when the tribes are fleshed out.
