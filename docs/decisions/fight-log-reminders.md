When you watch a fight in the playable game, a log down the side names every hit and every effect. While testing, we added one thing you hadn't asked for: the first time a keyword turns up in a fight, say "Hunker" or "Mire", a short reminder of what it does follows as a dim line underneath. After that, the keyword appears on its own.

**What we need from you:** keep the reminders, or take them out?

**Why it matters:** it's a small thing, but it's how the game teaches its own words while you play, which matters more as the number of keywords grows.

We'd suggest keeping them. They only show once per keyword per fight, so they don't clutter the log.

## Detail

- **What it does:** the first time a keyword shows up in a fight, its reminder text follows as a dim line in the fight log.
- **Not asked for:** it was added during QA.
- **"Once per keyword per fight"** is how we read "the first time a keyword shows up in a fight". The suggestion is ours.
