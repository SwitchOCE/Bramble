In the playable game, each round all eight players fight at once, but you only see your own fight. The bots' fights against each other run in the background, and you just see the results in the lobby list: who won, who lost, and everyone's health.

We didn't build a way to watch those other fights.

**What we need from you:** would watching them be any use to you, for example to see how a bot's board plays before you meet it?

**Why it matters:** it's extra work on the prototype, and in the finished game your opponents are recorded boards rather than people playing at the same time, so it may not carry over.

We'd suggest leaving it out for now, unless you find yourself wanting it while you play.

## Detail

- **Not built:** watching other players' fights (bot against bot). Listed under "Not in yet" in the playtest guide.
- **"May not carry over"** is our reading: rulebook 2.1.4 has fights against snapshots of other players' boards, so in the finished game there may be no live fights between other players to watch. How spectating would work under async play isn't covered in the rulebook. The suggestion is ours.
