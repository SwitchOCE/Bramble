At the start of a game, as in Battlegrounds, each player picks a hero from the ones offered, and the hero's power shapes how you play. The rulebook follows Battlegrounds and offers two. Battlegrounds also sells extra choices, and rerolls of the ones you're offered, through its paid Season Pass. Players have criticised that as pay-to-win.

The open question is whether Bramble offers more choices or rerolls, and whether any of that would ever cost money.

There are no heroes in the prototype yet, and none have been designed.

**What we need from you:** do you have a view now: two heroes with no extras, more choices or rerolls for everyone, or leave it until heroes exist?

**Why it matters:** more choice makes a strong start easier to find; selling it would make it pay-to-win.

We'd suggest leaving it until heroes are designed.
