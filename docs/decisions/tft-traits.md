In Teamfight Tactics, each unit has a couple of traits, and having several units with the same trait on your board switches on bonuses: three Knights give your team armour, say. Battlegrounds has no such thing; its tribes only matter through card text.

You said early on that we could explore a trait system like TFT's, "although I'm not sold on that at all". The rulebook has none for now.

**What we need from you:** shall we close it at no traits, or keep it as something to explore later?

**Why it matters:** traits would add a second layer of board-building on top of tribes and card text, and change how every unit is designed.

We'd suggest no traits for now. Your lean was against it, and tribes plus position give plenty to design with.
