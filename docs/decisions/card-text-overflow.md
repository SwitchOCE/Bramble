Cards on the board and in the shop are small, and some rules text is long. In the playable game, when a card's text doesn't fit, it gives way in three steps. First, a sentence that is only a keyword, like "Hunker.", moves up into a little chip above the name, so the rest has room. Then the text shrinks. Only if it still doesn't fit is it cut short with "…". Today nothing needs cutting on a computer screen, and tapping a card always shows the full text.

{image:1}

**What we need from you:** is that order fine? Or would you rather small cards didn't try to show everything, and showed only the keywords, or only when the card does its thing (like "Start of Combat")?

**Why it matters:** it sets how much a card tells you at a glance on the board, before you tap it.

We'd suggest keeping it as it is for now. It loses nothing, and it can change with the look you pick.

## Detail

- **Order in the build:** (1) keyword-only sentences move to a chip above the name (question 13); (2) the font shrinks; (3) only then is the text cut with "…". Nothing needs cutting at desktop sizes today. The full text is always in the inspector.
- **The question is a little vague** about the alternatives: "keywords only" and "the trigger only" aren't spelled out further in the source. Read as: on small cards, show just the keyword chips, or just the trigger words, and leave the rest to the tap.
- **Related:** phone text size (question 12), the keyword chip (13), shorter art for long text (14). In the design lab, round 2 of Folk Print already shows keyword and trigger chips instead of text on phone shop cards.
- The suggestion is ours.
