A few cards have a lot of rules text. In the playable game, if a card's text still doesn't fit after its keywords have moved into chips, the card gives up some of its picture: the portrait gets about 30% shorter, and the text gets that space.

**What we need from you:** is that the right trade? Or would you rather every card kept its full-size picture, and long text got cut short (you can always tap the card to read it all)?

**Why it matters:** it decides whether all cards look the same shape, or whether wordy cards look a little different from the rest.

We'd suggest keeping the shorter picture for now. It only touches the few wordiest cards, and you can still read the whole card at a glance.
