Your income goes up by one gold each turn, from 3 on turn 1 to a ceiling of 10 from turn 8. In Battlegrounds, "maximum gold" is both that ceiling and a cap on how much you can hold. Bramble has no cap on holding gold, so a card that says "raise your maximum gold" needs a new meaning.

The rulebook's default is that it lifts the income ceiling: from 10 to 11, say. That only pays off once you've reached the ceiling, on turn 8 or later, so it's a small effect, worth one or two stats on a card. The other reading, closer to how it feels in Battlegrounds, is that it gives you one more gold of income every turn from now on. That's the same as a "gain 1 gold every turn" card, and it would be priced and limited like one.

**What we need from you:** which should it be?

**Why it matters:** it decides whether "maximum gold" is a small late-game bonus or a full economy card.

We'd suggest keeping the rulebook's default. It keeps the effect simple and cheap, and "gold every turn" already has its own wording.

## Detail

- **Rulebook 4.1.4 [PROPOSED]:** "Effects that raise or lower 'maximum gold' change the income ceiling in 4.1.1 (10), not a holding cap. Why: in HSBG maximum gold is both the refill and the cap; Bramble has no holding cap, so only the income meaning is left."
- **Rulebook 4.1.1:** 3 gold on turn 1, 1 more each turn, up to 10 a turn from turn 8 on. 4.1.3: no limit on held gold, no interest.
- **Pricing (§9 question 4):** under 4.1.4 it is worth 1–2 SP. If it raised income at once, as in Battlegrounds, it would be recurring +1 gold under the 4.1 caps (see the recurring-gold card).
- **Overlap:** 4.1.4 is also one of the proposed defaults in the rulebook-defaults bundle. This card is the place to decide it; the bundle's "accept all" would accept the default.
- **The suggestion** is ours.

**The original question (power-budget §9, question 4):**
> "What does "increase maximum gold" do? Under rulebook 4.1.4 it only lifts the 10-gold ceiling and is worth 1–2 SP. If it should raise income at once, as in Battlegrounds, it is recurring +1 gold and comes under the caps."
