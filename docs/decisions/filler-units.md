The playable game's shop draws from 40 different units. Only 16 of them are real designs, taken from the mechanics library to show off its ideas: Hunker, Shell, Whistle, Nightshade and the rest. The other 24 are plain filler: placeholder names and stats, with no rules text at all. They're there so shops stay full and every tribe has something to buy at most tiers.

The catch is that the 16 designed units turn up less often than they would without the filler. They're also spread unevenly: tier 4 has none of them at all, and tier 6 has just one.

**What we need from you:** keep the filler while you playtest, cut it back so the designed units come up more often, or have us bring more units from the mechanics library into the game?

**Why it matters:** it decides how often you actually meet the mechanics you're meant to be judging when you play.

We'd suggest keeping the filler for now. Cutting all of it would leave tier 4 with nothing to offer, and bringing in more designs is content work, which we'd rather do with you than ahead of you. If you want to see a mechanic more, the sandbox has a ready-made fight for each one.

## Detail

- **The pool:** 40 units: 16 library units and 24 plain stat-line filler units. Filler is sized to the power budget's stat line (Attack + Health = V(t)), with placeholder names. "They exist so shops and tribes work. They are not designs."
- **Spread:** Tier 4 has no library unit; Tier 6 has one.
- **The library:** 75 mechanics entries, each with example units, on `design/tribes-and-budget`.
- **Sandbox:** 15 one-tap scenarios, one per mechanic, taken from the library's scenes.
- **The morning report's wording:** "24 of 40 pool units are plain stat-line filler. Add more library units, or wait for content design?" That third option is why "swap in more units" is offered here.
- **"Content work … with you"** follows the working rule "Supervised from the start" and "Fundamentals before content". The suggestion is ours.
