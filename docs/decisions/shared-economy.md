Gold effects can live in two places. They can be on spells in the shop, which any player can buy whatever they're playing. Or they can be on units, so a tribe has its own way of making money.

Battlegrounds today puts most of its big economy on things every player gets: over a third of its spells have economy text, while gold text on units is rare and small. Our pricing assumes the same: about a third of our spells carry economy, and units keep to the per-tier limits on gold.

You've said you like that everybody has economy effects in today's Battlegrounds, and also that every tribe should have some economy of its own, even if it's not the best at it.

**What we need from you:** where should the strong economy live? Mostly on spells, as now, or more on tribe units (still within the limits)?

**Why it matters:** it decides whether being rich is something any build can do, or something certain tribes are known for, and how many spells need economy text.

We'd suggest mostly on spells, with every tribe getting some smaller economy of its own, tied to what that tribe does.

## Detail

- **v1 assumption (power-budget 4.2, 4.5):** Tavern spells carry most shared economy: about a third of spells with economy text and about 15% strict (Battlegrounds 38% and 14%). Spell pool mix, judgement on data: about 40% stat buffs, about a third with economy text, the rest utility.
- **Battlegrounds today (economy-text-by-era):** gold, refresh and discount text is 3.7% of current minions (10 of 273), median effect 1 gold. Card generation is where minion economy grew (26% of minions). The big economy is on systems every player gets: 38% of Tavern spells, about half of trinkets, 53 of 123 hero powers.
- **The alternative (§9 question 3):** more economy on tribe units, under the 4.1 caps (see the recurring-gold card).
- **Your words:** "I like Battlegrounds' current philosophy, where they've got a lot more of those effects in the current patch … I think it's a better situation where everybody has them" (2 Nov); "I think you want every single tribe to have some economy ability … They can have poor economy, not as good as other tribes, but none is probably not great" (2 Nov read-through); "I like a lot of the economy ideas of tying economy effects to specific actions that kind of reward you for being in that type of strategy" (2 Nov read-through).
- **The suggestion** is ours: v1's assumption plus your read-through notes.

**The original question (power-budget §9, question 3):**
> "Where should strong shared economy live? v1 assumes Tavern spells carry most of it: about a third of spells with economy text, as in Battlegrounds (4.2, 4.5). The alternative is more economy on tribe units, under the 4.1 caps."
