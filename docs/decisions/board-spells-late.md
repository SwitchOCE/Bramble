The shop's spell slot offers spells of your tier or lower, as in Battlegrounds. So a cheap tier 2 spell like "give your units +1/+1" keeps turning up all game, and late on you cast it on a full board of seven units.

That makes it hard to price. We've priced such spells for a full board, because that's how they get used in the end. The catch is that early in the game, when you have only two or three units, they're weak for what they cost. The other way would be a rule change: once your tier is above a spell's tier, it stops appearing in your shop, so it can be priced for the small boards it would actually meet.

**What we need from you:** keep low-tier board spells in late shops, priced for a full board, or take them out once you've tiered past them?

**Why it matters:** it decides whether cheap board buffs are an early-game buy or a late-game one, and it changes a Battlegrounds shop rule.

We'd suggest keeping them, as the budget does now. It follows Battlegrounds, and a spell that's a weak early buy but a strong late one is a choice in itself.

## Detail

- **Power-budget 4.5:** several units or the whole board: price on a full board, whatever the tier. A full board is 4 front units at 1.0 per stat and 3 back units at 0.75 per Attack and 1.0 per Health; "+1/+1 to your units" is 13.25 realised SP. "Early in the game such a spell is weak for its cost, and that is the price of staying in the pool. The other choice, taking low-tier board spells out of later shops, is a rule change (question 6)."
- **Rulebook 6.1.5 [BG]:** one shop slot per refill offers a spell of the player's tier or lower.
- **The "tier 2" example** is ours. "A choice in itself" is our reasoning; the budget draft calls this v1's choice and leaves the call to you.

**The original question (power-budget §9, question 6):**
> "Low-tier board spells in late shops: price them for a full board (weak when bought early; v1's choice), or take them out of the shop once the player's tier is above theirs (a change to rulebook 6.1.5)?"
