Eight looks for the woodland

Eight UI directions, each drawn from the same mock game: the same shop, the 4–3 brick board, the hand, the lobby, and one combat moment (a Hornetling hits a boar marked with Nightshade, which halves its Health first). Four judges with different tastes scored them blind, from screenshots only. They are agent judges, so treat the ranking as a starting point for your own reaction. The top three and the wildcard then had one refinement pass on the recruit screen and card, fixing the judges' notes and borrowing each other's best ideas, and a third pass on combat and a settings menu. Their sections open on round 2; rounds 1 and 3 are one tap away.

Scores

Each criterion is summed over four judges (4–40). The total is out of 280. Green marks the best direction on a criterion and red the worst. "Top 3" counts the judges who put it in their top three.

Judges: a storybook art director, a casual phone player who has never played Battlegrounds, a high-MMR Battlegrounds veteran, and a UX and accessibility designer for mobile games.
