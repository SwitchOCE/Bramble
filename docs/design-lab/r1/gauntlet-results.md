# Round 1 gauntlet results (4 Nov 2026)

Four judges, each with a persona, scored the 8 directions blind: lettered A–H, screenshots only, no names or notes. The criteria are in [judge-brief.md](../judge-brief.md): phone readability, board, cards, combat, distinctiveness, woodland feel and the judge's own taste, each scored 1–10. These are agent judges, so the scores are a starting point for your own reaction, not a verdict.

The judges were a storybook art director, a casual phone player who has never played Battlegrounds, a high-MMR Battlegrounds veteran, and a UX and accessibility designer.

## Ideas to borrow (named by several judges)

1. **A fixed breakdown of each hit:** before, then halved, then after the hit. Clean Arena's "26 BEFORE → ½ NS → 13 HALVED → −1 → 12 AFTER HIT" panel, Folk Print's numbered "This attack" steps and Field Journal's crossed-out pencil strip all do this. Three judges named it.
2. **A brick you can read in half a second:** Woodcraft's tray cut to the 4–3 shape, with slot labels, plus Clean Arena's and Storybook Ink's lines from each back slot to the front slots in front of it. Three judges named it.
3. **Plain-language notes on the screen itself:** Field Journal's "B3 sits behind F3 & F4", "next fight!" and "End turn → fight Old Tansy" labels, which teach the rules where they apply. All four judges named it, two of them as onboarding hints.
4. **Named once each:**
   - Folk Print's stat shapes, which differ in brightness as well as colour (black shield for Attack, vermilion heart for Health), for colour-blind players.
   - Chunky Cozy's big round touch targets and long-press keyword reminders.
   - Woodcraft's in-world objects: peg-board standings, leaf buttons, acorn gold.

## What to fix in round 2

- **Folk Print:** lighten the black board plates so the units lead, enlarge names on board cards, show keyword chips on phone shop cards with the full text on tap, and anchor the damage burst on the target.
- **Woodcraft:** lighten the walnut to pale oak or limewood, make Frozen a frost tint that leaves stats visible, put the "Attacking" label on the attacker, and add opponents' tiers to the lobby strip.
- **Field Journal:** use bolder, larger names and buttons on phone (a slab or serif instead of the typewriter face), cut the annotations down to two or three, and drop the Latin name at board size.
- **Storybook Ink:** show the gold count on the recruit screen, add one signature material, and make the back row's offset stronger.
