# Night Wood (direction 4)

**Idea:** the game after moonrise: pale ink lines on deep blue-green, flat fills, and light only where something is alive (firefly gold, glowcap fungus for what you can touch, violet Nightshade).
**For:** players who play in the evening or on phones in bed, and anyone who likes a calm, moody board where the action glows; a dark-mode-first Battlegrounds.
**Distinct:** one rule drives everything: glow means "live". Empty slots are dotted fairy rings, freeze is hoarfrost, players are lanterns (Sedgewick's has gone out), and faint dashed roots link each back slot to the two front slots in front of it, so the 4–3 brick reads as a structure. Stats are dark numerals on bright shapes (orange diamond = attack, red heart = health) and are the brightest thing on every card.
**Godot 4 cost: low to medium.** One Theme (StyleBoxFlat panels with borders and shadow glow, two fonts), cards as a scene of Panels + TextureRect + Labels, no nine-patches needed. The moonlight wash on portraits is a 5-line canvas_item shader (desaturate, darken, cool gradient). Glows are StyleBox shadows or one additive sprite; fireflies are a GPUParticles2D. The brick board is two HBoxContainers, the back one offset by half a slot.
**Risks:** the Ink B art is painted for daylight, so the wash dims it and its warm colours fight the cool frame; real art may need night palettes. Too many glows at once (golden + Nightshade + selection + frozen) muddy the "glow = live" rule; needs a priority order. Dark UIs read badly on phones in sunlight, and glows add GPU fill cost on low-end Android. Card names truncate at board size on phones (the stats don't).
