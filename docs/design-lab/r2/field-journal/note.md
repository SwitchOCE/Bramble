# Field Journal, round 2

- **Bolder phone type:** names, buttons, lobby names and numbers are now bold Zilla Slab; rules text is Crimson Pro 600; Special Elite is kept for small labels (tape, tier, slot tags, type line). Courier Prime is gone (5 fonts down to 4). The phone gets a shorter stage (860 instead of 1000, about 16% larger), with shop text clamped to 3 lines. Phone sizes: names about 10.5–11 CSS px, rules about 10 px, buttons 15 px and 51 px tall.
- **Annotations cut to three on recruit:** "next fight!", "B3 sits behind F3 & F4" and the red-pencil "tier up: 7 − 6 = 1 left". "Day 6 — recruiting", "gold in purse", "shop tier", "4 offers · drag to buy" and "tape colour = tribe" are now small typed labels or gone.
- **Latin binomial:** shown on the full card only. It is gone from the shop and board sizes.
- **Three yellows:** gold is now a brown acorn. Attack is a light amber, the brightest stat, with dark numerals. Golden is pale gold-leaf foil with white stripes and an ink outline. Cards has a "pencil test" swatch row in colour and greyscale, which shows the four still separate without colour.
- **Lobby marks:** W / l / † are now the plain words WON (moss), LOST (red) and OUT (grey, with the name struck through).
- **Hero element:** a kingfisher-blue silk bookmark ribbon carries the turn number, the one saturated colour on the page.
- **Borrowed, Woodcraft:** the tray is cut to the 4–3 brick shape (full width for the front row, inset for the back, so the stagger reads at a glance), and gold is an acorn, drawn as a pressed specimen.
- **Borrowed, Chunky Cozy:** keywords open their full text on tap. Cards shows the tapped Nightshade as a reminder slip taped under the card, and the phone shop clamps the rules text and relies on the tap.
- **Borrowed, Folk Print:** stats differ in brightness as well as colour (amber about 0.38 luminance, rosehip about 0.10). Both keep their shapes.
- **Kept:** "End turn → fight Old Tansy", "Freeze / keep shop", specimen cards with tribe tape, the stat shapes, graph paper, and the anatomy callouts on cards (a reference page, so the callouts are its job).
