- **Breakdown:** the "This attack" box is now a printed strip of heart stamps with numbered seals between them: ♥26 before → ① plum ½ → ♥13 halved → ② red −1 → ♥12 after, then two one-line steps ("Nightshade first…", "Then Hornetling hits for 1"). The same ① ½ seal and ② −1 burst are pinned to the boar's own frame (left and right edges) on the board, so the box and the board match by number, colour and shape.
- **Round 1 fixes:** bursts sit on the target, not on F1 or any slot label; the boards are the round 2 paper trays (black only for lines, ribbons and the Back label) with the 4–3 cut, stitches on both boards (red on yours, plum on theirs); "Attacking" sits on the Hornetling, "Target" on the boar; divider labels moved clear of the quill.
- **Settings:** volume sliders (stitched track, rosette knob), 1×/2×/4× printed buttons, two On/Off switches with the word printed in them, three text-size tiles each with a live board-size Sett Warden, a colour-blind aid shown Off vs On on the dark pair (Hunters ink vs Bog folk moss): patterned plates, a bigger rimmed tribe mark, a rimmed heart, numbered on the card. Language is a placeholder button in the header. The page is a working mock (switches, radios and sliders respond).
- **Borrowed:** Clean Arena's before/halved/after labels on the strip; Field Journal's plain notes ("you went first", "to the result", "go straight to the result").
- **Phone shortfalls:** card names (20 stage px ≈ 10.8 CSS px), keyword chips and the Token tag (18 ≈ 9.7 CSS px) and tier numbers on combat cards are under 11 px; everything else is 21+ stage px and every control is 82+ stage px (≈44 CSS px).
- **Flags:** the ½ seal half-covers the boar's tier star. At Large and Largest text size, two-word names wrap to two lines on the same card width: honest, but a real build may want the card to grow. The colour-blind patterns live in folk.css (`.fp-cb`) and cover all eight tribes, but only two are shown.
