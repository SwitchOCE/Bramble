# Judge brief: two Ogre tribe designs for Hearthstone Battlegrounds

Two designers made a 24-minion Ogre tribe for Battlegrounds from the same brief (`BRIEF.md`), and by the same design route. The files are `<route>-A.md` and `<route>-B.md`; your prompt names the route letter. Read only those two files and `BRIEF.md`. Don't open anything else in this folder or elsewhere, and don't guess how the two were made. Judge them as designs.

Judge on two things only:

1. **Power for place.** Does each unit do its tier's job at about the right size for its tier, compared with real Battlegrounds units of that tier? The tier jobs, roughly: T1 an early body or a seed; T2 first buffers and economy; T3 enablers that name the composition; T4 a strong composition piece that sets up future things; T5 strategic direction or big tempo; T6 a capstone, a pivotal engine piece or a big scaling payoff. Measured Battlegrounds medians (normal copies, good setup), Tiers 1–6:
   - printed stats: 4.5 / 6 / 7 / 9 / 10 / 12.5;
   - permanent stats added a turn: 2 / 2 / 5.5 / 12 / 20 / 53;
   - in-fight value a fight: 2.5 / 6 / 4.5 / 20 / 42 / 80.
   List the units that are well outside, meaning about twice as strong or half as strong as their tier with no good reason. Give a rough number for each.
2. **Distinctiveness.** How new and bold is the tribe's core idea and its play pattern compared with existing Battlegrounds tribes? Is it a fresh decision for the player, or a reskin of something Battlegrounds already has?

Then say what each design has that the other lost. Be concrete and name cards. Skip minor nits.
