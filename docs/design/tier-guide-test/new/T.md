# Ogres: a Battlegrounds minion type (24 minions)

## 1. Identity

**Core mechanic: the Belly.** Every Ogre has a Belly that holds one minion (some hold more).

- **Feed (a minion):** put it into the Belly of a friendly Ogre that has room. In the Recruit Phase you choose the Ogre; in combat it's random. If no Ogre has room, nothing happens.
- **While in a Belly**, the minion doesn't fight and can't be targeted (except by effects that name Bellies). The Ogre **has its text, keywords and minion types**. "This" in the borrowed text means the Ogre. A fed minion's Battlecry doesn't trigger.
- **Burst:** when an Ogre dies, its Belly minions are summoned in its place, after its Deathrattles. This counts as summoning.
- **Digest:** destroy the minion in this Ogre's Belly. Its Deathrattle triggers, even in the Recruit Phase. The Ogre permanently gains its stats.
- Selling an Ogre puts its Belly minions in your hand. Belly minions count toward triples, and the golden goes to your hand.
- An Ogre in a Belly lends only its own text, and keeps its own Belly.

**How the tribe plays.** Ogres eat what other boards waste: leftover Gold, Tavern minions you can't afford, summons that don't fit a full board, enemies they kill. A minion in a Belly works twice. Its text runs on the Ogre now, and the minion runs it again when it bursts out, so a Divine Shield pops twice and a Deathrattle fires twice. It also leaves a board slot free, so seven Ogres can hold fourteen minions. Every turn you make the same trade. You can keep the minion (text plus a second body), or Digest it: you get its stats now, gain from Digest bonuses and free the Belly for something new, but you lose the text and the second body. Ogres take on their Belly minion's types, so they slot into whatever other tribes the lobby has.

## 3. Builds

**A. Second Stomach (text stacking).** Bickering Ogre Magi, Cage Master, Twilight Ritualist, Bloodmixer, capped by Cho'gall. Feed the best text in the lobby into Ogres: Mech Divine Shields, Beast Deathrattles, Undead Avenges, Dragon Start of Combat effects. Each runs on the Ogre, then again on burst, and twice with Cho'gall. Some lines a Johnny player will look for:
- Feed a scaler like Grog Tapster into a Taunt Ogre, so the scaling lands on a body that's built to survive.
- The Ogre takes the Mech type, so Magnetic minions can now attach to it.
- Feed a golden minion: the golden text runs on the Ogre and then bursts out.

**B. Food Chain (Digest).** Tollkeeper, Hungry Sellsword, Chomper or Meat Cleaver, Butcher's Boy, Gourmand, Gruul. Eat the Tavern for stats at 2 Gold a minion, Digest every turn and grow a few huge Ogres. Digest Deathrattle minions in the Recruit Phase to make lasting tokens, then Feed the tokens back in. Weak to Venomous and destroy effects, which pick off its big Ogres.

**C. Burst Bodies.** Stewpot Cook, Cauldron-Keeper, Arena Crier, The Butcher, Crushridge Bottomless. Buffs given to Belly minions are banked: they show only when the minion bursts out. Every death becomes a big second body, and The Butcher adds a copy. Wide boards that summon past seven (Beasts, Undead) lose those summons into Bottomless and get them back later.

**D. Leftovers tempo (early).** Tapster and Brewmaster pay for leaving Gold unspent. It's a weaker turn on paper, but it beats rolling one last time for nothing. This build bridges into A or B.

**In a five-tribe lobby.** Ogres mix more easily than any other tribe, because an Ogre takes on its Belly minion's types:
- **Beasts:** burst counts as summoning, so "whenever you summon a Beast" effects trigger on bursts.
- **Mechs:** Divine Shield runs twice, and Magnetic minions attach to an Ogre holding a Mech.
- **Undead:** Ogres holding Undead receive the Undead Attack auras.
- **Murlocs:** Ritualist repeats a Battlecry, and then you sell the Ogre to replay the Murloc.
- **Elementals:** let Elementals buff the Tavern, then Tollkeeper eats the buffed minion.
- **Demons:** they compete with Ogres for Tavern minions.
- **Quilboar:** an Ogre holding a Quilboar takes Blood Gems.
- **Pirates:** they compete with Tapster and Brewmaster for the same Gold.

## 4. Risks and holes

- **Readability.** Players need to see what's in each Belly at a glance, both on the board and in combat replays. Without that the whole tribe is unreadable. Treat it as a UI problem to solve first.
- **"This" redirection.** Borrowed text that says "this" now means the Ogre. That's powerful, and some cards will need rulings. For example, an Ogre holding Gristle Chomper doesn't Digest itself.
- **Double text, stacked with Cho'gall.** Deathrattle and Avenge minions could run four times or more. Watch golden Cho'gall with Bickering Magi. A possible fix is that Cho'gall doubles only Deathrattle and Start of Combat.
- **Cheap triples.** Tollkeeper and the Lesser trinket buy at 2 Gold, and Belly minions count toward triples. If triples come too fast, make Belly minions not count.
- **Golden Dunemaul Scavenger** takes a Tier 6 minion for free. It's a fun high roll, but if it's too much, change it to "two lowest-tier minions".
- **King Gordok** can neutralise an opponent's capstone for most of a fight. It hands that minion back when it dies, and it has no Taunt, but it may still need to be limited to "highest Attack, Tier 5 or lower".
- **Gruul with Gourmand.** It's unclear whether Gruul copies the doubled amount. Rule that it copies the minion's base stats, or it runs away.
- **Type counting.** "All"-type minions count as one type for Bloodmixer, or it breaks.
- **Nested Ogres.** An Ogre in a Belly that also holds a minion bursts as a chain. It's legal and fun, but it slows down replays.
- **Hole: the Aberration lobby.** Ogres have no use for discards. A possible hook: a card that Feeds a minion from your hand and counts as a discard.
- **Hole: Ogres lose to wide boards early.** One concentrated Ogre lands one hit while a wide board lands many. Tier 1 relies on Scavenger's two-for-one to keep up.

## 5. Tier check

Body sizes in stats, from Part C: 4.5 / 6 / 7 / 9 / 10 / 12.5.

- **Tier 1:** the bodies are 5 / 5 / 3, with Scavenger paying for a free minion in its stats. Tapster gives about 2–4 a turn, around 0.4–0.9 of a body, which is on target. Gobbler and Scavenger are the seeds.
- **Tier 2:** the bodies are about 6. Stewpot Cook gives 4 a turn per Belly, which looks high, but it's banked and pays out only on burst or Digest. Feast-Caller gives 4 per feed, Sellsword gives 1 Gold a turn, and Chomper is a one-off enabler. All within 2–6.
- **Tier 3:** the bodies are 7–8. Tollkeeper saves 1 Gold a turn. Butcher's Boy gives 8 per Digest, above the 5.5 target on purpose, because each Digest costs a minion. Arena Crier gives about 6–12 in a fight. Cage Master and Ritualist are the enablers that name the build.
- **Tier 4:** the bodies are 9–10. Chieftain gives about 10 per feed and about 20 a turn, which is high, but it needs two feeds. Brewmaster gives 10–20 but costs 1–2 Gold of tempo. Gourmand is the first multiplier; Bickering Magi and Bottomless are enablers.
- **Tier 5:** the bodies are 10–12. Bloodmixer gives about 30 a turn with three types, the direction card, on the high side but below the top of about 50. Cauldron-Keeper gives about 24 banked plus one Digest. The Butcher gives about 30–60 in a fight, the multiplier. Gladiator is the combat engine, with an above-curve body because its text needs kills.
- **Tier 6:** the bodies are 13–14. Gruul gives about 60 per Digest, near the target of 50, and 100 or more on a two-Digest turn, deliberately close to the top of 125. Cho'gall is the multiplier capstone. King Gordok is a removal-and-steal payoff in fight, about 80 or more, the deliberate break: a "might makes right" moment the build is aimed at.

## 6. Design rationale

1. I started from leftover Gold. Tapster pays a better rate for 1–2 wasted Gold than a third of a minion would, but it pays on a small body.
2. Next came a two-for-one that isn't usable now: Scavenger's minion is locked away. That's the "better rate, wrong moment" trade.
3. A locked minion needed a payoff, so it lends its text now and bursts out later. That made text run twice, which became the engine for Johnny players.
4. Bursting frees board slots, which makes the seven-slot limit into a puzzle. Concentrating power makes Venomous a natural counter, which keeps Spike players fair.
5. Digest is the cash-out choice: stats now, at a better rate with bonuses, in exchange for the text and the second body.
6. Digest triggers Deathrattles in the Recruit Phase, which creates the food chain: tokens get eaten again.
7. I folded two verbs into one, Feed, so cheap Ogres can stock the Bellies of expensive ones.
8. Ogres take their Belly minion's types, which turns them into the lobby's mixers. Bloodmixer pays for diversity.
9. Belly buffs are banked: a high rate, paid out only on burst or Digest, which is the same trade at another layer.
10. Full-board summons and enemy kills became more waste to eat, which set the identity: Ogres eat what others waste.
11. Each Tier 6 card multiplies a different part of the mechanic: text (Cho'gall), Digest (Gruul) and stealing (Gordok).
12. Names and lore went on last, and the fit was easy: two-headed Magi have two Bellies, Cho'gall's two heads run text twice, Gordok's challenge for the throne steals the strongest, and the Mok'nathal half-breeds mix types.
