# Ogres

## 1. Identity

**Core resource: Grog.** Ogres brew Gordok Green Grog, a card held in hand that **gets better the longer you keep it**, and they pay their King tribute every time anyone drinks.

| Term | Rule |
|---|---|
| **Grog** (token Tavern Spell, 1 Gold) | "Give a minion +X/+X, where X is this Grog's **Age**. Tribute." Age starts at 1 and goes up by 1 at the start of each of your turns while the Grog is in your hand. Playing a Grog on a minion is called *drinking* it. Any minion type can drink. |
| **Chug:** (new keyword) | Triggers when this minion drinks a Grog. |
| **King / Tribute** | You have one Crown. The minion wearing it is your **King**. **Tribute** = your King gains +1/+1. If you have no King when a Tribute happens, your highest-Attack minion gets the Crown and keeps it until it leaves the board. |
| **Two-Headed** | Drinks every Grog twice: double the buff, Chug twice, Tribute twice. Each extra head adds one more drink. |

**How the tribe plays.** Every Ogre turn asks the same question with more parts each time: *drink now, or let it age?* An Age-1 Grog drunk now is tempo. The same Grog drunk three turns later is +4/+4 plus a Tribute, but it took up a hand slot the whole time and Hoarder-style cards wanted it kept. Who drinks matters: a Two-Headed magus doubles the Grog, a Chug minion turns it into supply, aging or a team buff, and every drink feeds whichever minion wears the Crown. That King can be any minion type, so the tribe's stats can pile onto another tribe's best body. Beside this runs a barroom thread. Some Ogres hit their own side, which wastes stats unless your board wants to be hit: Deathrattles, Reborn, minions that get stronger when damaged, Aberrations waiting to die. The pieces lock together. Gladiators turn kills, including kills of your own minions, into Grog. The twins copy whatever the King gets. A Ritualist can put a second head on a non-Ogre, so a Divine Shield Mech King drinks twice and pays itself double Tribute.

## 3. Builds

**The Cellar (age and hoard).** Brewmaster, Ogre Magus, Mog'dorg, Tribute Hoarder, Keg-Roller, then Mar'gok. You hold four to six Grogs, so the Hoarder is huge in every fight and the Keg-Roller's Deathrattle hands the whole stockpile to the board when it dies. Then on a big turn you drink them in order under Mar'gok, and each drink ages the rest. The skill is choosing the order and the turn. This build fights the 10-card hand limit and triples hardest.

**The Court (one huge King).** Kromcrush, Taxman, King Gordok, Gog, with Phemos and Pol as the twin King. Kromcrush can give the Crown to *any* minion. Johnny's choices: a Divine Shield Mech, a Windfury or Cleave body, a golden Brann, or Pol, so Phemos copies every Tribute. Gog spreads each Tribute across the Ogres, so the Court isn't only one minion. Venomous and "destroy" effects are its natural weakness.

**The Two-Headed table (Chug engine).** Magi, Ritualist, Stonemaul Mercenary, Guzzler, Rexxar, Cho'gall. One Grog on a Ritualist-boosted Bladespire Magus under Cho'gall makes four to six team-wide Chugs. A Ritualist head on the Guzzler turns each paid Grog into two new ones. A head on Misha (a Beast) makes her drink twice per Rexxar Chug.

**Barroom Brawl (friendly fire).** Bruiser, Bar Brawler, Pit Boss, Butcher, Gladiator. On its own it's weak. Its value comes from the other tribes in the lobby, below.

**Mixing in a five-tribe lobby.**
- *Undead:* Bar Brawler kills 1-Health Reborn bodies at Start of Combat, they come back, and Avenge counters tick up before a single enemy attack.
- *Beast:* the Butcher and Bruiser pop your own Deathrattle summoners on purpose. Misha is a Beast for Beast buffs.
- *Aberration:* Bar Brawler can kill several 1-Health Aberrations at Start of Combat and bring the Deity in early.
- *Mech:* a Divine Shield Mech is the safest King. Brawlers popping your own shields is bad unless you have Mech payoffs for losing a shield.
- *Pirate:* gold makes Grog chains (each Grog costs 1 Gold) longer.
- *Dragon:* Grog is a Tavern Spell, so whatever improves Tavern Spells improves Grog.
- *Quilboar:* Blood Gems compete for gold and hand space. A Gem-scaled Quilboar is a fine King.
- *Demon:* the weakest pairing. Nothing shared beyond a King body.

## 4. Risks and holes

- **Hand clog.** Aged Grog, triples and other tribes' cards share 10 slots. The Cellar may block its own triples. Needs testing, maybe a cap on Age instead.
- **1 Gold per Grog** may feel too slow on turns 2–4. Barkeep softens it, and the cost may need to be 0 with a per-turn limit.
- **Tracking load.** Age on each Grog, heads on each minion, who wears the Crown. The UI has to show all three at a glance, or Johnny's puzzles become guesswork.
- **Long turns.** Guzzler + Cho'gall + Mar'gok + Pirate gold can chain many drinks. Gold limits it, but the click count could get tedious.
- **Auto-crown surprise.** The first Tribute crowning your highest-Attack minion may hand the stats to a body you planned to sell.
- **The brawl thread has no Tier 6 capstone** and depends on the lobby. Without Deathrattle/Reborn tribes it's a trap, and Bruiser in particular.
- **King concentration** is easy for Spike to counter (Venomous, destroy effects) and feels bad when it happens. The Greater trinket's Divine Shield helps, but only once per fight.
- **Phemos mirroring** needs the "no copy-back" rule stated clearly and tested with Two-Headed and Tribute stacking.
- **Combat-time drinking** (Keg-Roller) fires Chugs mid-fight. A Guzzler then makes Grog during combat. That Grog is real and stays. Check this doesn't snowball.

## 5. Tier check (24 units: 3 / 4 / 5 / 5 / 4 / 3)

- **T1:** Tapper is the seed: about one body once, when the Grog is drunk at Age 2–3. Bruiser is the tempo body at 1.5 bodies, paid for by its downside. Guzzler is under body (4) because it's an engine. On target.
- **T2:** Barkeep is economy (1 Gold a turn ≈ a third of a unit). Mercenary is the buffer (+4 stats per Chug). Gladiator is economy/supply. Magus is the enabler (+2X/+2X per Grog, about 1–1.3 bodies once). On target.
- **T3:** Brewmaster, Kromcrush and Taxman are the enablers that name the build (roughly 5–7 permanent a turn, or 6 once). Brawler is the combat engine (about 10 in fight). Hoarder is about 9 in fight with three Grogs held. One more than the 19-unit shape, to give each thread a Tier 3 piece.
- **T4:** Bladespire Magus is the buffer, about 12–20 a turn with two drinks: the top end, on purpose, as the Chug build's first payoff. Ritualist is the first multiplier. Pit Boss is about 12 a turn with Brawler. Mog'dorg is about 12 with three Grogs held. Rexxar is a tempo body plus a token, a deliberate break that gives Beasts a crossover.
- **T5:** Gordok is the direction card (about 20 a turn with Taxman). Butcher is the combat engine. Phemos is tempo (two bodies) and a multiplier. Keg-Roller is about 40 in fight from four aged Grogs. On target.
- **T6:** Three capstones, one closing each thread. Mar'gok is about 50 on a five-Grog chain, Gog about 50 from five Tributes over five Ogres, and Cho'gall doubles Chugs. Each Tier 6 card does what a Tier 4 or 5 card does, roughly three times over (Gog vs Gordok, Cho'gall vs Ritualist, Mar'gok vs Ogre Magus). The goldens' ceiling (Ritualist heads + golden Cho'gall) is the runaway risk to test.

## 6. Design rationale (in order)

1. Started from Gordok Green Grog: beer that gets better with age, so the tension is *hold or drink*. Blood Gems have a fixed value, so this is a different decision.
2. Grog costs 1 Gold because ogres pay for their round. It also caps loops and makes Grog compete with buys.
3. Added Chug as the one new keyword, so "who drinks" becomes a card-by-card decision.
4. The bickering ogre magi became Two-Headed = drinks twice: the simplest rule that makes a second head matter.
5. The Altars of Storms became the Ritualist, which grants heads to *any* minion, so non-Ogres can join the engine.
6. Dire Maul's tribute run became King and Tribute. I then put Tribute inside every Grog, so the Crown runs on drinking instead of being a separate engine.
7. Auto-crowning on the first Tribute keeps that rule from ever doing nothing. Kromcrush exists so you can choose the King, including another tribe's minion.
8. Mogor's "attacks the wrong enemy" became friendly fire. It is meant to be used against its intended purpose, with other tribes' Deathrattles, Reborn and Aberration deaths.
9. The Highmaul arena gave the Gladiator kill-for-Grog. "Either side" deliberately links the brawl thread to the Grog thread.
10. Mog'dorg the Wizened ages Grog (old sage), Phemos and Pol mirror each other (twins), and Rexxar shares his drink with Misha (Beast crossover).
11. Only after these cards did the shared mechanic become clear: everything runs through Grog. So I filled the remaining slots with supply (Tapper, Brewmaster), aging and hoarding (Hoarder, Keg-Roller) and Tier 6 capstones that close the threads.
12. Shape 3/4/5/5/4/3, scaled from the 19-unit guide. The brawl thread has no Tier 6 card on purpose, so it stays a crossover tool rather than a fourth standalone build.
