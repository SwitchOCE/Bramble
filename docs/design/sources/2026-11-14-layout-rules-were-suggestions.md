# Layout rules were suggestions; what is fixed and what can move (11 and 14 Nov 2026)

The owner's words in the UI layout session, in chat. These are the messages the `design-critique` skill quotes. They are given whole and as typed or dictated: nothing removed, nothing reworded. *[Editor notes in italic brackets.]*

## 11 Nov 2026: the hex shape and the centred board

*[Said after seeing the unit shapes tried across the visual styles.]*

Okay, Claude, I like this pretty much for all the visual paradigms. You can put rounding on the hexes maybe and some of the other ones if you want to try that. But I like the somewhere between the mid hex and the tall hex for pretty much every one of the visual paradigms is the go. So do a revision with everything, but using these shapes because it works for the packing really nicely. Don't worry too much about the placeholder art and the fit, because obviously it's not going to. We're more worried about layout and stuff. One thing I really think is important that we get happening is the player's board needs to be centered in the screen in terms of the horizontal centering in the top and the the vertical stacking mode, and vertically centered in the horizontal mode, the book version. Other stuff needs to take place around it. There's a lot of, I think I prefer putting the opponents on the right-hand side of the screen, like the scoreboard, the leaderboard thing on the right. That seems better to me. And yeah, do another iteration of the existing visual styles with those changes.

## 14 Nov 2026: the earlier rules were suggestions

*[Said when the layout work was being held to the 11 Nov notes as rules.]*

Those rules were just things I suggested for improvements. I did ask you specifically at the beginning of this switch from Opus to Fable, at the beginning of your context window, to specifically call out rules that need to be broken to fix this issue. Some of the rules that I've stated need to be examined because they were suggestions that became rules, but were not and are not necessarily going to be productive for the long term. I'm not completely attached to anything. I think the hexagons, or the tall hexes, or whatever we're calling them, these are the right path. I think, you know, I'm willing to move on hand size and whether I will change the game systems to fit what we can display well in the UI, hand size wise. If we need to not render a leaderboard because there's no good way of doing it, I'm okay with that. What is most important to me is the game's look and feel, its moment-to-moment gameplay is good. I don't want to have to move on some of the other design systems. I want the board to be 4 and 3 if I possibly can get it. If we really can't do it, okay, I'll reevaluate.

## 14 Nov 2026, a few minutes later: the board may move

What if we also got rid of the rule that your board never moves in state? I think it should stay centered, but what if, like, I don't think your hand is needed for combat, right? And maybe the board can move a little bit. Maybe it's fine. Other games don't do it, but maybe it's fine if we do.
