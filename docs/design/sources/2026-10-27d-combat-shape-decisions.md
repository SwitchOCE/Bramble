# Owner decisions: combat shape

Made by the owner on 27 October 2026, answering the combat-shape questions (section 1) of `../review-queue.md`. The owner chose from offered options; the one free-text answer (Taunt) is quoted as written.

| # | Question | Owner's choice |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Attack order | **Front row left to right, then back row left to right, repeat.** Kept as is. The back row plays a reserve and support role. |
| 2 | Can single-target random effects ("deal 3 damage to a random enemy") hit the back row while front units live? | **Any enemy.** Random effects ignore rows. |
| 3 | When does a unit summoned mid-combat attack? | **The proposed rule.** Summoned into the slot of the unit that just attacked and died: it attacks next. Into an earlier slot: it waits for the next cycle. Into a later slot: it attacks when the order reaches it. |
| 4 | Where do summoned units go? | **The proposed rule.** A dying unit's first summon takes its slot. Others go to the nearest empty slot, same row first, ties to the right. No room means the summon is lost. |
| 5 | Leftmost and rightmost | **Per row.** There's no board-wide leftmost; card text names the row ("leftmost front-row unit"). |
| 6 | Combat buffs | **They end with the fight,** as in Battlegrounds, unless the card says "permanently". |
| 7 | Keep Taunt? | Owner's words: "Taunt changes legal targeting. A back row taunt would not cause targeting to change to the back row from the front, but would stop other back row units being attacked." |

*[Editor notes:*
- *Taunt works within the row that can be attacked. While the enemy front row has units, a back-row Taunt does nothing. Once the back row can be attacked, a back-row Taunt must be attacked before the other back-row units.*
- *Random single-target effects reaching the back row lowers the value of back-row protection. The power budget's back-row protection numbers assumed otherwise and need re-running.]*
