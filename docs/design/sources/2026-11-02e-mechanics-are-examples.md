# Owner direction: Battlegrounds keywords are examples, not Bramble mechanics

Said by the owner in chat on 2 November 2026, while reviewing the rulebook.

I think this rulebook has a lot of Hearthstone Battlegrounds mechanics in it. Not systems: mechanics. Reborn, Deathrattle, Divine Shield. All of these are mechanics and keywords. Windfury, Taunt, Stealth. None of these are Bramble mechanics. We haven't designed those yet.

Those are illustrations of how the rules might be broken in Hearthstone Battlegrounds, and of how our code systems may need to adapt. They should be in there as illustrations only, because we will be developing our own things, some of which do not exist in Hearthstone Battlegrounds.

We'll also have things in here from elsewhere, like Galaxy. We have set slots, so we can experiment with slot buff mechanics, and we should have the ability to do that, I think.

*[Editor notes:*
- *In rulebook v0 the Battlegrounds keywords are tagged [BG] as if adopted (chapter 11), and several core rules are written in their terms (Taunt and Stealth in targeting 9.5, Reborn and Deathrattle in summons and deaths 8.4, 9.7 and 9.8, Windfury in attack order 9.4, Magnetic in playing units 7.2).*
- *Galaxy's slot buffs are effects that stay on a board slot when the unit in it changes (research/galaxy/design-notes.md idea 9; slots are referenced on 62 Galaxy cards, content-library.md).]*
