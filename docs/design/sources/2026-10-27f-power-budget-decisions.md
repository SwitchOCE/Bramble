# Owner decisions: power budget and the refresh cap

Made by the owner on 27 October 2026, answering section 3 of `../review-queue.md` and the proposed free-refresh safety cap (rulebook 4.4.8). Where the owner wrote an answer in their own words, it is quoted as written.

## Free-refresh safety cap

Owner's words:

> We handle it with content design. Not every problem needs a system to prevent it, we can have content safeguards as well. Refreshing anomaly for example becomes an issue when paired with golden brann and cards that retrigger battlecries, but it there was no such thing as an easily repeatable source of free refreshes, it wouldn't be an issue. We just need to make good balance and design decisions.

*[Editor notes:*
- *No refresh cap is added; the proposed rule 4.4.8 is withdrawn.*
- *The principle is recorded as a content guardrail: no easily repeatable source of free refreshes. See `../content-guardrails.md`.]*

## Plain units

**On budget, as recommended.** Owner's words:

> However, I want to review proposed power budgeting in depth. I.e. How many turns would it take for the scaling card to realistically catch up and be better than the immediate power card? At what level of econ? etc. These are the questions that matter.

## Golden units

**Double stats and effect sizes; thresholds stay the same** (Avenge (3) stays 3).

## Tolerance band

**±15%** of the tier budget counts as on budget.
