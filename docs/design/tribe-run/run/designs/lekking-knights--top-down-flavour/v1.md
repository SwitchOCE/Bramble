# The Lekking Knights (v1)

## 1. Identity

**What playing them should feel like:** you groom one magnificent, ridiculous knight who holds the lek alone at dawn. The rest of the order are squires and elders who feed him, and hens who sit in the bilberry and judge. Each year the order gets smaller and its last knights get grander. The tribe offers few units, each one enormous, on a board full of deliberate gaps.

**Three rules words (one keyword, two terms):**

- **Duel** (keyword). *Start of Combat: this and the enemy across hit each other once with their Attack.* If the slot across is empty, it Duels a random enemy in that row instead; if that row is empty, nothing happens. A unit in your back row therefore Duels the enemy back row. A unit **wins** a Duel if the enemy dies and it survives. A Duel is a rite, not an attack, so the rules that say "when attacked" or "attacking" (Hunker, Nightshade, Bitter, Mire, Cleave, Pack) don't apply. Shield, Bristle and Damage Reduction still do. "Duels X" on a card means the same exchange against X.
- **Proud** (term). A unit is Proud while no friendly unit is **beside** it (left or right in its row). Diagonal neighbours don't count. It's checked when an effect asks. Since gaps never close, you build Proud boards by leaving slots empty. A full Proud board has at most 4 units (for example F1, F3, B1, B3).
- **Champion** (term). Your **biggest Knight**; on a tie, the first in reading order. It's re-checked whenever an effect asks, so it can change mid-fight when the Champion dies. **Hens** (Bilberry Hen, Dame Reisig) belong to the tribe but are not Knights, so they are never Champion.

**How it plays.** Early on, Duel bodies win fights by killing the enemy across before the fight begins. From Tier 2 you choose what to feed. One path feeds the *Champion*: squires are sold or crossed out into him, and empty slots pay him. Another feeds *Proud* units: a chequerboard of four isolated knights, each paid for standing alone. The third feeds *Duel wins*: hens and the Cork-Pop Master turn every win into growth. All three overlap. A Proud Champion who wins Duels is fed by every card in the tribe, so the puzzle is how few units you can field and still survive. Start of Combat resolves in reading order, so where you put a unit decides whether the Shield lands before or after the Duel. Keeping slots empty costs you: the side with more units attacks first, and survivors deal hero damage. The Knights pay that price on purpose.

## 3. Builds

**A. The Last Knight (lone Champion).** Use Squire Konrad, Elder of the Centre, The Chronicler, Herald of the Last Dawn and Sir Gottfried. You grow one Knight by selling and crossing out the units around him, and the empty slots pay him every turn. By the end you field two or three units: Gottfried as Champion, Herald and perhaps a Fantail Warden for his Shield. Herald Duels before the fight starts. Gottfried then multiplies himself by the empty board. The Chronicler wants 4 units at Start of Turn, but the Elder and Gottfried want few at End of Turn and in the fight. So each turn you buy a fat unit for the Chronicler to cross out, and you fight small. Konrad, grown Proud in a corner, is the best thing to feed him: a 6/7 Konrad crossed out gives +26.

**B. The Proud Court (chequerboard).** Use Scarlet-Brow Marshal, Grit-Pit Forager, Fantail Warden, Dame Reisig and Ottokar. Four units sit at F1, F3, B1 and B3 or similar, with no one beside anyone. Marshal and Reisig grow all four, and Forager turns its isolation into Gizzard Stones. Ottokar gives the whole court Duel and makes each first Duel free, so four enemies take a hit before the fight. The Proud board is also immune to Cleave by construction.

**C. Dawn Duellists (wide Duel chains).** Use Fir-Ridge Yearling, Pennon-Squire, Bilberry Hen, Sir Hartmut, Master of the Cork-Pop and Sir Altmark. Here the Knights are allowed a full board, and every unit Duels. Each win feeds the Hen. The Cork-Pop doubles the Hen, Hartmut's re-Duels and Altmark's trophies. Sir Brennbart Duels the enemy carry in the back row to soften it before the fight. Pennon-Squire gives Duel to anything, so this build takes other tribes in.

**Mixing with other tribes in a lobby:**
- **Oozes:** units inside a **Belly** aren't on the board, so an Ooze can hold your court while the empty slots pay Elder, Herald and Gottfried. If The Chronicler crosses out a full Ooze at Start of Turn, the units in its Belly come back out onto your board. With Dame Reisig, a huge Ooze can be Champion and take every Knight buff, and Eating a grown Konrad keeps his stats.
- **Hunters:** give a Hunter Duel with Pennon-Squire. Its Quarry is also the enemy across, so a Duel kill fires **Take** at Start of Combat.
- **Birds:** Fly South empties a slot for two turns, which feeds the empty-slot cards. The Egg in the Nest is a unit, though, and it breaks Proud beside it. With Reisig, a Bird coming home with doubled stats can become Champion.
- **Bog folk:** Mire and Bitter don't touch Duels, so Knights can clear a Mire wall before it starts taxing attacks.
- **Hedgerow folk:** Pass X goes to your rightmost other unit, so put the Champion at F4 or B3.
- **Neutral Shield units** turn any Duel into a free kill.

## 4. Risks and holes

- **Pre-fight wipes.** Herald, Hartmut, Brennbart, Ottokar and Cork-Pop together could kill most of an enemy front row before anyone attacks, which makes the fight a non-event. Each one is capped, and every Duel costs health (except under Ottokar), but this combination needs a sim first.
- **Too few units.** A one- or two-unit board attacks second, deals almost no hero damage when it wins, and loses to many small attackers or summons. That is the intended price, but the Last Knight build could be unplayable or binary. Watch how often it wins against wide boards.
- **Gottfried's ceiling.** Alone, he fights at ×4 (×7 golden) of an already-fed Champion. That's well past the Tier 6 in-fight guide, on purpose. If it runs away, change the rate to +50% per empty slot beyond the second.
- **The Chronicler feels bad** if it destroys something you wanted. You control "smallest", but a "you may" version may test better.
- **Reading-order puzzles** (Warden's Shield before or after the Champion's Duel) are Johnny's joy and a newcomer's confusion. The UI needs to show Start of Combat order.
- **Reisig makes the Knights a support package** for other tribes' carries. That's good for Johnny but could make the Knights a splash rather than a tribe. Watch the pick rate of Reisig without other Knights.
- **Refectory Prior is free gold early,** because everyone has 4 or fewer units on turns 2–4. It probably needs to be "3 or fewer" or "Once you have Tiered up to 3".
- **Rules holes to settle:** whether Proud counts Created units (the Bird Egg, summons) beside; what a Duel does when both units have Shield; and which side's Start of Combat goes first, which decides Duel order between two Knight boards.

## 5. Tier check

Bodies: 4 / 6 / 7 / 8.5 / 12 / 14.

- **Tier 1:** Yearling 2/2 Duel is the tempo body at exactly 4. Konrad, the seed, is 1/2 (below body; the value is in his text) and grows +2 a turn when Proud (~0.5 body against ~0.4). His sale banks the stored growth.
- **Tier 2:** all 5–6 body. Pennon-Squire's once value is low in stats (+2) but it hands out a keyword. Bilberry Hen gives 2–6 a turn at 1–3 wins (0.3–1 body). Prior gives 1 gold a turn, the standard Tier 2 economy, but it's too easy early (see Risks).
- **Tier 3:** all 7 body. Elder gives 6–10 a turn on a 3–4 unit board (0.8–1.4 body), above the guide when the board is tiny, on purpose. Forager gives 4–8 through stones. Hartmut's up-to-three Duels sit inside the 0.7–1.4 in-fight range at Tier 3 sizes.
- **Tier 4:** 8–9 body. Marshal gives 8–16 a turn on 2–4 Proud units (target ~11). Cork-Pop is the first multiplier. Brennbart is an enabler: he reaches the back row. Envoy's one or two Discovers are ~2 bodies of value once.
- **Tier 5:** 12 body. Reisig gives 24 at four Proud units (target ~24). Chronicler gives twice the smallest unit, ~20–40 late, at the cost of a unit. Herald's up to three Champion Duels are in-fight value near ~4 bodies.
- **Tier 6:** 14–15 body. Altmark scales with the enemy's size, not your own, at ~12–60 a turn (target ~56). Ottokar's free Duels across a court are around the ~6-body in-fight target when the Attack is high, and above it when Duels kill. **Broken on purpose:** Gottfried's in-fight multiplier goes past the guide, because it only works on a board that has given up most of its HP.
- **Shape changes:** Tier 2 has two enablers and one economy piece, as the guide suggests. Tier 4 has no second buffer; Envoy holds the economy slot so that the Knights' small boards still find their pieces.

## 6. Design rationale

1. **The fantasy came first:** one vain giant holding the lek while the order shrinks and the hens judge. Everything else had to serve "few, enormous, alone."
2. **I kept four themes:** display, the dawn duel, being judged and splendid decline. I dropped forest-guarding, snowshoes and the Fence Line because they don't map onto a board.
3. **Display became Proud,** a rule about gaps. Gaps never close in Bramble, so standing alone is a real, visible board shape, and it caps a Proud board at four.
4. **The single champion became one named target (Champion),** so that every feeder has an aim and players can steer who holds the title.
5. **The dawn duel became Duel at Start of Combat.** It is "a rite, not an attack," which is both the flavour and the source of cross-tribe rules interplay with Mire, Hunker and Quarry.
6. **A back-row Duel hits the back row,** because the lek has stations, and placement should change who you face.
7. **Decline became empty slots as fuel,** plus selling or crossing out units into the Champion (Konrad, Elder, Chronicler, Gottfried). Shrinking is the engine, not a side effect.
8. **The judging hens became back-row Hens that reward wins and are never Champion,** as in the seed: "not members, not impressed."
9. **Dame Reisig is the one bridge that crowns any tribe,** giving Johnny a sanctioned way to use the Knights outside their purpose.
10. **The multiplier repeats Duel-win triggers, not buffs.** Wins are bounded by the number of enemies and by health lost, which keeps the runaway check simple.
11. **Each Tier 6 caps one build:** Gottfried the lone Champion, Ottokar the Proud court, Altmark the duellists. Each still pays into the other two.
12. **Numbers were costed last** against Part C. The places above the guide all depend on a small board, which is the tribe's own cost.
