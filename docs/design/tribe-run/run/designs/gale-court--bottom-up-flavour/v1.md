# The Gale Court (v1)

## 1. Identity

**The court decides when things happen in a fight.** Its units strike before their turn, arrive halfway through, knock enemies out of their turn, or hold everything back and let it go at once. Two new keywords:

- **Gust:** this unit attacks right now, out of turn. It's a normal attack in every way (random front-row enemy, takes damage back), and the unit still makes its usual attack in the cycle.
- **Late:** if this is in your hand when the fight starts, it waits, then arrives after your side's third attack (Gusts count), in your first empty slot in reading order, or as soon as a slot opens. It didn't start the fight, so it doesn't count for who attacks first or for hero damage. After the fight it's back in your hand. Played to the board, it's an ordinary unit.

Card text also uses **knocked flat:** the unit skips its next attack.

**How it plays.** Early, Gust Pages and Sir Vane win fights by hitting before the enemy can. Then the hand becomes a second board. A Late unit is an eighth (or ninth) unit that the enemy's Start of Combat can't touch, and it lands mid-fight onto whatever the enemy has left. Most of the court's growth comes from counting how often it Gusted, so every Gust source also feeds growth somewhere else. The puzzle is ordering: who you played last (Vane), who stands next to the empty slot (The Helm), how many attacks happen before the Duke arrives, and when Lady High Pressure should die. Many cards do a second job: Mr Fair's exit dodges a death, Black Cloud's speed-up shrinks the Duke's count, and the Wild Hunt makes the dead count as arrivals.

## 3. Builds

**Squall (Start of Combat Gusts).** Gust Pages, Sir Vane, Beacon-Keeper, then Mistral and the Steward. You clear most of the front row before the enemy swings. Growth comes from counting Gusts: Windhover and Föhn early, the Heron at Tier 4, the Tower at Tier 6. The Tower rewards *different* Gusters, so you spread Gusts across the board instead of doubling one unit. Example turn: Beacon-Keeper on F1 lights B1 and F2; Vane points at the Bora you just played (as a board unit), so her Gust knocks a big front-liner flat before it attacks.

**Late Entrance (the hand as a second board).** Couriers, Mrs Foul, Bora, the Duke, the Wild Hunt, with Black Cloud and The Helm. You fight with up to seven on the board and two or three more in the wings. Leave a slot open next to The Helm in reading order so each arrival lands there and takes his buff. Vane makes every arrival Gust. Black Cloud's permanent +2/+2 grows the Late units in your hand each fight. The tension: Black Cloud brings arrivals in sooner, which shrinks the Duke's count. Johnny chooses which one wins.

**Calm Before the Storm.** Lady High Pressure, the Steward, the Duke and a Hunker or Shield neutral. Every Start of Combat Gust and every arrival is held while the enemy spends its own Start of Combat effects into her 12 Health. When she falls, the held Gusts happen twice each (four times with the Steward), then the Late units land at once, and the Duke counts a long gap. Put her in the front row for a fast storm, or in the back row to make it break on the sixth attack instead.

**Wild Hunt finish.** Let cheap units die early (Gust Pages trading into the front row), then the Master brings them all back as arrivals. Each one Gusts, takes Black Cloud's permanent +2/+2, and triggers Vane and The Helm again.

**Mixing in a lobby.**
- **Hunters:** Pack joins attacks by units beside it. A Gust is an attack, so a Pack unit beside a Gust Page joins its Start of Combat swing. Knocking a Quarry flat keeps your Hunter's target in place.
- **Fairies:** Late units land in your first empty slot. Put a Ring on that slot and every arrival gets the Ring size.
- **Bog folk:** Mire forces the enemy across to attack your Mire unit. That protects Lady High Pressure in the back, or forces hits onto Mr Fair just before he goes indoors.
- **Oozes:** an Ooze's Belly is the board-side twin of Late. Föhn's "blamed" Attack fires on every Ooze death.
- **Birds:** the Couriers are swifts. Selling Birds to Fly South leaves Nests, which are empty slots Late units can land in once the Egg dies.
- **Neutrals:** Swoop on a Gusting unit lets Start of Combat hits reach the back row. Shield keeps Gust Pages alive through the counter-damage.

## 4. Risks and holes

- **Fights get hard to read.** Out-of-turn attacks, held Gusts and mid-fight arrivals pile up. This tribe needs the clearest combat animation: a visible queue, arrivals entering from the hand, a counter for the Duke.
- **Gusts take damage back.** Early Gusts into big blockers or Bristle kill your own small units. That's intended friction, but the T1–2 experience may feel like suicide against Bog folk.
- **The hand is crowded.** Late units compete with triples in progress, Acorn items and the 4-card limit. Hand-locked Couriers and Mrs Foul need selling from hand, which Bramble hasn't settled.
- **Recorded boards must include the hand.** Asynchronous play has to store the hand and the Late rules.
- **Late builds attack second.** Arrivals don't count for who goes first. Fine, but test whether Late is too weak against fast Start of Combat tribes.
- **In-fight multipliers stack.** High Pressure ×2 with the Steward ×2 is ×4, and two goldens make it ×9. Every permanent gain is capped (once a fight, three times, up to 8 faces), so this is fight-only, but one-sided wipes could feel unfair to the loser.
- **Thin economy.** The Glass is the only gold card. The tribe may lean on neutral gold support.
- **Lady High Pressure can stall.** If she survives and your side never reaches six attacks (a wide enemy kills you first), the storm never breaks. Watch for feel-bad losses.
- **The Duke's count includes enemy attacks,** so he's strongest against wide boards. That could be swingy.
- **The Wild Hunt plus Black Cloud** permanently buffs every revived unit. Bounded by deaths, but check it in practice.

## 5. Tier check

- **Tier 1:** Page 2/2 at body 4, about 0.7 body in fight with a partner (target ~0.6). Windhover gives +2 stats a turn, about 0.5 body (target 0.4). On guide.
- **Tier 2:** Vane makes one Gust plus arrival Gusts, about 1 body in fight (target ~1). Glass pays about 1 gold. The Couriers are about 1.5 bodies in fight (an extra body plus a Gust), **over on purpose**: they cost a hand slot and arrive late.
- **Tier 3:** Föhn up to 6 permanent stats a turn (0.86 body, target 0.8). The Helm about +8 once per play (target 1–1.4 bodies). Beacon makes two Gusts, about 1.3 bodies in fight. Mr Fair gives 4 permanent plus about 5 in fight across two cards. All within range.
- **Tier 4:** Bora is about 2 bodies in fight (two enemy attacks skipped plus a Gust). The Heron averages about 10 stats a turn (1.2 bodies, target 1.3). Black Cloud is about 1 body permanent plus faster arrivals. Mistral is about 3 bodies in fight, **over on purpose** as the tribe's first multiplier, and capped at three Gusts.
- **Tier 5:** The Duke typically gains +16 to +24 stats plus a Gust, about 4 bodies (target ~4). The Steward is about 3.3 bodies. The Sí Gaoithe's denial is hard to price, roughly 3–4 bodies of tempo. On guide.
- **Tier 6:** The Tower with five faces gives about 70 permanent stats (5 bodies, target ~4, top ~10), slightly high as the Squall payoff. The Wild Hunt is about 5–6 bodies in fight. High Pressure is about 4 bodies alone, more with the Duke and the Steward, as the capstone multiplier.

## 6. Design rationale

1. **Gust Pages first** ("all want to go first"): a unit that attacks out of turn, so Gust came from them.
2. **Sir Vane** changes sides with every arrival, so he points at the last unit played. Play order becomes a decision.
3. **Duke Cumulonimbus** arrives late and counts lightning to thunder. A unit entering mid-fight that grows with the count became Late, played from the hand.
4. **Swifts never land:** a hand-only Late unit gave Late a cheap entry at Tier 2.
5. **The weather house, never home together:** a board-and-hand pair that swap mid-fight, so Late is more than an extra body.
6. **Seen together,** the cards all bent *when* things happen: first, last, skipped or held. That precedence became the identity, with Gust and Late as its two halves.
7. **Lady High Pressure forbids anything to happen:** holding both Gusts and arrivals, then releasing them doubled, ties the two keywords into one capstone.
8. **Bora and Mistral** took their real winds' effects (knocking people flat, clearing the room) so the Gust build has texture beyond more damage.
9. **Growth counts Gusts** (Windhover, Föhn, the Glass, the Heron, the Tower), each with a cap, so permanent growth stays priced while fight multipliers can run wild.
10. **The Wild Hunt** rides the storm and brings the dead back as arrivals, so every arrival trigger fires again. That's the Late capstone.
11. **The Sí Gaoithe** turns the court's timing on the enemy: the one card that delays their side.
12. **Economy stays one card,** because the hand-as-reserve already makes the tribe pay in hand space.
