# The Salmon Run, v1

## 1. Identity

**Stamps.** Every Run unit carries a passport. At Start of Turn, each Run unit gains a Stamp for each attack it made in the last fight and survived (each weir it leapt). Stamps last until a card spends or clears them.

**Home N.** At End of Turn, after other effects: if this unit is in your **front row** with N or more Stamps, it comes home. Its **Spawn** effect happens, then it is **spent**. Homes are checked in reading order, and checked again until none is ready, so one homecoming can set off another.

**Spent.** The fish dies (outside a fight). Its Attack and Health are split evenly among your units next to it, permanently (the gift to the forest). A **Fry** takes its slot.

**Fry** (token, Created, Run, 1/1): *Start of Turn: gains a Stamp. Home 2. Spawn: becomes a fresh copy of the fish that laid it, keeping the Fry's own buffs (it isn't spent).*

**How it plays.** Your back row is the resting pools: fish there attack, collect Stamps and never go home. Moving a fish into the front row decides that it goes home at End of Turn, if its passport is full. You choose between two pulls. Held fish pile up Stamps that Kype, Llyn Llyw and the Grandfather pay out. Fish sent home fire Spawn effects, pass all their stats to whoever stands beside them, and come back two turns later as a Fry that turns into a fresh copy. So the Run works as a stat pump for whatever unit you stand next to the spawning slot, Run or not, and as a loop of Spawn effects that Little Jack Gravel and Old Bradán repeat. Most of the play is arranging the board: who sits beside the redd, who rests in the pools, and whose Stamps count.

**Rulings that matter.** Bradán triggers Spawn *effects*. Nothing else spawns or is spent, so the Bend-Wife, Old Kelt, the Grandfather and Little Jack don't fire off the units he triggers, but Jack does repeat Bradán's own Spawn. Mother Redd's count only rises when she really spawns. A golden's Home number stays the same, because it's a threshold. Being eaten isn't being spent: no gift, no Fry.

**Token:** Fry (above). No tribe spells.

## 3. Builds

**The Spawning Run (cycle).** Grilse, Parr, Pilgrim Guide, Mother Redd, Little Jack, Fish-Pass Keeper, the Bend-Wife, Old Kelt, Old Bradán. Keep two front slots as redds with Jack Gravel diagonally behind them. Each turn something comes home, its Spawn fires twice, and its stats land on a fixed carry beside it. Fish-Pass makes Fry turn back in one turn. Endgame: Bradán beside Jack triggers every Spawn on the board twice, Guide's gold included.
*Example turn:* Bailiff stamps everyone, Parr reaches 3 in F2, comes home, its Spawn (repeated by Jack in B1) gives everyone 2 Stamps, Grilse in F3 reaches 1 and comes home in the same chain. The Hen turns three of those Stamps into stats, and the Bend-Wife gives everyone +2/+2 twice.

**The Long Road (hoard).** Bailiff, Rust-Flank Hen, Reunion Secretary, Captain Kype, Big Tam, Seal-Scarred Veteran, the Salmon of Llyn Llyw. The Run sits in the back row and never goes home. The front row is someone else's job: Bog folk with Mire, or Hedgerow folk, holding the line. Stamps climb every turn. Kype and Llyn Llyw turn the total into Attack, and Tam turns each Stamp into stats for the front. You cash out once: move a 12-Stamp fish forward with the Grandfather out, and every unit gains +12/+12.

**Forest Feed (cross-tribe).** Passport Seller, the Grandfather, Old Kelt, Big Tam, with another tribe's carry. Passport something odd, and its homecoming splits its stats onto the carry. Its Fry comes back as a fresh copy, so you can re-buy its When-played value on a Kelt copy. Pairings:
- **Oozes:** passport a full-Belly Ooze. It comes home, dies, its Belly empties, and its stats feed a neighbouring Ooze that then Eats a Hen-buffed Fry.
- **Hunters:** a Pack unit beside the redd catches every gift.
- **Fairies:** a redd on a Ring slot means each Fry hatches with Ring size on top.
- **Birds:** they share the leave-and-return feel, but don't interact much. Nest Eggs and Fry both fill empty slots, which is about it.

**In a lobby.** The Run is a support tribe as much as a carry tribe. Tam, Llyn Llyw and the Grandfather buff *all* units, and the gift goes to whoever stands next to the redd. A Run player often ends on a Bog front row with a Run back row, or an Ooze fed by spent fish. Neutral Shield and Hunker units are worth more to the Run, because surviving earns Stamps.

## 4. Risks and holes

- **Losing streaks starve it.** Stamps come mostly from surviving. The Claw-Marked Cock, the Bailiff, Fry clocks and Llyn Llyw soften this, but a Run that falls behind early may stay behind. First fix to try: a Stamp for each attack, survived or not, with survival giving a second one.
- **Tempo hole after a homecoming.** A 1/1 Fry stands in the front row for two fights. Is that a fair price, or does the cycle lose too many fights? Needs play.
- **Old Kelt and triples.** With a copy every turn plus the Fry's copy, a Run player can triple a chosen fish in about two turns, and on a passported unit, another tribe's fish. Most likely the strongest economy in the tribe; watch it first. Fix: Kelt copies only Run units of Tier 4 or lower.
- **Jack Gravel and Bradán gold.** A golden Jack beside Bradán with the Guide on board pays out 12+ gold every few turns. Fix: the Guide's Spawn says "once a turn".
- **Bookkeeping.** Each unit's Stamps, Home, year-classes and chains of homecomings. The UI needs a "comes home at End of Turn" marker on ready fish.
- **The Bailiff eats Little Jack Gravel** (1/3) if no Fry is smaller. It's meant as a puzzle, but it may read as a trap.
- **Spent = dies outside a fight.** This is untested against other tribes' death triggers (Ooze Bellies, Hunters' Take). It might need a ruling that spent fish don't count as deaths, except for the Belly.
- **The early game is thin.** The only steady early buff is Rust-Flank Hen. Spike may find Tiers 1–2 weak until the Bailiff turns up.
- **The Reunion Secretary** may be more fiddly than fun.

## 5. Tier check

Bodies: 4 / 6 / 7 / 8.5 / 12 / 14.
- **Tier 1:** Parr (5) is a decent tempo body that later becomes a Stamp seed. Grilse comes home every ~3 turns for +1/+1 across ~4 units, about 0.6 body a turn. A little over the 0.4 target, but it leaves a Fry gap, so it's fine.
- **Tier 2:** The Hen's cap of 6 stats a turn is 1 body, the top of the range on purpose: it's the tribe's main early buffer. The Cock is a pure enabler. The Guide's 2 gold every ~4 turns is modest economy.
- **Tier 3:** The Bailiff (8) adds 4–6 Stamps a turn: the enabler that names the comp. The Secretary gives about 6–8 stats a turn, about 1 body (target 0.8). Kype gives +8–12 Attack in a fight, about 1.1–1.7 bodies. That's at or slightly over the top in a hoard build, on purpose: Kype is the reason to hoard.
- **Tier 4:** Redd gives 10–14 stats a turn over her three runs (about 1.3–1.6 bodies). Tam gives ~12 stats a turn (1.4), plus a one-off +2/+2 to everyone when spent. Jack's 1/3 body is far under the guide on purpose: he's a weak multiplier who has to be protected and positioned. Fish-Pass is a speed enabler.
- **Tier 5:** The Bend-Wife gives 20–40 stats a turn (1.7–3.3 bodies; target 2, top 5). The Veteran gives ~36–48 in a fight (3–4 bodies; target 4). Kelt gives a free unit a turn, above the usual economy share. Flagged above.
- **Tier 6:** Bradán answers for ~50–100 stats every ~2 turns, plus gold (2–3.5 bodies a turn), under the target until goldens double him. Llyn Llyw gives ~90 Attack in a fight (about 6.4 bodies; target 6). The Grandfather gives ~60 stats per homecoming, with 1–2 a turn (4–8.5 bodies; top ~10).
- **Runaway:** The gift moves stats and makes none, so it can't compound. The Fry resets to base. Redd's count stops at 3, and Kelt is once a turn. No gain feeds its own source.

## 6. Design rationale

1. **The stamped pilgrim passport came first.** Stamps are a lasting counter, earned by attacking and surviving, because the seed's themes are endurance and being hunted at every weir.
2. **"The ones that reach the head spawn and are spent"** became Home N. It's checked only in the front row, so the back row reads as resting pools and the player decides when a fish goes home.
3. **Sea-nitrogen in bankside trees** became the gift. A spent fish's stats move to its neighbours rather than vanishing, which made the Run a stat pump for any tribe.
4. **"Spending yourself so the next run can start"** became the Fry, which turns back into a fresh copy. That keeps the loop and the board count without minting stats.
5. **Mother Redd's rare third run** became a counter that grows with each spawn and spares her twice. She mentions it, in +2/+2 steps.
6. **Little Jack Gravel turning up at every spawning** made a tiny repeater, the tribe's multiplier, deliberately fragile.
7. **Bradán "answers everything, then forgets"** became the capstone that fires every Spawn effect and resets instead of dying.
8. **Silver turning red and the Faroes bore** made Stamps turn into stats. This gave a reason *not* to go home, and the hold-or-spawn tension appeared.
9. **That tension became two builds.** Kype, Llyn Llyw and the Grandfather pay for hoarded Stamps, and the Bend-Wife and Bradán pay for homecomings.
10. **The Bailiff stamps passports and sometimes eats a pilgrim.** That's the Stamp engine with a positional cost, which can land on Jack Gravel.
11. **Sea-trout cousins passing as salmon** became the Passport Seller, the main way to use the Run outside its intended purpose, on other tribes.
12. **Kelts drifting back to sea and year-classes** filled the economy and buffer slots: Kelt returns a copy, and the Secretary rewards matching Stamp counts.
