# The Changelings (v1)

## 1. Identity

**The fantasy.** You are the Board of Exchanges. Your units have no faces of their own. They step into someone else's place and wear that shape, almost right. The longer a swap goes unnoticed, the more it is worth. Being found out isn't a loss: the changeling comes home to polite applause, and something was always left in return.

**New keywords**

- **Guise X:** this unit permanently gains X's printed text and tribe, on top of its own. It counts as both tribes. It never copies X's stats or X's own Guise. "This" in the copied text means the wearer. A golden X's doubled text is copied as doubled. A copied "When played" doesn't trigger, because the wearer is already on the board. A unit wears one Guise at a time, and any unit (not only Changelings) can wear one if a card gives it one.
- **Unmasked:** triggers when this unit is found out. That happens when something Unmasks it, or when it takes a new Guise (it is Unmasked first, then swaps). It keeps its Guise. Its Worn count is read, then resets to 0.

**Terms**

- **Worn:** how many Start of Turns a Guised unit has spent in its current Guise. It shows on the card.
- **Stock (token):** "carve a Stock of X" makes a Created Changeling with X's Attack and Health as they are now, and no text. It sells for 0.
- **Exchange a unit:** swap a unit you own with a unit in your shop. The shop unit comes to your hand. Yours takes its shop slot, frozen, keeping its stats, and you can buy it back for 3. If the shop is refreshed, it's gone.

**How it plays.** Early on, Changelings copy whatever is useful: a shop unit's text, your best unit's text, or a Stock with its stats. In the midgame you choose between letting Guises run (Worn climbs, and the payoffs read Worn) and cashing them in (Unmask for applause buffs, then re-Guise for another trigger). Stocks give you a second resource: bodies with no text that are free to give away, ready to wear a Guise, to be Exchanged, or to feed the Registrar's ledger. Late on, the tribe makes copies of your carry, makes triples from units you only hold one of, and ends with the Lady Under the Hill, who wears every face on your board at once. The tribe's text is mostly "copy that" and "swap this", so its value comes from what it copies. That includes other tribes' units, the shop's best and your opponent's front rank.

**Tokens and spells**

- **Stock:** Changeling token, stats as carved, no text, sells for 0. With Wicker Moll it also has the text of what it was carved from.
- **Eggshell Brew** (shop spell, 1 gold): Unmask a unit. *("I have seen the acorn before the oak, but never brewing in an eggshell.")*

## 3. Builds

**The Long Con (let Guises run).** Foundling, Hob's Ring Dancer, Keeper of the Lost Hour and the Hundred-Year Nurse. You don't Unmask for turns. Keeper doubles Worn, and the Nurse reads the longest-Worn Guise each turn. Then you cash in once with Eggshell Brew or a re-Guise from Glamourer, while Knowe Usher is out, for one huge round of applause. Grandfer works against this build, so whether to keep him is a real decision.

**Applause (Unmask churn).** Grandfer Acorn, Glamourer, Knowe Usher and Eggshell Brew. Every Unmask buffs all your Changelings, so the build wants many cheap Unmasks rather than long ones. Pairing Glamour-Veil Seamstress with Glamourer is the tribe's Johnny engine: each re-Guise Unmasks the target and also fires the copied "When played". The Lady copies Grandfer, so you get two Unmasks a turn.

**The Stock Yard (something left in return).** Wicker Prentice, Alder Carver, Clerk of Exchanges, Bettie Underhill, Exchange Assessor and Wicker Moll. Stocks go wide and soak up Bettie's buffs, because they are Changelings. The Clerk turns a free Stock into a fresh shop unit. Once Moll is down, a Stock of your carry is a half-stat copy of your carry with all its text. A Stock of the Lady is a second Lady.

**Who Is the Real One? (identity and triples).** Penny Second, Tam Twice, Glamourer and Pip Unlike. Penny Guises your carry, takes half its stats and sends the original to the shop. Buy it back and Glamourer a third unit into its Guise: with Tam Twice out, that's a golden carry from one real copy. Every triple Discovers a higher-tier unit.

**With other tribes.** Guise carries the tribe, so a Changeling counts for the other tribe's synergies and for both tribes' Harvest Supper bonuses.
- A Changeling Guised as a Bird can Fly South and come home doubled, Changeling buffs included.
- One Guised as an Ooze gets a Belly and can Eat.
- Pip Unlike flips a Hedgerow "Pass X: your rightmost other unit" into "leftmost", so the Pass lands on the other end of the line.
- Glamourer can put a Hunter's Quarry onto a Bog Mire unit, so the Mired enemy is also the one it hunts.
- You see your next opponent's last board, so you can place Fetch of the Front Rank across their best front-row unit.

Changelings are strongest in lobbies with rich unit text, and they never need the other tribe's whole package, only one good unit to copy.

## 4. Risks and holes

- **Rules load.** Copying text needs exact rules: what "this" means, copied counters, copied static effects, how Guises interact with goldens (which Guise does a golden keep?), and what happens when a Guise's source leaves the pool. The game needs a single written rule for copying.
- **Card UI.** A Guised unit shows two texts. The look-and-feel work has to make a Guise readable at a glance (the copied text shown on the borrowed coat?) or the tribe will be unreadable.
- **Cross-tribe double dips.** A Bird Guise on a heavily buffed Changeling doubles Changeling buffs. Birds may need a rule such as "Fly South doubles base stats only".
- **Tam Twice makes triples too easy.** It may need "Once a turn". Watch how many Discovers it chains in a game.
- **Bettie has no cap.** With Moll, the Prentice and the Assessor she may fire 4+ times a turn. If so, make it "Three times a turn".
- **Copies of copies.** A Stock of the Lady under Moll makes a second Lady: everything ×3, or ×6 golden. That's within the ×2–4 golden ceiling only if it stays rare. Golden Moll makes a full copy of your carry every turn, bounded only by the 7 slots.
- **Exchange feels bad** when you refresh and lose a unit you sent to the shop. That's intended, as the bargain that looks fair, but it may read as a trap.
- **Tier 2 has no true buffer.** Grandfer's +1/+1 and Foundling's Unmask payoff stand in for one, so the early game may be weak.
- **Fetch is swingy.** It's only as good as the enemy across.

## 5. Tier check

- **Tier 1:** Foundling is the seed, worth about 2 stats a turn of Worn (about 0.5 body, target 0.4). Wicker Prentice is tempo at about 6.5 stats once (about 1.6 bodies, target about 1). I broke this on purpose, because Stocks are the canvas the tribe builds on. If it plays too strong, carve at half stats.
- **Tier 2:** two enablers and one economy unit. Grandfer gives about 0.3 body a turn plus a trigger. The Clerk is worth about one free unit. There's no pure buffer (see Risks).
- **Tier 3:** two enablers, one buffer and one combat engine, as the guide shapes it. The Carver gives about 7–10 stats once (1–1.4 bodies). The Dancer gives about 4 a turn (about 0.6 body). Fetch varies.
- **Tier 4:** two buffers at about 10 stats a turn each (about 1.2 bodies, target 1.3), one enabler (Seamstress) and one multiplier (Keeper, ×2 Worn).
- **Tier 5:** Tam Twice is the direction card (triples economy). The Assessor makes about 40 stats in a fight (about 3.3 bodies, target 4). Penny is tempo at about half your carry plus a unit (about 2.5 bodies).
- **Tier 6:** two capstones. The Lady roughly doubles your board's text. Moll adds about 3 bodies a turn plus text. The Nurse is the payoff, at about 5 bodies a turn with 6 Changelings at Worn 6, and capped at +8/+8 to keep it under the top of about 10 bodies.

## 6. Design rationale

1. I started from "no face of its own". The tribe's core verb had to be a copy, which became Guise.
2. Guise copies text and tribe, never stats. "Almost right" is the soul without the body, and copying the tribe opens up cross-tribe Johnny play.
3. "The swap noticed too late" became the Worn counter, so time itself is a resource.
4. Being unmasked is "only an embarrassment", so Unmasked is a reward, not a loss. The Guise stays, and only Worn resets. That set up the choice between running and cashing.
5. Re-Guising Unmasks. This joins the copy verb to the reward verb, which makes loops possible.
6. "Something was always left in return" became the Stock: the body without the soul, the mirror of Guise. Together they make a whole copy.
7. "Board of Exchanges" and "whatever they're traded for" became Exchange, which turns spare Stocks into shop units.
8. I gave each named figure their folklore role: Grandfer gives himself away (he Unmasks), Pip copies mirror-backwards, Moll's Stocks sigh (they get text), and Penny's original is turned away (sent to the shop).
9. "Who is the real one?" became a triples rule (Tam Twice): a copy counts as the real thing.
10. "One night under the hill is a hundred years" became the Worn multiplier (Keeper) and the Worn payoff (the Nurse).
11. The Lady "may be any of them", so the capstone wears every face, with a no-copies-of-her rule to stop loops.
12. Last, I set numbers against the tier guide. I kept the tension between Grandfer and the Nurse on purpose, so builds have to choose.
