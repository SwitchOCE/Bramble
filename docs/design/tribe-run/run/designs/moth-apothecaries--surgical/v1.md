# The Moth Apothecaries

## 1. Identity

**Core mechanic: one shared Recipe, poured out as Phials.**

- **Phial** (the tribe's spell token): a Created spell that costs 0 and goes to your hand. Its text is "Cast your Recipe on a unit." If your hand is full, the Phial is lost.
- **Recipe:** what every Phial does, and the same for all of them. It starts as one line, **+1/+1**. **Add a line** puts one more effect on it for the rest of the game. It holds at most **5 lines**. Once it is full, a new line replaces one of your choice. Stat lines add up, and they follow the usual spell scaling (each printed +1 is 10% of the target's stat, never less than printed). A keyword line gives that keyword permanently. The Recipe is also used in fights: some units cast it there, and a cast in a fight lasts only for that fight.
- **Drawn** (new keyword): after a spell hits a unit next to this, this gains that spell's stats too, scaled to this unit. It gains at most once from each spell. It copies stats only, never keywords.

Moths cast more spells than anyone, and their spells get stronger as the guild grows. Every apprentice you play adds a line to the shared Recipe, so a Phial you pour on turn 12 might be +2/+2, Hunker, Drawn and "Start of Combat: give the enemy across Nightshade", on any unit you like, of any tribe. Each turn you make two choices. First, where the Phials go: pile them onto one lamp-unit and its Drawn moths, or spread their keywords over the board, since a keyword is wasted on a unit that already has it. Second, when to cast: casting now grows the board, while holding a Phial in hand earns gold (The Uncertain) at the cost of a hand slot. The Recipe also feeds the fight. Psyche pours it out when she dies, the Herald pours it over the whole board, and Mother Shipton turns it into a curse on the enemy's biggest unit. Because Phials go on anything, Moths are the tribe that makes another tribe's best unit better.

**Tokens and spells:** only the **Phial** (above). Nothing else is added.

**Rulings that matter:**
- A spell that hits several units, such as one splashed by the Warden, counts as one cast. Drawn checks each hit, but gains at most once for each spell.
- Lines the Herald gives at Start of Combat do fire if their unit's own Start of Combat hasn't resolved yet. Start of Combat goes in reading order, so a Herald in F1 makes every Burnet line behind it fire, and a Herald in B3 makes none of them fire.
- A golden copy's Add a line keeps the line it adds. A Primrose Apprentice played twice and then golden puts +1/+1, +1/+1 and +2/+2 lines on the Recipe.

## 3. Builds

**The Lamp (concentration).** Pick one unit to be the lamp: Old Carbonaria, the Warden, or another tribe's carry. Pour every Phial on it and put Drawn units on the slots next to it. The lamp gains the Recipe, and each Drawn neighbour gains the Recipe's stats from the same Phial. The core is the Warden in F2 with Drawn units in B1 and B2: one Phial hits F1, F2 and F3, and each Drawn unit behind them gains once. Pavonia doubles all of it. Stat lines matter most here (Primrose Apprentice, Courier's +2 Attack). Good when your next opponent's board is wide but small. The weak point is Mother Shipton or Bog's Bitter aimed at your lamp.

**The Dispensary (wide riders).** Fill the Recipe with keywords instead: Hunker, Bristle 2, Swoop, Burnet's Nightshade line. Spread each Phial to a different unit until the whole board carries the Recipe, then add the Honeysuckle Nurse and Mortar Grinder to grow your moths each cast. The Herald in F1 is the capstone: every fight, everyone gets the Recipe again and every Nightshade line fires. Good against single carries, because Nightshade halves a big unit's Health and Bristle punishes Cleave.

**The Pharmacy (bridge to another tribe).** Two or three Moths make Phials (Peddler, Show-Globe Keeper, Treacle Painter), and the rest of the board belongs to another tribe. The Alchymist is the high roll: he copies that tribe's keywords into the Recipe, so your Phials hand out Mire, Bitter, Pack or Cleave to any unit. The Courier's Drawn line lets non-Moths join a Lamp too.

**The Night Market (economy into tempo).** Peddler, The Uncertain, Show-Globe Keeper and Atropos. Hold a Phial over the turn for gold, buy the shop's spell twice a turn, and turn each purchase into a Phial through the Treacle Painter. Tier up fast, then become a Lamp or a Dispensary.

**Mixing in a lobby:**
- **Fairies:** a Phial is an aimed spell, so it buffs Shy units. Each Phial on a Shy Fairy is worth 2 gold next turn, and The Uncertain stacks on top of that. Put the Warden on a Ring slot.
- **Birds:** pour Phials on a Bird, then sell it. It comes home with doubled stats, Phial gains included. An Egg next to the lamp can be Drawn through the Courier's line.
- **Oozes:** Phial a unit before an Ooze Eats it, so the Recipe's stats go into the Ooze. An Ooze is also a good lamp, since what it eats keeps the Phials' gains.
- **Hedgerow:** each Acorn used is an item, so it is a Phial from the Treacle Painter. Pass X goes to your rightmost unit, so make that your lamp.
- **Bog folk:** the Alchymist can copy Bitter or Mire. Mother Shipton plus a Bitter wall shuts down enemy carries.
- **Hunters:** the Alchymist can copy Pack, and Phials make one Quarry-hunter huge.

Because Moths take the shop's spell more often, they compete with any other spell-hungry player in the lobby, Fairies most of all.

## 4. Risks and holes

1. **Compounding stats.** Stat lines scale at 10% of the target per point, so a +4/+4 Recipe cast twice by Pavonia, three times a turn, multiplies a carry about ×2 each turn. This needs the runaway check. Dials, in order: cap the Recipe's stat lines at +3/+3 in total; let Pavonia's extra cast carry keywords only; take the 5-line cap down to 4.
2. **Drawn plus the Warden plus the Courier's Drawn line** spreads a single Phial to five or six units. The "once for each spell" rule holds it in check, but it needs a simulated board.
3. **Hand pressure.** With a hand of 4, Phials crowd out bought units. Losing a Phial to a full hand will feel bad, and The Uncertain makes you want to hold Phials.
4. **The Recipe must be on screen.** It is a list of up to 5 lines that changes all game. Without a clear, always-visible Recipe card, the tribe can't be read. This is a look-and-feel task.
5. **The Alchymist's copied keywords** each need a ruling as a line: Belly with nothing to hold, Snap without Mire, Pack on a lone unit. Shy is excluded because a Shy line turns every Phial into 2 gold for a unit you throw away (an Egg, say). It is worth testing as a "fun broken" option, but not as the default.
6. **Mother Shipton is dead with a keywords-only Recipe**, and she is unpleasant for the opponent when your stat lines are big. Players may find her a feel-bad anti-carry card.
7. **Mono Moth bodies are small** (median 6.5 at T3), so early fights rest on Phials landing well. If Moths lose turns 2 to 5, add +1 Health to the T1–T2 units.
8. **The shop's spell price isn't settled.** Atropos and the Treacle Painter assume it is around 1–3 gold. At 0 gold, the Painter is far too strong.

## 5. Tier check

- **Tier 1:** the Peddler's one-off Phial is about 1 body once; the Noctuid is a 2/3 body that grows about 0.5 body a turn once Phials flow. Both fit.
- **Tier 2:** Treacle Painter and The Uncertain fit (about 1 Phial or 1 gold a turn). **Primrose Apprentice breaks it on purpose.** Its line adds +1/+1 to every later Phial, far more than 1.3 bodies once. It is the tribe's "spells grow" piece, like a tribe bonus, and it is held back by Phial supply and the 5-line cap.
- **Tier 3:** the Show-Globe Keeper (1 Phial a turn) and Mortar Grinder (+3/+3 at 3 spells, about 0.85 body) fit. Cocoon Spinner adds a lasting keyword line, which is on the high side of "once". Psyche's death cast is about 1–1.5 bodies in a fight.
- **Tier 4:** Nurse (about 12 stats a turn, 1.4 bodies) and Atropos (about 1 gold plus a spell) fit. **The Warden is a deliberate multiplier:** 3 recipients per spell, the "same job, three times" step up from the T3 Phial maker. The Courier's Drawn line is the bridge's key and is priced like the T3 line card.
- **Tier 5:** Carbonaria comes in at about 2.5 bodies once (15–20 spells cast) and grows slowly, a stat holder that wants to be the lamp. Burnet and Shipton are about 3–4 bodies in a fight against carries, and less against wide boards.
- **Tier 6:** Pavonia, the Brann-style double, is about 5 bodies a turn in a Phial build, inside the "top ~10". The Herald's Recipe on six units is about 5–6 bodies in a fight. The Alchymist is a capstone of reach rather than stats; his value is what he unlocks.
- **Goldens:** a golden Pavonia (three casts) or a golden Warden is the high roll worth chasing. A golden Primrose Apprentice plus Pavonia is the runaway risk in section 4.

## 6. Design rationale

1. **Gap:** no tribe makes spells. The shop shows one spell, Fairies only take them in, and neutrals only "help" spells. A spell engine is the clearest hole in the game.
2. **Gap:** every tribe's engine feeds itself. Spells hit any unit, and spell scaling rewards a big target, so a tribe whose payoff can be *someone else's* carry is the missing bridge.
3. **Gap:** few recruit-phase sequencing puzzles: which target, cast or hold, which neighbour. That suits Johnny.
4. One shared Recipe instead of many different spells: recipes that build on one another, and one place where every Moth card meets every other.
5. Lines come from playing apprentices, not from each cast. Cast-count scaling plus 10% spell scaling would compound out of control, so counts feed units (Grinder, Carbonaria) instead.
6. The 5-line cap with replacement turns the late game into choosing lines, and keeps stat lines in check.
7. Phials go to the hand. The hand of 4 limits supply for free and creates the cast-or-hold choice, which The Uncertain prices.
8. Drawn is the positional "more recipients" lever, so Phial counts can stay small. The Warden and the Courier's Drawn line let non-Moths join in.
9. The Recipe is used in fights too (Psyche, Herald, Shipton), so line choices matter outside the shop as well, for defence and anti-carry.
10. Shape kept at 2/3/4/4/3/3. T4 holds a multiplier (Warden) because the tribe's growth is recipients, not numbers.
11. The Alchymist steals keywords from any tribe, for "ways of using them outside their intended purpose". Shy is excluded only because it turns Phials straight into gold.
12. Names and flavour came last: the Uncertain holds the dose, Atropos robs the shop, Carbonaria keeps the ledger, Shipton casts the omen.
