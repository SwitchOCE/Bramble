# The Dyke Board — v1

## 1. Identity

**Level.** Each player has a Level, a permanent number shown on a gauge staff beside the board. It starts at 0 and can go below 0.

**Vote:** Raise or Lower your Level by 1, your choice. Raising and Lowering are both "the Level changing".

**The water.** Before any Start of Combat effect, the water rises on **both boards** to your Level plus your opponent's Level, never below 0. After that, "Raise the water by N" effects add N for this fight only.

**Under:** a unit whose Health is at or below the water. It attacks with half its Attack. Units sink and come back up as their Health changes during the fight. A unit "goes Under" each time it sinks: when the water rises, when it is damaged, or when it is summoned into the water.

**Dive:** while Under, this keeps its full Attack, and enemies attack it only if they have no other target.

Cards that read "your Level" treat a Level below 0 as 0.

**How it plays.** Every turn the Board votes, and you can see your next opponent's last board when you do. Against glass cannons, Eggs and token swarms, you Raise: their small units sink and attack at half strength, while your beavers are built tall and stay above the water. Against tall walls, the water does little, so you Lower: drained land pays gold and stats, and a Level below 0 even drains away a rival Board's flood. Mills pay whichever way you vote, so a Board that keeps changing its mind grows too. The water treats both sides the same, so the puzzle is your own board's shape: who stays above, who dives on purpose, and who you can afford to let drown.

## 3. Builds

**High Water (Raise).** Pup and Sluiswacht Raise from the start. Stretch-Holder goes in the back row, with the units it lifts beside it, so its neighbours stay above any water you set. Ditch Coot and Rafting Coot sink on purpose, Diving and keeping their full Attack while the enemy's front line halves. Board Almoner tops up anything left below your Level. The finish is Gauge-Keeper doubling the water and Tiddy Mun drowning everything at half of it. The weak point is Stretch-Holder: if it dies, its neighbours sink.

**Drain (Lower).** You vote Lower every turn and let the Level go below 0. Sluiswacht and Penningmeester pay gold, Smallholder and Sail-Setter pay stats, and Master of the Nine Mills turns every Lower into stats for the whole board. You fight in no water at all, so this build can carry any other tribe, and with roll gold it chases triples. Keeper of the Overflow is the hinge: it turns your Lowers into fight-only water, so a draining Board can still flood the fight without pushing its Level up.

**The Seesaw (vote to the matchup).** Check the next opponent and vote toward them: Raise against Hunters and Birds, Lower against Bog walls. Bram Stuw echoes the first change each turn, Hendrika doubles every Vote, and Sail-Setter and Master of the Nine Mills pay for each change in either direction. A Level that swings from +4 to −2 and back is pure income for the mills.

**Splashes in a lobby.**
- **Bog folk:** Mire walls are tall and stay above the water while the units attacking them sink. Their Bitter halves Attack on top of the water's halving.
- **Oozes:** Eat makes units fat enough to stand above the water. Enemy Belly releases arrive small and sink at once, which triggers Rafts and gets them drowned.
- **Fairies:** Rings' +4/+4 lifts units over the line.
- **Hedgerow:** Pass feeds Health to your rightmost unit.
- **Hunters and Birds:** glass cannons and Eggs drown, yours included. Pair those with the Drain build, or keep them beside a Stretch-Holder.
- **Cross-purpose tricks:**
  - Raise the water to sink your own coots so they Dive.
  - Keep a cheap support unit below your Level so Almoner feeds it every turn.
  - Go below 0 to blunt a rival Board's flood.
  - Give an enemy Nightshade unit a reason to sink: it loses half its Health the moment it is attacked, and that triggers your Rafts.

## 4. Risks and holes

- **Readability.** Both boards change, and every Health change can flip a unit Under. This needs a clear waterline drawn across both boards, with sunk units visibly submerged. Otherwise the fights feel random.
- **Vote prompts.** With four or more Votes a turn, asking every time is tedious. It may need a standing "Raise or Lower this turn" switch that you set once.
- **Water runaway.** Gauge-Keeper and Tiddy Mun plus a big Level or Overflow could drown a whole board at Start of Combat. Drowning at *half* the water is the brake. A further cap ("the water is at most twice your Level plus 10") may be needed after sims.
- **Overflow grows without limit.** It only counts Lowers, so it grows slowly, but a long game could make it huge. Possible fix: it keeps only the last three turns' Lowers.
- **Drain economy.** Sluiswacht, Penningmeester and Hendrika together could pay six or more gold a turn. Gold carries over without a cap, so watch this for triple-chaining.
- **Cliff at the threshold.** At Health equal to the water a unit halves its Attack; one more Health and it doesn't. Small buffs swing fights, which is good for Johnny but noisy for balance.
- **Pricing the Level.** One Level has no fixed stat value. It is worth a lot against tokens and nothing against walls, so the tier numbers below are estimates.
- **Mirror rules.** Two Boards add their Levels. A Drain player's negative Level partly cancels a Flood player's Level. That is intended, but confusing to read.
- **Edge rules.**
  - If every attackable unit Dives, attacks go ahead as normal.
  - If neither side has a unit that can attack, the fight is a tie.
  - Tiddy Mun drowns only enemies.

## 5. Tier check

- **Tier 1:** bodies of 4, on target. Kees grows +2 stats a turn and Pup +1–3 Health, against a target of ~1.6. Kees is a little high, held back by his once-a-fight limit.
- **Tier 2:** bodies of 5–6. Schouw-Walker gives 3 stats a turn (target 2–6). Sluiswacht is a gold-or-Raise choice worth up to 1 gold a turn. Ditch Coot gives 1 point of water; it has no stat price, so it is a low-body enabler.
- **Tier 3:**
  - Bodies 6–7.
  - Kobus gives 4–8 stats a turn and Sail-Setter up to 8 (target ~5.6), both slightly high.
  - Stretch-Holder gives 2×Level Health in the fight. That is ~12 mid-game (target 5–10) and more later.
  - Rafting Coot gives ~8 Attack in a fight (target 5–10).
  - I broke the guide on purpose with Stretch-Holder: it is the tribe's whole defence, and it scales with what the player has put into the Level.
- **Tier 4:** bodies 8–9. Almoner (~12 a turn) and Smallholder (12) match the target of ~11. Bram is a multiplier that adds one Vote a turn. Overflow is priced like a slow Raise.
- **Tier 5:**
  - Winter Raft gives ~56 in a fight, typical, and 84 at most, against a target of ~48. That is the T3 Raft's job spread over seven units instead of one.
  - Penningmeester gives 3 gold, about a unit a turn.
  - Gauge-Keeper doubles your share of the water: a multiplier that also hurts your own short units.
- **Tier 6:**
  - Master of the Nine Mills gives ~56 a turn at four changes (target ~56) and 112 with Hendrika; the nine-a-turn cap keeps the golden at ×4.
  - Hendrika doubles the Votes.
  - Tiddy Mun's value depends on the enemy board. It is high against wide boards and is the tribe's deliberate outlier, flagged under Risks.

## 6. Design rationale

1. **Gap:** no tribe acts on both boards at once. Bog folk and Hunters touch only the unit across.
2. **Gap:** seeing your next opponent's board is a system no tribe uses. A per-turn up-or-down choice makes scouting a decision.
3. **Gap:** nothing cares about a unit's shape (Health against Attack). A Health waterline does, and it gives Bog's walls and Oozes' fat units a partner while punishing glass cannons and tokens.
4. Under halves Attack and doesn't kill: it holds the line without wiping boards, and drowning is kept for one capstone.
5. The Level can go below 0 and the water adds both Levels, so Lowering is a real choice and the mirror has a counter.
6. Votes are always 1 point each, so every payoff counts changes, and goldens and Hendrika multiply the count instead of the size.
7. Three payoff families (Level read, Lower paid, any change paid) give three builds that share their Vote sources.
8. Dive was added so the tribe's own small coots want the water. That is the main use against the rule's intent.
9. Stretch-Holder and Almoner were placed at T3–T4 to make flooding one-sided only if you build for it.
10. Keeper of the Overflow and the Motion Carried spell were added as bridges: Drain to Flood inside the tribe, and any tribe to the Level.
11. The tier shape follows the guide (T5: multiplier, combat engine, direction). T6 has one capstone for each build plus the shared multiplier.
12. Names and flavour came last: beavers hold, build and pay; coots kick, dive and object.
