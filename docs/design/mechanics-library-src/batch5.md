### 75. (working name: Venom) — NEW, the owner's idea
- **The owner's words.** A debuff "that goes on creatures. Whenever they're attacked, they lose half their HP, their current HP, rounded down. So it helps wide boards deal with really, really big boards. It would have to be a powerful effect that goes on high-tier stuff."
- **Group.** H. Kills, overkill and survival (or another group if it fits better).
- **From.** owner, 2 Nov (02k).

Design notes for this batch:

- Decide, and say why: when the debuff is applied (Start of Combat, on hit, by a spell in the Recruit Phase?), whether it lasts the combat or longer, whether the loss comes before or after the attack's damage, whether it stacks, and what it does to a 1-Health unit.
- Show how it lets a wide board beat one huge unit, with numbers, turn by turn.
