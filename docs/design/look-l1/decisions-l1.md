# The owner's decisions on the L1 look

*The owner's decisions L1 to L51 on the buy screen and card built in the L1 look, each with the owner's words, and the earlier rows they and the [build spec](buy-screen-l1.md) cite. Copied without rewording from `docs/workstreams/ui-ideation/owner-decisions.md` on the branch `ideation/ui-ux` (commit `e9fe2886`).*

Paths in the rows: `looks/...` and `briefs/...` are under `docs/workstreams/ui-ideation/` on `ideation/ui-ux`; `/data/...` is on the Optiplex. Neither is in this repo's main line.

## 24 Nov 2026 on: the look

The owner rejected every card frame from the frame pass ("All of these frames feel bad") and asked for whole looks to react to (`briefs/30-look-pass.md` holds the words). After seeing three (`looks/`), the owner chose L1, "the card is a window" (`looks/L1/look.md`):
