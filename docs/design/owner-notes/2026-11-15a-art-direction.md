# Owner direction: the art direction is "flat figure"

Said by the owner in chat on 14 and 15 November 2026. The owner asked for it to be documented. This replaces the 1 November 2026 art style choice ("Ink + flat B", `../sources/2026-11-01-art-style-decision.md`), which no longer holds.

*[Editor note: these are excerpts as they were passed on from the chat session, not the full dictation. "..." marks words left out.]*

## The decision

On the six flat-figure images (hedgehog, owl, toad, fairy duellist, fox, ooze):

"that stuff is great. That is perfect."

"Okay, that's our new art direction."

"I want this documented because that style is what I want to go with."

## More in the same style

"So those 6 are great, 12 more to go with."

"Give me 12 more with the same style of art and everything. You need to run on the Optiplex though ... with codecs *[probably "Codex"]* on the Optiplex as well."

## How the owner got there

"let's just completely try and do a similar art style to Ori and the Blind Forest, but remove detail ... less color gradients, more like just easier to execute for human artists ... Not as much detail ... Less intricate work."

On flat figure: "the flat figure stuff's great, but they don't need to limit the color palette as much."

## Biomes

"I want the art to feel like different tribes should have different biomes that they inhabit, right? That are a natural fit for them."

## What was wrong with the earlier card art

"far too big and zoomed in"

"we've gone far too cartoony and Disney-ish"

"these are fighters. They're supposed to be warriors"

"Not enough ... actual arms and legs"
