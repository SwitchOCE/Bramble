# Owner decision: art style

Made by the owner on 1 November 2026, choosing from the style trials in the Woodland Folio (`../../folio/woodland-folio.html#trials`).

## Choice

The owner's answer: "Ink + flat B".

*[Editor notes:*
- *Picked from four options Claude offered: ink + flat B, comic ink B, gouache B and gouache C.*
- *Take B is "Bold and clean": thick brush-pen silhouette, no hatching, one cel shadow per colour, chunky proportions.*
- *Claude's reasons, given with the option (not the owner's): it read clearest at token size (about 80px), and it scored among the cheapest styles for a human artist to match and animate.*
- *Risk noted with the option: it can look generic, so the woodland character has to come from costume, props and biome palettes.*
- *Prompts for this take are in `tools/art/prompts/trials-ink-b.txt`.]*
