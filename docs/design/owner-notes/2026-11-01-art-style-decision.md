# Owner decision: art style

Made by the owner on 1 November 2026, choosing from the style trials in the Woodland Folio (`../../folio/woodland-folio.html#trials`).

## Choice

**Ink + flat color, take B: "Bold and clean".** Thick brush-pen silhouette, no hatching, one cel shadow per colour, chunky proportions.

The owner picked it from four options: ink + flat B, comic ink B, gouache B and gouache C.

*[Editor notes:*
- *Reasons given with the option: it reads best at token size (about 80px), and it is the cheapest style for a human artist to match and animate.*
- *Risk noted with the option: it can look generic, so the woodland character has to come from costume, props and biome palettes.*
- *Prompts for this take are in `tools/art/prompts/trials-ink-b.txt`.]*
