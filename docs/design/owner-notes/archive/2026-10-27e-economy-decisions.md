# Owner decisions: economy

Made by the owner on 27 October 2026, answering the economy questions (section 2) of `../review-queue.md`. The owner chose from offered options.

| # | Question | Owner's choice |
|---|---|---|
| 8 | What do units drawn from the shared pool by an effect (Discover, Triple Reward) sell for? | **0 gold, like created units.** Anything the player didn't pay for sells for 0. |
| 9 | Refresh cost thresholds within a turn | **Refreshes 1–10 cost 1, 11–20 cost 2, 21+ cost 3.** All values stay tunable. |
| 10 | Do free refreshes from card effects count toward the thresholds? | **No.** Free refreshes stay fully free, and only paid refreshes escalate. |
| 11 | How to price economy and scaling text | **Stricter than Battlegrounds.** Economy and scaling text are priced in the power budget, as combat text is. |

*[Editor notes:*
- *Answer 8 changes the meaning of "Created". It now means "not paid for", not just "made from nothing". A Discovered unit is still a real pool copy and still returns to the pool when sold; it just sells for 0. A triple still sells for 1 gold (rulebook 4.3.4).*
- *Answer 10 means nothing in the refresh rules stops a chain of free refreshes. That limit now has to come from card costs and from an engine safety cap. The rulebook proposes one (4.4.8), following Galaxy's cap of 30 bonus shops per turn.]*
