# Owner idea: a venom debuff that halves current Health

Said by the owner in chat on 2 November 2026, as a sidebar while reading the mechanics library. The owner asked for it to be recorded, fleshed out by agents, and added to the next version of the library: "It's one of mine."

Here's an effect I thought of that we can add to the library. I like the idea of something like venom, or I'm not sure what to call it: venom, poison, some sort of debuff, whatever it might be, that goes on creatures. Whenever they're attacked, they lose half their HP, their current HP, rounded down. So it helps wide boards deal with really, really big boards. It would have to be a powerful effect that goes on high-tier stuff.

I'm still working through the document. I'll come back with more feedback later. This is just a sidebar I wanted to capture.

*[Editor notes:*
- *Read as: the debuff is placed on enemy units; each time a debuffed unit is attacked, it loses half its current Health, rounded down, on top of the attack's damage. Whether the loss comes before or after the attack's damage is not stated.*
- *Not to be confused with Battlegrounds' Venomous, which destroys the first unit it damages. The library's rules review lists that as an example mechanic (rulebook 11.2.5).]*
