# Card and mechanic text is too wordy (8 Nov 2026)

The owner's words after playing ideation build 6. Relayed verbatim by another Claude session ("Project gaps and parallel work opportunities") to the ideation orchestrator.

Okay, I played the build, and what I can say immediately is that our descriptions of mechanics are terrible. Absolutely awful. Far too long, far too wordy. I want agents to carefully inspect how mechanics are described in other games. Look at Hearthstone Battlegrounds. Look at all the mechanics and how they're worded, how simply they're explained in their wording, how keywords are used to reduce the length of paragraphs, etc. We are spending far too much text on screen on explaining stuff. There needs to be... I'm sure that all the effects are fine, but a lot of the explanations of them are incredibly convoluted and need to be simplified greatly. This is an absolute must happen before anything more occurs. I'll test the build after that additional iteration, but contact whichever session is responsible for that build. Is that an ideation build? I'm not sure. If it is, then that needs to be the focus of it.

## The principle for the wording pass (8 Nov, same relay)

Clear and concise wording. Make complicated things simple. That is the goal of explanations. Not to be overly specific, not to handle every use case. You do not need to explain how something that's a little bit marginal or implied by a mechanic is going to work. If it's implied, let it be implied and the player can find out in additional details some mechanism elsewhere.
