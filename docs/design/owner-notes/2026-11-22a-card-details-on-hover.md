# Owner rule: card details show on hover; keyword reminders sit in a fixed place

Said in chat on 22 November 2026, after looking at the three concepts from the UI ideation council (`ideation/ui-ux`). In that council's fix rounds the concepts had gained name plates, cue lines and rules text beside cards, and "Front row" / "Back row" labels, and their layouts had been changed to make room for them.

> We've got, I think, tooltips for cards should be handled as on hover states, right? All this, the direction that Sol continually gave in a lot of these committees, I think, was pretty poor in terms of having all these tooltips on screen, especially having the front and back row labeled, right? The game needs to not be visually cluttered and telling experienced players what cards they recognize do is not what we want. And I've given the steering in the past, and I will continue to give it in future.

> I do not like a lot of the changes that were made to accommodate the spacing of the tooltips, and I still want to reiterate this: that I feel like the tooltips should be hover states, like modals or toasts or whatever they're called. And keyword reminders should not be anchored or rendered in the same way as the tooltip that follows the cursor. They should go in some consistent part of the screen. Either they're at the top or the side or out of the way, essentially, like the keyword stuff, because *[probably "it's"]* an option that players can turn off in the settings, and even if they don't, it sits in some part of the screen the player can move their eyes towards that isn't the active state. So they're available but not obtrusive.

*[Editor note: dictated. "Seoul" was heard for "Sol", the GPT model on the council. In short: what a card does is shown when the player hovers it, not written on or beside the card at rest, and the board rows carry no labels. Keyword reminders are a separate thing from that tooltip: they appear in one consistent, out-of-the-way part of the screen, do not follow the pointer, and a setting turns them off.]*
