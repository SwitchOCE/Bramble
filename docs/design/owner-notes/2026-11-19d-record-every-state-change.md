# Owner rule: fight logs always on, and every state change recorded

Said in chat on 19 November 2026. The question was whether the new per-fight logs (kills and summons, PR 166) should stay on in every league match at about 10% extra cost, or only in balance measurement.

> Fight logs should always be on, and we may even need to enhance them so we can measure things even better. I'm not sure what the limitations of the current data structures are in terms of their analytics. But basically, every state change needs to be recorded

*[Editor's note: "fight logs" is the owner's term for the league's game and fight logs. The rule covers the Rust league harness and its analysis. It also fits the ghost format, which records a per-unit buff ledger (docs/workstreams/third-run/ghost-patches-long-term.md).]*
