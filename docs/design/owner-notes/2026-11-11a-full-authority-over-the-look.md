# Full authority over the look (11 Nov 2026)

The owner's words, sent as a message in the ideation orchestrator session during loop 1 of the look-and-feel phase, after seeing the first Bazaar Stack screens in the game. Recorded verbatim.

You have complete authority to design the look and feel of the cards and the in-game assets, even using different art if you choose. I want to see this brought to its conclusion. You can change anything about appearance that you want. Any visual asset you choose to change, go for it. I want to see where you end up with this.

*[Editor note: on `ideation/*` this lets the orchestrator change the units' art style, which `2026-11-01-art-style-decision.md` set as "Ink + flat B". `main` is unaffected: nothing from ideation reaches main without the owner.]*
