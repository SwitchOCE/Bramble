# Owner direction: bounding is the fallback; start tribes and archetypes

Said by the owner in chat on 2 November 2026, after the Stage 1 status update.

## Bounding

I think as long as we have rising reroll costs and no way of achieving infinite free rerolls, all turns should eventually be bounded. That's our fallback. How long and how ambitious our economy effects go, we can determine later. We don't need to worry about it for now. We can power budget it later.

I would expect that 30 gold is a lot for a starting amount of gold, especially if we're adopting a similar model to Battlegrounds where we start on 3 and go up by 1 per turn.

*[Editor notes:*
- *The economy-bounding work tested long turns from a bank of 30 gold. Under rulebook 4.1.1 income is 3 on turn 1, rising by 1 a turn to 10. A 30-gold bank is reachable only by saving across turns, which carry-over gold allows (rulebook 4.1.2, the owner's decision of 27 October: unspent gold carries over, with no cap). The economy model's tier-6 player spends 10–14 gold a turn and banks almost nothing.*
- *Read with Stage 1: the open questions on whether "about 20–30 shops" is a floor or a ceiling, how long a Recruit Phase may run, and the exact price tail are deferred to later power budgeting. The bounding rule that stands is rising paid refresh costs (rulebook 4.4) plus no repeatable free-refresh source (content-guardrails.md G1).]*
