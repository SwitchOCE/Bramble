## Packing *[question 3]*

I think units should pack, same as in Battlegrounds.

## Selling created units after a triple *[question 4]*

Any created unit that is tripled: basically, any time it merges with a real unit, it now sells for money. Or if it's tripled, I think it should sell for money as well. We can expand on the token rules later, and perhaps change that if we run into issues with it.

*[Reading: a tripled unit sells for gold whether its copies were bought or created.]*

## Rows dictate order *[question 2]*

Yes, exactly. The rows just dictate the order. Unit behavior is: attack once, then let the next unit auto-attack. The rows just dictate the tiebreaker, if you will.

*[Reading: rows only set attack order and default targeting. If the front row is empty, the back row is simply what's left, and attack order carries on through the units that remain.]*

## Row caps, and whether units pack *[question 1]*

Open question on the rows. My initial idea was: let's just go with a cap of 4 at the front and 3 at the back, and the player can position freely within that cap, and units pack. I.e. you could go 4–1, or 3–2, or 1–3, but units would always pack.

I'm not sure. Maybe we need to have slots and they don't pack. Propose some ideas and problems with it. Galaxy's paradigm, I guess, does allow you to not have units pack, and maybe that's the way. Maybe we just go with the 4–3 and units don't pack. I'm not sure; I'm unsure on this. Give me some pros and cons.
