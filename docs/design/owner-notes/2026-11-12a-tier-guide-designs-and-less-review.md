# Designers get the tier guide; less review, more designs to the owner (12 Nov 2026)

The owner's words in the admin query session, after reading the Ogre tier-guide test (`docs/design/tier-guide-test/`). In that test, five Ogre tribes were redesigned with the tier guide in the brief. Blind judges preferred the old designs on power for place in 3 of 5 routes, and the new ones on distinctiveness in 4 of 5. Transcribed: filler and repeats removed, punctuation added. *[Editor notes in italic brackets.]*

**Replaces:** the rule that designers are briefed without the power budget (2 Nov, `02f`, archived; the "Budgets and taxonomy are guidelines" bullet in `CLAUDE.md`).

## On the Ogre designs

I like these designs better. They're more creative. They feel more powerful. I can balance them afterwards. I think this is actually better and I prefer these designs. I don't want to change it. Even if it ships a bit overpowered, these tribes are cooler. They feel more interesting than the previous ones. So no, I don't want these changed. I want this skill shipped. *[Probably the process that made these designs; see the next section.]* This is a really good skill. And can we make sure it combines with the previous skill that made this? Because this option, like the next run, I like these a lot.

## On the skill and the next run

I don't want it folded into the skill. I want it folded into the next run. I wanted them to use the skill, and I want the process that produced these results in our next run. Obviously the skill is independent; it's a generic skill. It's not supposed to be global *[probably "specific"]*, but this guide is about our designs.

## On the rule and on reviews

That previous rule was designed to get better results. Whatever produced those designs *[the new, tier-guide designs]* that you said weren't as good as the ones before, I looked at them and I liked them more. The reviewers were wrong about them. So I want more unreviewed stuff coming to me, because those designs slapped. I liked all the newer ones. They were really pretty good, and I felt they were way more ready. I think we're way too worried about balance, and way too worried about reviews, and we're probably stopping good content getting in. So I think I need to have less long review cycles. The previous rule says no power budgets, and whatever gave me those results I want to be the new standard. So we replace the previous rule, because I have changed my mind about what is producing good results.

*[Scope: this is about design content, meaning tribes, cards and mechanics. Code steps keep their test gate and scope review unless the owner says otherwise.]*
