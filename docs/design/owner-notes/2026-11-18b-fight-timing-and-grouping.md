# Owner rule: fight timing may change; group repeated animations

Said by the owner in chat on 18 November 2026, answering the night run's question of whether attacks may take more time than the old 0.22 s step.

> Yeah, the attack timings can change and vary a bit. They shouldn't vary too much, but let's use Battlegrounds as inspiration. Battlegrounds often has animations at fixed speeds, but at one point it introduced animations changing speed based on how many are queued up. I don't mind attacks that trigger more effects, or trigger more complicated chains of interactions, taking longer. And I don't mind changing the step duration, but we need to make sure we don't have wildly disparate stuff. At some points in Battlegrounds one attack could take, I don't know, 3 or 4 seconds. It could set up such a long animation queue because it would play all the animations individually.
>
> We need to have multiple different animations for situations like that. One animation has an X animation, but if the same animation is triggering 10 times, we might need a separate animation that's designed to feel like that animation triggering 10 times really quickly, aggregated, and communicate it maybe with an ×10, an exclamation mark or whatever, like the floating numbers you might get as hit markers in a game, hit damage in a fighting game or whatever it might be. Combo ×3, ×4 … rises up at increasing pace, that kind of thing. I just want smart ways of grouping the animations, essentially.
>
> I'm more worried about feel than a specific timing, but obviously specific timing needs to be kept in some ways for feel. It's murky. You can change the rules, yes, is the short of it. And all that context is why you're allowed to change the rules.

*[Editor note: this replaces brief 10's rule that each attack's step, the replay holds and the whole fight's length stay unchanged ("Ask first. Making fights longer than they are now."). Night-run docs that say "replay holds are not edited" or "the whole-fight duration must match" describe what brief 10 did under the old rule; they are not rules now.]*
