# Give power generously, then dial it back (9 Nov 2026)

The owner's words in the orchestrator session. Transcribed: dictation errors, filler words and repeats removed, and punctuation added. *[Editor notes in italic brackets.]*

One thing I'd recommend is giving out power overly generously and then dialing it back, rather than starting from too weak and dialing up. It's not necessarily guaranteed to be better in all situations, but it's probably going to be more enjoyable to have overly powerful effects and an unbalanced game than weak effects and a boring game.

## Why, and what the game should do differently

Due to how human psychology works and variable reward scheduling, we don't actually respond rationally to winning sometimes and getting a bunch of frustrating losses. It's almost like a gambling addiction-like quality. So we're actually ill able to deal with a bunch of frustrating defeats to overpowered things we had no agency against. It's strange. It's not my preferred game design, and I want to get away from that. One of the goals of the game is to lessen how frequently that occurs. But that's just some of the reasoning behind why, and I think you might find that context interesting and useful.
