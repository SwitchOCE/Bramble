# Owner ideas log

A working log of design directions the owner thinks the game must have. Each entry is the owner's words, transcribed from chat with filler removed. These are directions, not decisions: nothing here is adopted into the rulebook until the owner says so. Agent notes are in *[italic brackets]*.

Newest entries at the bottom.

---

## 15 Nov 2026

### 1. A venomous tribe

> I think we have to have something analogous to Murlocs that's poisonous, right? Must have a poisonous tribe. Venomous, rather.

*[Battlegrounds' Murlocs carry Venomous (rulebook 11.2.5): destroys the first unit it damages. An earlier owner idea, a venom debuff that halves current Health when attacked, is in the note `2026-11-02k-venom-idea.md` (removed from `main` on 13 Nov 2026; in git history).]*

### 2. A counter to venom: halve, freeze growth, become immune

> One idea I had was that one of the counters to that could be some sort of effect, maybe a spell, maybe some other way of achieving it. At some point, it gives the player the ability to halve a unit's stats and prevent it from gaining more stats in the future, but it becomes immune to venomous or poisonous. Not other scam *[probably "not to other SCAM effects"]*, but those things.

*[Read as: a one-off choice on a unit. It loses half its Attack and Health, can never gain stats again, and from then on Venomous or poison can't destroy or harm it. It stays open to other SCAM effects. The owner confirmed this reading on 15 Nov. Whether "halve" rounds up or down is not stated.]*

### 3. Units move freely between hand and board; shop-phase economy units lock themselves on

> I want things to be able to move freely back and forth between the hand and the board, but that creates problems with units like any economy unit that works during the shop phase. So I want a keyword for any economy unit that works during the shop phase that it freezes itself onto your board for that turn. Basically at the beginning of the turn after a fight, it returns itself to your hand. And it can exceed maximum hand size to do so, I suppose, because that might create problems otherwise. And as soon as you play it in a turn, it locks itself onto your board, or maybe in the board space you play it to. So it's like committal to use it.

*[Read as two parts:*
- *A general rule: during the shop phase, units can be moved from the board back to hand and replayed. Battlegrounds doesn't allow this.*
- *A keyword for shop-phase economy units: once played in a turn, the unit is locked on the board (maybe locked to its slot) until the next turn starts. At the start of the turn after a fight, it returns to hand, even if the hand is full. So it can only pay out once per turn.*
- *The owner confirmed this reading on 15 Nov. Entry 4 below settles what "freezes" means.]*

### 4. Hand and board, continued: Battlecry becomes "on recruit", and the lock keyword

> Basically back to your hand. I basically want to solve the problem Battlegrounds has, where you can't move back and forth between your hand. But obviously in Battlegrounds that allows you to trigger battlecries. So I think our battlecry effect should be on recruit rather than on play, our equivalent. And any unit that gives you an advantage during the shop phase needs to have this mechanic, whatever we call it, the one that returns it to your hand at the start of the next turn. I think it just locks it onto your board. Maybe it's phrased as "it doesn't retreat" or something. I don't know.

*[Read as:*
- *Our Battlecry equivalent fires when you recruit (buy) the unit, not when you play it. Replaying a unit from hand then triggers nothing.*
- *Every unit that gives a shop-phase advantage gets the lock keyword, not only economy units.*
- *The keyword locks the unit onto the board, not to one slot. It can't go back to hand that turn, and it returns to hand by itself at the start of the next turn.*
- *Name not chosen. The owner's idea for its wording: "doesn't retreat".]*

*[17 Nov 2026: the owner adopted the general rule. It is rulebook 7.2.4, with selling from hand or board (4.3.7). The owner also confirmed "on recruit" as decided that day (7.2.3). The lock keyword is not a rule yet; it waits in the review queue, item 19.]*

### 5. A self-damage tribe with delayed damage, our equivalent of Demons

> I also had an idea for our equivalent of a demon tribe, a self-damage tribe. I don't like self-damage in Battlegrounds because of how uninteresting it is. Either you reverse it all or whatever. I like the idea of soul sacrifice, or debt to dark entities. But I think there needs to be some way of making it interesting to pick up in the first place. Because in Battlegrounds, you either have something that reverses it already, or the card is uninteresting to you. I think maybe we should have the idea of delayed damage. You take the damage, and when you lose a fight, your darkness deals damage to you, because the demons are unhappy with your failure. But either it goes away when you win a fight, or you can clear it at some point with other cards. That's my idea for the kind of self-damage. The equivalent of a Malchezaar *[transcribed as "Malkhazar"]* or an Imposing Percussionist or something.

*[Read as: self-damage effects don't cost Health at once. They add to a debt. When you lose a fight, the debt is dealt to you as damage. Winning a fight clears it, or (open which) other cards can clear it. The problem it answers: a self-damage card is only worth picking up if you already have something that undoes the damage.*
- *The Battlegrounds cards named: Malchezaar, Prince of Dance ("Two Refreshes each turn cost Health instead of Gold") and Imposing Percussionist ("Battlecry: Discover a Demon. Deal damage to your hero equal to its Tier").*
- *Open: whether the debt is dealt on top of the normal loss damage. Whether a tie counts as a loss.]*

Follow-up, same day, on what clears the debt:

> I thought that maybe some cards would clear it on a win. And some cards, like maybe a better card, would just clear it immediately. But a higher tier one that would replace it *[probably "replace the clear-on-win card"]*, perhaps, for the tribe. Maybe one card would require you to have a certain amount of it before it would clear it, though. Like a low tier one or something, like commitment, right? Got to sacrifice enough of your soul for it to deem you worthy or something.

*[Read as: the debt doesn't clear by itself. Cards in the tribe clear it, in three kinds:*
- *Some clear it when you win a fight.*
- *A better, higher-tier card clears it straight away, and takes over from the clear-on-win cards as the tribe's way to clear it.*
- *A low-tier card clears it only once the debt reaches a set amount. You have to commit to the tribe first.]*

### 6. Corvids: a dark summon tribe that buffs slots' Attack

> A tribe idea I had was maybe ravens or corvids in general, I guess. Crows, ravens, that kind of thing. Being dark, kind of like witches, sorcerers, necromancers, and them being the summon tribe. Our analogous thing to undead. I think their thing would be that they would buff slots' attack. The attack of your slots, basically. But not really the defense very much. Or maybe if they did buff defense, it'd only be on backline slots, and it'd be a pretty rare effect. Because I want their weakness to be not being able to have their big minions survive and trade. I want them to be fragile to stuff that kills multiple units with one effect.

*[Read as: corvids are our Undead-like tribe. They summon units, and their buffs go on board slots, giving Attack to whatever stands there, so summoned units arrive strong. Health buffs are rare and only on back-row slots. Their weakness: big units that hit hard but die in trades, and boards that are soft to effects that kill several units at once.]*

### 7. The demon tribe's species: maybe djinn

> I was thinking that for the demon stuff, I don't know, what kind of species would worship demons? Or Ginny *[probably "djinn"]* or something. Maybe it could be Ginny. Have a kind of Middle Eastern vibe. I'm not sure what a good species would be for that.

*[Links to entry 5, the delayed-damage tribe. The setting is an idealized European woodland, so a Middle Eastern tribe would be a deliberate exception to it. Species not chosen.]*

### 8. Fae as healers: back-row units heal instead of attacking

> I was thinking that with our game, maybe the fairies tribe could be healers, and backline units, instead of attacking, could heal a frontline unit on their attack, using their attack, if there was one in front of them that was still alive.

*[Read as: when a back-row fae's turn to attack comes, and a living front-row unit is in front of it, it heals that unit by its Attack instead of attacking.]*

Follow-up, same day, on the two open questions:

> Not sure what the fairy does when something's *[probably "nothing's"]* in front of it. That's a good question to leave open. I was thinking they might heal any unit in the front row, but maybe just those in front of them? I'm not sure.

*[Both questions stay open:*
- *What a fae unit does when nothing is in front of it.*
- *Which units it can heal: any front-row unit, or only the ones in front of it. The board is 4 front, 3 back, offset half a slot, so "in front of" means the two front slots it sits between.]*

### 9. "Fae", not "fairy"

> I think I prefer the term "fae" to "fairy". F-A-E. It allows them to be more adult and scary, and more males. They feel a bit more dangerous and less childlike.

*[Applied to this log's headings and notes. The owner's quotes keep "fairy" where it was said. About 20 other docs still say "fairy" or "fairies", among them CLAUDE.md, STATUS.md, direction-summary.md, pillars.md and the rulebook. They aren't renamed.]*

### 10. A fire tribe that summons phoenixes

> What about a tribe of magical creatures or something like phoenixes? Could be maybe a summoning tribe. We're doing bird tribes. Maybe there's some sort of fire-themed tribe in general, and phoenixes can be some of the summons for that tribe, and it can splash summons at some idea *[probably "as an idea": other builds can splash in its summons]*.

*[Read as: a fire-themed tribe whose summons include phoenixes. Its summons may also work in other tribes' builds.*
- *Related in the tribe run (`ideation/tribe-run`, 14 Nov): the Salamander Forge is already fire-themed (Heat, Quench, Reforge). Its Guildmistress Ottilie Ashbelly gives units Regrow (they come back from death at full Health), which is close to a phoenix's rebirth.*
- *Bird tribes so far: a Birds tribe in the ideation builds (not decided), and in the tribe run, the Cuckoo Clockworks and the corvid Shiny Syndicate. A phoenix could count as both a bird and part of the fire tribe.]*
