01

What changes

On the cardA cream strip set in lead across the card, the card's types from its data in dark letters, 15 px at board size. A long type ("Mouse Standard-bearer") shrinks to fit the frame.
Where on the cardTwo places to compare. Above Attack and Health, like a Battlegrounds tribe banner but higher: it covers the bottom 20 px of the art. Under the top point: it covers the art's sky instead.
In the hover"TIER 1 · UNIT · SELLS FOR 1" becomes "TIER 1 · BADGER KNIGHT · SELLS FOR 1". Nothing else in the hover moves.
02

Words and choices

In-game wordsEach card's own types from units.json, joined with a space: "Badger Knight", "Badger Squire", "Dormouse Duellist", "Shrew Skirmisher", "Locust", "Pigeon", "Briar".
ArtBoth places cover a strip of art; the keyword marks stay where they are. The owner's rule that keyword marks never cover the art doesn't name the type line, so this needs your call.
For you to decideAbove the stats or under the top point. Cream strip or a dark one. Whether one-word types (Locust, Pigeon, Briar) print at all, since the tribe already says it.
Cards and types are the build's own content (src/content/*/units.json, 20 Nov).
Checked at 1920 × 1080 only. At 1366 × 768 the strip's type is about 11 px: needs a look on real hardware.
