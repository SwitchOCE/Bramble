What changes

The buttonThe shop buttons' leaded glass, 150 × 68, a skip icon and "Skip". Bottom of the right light: the same corner your hand goes to for End turn, so the habit carries over.
ClicksA left click on the board, a card or the empty glass no longer skips. Pointing at cards to read them during a fight becomes safe.
KeysSpace still skips. The footer line "Space or click to skip" goes; pointing at the button shows the key instead.

In-game wordsButton: "Skip". Hover: a Space key cap and "to skip".
After the fightThe result that follows also moves on with any click today. Suggested: the same rule there, so it moves on by itself after 2.5 s or with Space. Not drawn.
