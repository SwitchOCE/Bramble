A full hand never loses a card

The hand stays at 7. A card you gain with a full hand waits in a small pile at the end of the hand instead of being lost. When a place frees, the oldest waiting card slides in.

How it works

The pileWaiting cards lie face up, dimmed, tucked at the right end of the hand, the newest on top with its art showing. A cream plate gives the count: "+1", "+2". Nothing else is added to the screen.
Can't be used yetWaiting cards can't be played, cast or sold. They stay across turns until there is room.
ArrivingPlay, sell or cast anything from the hand and the oldest waiting card slides into the free place at the end of the hand. The plate counts down; at 0 the pile is gone.
HoverPointing at the pile shows the waiting cards in the order they will arrive, the next one marked "Next". Pointing at a card there shows its normal tooltip.
02

Words and choices

In-game wordsPlate: "+1", "+2". Hover title: "Waiting". Hover line: "They join your hand as places free up." Tag under the first card: "Next".
What waitsEverything that would be lost today: the Triple Reward, a Discover pick, a Hopper from Horde-runner. Buying still needs a free hand place or a board slot (item 2), so the pile never fills from the shop.
For you to decideShould the pile have a limit? This draws none. And does the newest card show on top (drawn) or the next to arrive?
Cards are the build's own content. The Triple Reward uses the build's stand-in spell art.
Motion is not checked: "A place frees" is a still with a trail to suggest the slide. The slide's speed and feel need a filmed check in the build.
