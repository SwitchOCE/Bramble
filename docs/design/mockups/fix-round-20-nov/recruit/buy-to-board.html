Bramble · fix round 20 Nov · item 2

Buy straight onto the board when the hand is full

With 7 cards in hand, dragging a shop unit lights the empty board slots. Drop it on one and it is bought straight into that slot.

01

What changes

While draggingEvery empty board slot lights, as it does for a hand unit today. The slot under the card lights brighter. The price coin travels with the card, so you see what you'll pay.
On the dropThe gold goes down by the price, the unit lands in the slot, and the hand stays 7/7. Dropping anywhere else puts the card back in the shop, as today.
Same as a hand playA unit bought onto the board fires its Recruit effect and gets auras the moment it lands, as if it were bought and then played (Master-at-Arms still asks for its target).
02

Words and choices

In-game wordsNone new. The price coin is the one already under the card.
Rule changeRulebook 4.2.2 says a player with a full hand can't buy. This would change it to: can't buy unless the unit goes straight to an empty board slot.
For you to decideShould this also work with room in the hand? The build already lets a drop on an empty slot buy and play in one move; this page only shows the full-hand case.
Cards are the build's own content (src/content, 20 Nov).
Motion is not checked: the lift, the slots lighting and the landing are stills.
