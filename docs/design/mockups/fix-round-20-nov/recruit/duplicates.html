Duplicate and triple marks count only what you own

A shop card glows softly when you own one copy, and strongly, with foil, when you own two, so buying it makes a triple. Copies in the shop never count toward each other.

01

The rule

CountCopies you own, on the board and in the hand. Shop copies are what the mark is drawn on; they never add to the count.
One ownedA soft, steady glow on each matching shop card. Nothing on the copy you own.
Two ownedThe strong triple glow and the foil on the matching shop card, and a faint gold edge on the two you own, so you can find them.
LiveThe marks redraw on every change to the hand and board: buy, sell, play, move to hand. In (a), buying one cub turns the other shop cub into (b) at once.
02

What it fixes, and what to pick

BeforeIn (a) the build lights all three cubs with the triple glow, though buying one gives only two copies (before shot b04). Two shop copies with none owned also light each other today.
GoldensA golden you own doesn't count, because goldens don't combine. Today it still gives the soft glow; this page assumes it shouldn't.
For you to decideThe faint edge on the copies you own in (b): keep it, or mark only the shop card.
Cards are the build's own content (src/content, 20 Nov). Glow colours and blur are the build's (glass_duplicate_glow.gd); the foil is a still of the build's moving sheen.
Motion is not checked: the build's triple glow breathes and its foil moves. Neither shows in a still.
