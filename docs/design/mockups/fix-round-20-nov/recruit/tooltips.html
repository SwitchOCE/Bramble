01

The two options

Option 1 · Left lightThe keyword boxes stack down the left light, under the turn, always in the same place whatever you point at. The left light is empty in the middle of a turn, so nothing is covered.
Option 2 · Along the topThe keyword boxes sit side by side across the top of the arch, over the shop's spells while you point. Wider and shorter boxes; it reads left to right like the card's own marks.
BothThe card's tooltip (the card large and its words) is unchanged and stays by the card. Keyword boxes appear and go with it, in the card's own order, then any keyword their lines name (Outnumbered).
02

Grudge, and words

Grudge (item 13)New line: "At the start of your turn, gain +1 Health, and +1/+1 more for each unit you were outnumbered by last fight. Works even if this died." Today's: "After each fight, gain +1 Health, and +1/+1 more for each unit you were outnumbered by."
Other in-game wordsFury, Guard and Outnumbered keep the build's lines. The kind line uses item 8's type: "TIER 1 · BADGER KNIGHT · SELLS FOR 1".
For you to decideLeft light or top. And Grudge's rule: it pays every Grudge unit on your board, alive or dead in the fight, because the board comes back after each fight. Confirm that is what you want.
