Bramble · fix round 20 Nov · item 3

Drop a hand unit on a board unit to swap them

Hold a unit from your hand over an occupied slot: the unit there shows where it will go, a pale copy in the hand card's place. Let go and they swap.

01

What changes

While holdingEmpty slots light amber as today. An occupied slot under the card lights a cool blue instead, so a swap never looks like a plain play. The unit standing there dims and lifts toward the hand, and a pale copy of it waits in the gap the hand card left.
On the dropThe two change places in one move: the hand unit stands in the slot, the board unit goes to that place in the hand. No message line.
Same rules as each halfIt works like moving the board unit to the hand and playing the hand unit, so it needs no free hand place: the hand stays the same size.
02

Words and choices

In-game wordsNone. Today's "choose an empty slot" message goes away for this drop.
Board to boardUnchanged: dropping a board unit on another already swaps them (rulebook 8.3.3). This uses the same blue.
For you to decideA swapped-out unit with a Recruit effect (Master-at-Arms, Harvest Piper): when it comes back from the hand later, does it fire again? Today moving a unit to the hand and back does not re-recruit it; the swap keeps that.
Cards are the build's own content (src/content, 20 Nov).
Motion is not checked: the lift and the swap are stills. The blue and the amber need checking side by side in the build.
