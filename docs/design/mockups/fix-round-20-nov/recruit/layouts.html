The three ideas

A · Two wingsLeft wing is your money and your tier; right wing is the shop's own controls. The two readouts leave the sill: "+13 next turn" sits under the gold, "−3 next turn" under tier-up. Smallest change. Moved: only the two readouts.
B · One purseAll four controls in one block left of the shop: gold top-left, refresh beside it, tier-up and freeze below. Every readout sits under the button it explains. The right of the shop is left empty. Moved: refresh, freeze, both readouts.
C · Gold on topThe gold goes to the middle of the arch, between the two spells, where every price is about the same distance away. Tier-up stays left, refresh and freeze right. Moved: the gold, the two shop spells (one card outward each), both readouts.
