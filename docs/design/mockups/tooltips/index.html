Keywords and tooltips

Keywords become coloured marks with an icon and their number. The long reminder lines leave the card's tooltip; hold Alt to freeze it and point at a keyword to read what it does.

02

Every keyword in the build

Six colours, one for each family of keyword, and one icon for each keyword. The word is always there in the tooltip, so colour is never the only clue. Lines are the build's own reminder text.

03

Taste calls I made

Colour by familyThirty-nine keywords can't each have a colour you'd tell apart, so the colour says the family (attacks, protection, stats that stay, extra bodies, shop, counts) and the icon says which keyword.
Keywords on cardsRound icon marks with their number, never over the art: either between Attack and Health on the bottom border, or in a band across the card's bottom. The word is in the tooltip.
Reminder linesGone from the card's tooltip. Each keyword's line opens in its own tooltip when Alt is held, and a keyword inside that line (Medal in Decorated) is marked too, so you can keep going.
The hint"Hold Alt for keyword details" sits at the foot of every card tooltip, so new players find it.
Placeholder iconsDrawn as plain line icons on dark glass. A proper set in the glass look is art work for later.
For you to pickBottom border or bottom band for keywords on cards.
