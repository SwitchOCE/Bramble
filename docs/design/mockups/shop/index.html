01

What to look at

CostsEvery cost is now one gold coin with its number inside: shop prices, spells, tier-up, refresh and the sell value. The gold you have stays a coin and a number, because it is a count, not a cost.
PricesTags: the price coin hangs on a string from each card's bottom point. Pedestals: the coin is set in a glass dais. Top-left edge: the coin sits on the middle of the card's top-left edge, the same place for units and spells; the shop is spaced wider for it.
Keywords on cardsNothing over the art any more. Bottom border: small round marks between Attack and Health. Bottom band: a dark leaded band across the card's bottom section, under the two discs, with room for four.
Refresh counterWords: "7 left at this price" above the button. Pips: ten small panes, lit for each refresh left at this price (rulebook 4.4: 10 at 1 gold, then steps of 5).
DuplicatesNo pips. Brock Cub would make a triple, so it glows gold in the shop, and so do the two you own, on the board and in your hand. Hedge Knight, owned once, keeps the softer glow in the shop only.
Can't affordSet gold to 1: unit prices turn red and their coins go dull. The cards themselves stay bright, as in Battlegrounds.
02

Taste calls I made

Tier up and refreshWider, taller buttons. Tier up says "Tier up 3 → 4" beside a 46 px price coin; refresh is its icon and a price coin of the same size.
Room for pricesThe shop row moves up by about 50 px to make room under it. The board and hand don't move.
Cards buttonTop of the right window, where the settings cog sits on the left: opens the card browser over the game. On the fight screen it would sit in the same place.
Sell valueHolding a board unit over the shop dims it and shows "Sell for 1". The build has no sell cue yet; this is one way.
For you to pickTags, pedestals or top-left edge. Bottom border or bottom band for keywords. Words or pips for the refresh counter.
