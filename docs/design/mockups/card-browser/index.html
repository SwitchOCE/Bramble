Taste calls I made

Where it opensA "Cards" button on the start screen, between Play and Quit, and a round Cards button at the top of the recruit screen's right window, where the cog sits on the left. In game it opens over the dimmed screen, with Close in place of Back. The fight screen would have the same button in the same place.
Units and spellsA Units / Spells switch at the top of the filters. Spells shows the build's three (Sharpen, Seek, Forage) with the tier filter; tribes don't apply to spells.
Filters in the side windowOut of game the left window holds Back, the switch, the tribes and the six tiers, so the middle is all cards. Filters combine.
Grouped by tierTier headings, then tribe order, then name. Tokens (Cousin, Hopper, Private, Pip) come last under their own heading.
Keywords on cardsEach card shows its own keywords at the bottom, as on the shop mock-up (switch above): taken from the card's text, any sentence that is only a keyword.
SearchDrawn but not working on this page. It would match names and card text.
For you to pickWhether golden versions are shown (a toggle, as Battlegrounds has). Whether the in-game button stays top right.
All 99 units, the 3 spells and their art are the build's own (ideation/track-2 at f44f2f8f); stats and text are the build's, so they show its unit balance as it stands. Briefs: docs/workstreams/backlog-briefs.md, PR 118.
