# Once Upon a Galaxy: system-level patch history

**Sources.** All 72 official articles on galaxy.fun/articles, saved as text in `tools/research/.cache/explore/arttxt/`. That covers patch notes, set releases (Candylar, Mythbound, Chromaria), mechanic articles (Candy, Boons, Prophecy), monthly season posts, Monthly tournament posts and the two roadmaps. Every article was read in full. Two June 2024 "Meet the Dreamers" profiles hold no rules changes.

**Coverage.** 3 July 2024 (closed beta, before GenCon) to 26 October 2026.

**What counts as system-level here.** Rules for the shop, board, combat, hearts, captains, treasures and upgrades. Also new set mechanics, game modes, matchmaking and ranks, monetization and collection, and platform milestones. Individual card balance changes get a one-line summary per patch. They are spelled out only when the developers' stated reason shows a design principle.

**Conventions.**
- Quotes are short and taken word for word from the article.
- **INFERRED** marks a conclusion I drew from the text. The text does not state it.
- Section headings use the date in the article's title where one is given. The URL is the source of record.

## 2024-07-03 — Patch Notes: July 3, 2024
- Rules change: "Allow for clones to occur when resummoning but restrict resummons to 5 times per character." This is the earliest hard cap on a loop.
- Rules change: "Enforce 12-card decks during deck building."
- Treasure terms already in place: "Secret Rare shop", Legendary treasures, "2-pick treasure" (Trifork, Dorothy). INFERRED: treasures were offered as a pick from a shop of choices from the start.
- Cards: 6 new captains, 9 characters, 1 spell, 3 returning treasures, ~9 reworks (several "Your next shop contains all X" recruit effects) and ~37 renames.
- Open Beta is announced for GenCon (1–4 August).
- Source: https://galaxy.fun/articles/patch-early-july/

## 2024-07-23 — Patch Notes: July 23, 2024
- Collection test: every captain gets a 12-card theme deck (3 Common, 3 Uncommon, 3 Rare, 2 Epic, 1 Legendary). Each lobby win unlocks one card for that captain, never a duplicate. Every 100 XP gives a booster for a random captain.
- Players who don't edit decks "shuffl[e] the base theme decks into the pool as normal". INFERRED: each player's deck is shuffled into a shared shop pool for the lobby.
- Stated goal for collection: "There was a ton of stuff to unlock, but i felt like I was being given rewards at a good pace."
- Progression: ranks Bronze, Silver, Gold, Platinum, Diamond, then Legend. Each rank takes 15 stars, earned in async games. "You can never lose stars."
- First new-player experience, a story-driven tutorial run.
- Live lobbies already exist (a bug fix for Gloves of Thieving "in live lobbies").
- Cards: 16 new and ~16 functional changes. Some cards moved from the shared pool to Collectible (Don Quixote, Hercules). Underdog's ability became a slot buff.
- **Design-principle card note:** Angel of Mercy got a "when you Golden me" ability "so cannot go infinite with Mushroom Ring".
- Source: https://galaxy.fun/articles/patch-late-july/

## 2024-07-26 — Patch Notes: July 26, 2024 (Open Beta)
- **Unique captains:** "all lobbies (live and async) will have unique captains". No two players are offered the same captain in live captain select, and async opponents never share your captain. Starting captains also can't be transformed into or gained through other effects.
- Async matchmaking now aims for more variety in the boards you face.
- Cards: 2 rarity moves.
- Source: https://galaxy.fun/articles/patch-open-beta/

## 2024-08-27 — Patch Notes: August 27, 2024
- **Async and matchmaking:** you should "never face the same 'async run' twice in a gameplay session". MMR and lobby creation now try to "smooth the progression of board strength from Gold thru Legendary". Ranks changed: shorter Bronze, longer Gold, +1 star for 1st place with full health, -1 star for 5th or 6th. INFERRED: lobbies have 6 players.
- **Bank slot:** banking bugs fixed. The bank slot now shows only while you drag a card.
- **Selling:** "We have disabled selling from board."
- Bots in live lobbies fill empty seats and now "position ranged/support/hunt/etc. more intelligently".
- Live lobbies got reconnection support and concede fixes.
- Dragonmother can no longer find her egg on turn 1.
- Cards: ~10 golden-ability upgrades (archetype recruiters give a full bonus shop when gold; others double their effect), 5 buffs, 2 nerfs, 7 functional changes, 5 cuts, 4 new.
- **Design-principle card notes:**
  - Indiana Clones "now triggers on turn 10 instead of on Legendary (turn 9)". This dates Legendary shop rarity to turn 9.
  - Barrel of Monkeys moved to Legendary and the personal (collectible) pool. Reason: being in the shared pool "means that the blowout moments it can create likely happens too often". The move also takes it out of random-summon effects, "which removes the largest surprise blowout moments".
  - Ogre Magi moved to Collectible "to lower the frequency" of an interaction that confuses new players. The principle: rarity and pool placement are used to control how often something appears.
- Source: https://galaxy.fun/articles/patch-early-september/

## 2024-10-10 — Patch Notes: October 10, 2024 (index date 2024-08-27)
- **Planned economy:** a gem store with booster prices, a daily rotation of 3 captain bundles, and cosmetics. A free and a premium ($9.99) battle pass are planned for November. Some captains leave the general pool to become pass rewards. A premium-pass captain becomes the free-pass captain 2 months later.
- A collection reset is planned for the November store launch.
- Progression: personal pools now start at 0 cards per captain, and unlocks happen automatically.
- UI: dragging a character now shows its stats without support buffs, as it already did for slot buffs. INFERRED: support buffs and slot buffs are positional and get recalculated when a character moves.
- Hearts: "King Midas now shows 2.5 hearts during Captain select" (Midas starts with a cracked heart).
- Cards: ~12 changes and 23 new cards. Examples: Frog Prince transforms when goldened, then turns Bronze; Boon Willow buffs "both your back slots" and supports "the characters in front of me", which confirms 2 back slots and supports giving buffs forward; Ace of Hearts "When one of your hearts is emptied, get a bonus shop".
- Source: https://galaxy.fun/articles/patch-early-october/

## 2024-11-11 — Patch Notes: November 11, 2024
- Completing a captain's card collection now gives a Diamond captain frame.
- MMR fix: MMR "should no longer 'go up forever'". Values above 3000 were reset to 3000.
- Cards: 7 changes, 4 Legendary-to-Epic rarity moves and 2 removals.
- **Design-principle card note:** Sir Francis Drake's self-improving treasure hunt "now only goes up to legendary (and stops after you get your legendary)". This caps an escalating ability.
- Source: https://galaxy.fun/articles/patch-early-november/

## 2024-12-05 — Patch Notes: December 5, 2024 (Early Access)
- Open Beta ends with "over 10,000 players". Early Access begins.
- **Store:** gem packs from $4.99 to $99.99, a New Player Bundle, boosters at 50 gems each (a random uncollected card for a random captain), daily captain bundles of 500 gems for 20 captain-specific boosters, and cosmetics.
- **Galaxy Pass:** monthly, with a free track and a premium track ($9.99). The premium "Captain of the Month" moves to the free track 2 months after debut.
- Final collection reset. Beta players received 5,000 gems. "We do not plan on resetting collections again."
- Cards: 10 changes. The Fates moved to the personal pool.
- Source: https://galaxy.fun/articles/patch-early-december/

## 2025-02-01 — Patch Notes: February 1, 2025
- Galaxy Pass progress switched from XP to Stars, at 9 Stars per level.
- 6 new Legendary treasures. Cards: 2 changes.
- Source: https://galaxy.fun/articles/patch-early-february/

## 2025-02-12 — Patch Notes: February 12, 2025
- Galaxy Pass placement stars went from 3/2/1/1 to 4/3/2/1 for 1st to 4th. Reason: pass rewards "were not unlocking as fast as we would have liked".
- Cards: ~23 changes, mostly small stat buffs.
- New loss-triggered effects: Ace of Hearts became "If you lost your last fight, get a bonus shop". Mask of Infinite Rage doubles its bonus "whenever you lose a fight". INFERRED: comeback rewards are a recurring tool (see notes).
- Source: https://galaxy.fun/articles/patch-mid-february/

## 2025-02-28 — Patch Notes: February 28, 2025
- **New ranked system:** 6 ranks (Wood, Bronze, Silver, Gold, Platinum, Legendary), each with 5 tiers. You cannot be relegated after advancing. Ranks reset to Bronze each season, and you keep rewards for the highest rank you reached.
- **Wood:** for new players, against bots, until 5 wins or 10 games.
- **Bronze upward:** "exclusively against Async player boards". Jewels by placement:
  | Rank | 1st | 2nd | 3rd | 4th | 5th | 6th | Jewels per tier |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Bronze | 3 | 2 | 1 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 3 |
  | Silver | 3 | 2 | 1 | 0 | 0 | -1 | 6 |
  | Gold | 3 | 2 | 1 | 0 | -1 | -1 | 9 |
  | Platinum | 3 | 2 | 1 | 0 | -1 | -2 | 12 |
  | Legendary | as Platinum, no tiers | | | | | | |
- This confirms 6-player lobbies. A top-2000 Legendary ranking by internal MMR is planned.
- Bottled Starlight now doubles Galaxy Pass bonus stars instead of giving a card.
- Source: https://galaxy.fun/articles/patch-early-march/

## 2025-03-18 — Patch Notes: March 18, 2025

- Hearts: casting Midas Touch on Navi at 1.5 hearts "heals to 2.5 hearts instead of 3". Healing respects the half-heart structure.
- Mr. Lamppost was banned from Co-Captain Corsair. This begins a growing list of captain-specific bans.
- Source: https://galaxy.fun/articles/patch-mid-march/

## 2025-03-31 — Patch Notes: March 31, 2025
- The leaderboard now shows Legendary players' rank within the top 2000.
- New pass captain Artemis, built around Secret Rares. Captain-specific bans were added (Artemis can't be found via Thing in the Ice and can't discover Timeline Duplicator).

## 2025-05-01 — Patch Notes: May 1st, 2025 (index 2025-04-29)
- Card text was standardized: "level" was replaced with "rarity". Function is unchanged. INFERRED: "shop level" and "rarity tier" mean the same thing.
- Steam client in beta. A "More Treasures" button now shows every treasure you hold. The developers call it "probably the most requested feature".
- Cards: 9 changes. Underdog: "If you have fewer characters than the enemy when I attack, give my slot +2/+2."
- Source: https://galaxy.fun/articles/patch-early-may/

Faerie Dragon's support passes Treasure Hoard to "the characters in front of me".
- Steam beta: keyboard support and a streamer layout.

## 2025-07-18 — Patch Notes (titled "June 18th, 2025", index 2025-07-18)
- **Theme decks:** after picking a captain you choose a deck (Starter, Custom once you own 13 cards, or one or more hand-built theme decks). There are more deck slots.
- **Store:** Featured Theme Decks replace the random captain bundles. Each one unlocks all 12 cards, and the price drops for cards you already own.
- **Captain Select rerolls:** "one free reroll for Slot 3 each game". Slot 3 is "specifically where unowned captains can now appear". The free reroll "is guaranteed to offer a captain you already own". You can buy a captain from this screen and play it right away. INFERRED: captain select shows 3 options.
- Premium captains "become free to all players after two months".
- Multi-triggers: when an effect fires 3 or more times, it now plays one animation with a multiplier. Reason: to stop it "clogging up your screen, memory, and time" and to cut crashes.
- Questers must be on the board to see recruits (Scarecrow change).
- Source: https://galaxy.fun/articles/patch-mid-july-2025/

## 2025-07-29 — Patch Notes: July 29th, 2025 (Steam launch)
- "We are officially live on Steam!"
- **Bank slot rule:** "Banking is only enabled after all 5 character slots have been filled." This confirms a 5-character board.

- Placeholder music was added after Steam feedback, plus volume sliders.

## 2025-08-27 — Patch Notes: August 27th, 2025
- **Combat timeout rule:** "In Async mode, when the game abandons a complex fight mid-combat (like when you have a lot of hunt triggers), we now count it as a battle win for the active player. This will allow games to reach a conclusion." Engine work also aims to "abort complex battles less often".
- **Keyword wording rule:** "Generally, we lean towards +1 additional trigger when we use the term 'Double'". Jack Sparrow was fixed to always give +1 hunt trigger, not 2x.
- Shops now show type tags for faction captains.
- Candylar is announced for October.
- Source: https://galaxy.fun/articles/patch-early-september-2025/

## 2025-09-18 — Candylar / Candy mechanic articles
- **Candy, Sweeten, Supersweet:** Candy cards (spells, characters, treasures) all share one sweetness counter. "Sweeten – Increase the buffs on all of your Candy cards by +1/+1." "Supersweet 3" means the card gets 3x the sweetness bonus.
- Spells can turn existing characters into Candy (Candy Kiss).
- **Pool gating:** Candy spells "will only appear if you have at least 1 Candy card in your deck (or are playing a Candy Captain)".
- **Treasure Find:** the first time a character gives you a treasure (by goldening or completing its quest), you get that character's unique treasure.
- **Card acquisition:** captains unlock cards from their own release set first. After completing that set, they unlock cards from other sets at random. Every base-set captain gets Candylar theme decks. 8 free new captains at release.
- Sources: https://galaxy.fun/articles/candy-a-sweet-mechanic/ , https://galaxy.fun/articles/candylar/

## 2025-09-18 — Patch Notes: September 18th, 2025
- **Combat timing rule:** buffs such as Hercules' and Big Bad Wolf's hunt abilities now trigger before effects like Poison Apple. Stated reason: "to allow effects like Poison Apple to be better counters to large stated lads." Anti-scaling answers should land after the buffs.
- **Position:** Parting Gift "now only doubles farewells in your front row". This makes front and back rows matter for treasures.
- Shrink Tea now targets the enemy with the highest attack.
- "Happily Ever After", "All for One" and Zagreus now wait until the next shop to apply their effects.
- Cards: Hannibal nerfed (countdown 6 to 7; he "outperformed … every other captain"), 2 reworks, ~6 major and 4 minor changes. Weird Beard reverted to Goldened Pirates; it "Was in just too many of the top performing decklists".
- Source: https://galaxy.fun/articles/patch-mid-september-2025/

## 2025-10-08 — Patch Notes: October 8th, 2025
- **Every captain now has a free starter deck.** Stated reason: so "all players will have access to lots of Candylar content on release".
- Cosmetic switcher at captain select. It doesn't yet work in "Friend Lobbies, just Async".
- Opponents' boards from old patches were filtered out of the async pool ("No more Santa, Mop, etc. on opponents boards"). INFERRED: async ghost boards are stored per patch and purged after shared-pool changes.
- **Design-principle card note ("Chain Chain Change"):** "The power of friendship turns out to be way too powerful … Especially combined with World Shrub and the many ways to get extra shops off silvers." ## 2025-10-21 — Candylar - Out Now!
- Set 2 is live, with over 100 cards.
- **Victory Loot:** finishing top 3 in an async lobby unlocks every collectible card on the last board you played against, on top of the normal unlock.
- The gem reward for a completed captain dropped from 10 to 5, "due to increased card unlock rates".
- Mid-fight transforms now keep damage already taken.
- **Design-principle card notes:** "Early boards were edging a touch more powerful than we had anticipated." Nesting Scrubfowl lost Health (too strong "when an early silver and gold was found"). Navii dropped from Rare to Uncommon so she could be "goldened more often". Sailor's Spinach went to more Attack and less Health "to help lessen full heart damage early in games". Buried Chest was cut to make room for new uncommon treasure sources.

## 2025-10-29 — Patch Notes: October 29th, 2025

## 2025-11-20 — Patch Notes: November 20th, 2025 (State of the Game)
- **Two stated goals:** "More cards are fair" and "Reducing the amount of full heart damage being dealt in the early turns."
- **Matchmaking:** "a new behind the scenes matchmaking system that will reduce the frequency of very strong boards in the early game. They will still appear, but at a lower rate." The developers say this is about the "desired pace". INFERRED: async opponent boards are chosen per turn, and the selection is weighted against outliers.
- **Treasure Find selection rule:** if you hold a treasure-pick modifier (Lucky Chest, Boots of Magic Find), you see 3 options: 2 modified and 1 Treasure Find treasure. If you skip the Treasure Find treasure, you won't see it again from that character.
- Async Midas opponents now correctly start with a cracked heart.
- Infinite-loop protection now always grants the win to the active player.
- Holiday bundle (3,000 gems and 3 alt arts).
- Cards: ~18 changes and 2 made inactive.
- **Design-principle card notes:**
  - The Candyman's starting buff was lowered "to allow closer fights … in the first several turns" and to add "risk for Candyman players when taking early normally low-stat characters".
  - Sweet Tooth (Candy spell captain) was moved later, to "Starting turn 6", because she was "adding too much power in the early game … when you face them in turn 3 to 7". In return she got "a much stronger late game".
  - The Sour Patch Kid lost Health. It was "surviving attacks multiple times in a single fight" and was "an unstoppable wall that resulted in more full heart damage turns".
  - Blackbeard lost the Hunt keyword "to reduce the number of rounds that fail to complete due to too many triggers or unstoppable loops".
  - Ugly Duckling was made inactive; it "continues to result in full heart damage earlier than we would like".
  - Mr. Fox stole the same strong treasures repeatedly, so he was changed "to help increase variety in his gameplay".
  - Sword of Valor was buffed because its "positional requirements" meant players passed it over. Tink's Torches now adds attack to supports, which "are so lowly statted".
- Source: https://galaxy.fun/articles/patch-mid-november-2025/

## 2025-12-22 — Patch Notes: December 22nd, 2025

- Summoned tokens are now Common rarity.

## 2025-12-27 — Galaxy Monthlies announced
- First organized play: "progressive asynchronous events". Day One and Day Two each consist of runs of 5 rounds. Each round needs a minimum placement to continue.
- Entry is free for players who reached Legendary the previous month. They get 2 invites, plus 1 more at 100 and at 500 Legendary Jewels. Day Two gives one run.

## Current-state rules facts (checked against the Galaxy Guide)

What the articles confirm or suggest about the Guide's summary (5-slot board of 3 front + 2 back; 2 shops per turn of 3 characters + 1 spell from turn 3; rarity unlocks on turns 1/3/5/7/9; 3 hearts; crack or empty heart damage):

- **5 slots:** confirmed. "Banking is only enabled after all 5 character slots have been filled" (2025-07-29).
- **2 back slots / front row:** consistent. "both your back slots" (2024-10-10); "front row" (2025-09-18); "back row" (2025-10-29). The exact 3+2 split is not stated, but follows from 5 slots with 2 at the back (INFERRED).
- **Bank slot:** an extra slot outside the 5, shown only while dragging (2024-08-27), usable only when the board is full (2025-07-29). Banked questers and Rudolph don't trigger. Selling from the board was removed on 2024-08-27.
- **Shops per turn:** the base number is never stated.
  - Itty Aerie's "you only get one shop that turn" and Rumpelstiltskin's "2 Treasure Shops and then 1 Normal Shop" fit a base of 2 plus bonus shops (INFERRED).
  - Bonus shops can chain past 20 in a turn, which is why Limit Breaker caps them at 30 (2026-05-11).
- **Shop size:** Toucan Samuel's "shops only have two characters" implies a larger default. 3 is consistent but not stated (INFERRED). Spells: "random single spell shops" exist (2025-12-22). Spell gating by deck (Candy, Paintbrush) is a later addition the Guide may not cover.
- **Rarity unlock turns:** only Legendary on turn 9 is confirmed ("Legendary (turn 9)", 2024-08-27). Captains can shift the schedule (Bo Peep: one rarity sooner; Itty Aerie: a shop one rarity higher).
- **Hearts:** 3, in half-heart steps.
  - Midas starts on 2.5 ("cracked heart").
  - Healing respects the steps (1.5 to 2.5, not 3).
  - Gentle Giant halves win damage to "half a heart", so a normal win can deal a full heart.
  - The developers repeatedly measure pacing by "full heart damage … in the early turns".
- **Lobbies:** 6 players (Jewel table, 1st–6th). Galaxy Pass stars go to the top 4.
- **Ranked mode:** async ghost boards from Bronze upward. Wood rank plays bots. Live and friend lobbies exist; ranked live play was never announced as shipped.
- **Captain select:** 3 options (INFERRED). Slot 3 can show unowned captains and has 1 free reroll guaranteed to give an owned captain (2025-07-18). Slot 2 takes owned Set Rerolls (2026-07-20). Bought captains get priority in later selects (2026-06-11). Captains are unique within a lobby (2024-07-26).
- **Decks:** 12 cards, enforced since 2024-07-03. A custom deck needs 13 owned cards (2025-07-18). Your deck feeds the shop pool alongside a shared pool, which rotates via "Shared Pool Shakeups". Some cards are collectible (personal pool) and some shared.
- **"Double" convention:** it means +1 extra trigger, not 2x (2025-08-27).

## Design-principle notes (stated reasons that matter for a new auto-battler)

**Infinite loops and scaling limits**
- 2024-07-03: resummons are capped at 5 per character. This is the first hard cap on loops.
- 2024-07-23: Angel of Mercy was changed so it "cannot go infinite with Mushroom Ring".
- 2024-11-11: Sir Francis Drake's escalation stops at Legendary.
- 2025-07-18: repeated triggers collapse into one animation with a multiplier. Trigger volume was an engine and crash problem as well as a design one.
- 2025-08-27: if an async fight is too complex and gets abandoned, "we now count it as a battle win for the active player … so games … reach a conclusion". Restated as infinite-loop protection on 2025-11-20.
- 2025-11-20: Blackbeard lost Hunt to cut "rounds that fail to complete due to too many triggers or unstoppable loops".
- 2026-04-24: Pixie Dust was shelved for "exponential scaling" that dominated competitive finishes, to "give other finishers a chance to shine".
- 2026-04-30 / 05-11: a hard cap on shops per turn (20, then 30). Its goals: appear "rarely", let one-off goals resolve, and bring "long silver and spell chained shops to a conclusion so games can continue to progress to conclusions". Large shop counts "strain … the overall fast and fun gameplay".
- 2026-05-11: Boons were retimed to check at the start of the fight, so they can't be collected and completed again in the same turn. Medea and The Raven were reworked so they aren't "infinitely repeatable".

**Snowballing and determinism**
- 2024-08-27: blowout cards were moved out of the shared pool and random-summon pools so the "largest surprise blowout moments" happen less often.
- 2025-10-08: Chain of Friendship plus extra shops from silvers was "way too powerful". The fix aimed not to "invalidate all previous numbers players have worked to build". The developers protect the value of existing scaling when they nerf a combo.
- 2026-03-16: build-arounds should be "fun to do but not at the expense of other strategies" (Butcher).
- 2026-05-11: Nemesis' Anoint let boards "snowball a game faster and more consistently than we would like". The target is "not as deterministic or as quick".
- 2026-08-14: Sorcerer's Mop is the shared pool's "end-game strategy". Once new summoners made it "too easy", it was narrowed to Toys so it "should require a bit more planning".
- 2026-09-28: new set mechanics force re-tuning of old multipliers (Ham-Star with paint; Beanstalk with start-of-game paint).

**Game length and early-game pace**
- 2025-10-21: early boards were too strong after an early silver or gold. The fix moves stats from Health to Attack "to help lessen full heart damage early in games".
- 2025-11-20: "Reducing the amount of full heart damage being dealt in the early turns" was named a top goal. It was handled with card nerfs and with matchmaking that lowers how often very strong early boards appear. Early-power captains were pushed later (Sweet Tooth from the start to turn 6, with "a much stronger late game").
- 2025-11-20 / 2026-03-16: tanky early walls (Sour Patch Kid) and early buffers (Pix) are nerfed explicitly for causing early full-heart losses.
- 2026-02-19: Gentle Giant, a shared-pool card that deals only half a heart on a win, is meant to "help you survive the early game".
- 2026-04-24: multi-summon Farewells "cause more full heart damage and can build boardstates that feel tough to fight through".
- 2026-02-19: captains whose engine fires on a turn count (Jason, Melody) were sped up so they aren't "behind by a captain ability" late in the game.

**Board position, Support and back row**
- Supports give buffs to "the characters in front of me". Back slots are named on cards from 2024-10-10.
- 2024-10-10: stats shown while dragging leave out support and slot buffs. Both are positional.
- 2025-05-19: Mirror Mirror's support punishes Ranged attackers on the character it supports. Ranged is treated as needing a positional counter.
- 2025-09-18: Parting Gift was limited to the front row, a nerf done through position.
- 2025-11-20: Sword of Valor was buffed because its "positional requirements" made it a weak pick. Supports are "so lowly statted", so support-payoff treasures need extra help.
- 2026-01-15: being "attacked first" is a recognised weakness of the front slot (Big Bad Wolf got more Health).
- 2026-08-14: Hunt-matters Supports were buffed. Morgan Le Fae was retuned to fit Ranged.
- Slots carry persistent buffs (slot buffs, Paint). Several cards and captains interact with them (Vena Cava converts them on turn 8; Ham-Star multiplied them and was cut).

**Economy-like resources (Galaxy has no gold)**
- Shops are the main spendable resource. Bonus shops come from goldens, silvers, losses and captains. Most captain power is tuned as extra or altered shops (Toucan Samuel, Itty Aerie, Rumpelstiltskin, Legend of Loxley). This is why the shop cap was needed. INFERRED.
- Treasures are the second resource. Their supply is tuned often (Treasure Hoard values cut in 2025-08-18; Frequent Flyer Card shelved "enables treasure strategies a bit too well"; Secret Rare treasures kept off generic generators).
- 2025-08-18 / 2025-11-20: treasure choice is a pick of 3. Modifiers change the pick (Lucky Chest, Con Artist's single higher-rarity choice). Treasure Find adds a dedicated option.
- Candy's shared Sweeten counter and Paint are per-run build-up resources. Both are gated so their spells appear only if your deck uses them.

**Comeback and anti-snowball tools (INFERRED pattern)**
- Loss-triggered rewards appear again and again:
  - Ace of Hearts: bonus shop after a loss.
  - Mask of Infinite Rage: doubles on loss.
  - Zagreus: treasure when a heart is emptied.
  - Gloria Clementine: bonus shop after a loss.
  - Gokatoo: retriggers at low hearts.
  - Mount Mimic: Lesser Mimic on loss.
- Zagreus' pitch says it outright: "Every stumble fuels your rise."

**Collection and fairness**
- 2026-02-19: a collectible card's deck slot is a cost that justifies extra power (Jumping Jelly Beans).
- 2026-08-12: competitive events give every player all Set Rerolls so purchases create no "uneven advantage".
- 2026-06-11: low pick rate is a reason to buff even when win rate is fine (CEO and Arthur).
- 2026-10-26: daily-session players are the majority. Rewards are meant to avoid "the kind of FOMO you see in other games".
