# Taste calls: rough power check

**Method and stopping point.** Needed a repeatable price without a balance simulator; weighed hand damage budgets against fight outcomes.
Chose ten fixed layouts, two size parameters and five plain-carrier rungs, frozen before results; the numbers rest on those layouts, not a metagame distribution.

**Unit of price.** A whole-board multiplier hides a single utility card's contribution; raw damage misses protection and can reward overkill.
Chose a plain replacement carrier matched on surviving-Health margin, with its total Attack and Health scaled; any plateau or failure of the calibration must be reported.

**Existing effects.** Hit's trigger varies, and the pigeon row volley is slot damage rather than full Attack; weighed one generic Hit against named deployments.
Chose separate Mouse deployments and the real Lieutenant hook, stripping other text and holding setup stats equal; conclusions depend on that named trigger and established slot buffs.

**Calibration failure.** The frozen trial missed Cleave's bar and hid the prepared pigeon board behind inactive cases; weighed a revised average against reporting the miss.
Kept the cases, parameters and median intact, added the good-setup range and a provisional warning to costing; a single unconditional multiplier remains unvalidated.

**One authorized control repair.** After seeing trial one, its buff control required a measure that sees known stats when final Health ties; weighed a weighted score against a tie-break.
Chose starting Attack + Health only to break equal final margins, tested the buff alone (all ten ×1.5), then froze it; utility with unchanged final outcomes can still be missed.

**Built-around report and ceiling.** Needed one prewritten meaning of setup, without dropping weak outcomes; weighed hook calls against actual effect use.
Chose at least one actual Hit, splash, summon, stat addition or positive volley, with built-around median first and all-ten second; extended comparisons to ×5/×6/×8 and kept any remaining bound.

**Freeze after the one rerun.** The repaired control reads ×1.5 in all ten; built-around Cleave reads ×3, while its all-ten result remains ×1.25.
Kept both complete tables and all weak fired cases; no further adjustment. The setup summary, rather than the measure repair, explains Cleave's different headline, and single-unit Hit uses retain their own prices.
