# Our attack, measured on film (R3, 24 Nov 2026)

Today's build (`ideation/fight-animation` at `ec669629`, the same fight code as `dev`), filmed at a true 60 frames a second with `tools/feel`. Frames are film frames (1 frame = 16.7 ms). Same columns as the footage tables (R1, R2).

## What this means for the owner's three claims, our side

- **"Travel all the way to the card they're hitting":** ours stops at 0.62 card heights between centres, overlapping the target by 38 %. It is a collision, but not a full meeting.
- **"Don't move at a consistent speed":** ours eases in (accelerates into the hit), but every attack takes the same 8 frames whatever the distance, so speed scales with distance. No slow-down before contact, no anticipation pause at the end of the wind-up.
- **"Don't pause mid-animation if they trigger something else":** ours does pause. Anything logged between the attack and its damage freezes the attacker at contact for 0.3 s per step (measured 23 and 41 frames); anything logged after the damage plays after the attacker is home, with the hit already gone.
- **"Wind-up very clearly visible":** ours is 4 frames and 12 px (about 5 % of a card's height). The 17 Nov work filmed a 6-frame option (`fight-windup-long.json`); 4 is live.

## Method

- Films: `tools/feel/film` (Godot 4.7.2, `--write-movie --fixed-fps 60`, software OpenGL under Xvfb, sound off). Scene: a scratch copy of `tests/screens/feel/fight_film.gd` (the repo's fight film fixture) that also logs every card's and burst's position, turn, scale and opacity on `RenderingServer.frame_pre_draw`, so each logged frame is the frame drawn and written. The fight is `tests/screens/fight_stand_in.gd`'s fights 0 and 1: real rules core and real fight screen, but the core's neutral test units (Divine Shield, Windfury, Cleave, Venomous, Reborn, Taunt, Stealth, a start-of-fight hit, death summons, an Avenge buff, a Rally gain) with stand-in art, not tribe cards. The motion code does not depend on which cards fight.
- Positions are **from the engine, frame by frame, during the filmed run** (card node position plus half its size), not tracked from pixels. Cross-checked by eye against film frames (strips at frames 41–86 of fight 0 and 588–700 of fight 1: contact, hold and the hit appear on the frames the log says), and against the tween maths in `fight_motion.gd` (the quad ease-in progress values match to three decimals).
- `ours_positions.csv`: one row per frame per attack, from 2 frames before the wind-up to the next attack's start. `clip_id` = `ours-f<fight>-a<attack>`; `x_norm`, `y_norm` = the attacker's card centre as a share of the 1920 × 1080 film; `note` = progress along home→contact (`p`, 0 home, 1 contact), turn in degrees, scale, replay step, and markers (`windup_start`, `windup_end`, `contact`, `hit_shown`, `leaves_contact`, `home`). Attacks: fight 0 a1 (Divine Shield), a2, a4, a7 (plain), a3 and a6 (with summons after), a5 (Venomous); fight 1 a1 (shield), a6 (Rally: the attacker is also lifted as a trigger, so "home" is 14 px off and never marked), the rest plain.
- Step lengths are whole frames: 0.22 s plays as 14 frames, 0.3 s as 19, 0.35 s as 22, 0.55 s as 34, 0.14 s as 9 (`FightScreen._process` moves on when the wait drops to 0 or below).
- Not measured: anything on real hardware, sound timing (the film is silent; sound delays use the wall clock), and how any of it feels.

Scratch (not committed): `/data/fight-anim-work/r3/` holds the films (`fight0.mp4`, `fight1.mp4`), their full reports, the fixture and the analysis script.
