All clips

The moments, in order (times as today plays them; the proposal reaches each a little later at first, then sooner): a plain attack straight across (0.8 s), one to the far corner (1.7 s), an attack into Thorns (2.8 s), a Thorns kill whose death summons a Hopper (5.0 s), and a death that damages all seven cards, after which Old Brock triggers six times (7.5–9.5 s). Today's Victory shows at 11.5 s, the proposal's at 10.6 s. No sound in any clip.

The proposal in five lines

  1. A wind-up you can see. The attacker lifts toward you (×1.14 over 10 frames, with today's small pull-back), then holds dead still for 16 frames. All waiting happens in that raised hold.
  2. One straight strike at constant speed, 7–9 frames at any distance, so far attacks fly fast. It overlaps the target, pops ×1.24 on contact, stays 0 frames, and returns fast then slow.
  3. A moving card never pauses. What happens before the hit (Thorns, Fury, Rally, pile-ons) plays in the raised hold; this is a guess, as no reference game showed an attacker's own trigger before its hit. The exchange lands at contact. Everything after waits until the attacker is home.
  4. After the hit, one beat at a time, each overlapping the last one's tail. A dead card falls 30 frames after the hit, so its number reads first. The next attacker lifts under the aftermath.
  5. Repeats become a combo that speeds up, so one repeat group stays under about 1.6 s (a whole aftermath with several groups can run longer). A plain attack takes 61 frames, against today's 57.

05

The decision tree

Every kind of event our fights produce (29 types), what is shown, when it starts, what waits for it, how repeats group, and its cost in frames. Events our game can't produce today (instead_of_attack, an in-fight counter) are left out. CB marks a branch decided above.

Ordering rules behind the tree

  1. Nothing pauses once it moves. A strike, return, fall or arrival always runs to its end.
  2. Each attack splits into three windows, keyed on the attack itself, not on its first damage. Before the hit plays in the raised hold, which stretches to fit any effect, arrival or nested attack (a guess; see decision 4). At the hit: the exchange, strike back, Cleave, shield, Venomous, all at C+2; Overrun hops from C+8. After the hit starts when the attacker is home (C+14 to C+17).
  3. A beat is one trigger and its effect: the source pulses, jabs toward its target (standing in for a drawn link, which needs art), and the effect lands 12 frames in. The next beat starts 6 frames after. One source on many cards is one beat. Different mechanics play one at a time, in the rules' order.
  4. Deaths fall together: C+30 when killed in the exchange, 20 frames after the killing effect otherwise. A lender's aura leaves at the killing hit, when the rules remove it.
  5. Combo: 3 or more of the same mechanic back to back. Each gap is 0.75 of the last, down to 5 frames; from the 9th, the rest land together.
  6. The next attacker lifts at C+28 or when 16 frames of the queue are left, whichever is later.
  7. Speed-up: if one moment's aftermath would pass 2 s, beats after the first second play up to 1.5× faster. Strikes keep their frames. A guess, untested.
  8. Extra attacks (Windfury, Oakfeller, Tourney Champion) wait for the death round before them: a short re-lift when 14 frames of that queue are left, the strike after it ends. Oakfeller always waits for its kill's fall. Another unit out of turn gets a full lift once its cause lands.
