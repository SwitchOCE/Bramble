# Mouse and Pigeon tuning, round 2

This proposal starts from dev `9182e381` with round 1 applied. Give the Mice enough printed Health to get more attacks and keep their combat setup alive. Give Pigeons one earlier source of Renown. The placement targets are 4.3–4.6 for Mouse-locked seats, 4.2–4.4 for Pigeon-locked seats, and within the other three tribes’ range in A/A. Round 1’s reference range was 3.55–4.22. Round 2 placements are unmeasured.

## Diagnosis

1. **More Attack helped, and the Health gap remained.** In round 1, Mouse lost 354 of 600 A/A non-ghost fights in rounds 6–10 where it started with more Attack and less Health than its opponent. Mouse-locked seats lost 559 of 882 such fights against open blue seats. At round 8 in A/A, Mouse front rows had 31 Health to their opponents’ 63 on the same number of starters. Across rounds 6–10 losses, about 3.5 opposing starters were still alive. [A/A games](/data/measure/reworks/tune/tribe-aa/games.jsonl) · [Mouse-locked games](/data/measure/reworks/tune/tribe-mouse/games.jsonl)
2. **Some Mice died without taking an attack action.** In 158 of 758 A/A Mouse losses in rounds 6–10, the entire fight had fewer attack actions on both sides combined than Mouse had attack-capable starters. That guarantees at least one starter never took an attack action in those fights. The same lower bound was 208 of 1,308 for Mouse-locked losses to open seats. In direct A/A fights against Locust, Mouse lost 210 of 242 despite starting at 92/50 Attack/Health to Locust’s 75/63. Across all their A/A losses in the same rounds, Locusts got about 3.35 extra lives per fight from arrivals and returns, versus 0.33 for Mice, estimated as deaths plus survivors minus starters. [Fight summary fields](/data/wt-m-tune/tools/league/league/LOGS.md:72)
3. **Pilers usually had a neighbour and often fired, especially in the rear.** At round 6, only 1 of 2,025 starting Mouse-locked pilers, including Drill-sergeant grants, lacked a front neighbour eligible to attack at setup. Among round 7–9 losses with exactly one Cutpurse, no Relic Seeker on the board and no Active used that turn, Cutpurse’s gold confirms a pile-on in 270 of 321 rear cases (84%) and 18 of 55 front cases (33%). Rear Fencing Masters averaged 2.02 credited kills versus 1.01 in front across all locked fights in those rounds. The row splits are observational. A payout establishes at least one proc on Cutpurse, and credited kills can come from a hit, swing or retaliation. [Mouse-locked games](/data/measure/reworks/tune/tribe-mouse/games.jsonl) · [Gold accounting](/data/wt-m-tune/tools/league/league/src/game.rs:881)
4. **Hand-me-down routing leaves the Sister line underused.** On round-8 Mouse-locked boards, 195 of 279 Needle Sisters started in front and 212 were outside first-heir position for every starting donor under default routing. That ranks all live starting candidates by neighbour steps and then slot order. Death order, arrivals and Widow Thimble can change those routes. A donor with no living Mouse heir loses its gift, and a gift on a unit that dies before using it can go to waste. The logs cannot quantify either loss. [Mouse-locked games](/data/measure/reworks/tune/tribe-mouse/games.jsonl) · [Hand-me-down rules](/data/wt-m-tune/src/content/mouse/lib/mouse_rules.gd:61)
5. **Overkill is plausible and varies by matchup.** In 67% of the 559 locked losses with more Attack and less Health, a Mouse-board starter’s pre-fight Attack already exceeded every opposing front-row starter’s Health. That is a threshold comparison and the target and connecting damage are unknown. Against Badger, the highest-Attack Mouse starter got zero credited kills in 57% of 172 losses, consistent with some boards still needing help to finish the first large target. The 400 replay records contain actions, pre-fight boards and checkpoints, and both `reports.jsonl` files are empty. The fight summaries in `games.jsonl` leave the individual attack sequence, unused charges, inherits and damage in excess of target Health unknown. [A/A games](/data/measure/reworks/tune/tribe-aa/games.jsonl) · [Mouse-locked games](/data/measure/reworks/tune/tribe-mouse/games.jsonl)

Fight counts are seat-fights at rounds 6–10 unless specified. A/A labels use the final board’s main tribe and can include mixed boards. Locked matchup counts use red Mouse seats against live blue seats. The placement and fight aggregates describe TribeBot play on these seeds.

## Proposed changes

Costs use round 1’s Bramble body proxies of 7.5, 9, 12, 14, 18 and 22 printed stats at T1–T6. Health costs below are printed body costs for a normal copy. The extra attack or trigger a unit may get from surviving is matchup-dependent. [Tier guide](/data/wt-m-new/docs/design/tier-guide.md) · [Round 1 costing](/data/balance-endcheck/tuning/proposal.md)

|---|---|---|---|---|
| Mouse | Hedgerow Scout (T1) | Health **4 → 10** | Give the common opening front unit time for a swing that the adjacent Page can join. | +6 Health, **+0.80 body**, 7 → 13 printed stats. Deliberately above a T1 body. |
| Mouse | Hayloft Aunt (T2) | Health **4 → 9** | Add a second early front unit that can hold a lane and eventually pass its Attack. | +5 Health, **+0.56 body**, 6 → 11 printed stats. Its Recruit already supplies a 2/2 Cousin. |
| Mouse | Needle Sister (T3) | Health **4 → 12** | Give her a chance to survive until a donor dies and to take the resulting attack. | +8 Health, **+0.67 body**, 8 → 16 printed stats. Her two possible immediate attacks still depend on receiving gifts. |
| Mouse | Thimble Sentinel (T4) | Health **10 → 24** | Give the Mouse Guard enough Health to take a second hit in some midgame fights, keeping neighbouring attackers and heirs alive. | +14 Health, **+1.00 body**, 13 → 27 printed stats. Deliberately high for a Guard. |
| Mouse | Parish Standard-bearer (T5) | Health **8 → 16** | Give a frequent late pickup enough Health to contribute after its opening buff. | +8 Health, **+0.44 body**, 14 → 22 printed stats. Its existing fight buff is already **6 bodies** at six Mice. |
| Mouse | King Speck (T6) | Health **14 → 24** | Give the capstone time to attack once slots open, so more fights can reach its existing refill. | +10 Health, **+0.45 body**, 20 → 30 printed stats. The Cousins still depend on empty slots. |
| Pigeon | Lance Corporal (T2) | Renown per slot **1 → 2** | Strengthen two early slots before the midgame losses, with the existing 1-gold Active. | Two starters gain 4 → 8 stats per use (**0.44 → 0.89 body** before gold). With one ordinary refill, next-fight value is 6 → 12 stats (**0.67 → 1.33 bodies**), above the T2 range at the high end. |

### Why these levers

- **Mouse.** The six edits add about **11/16/18/20/26** Health at rounds 4/6/8/10/12 if applied to the exact recorded A/A Mouse boards, including golden copies. About **11/14/13/14/18** goes to the front. On locked boards the corresponding total is **12/16/19/21/22**. These are estimates on fixed lineups. In the locked run the bot bought the Scout on 52% of buyable offers, the Sentinel on 61%, and King Speck on 93%. The Fencing Master already converts rear placement into kills, and Piper and Cook already pay above their tier budgets. Putting the new Health on frontline bodies, an heir and a late refill gives their existing plans more opportunities to fire. Scout and Aunt can inherit and trade, while Sentinel will sometimes delay its own gift as it protects the board. The T4 3/24 Guard and its 6/48 golden copy are the clearest early high rolls. Standard-bearer still casts its opening buff immediately, making its own Health a smaller addition relative to the buff. [Mouse-locked shops](/data/measure/reworks/tune/tribe-mouse/shops.jsonl.gz) · [A/A games](/data/measure/reworks/tune/tribe-aa/games.jsonl)
- **Pigeon.** Locked seats won about 40% of rounds 3–6 fights and 56% of rounds 10–12 fights against live blue opponents. They bought Lance Corporal on 842 of 2,135 buyable offers and activated it 519 times in round 6 across 800 locked seats. Old Campaigner grows one slot and rewards an existing survivor. Recruiting Sergeant already brings an extra body into a buffed slot. Lance Corporal is an earlier, reliable way to improve two lanes while the board is still filling. Empty slots bank the Renown, an ordinary refill reuses it, and the golden spends the same 1 gold for 4 Renown per slot. Stacking copies on the same slots is the main high roll. [Pigeon-locked games](/data/measure/reworks/tune/tribe-pigeon/games.jsonl) · [Pigeon-locked decisions](/data/measure/reworks/tune/tribe-pigeon/decisions.jsonl.gz)

## Bot verdict

I think number-only tuning can plausibly close the measured placement gap. The survivability deficit is large and measurable, and the rear pile-on results show that TribeBot can use the mechanic. Exact target placements and the power of a well-positioned Mouse board remain uncertain. If this Health pass still leaves a large gap, fix positioning before using the ladder result to justify another large buff.

The bot owners should score legal swaps and the final formation using actual Pile on hooks, including Drill-sergeant grants. Put protected pilers next to early, healthy attackers, keep non-attacking Pikemen out of exposed slots, and account for Guard. For Hand-me-down, estimate which donors will die first and apply the engine’s nearest-live-Mouse rule and reading-order ties. Prefer routes into a living Sister or another protected heir set up to use the Attack. Revisit the formation after recruits and sells. ## Tier check

- **T1.** Scout gets an oversized tempo body. Page is the early pile-on payoff.
- **T2.** Aunt is a sturdier enabler. Lance Corporal’s early growth runs high when refills reuse both lanes.
- **T3.** Sister gets the Health to use her existing charges. A working gift route still makes the difference.
- **T4.** Sentinel is intentionally near twice the median printed body so it can anchor the midgame.
- **T5.** Standard-bearer’s existing wide-board payoff remains the bulk of its power. It gains a moderate body for the tier.
- **T6.** Speck gets a better chance to deliver its existing refill. The rate and stats of Cousin summons are the existing values.
