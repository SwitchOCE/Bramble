THE PROBLEM, IN THE OWNER'S WORDS (19 Nov 2026)
- Pigeons: slots become companies with Renown; "renown gives stats only to starting units or reservists"; "the Pigeons' whole thing is that they can have summons that can be buffed by renown".
- Active: a global keyword shown as "a little zero ... or a one inside the gold icon", then a colon. "We don't need to say it's an Active."
- Charge: a global "set number of uses" to "make more intuitive to players, shorten our tooltips, and also give us more design space".
- Mice: "less fragile, and stronger and more varied"; the Needle Pikeman "needs to be a tier six".
- Process: "progress forward without asking for my input on this stuff ... go with your recommendations".
- Larger goal: a third bot run on the game "as it will really be played" (async, ghosts), and the owner playing those ghosts in the Windows build. The ghost orchestrator asked that Renown and Supply be saved in the fight snapshot.

CODE FACTS CHECKED (they change several verdicts)
F1. Per-fight limits already exist as a pattern. MechanicParams has Kind.THRESHOLD, "never doubled" by golden (src/core/mechanics/mechanic_params.gd:5,77). Padre, Recruiting Sergeant, Pikeman and Sett Sexton already count uses with CombatUnit.fight_count and log uses left with Combat.show_uses (src/core/combat/combat.gd:677). The fight replay already shows "uses" changes (src/screens/fight/fight_replay.gd:46,194), and there is a "Left this fight" display entry (pigeon_padre_uses). A golden that doubles its limit (Recruiting Sergeant, Bugler, Air Marshal) is a plain NUMBER param.
F2. Triples already add unit counters together (src/core/recruit/recruit.gd:759-760). So Supply stored as uses left in Unit.counters gets "a triple adds the copies' remaining Supply" for free.
F3. Snapshots: RecordState.fields() saves every script variable, so SlotState.renown reaches fight_boards slots and the state hash by itself. Unit counters already round-trip through FightSnapshot.unit/restore_unit, and tests/game/test_records.gd:248 already proves it. Nothing restores slots from a payload. That is the ghost orchestrator's async-game work (ghost-plan step 3).
F4. Once a turn for an ability is already counted (turn_counters, recruit.gd:353), and has_ability/ability_cost already exist. S1 really is display-only.
F5. Pills show a display entry's name ("Order (1)", src/screens/card_terms.gd:7). A visible "active" entry would print the word the owner said not to print. Entries have a "hidden" flag.
F6. After S5 and S7, no card gives slot stat buffs. Only Pigeon cards and the three Mouse cards S7 cuts (Whetstone, Grindstone, Edgewright) use them. FightSnapshot.LAYERS still carries slot_attack/slot_health into the ghost format.
F7. The Rust engine, core_fields.json and TribeBot are on orch/ghost-system, not on dev. TribeBot is a reference copy, "copied without change", that the Rust port is checked against. The frozen league bots (red-m2, blue-m2) build the first field of ghosts, and they never hold reservists or use Orders on purpose (pitch-pigeon section 6, risk 7).
F8. Approved cards with a Charge all refill per fight: Recruiting Sergeant, Padre, Flying Ace, Bugler, Air Marshal, and the Mice's four "(N a fight)" limits. No approved card has a Charge in the Recruit Phase, and none combines Active with Charge.

- Restoring slots from a payload, and a real restore-then-fight: the ghost orchestrator's async game (ghost-plan step 3).

- The neutral pool (Harrier, Ballista): the owner deciding the pool's rules.

OPEN QUESTIONS (only what the owner must decide)
1. Timing against the ghost work. Should the reworks land before the ghost orchestrator's first field of ghosts and the owner's async Windows build (about 24 Nov)? If yes, those wait on two Rust ports. If no, the owner first plays ghosts with the old Pigeons and Mice, and the field is refilled from bots once the reworks land.
2. How the reworks are measured. The frozen league bots never hold reservists or use Actives on purpose, and they have no Mouse-specific play. Owner note 19a's caveat says a bot's preference only counts where it can play the cards. Options:
   - Measure with the TribeBot changes only.
   - Teach the league bots Reserve, Active and Hand-me-down first.
   - Accept that Pigeons' reserve plan and the Kin will read low until the third run's teams learn them. The balance sign-off comes before the third run, so this question is circular.
3. (Minor, default given) Whether the new cards play with stand-in art in the owner's build. Default: stand-ins until they survive the first measurement.
