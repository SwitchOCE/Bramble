## What this round is

A layout round. Every card is an empty pane on purpose: there is no card art, and its colour is being handled separately as a recommendation to the owner. Do not judge colour harmony with card art, and do not ask for dimming, darkening or desaturating anything: the owner rejected that. Judge layout: card size, gaps, empty space, grouping, where things sit, and whether the scene has its joy.
