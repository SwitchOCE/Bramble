## 2. The frame, three takes (at 112 px, blank and with art)

- **A:** Much quieter than today's frame. It is mostly lead, but the lead is a hairline. A higher tier is only a different hue, not "more". The six tiers can be told apart, though T3 cyan and T4 violet are close because T3's petals are violet. A golden unit is only a thin brass edge. Next to a T5's gold glass in the fight it is missable. The big yellow discs crowd the bottom of the art, and Attack and Health look the same.
- **D:** Today's glass and ornament, unchanged, below plain dark lead. It is quieter than today's. Most of it is lead. The tier number sits in a coloured roundel at the top, and from T4 a coloured thread runs up the lead, so higher tiers stand out at a glance and all six are clear. Golden gilds the whole frame and can't be missed. The light hex and round stat discs read instantly. The heavy lead frames the art without fighting it.
- **E:** The quietest of the three. The ornament grows with tier, which is the right idea, but at 112 px the petals are too small to see. The glass is flattened into bars, so the owner's "current stuff… with the ornamentation" is lost. The tier tab carries no colour. Golden is unmissable.

**Ranking:**
1. **D:** closest to the owner's words (today's glass at the bottom, lead above), and the clearest tier and golden cues.
2. **A:** already fits the screen and is calm, but tiers differ only by hue and golden is weak.
3. **E:** calmest, but it drops today's glass, and its tier cue doesn't survive at 112 px.

**Proportion:** a third. A quarter cuts through the stat discs; a half brings the busy side glass back.
