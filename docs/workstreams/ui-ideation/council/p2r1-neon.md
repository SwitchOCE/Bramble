## Concept A — The great window, refit

**1. Against the owner's direction.**
Card explanations and row labels are gone at rest. Residual zone headers remain: `Shop`, `Standings`, `Hand` (`mid`, `first`), `Fight`, `OPPONENT` (`fight`), `March Hare's board`, `Turn 9 standings` (`after`), plus the turn-1 cue (`first`). Hover tooltips work beside cards and flip inward at edges (`hover-board`, `hover-spell`). Keyword reminders stay in one fixed left-light slot (`hover-shop`, `hover-board`) and disappear cleanly (`hover-spell`, `keywords-off`). The lead frame is calmer, arched spell tiles read distinctly, and tier numerals (`1`–`6`) stay instantly legible—though tier tints barely separate and Golden is far too subtle. No next opponent's board is shown.

**2. Where the eye goes.**
On `mid`: first the warm shop row, second your board, third `20 Gold` and amber `End turn` (competing with bright white `Standings` numbers). Right buying order, though the shop-to-board brightness drop is too gentle to quiet bright board art (Wing Commander).

**3. Defects.**
- `fight` (left light): hand/reserves vanish, hiding waiting reservists.
- `mid`, `first` (left light, Hand): cramped 4+3 slots shrink `Seek` into a dark sliver with no visible cost.
- `fight`, `after`, `cards` (Golden Gorger): Golden's brass seam barely reads; buffed (`mid` `15/11`) and wounded (`after` `9/2`) stats lack clear visual contrast.
- `mid`, `first` (shop right): stacked `Sharpen` tiles put prices to the right instead of on the shop baseline.
- `mid`, `fight` (board slots): Renown pills (`☆7`, `☆2`, `☆3`) sit over card art and share the tier seals' muted palette.

**4. Next round.**
1. Make Golden unmistakable and distinguish buffed/wounded stat states visually at rest.
2. Restore fight reserves and give the recruit hand room to show cards and costs.
3. Deepen shop-vs-board lighting contrast and drop redundant zone headers (`Shop`, `Fight`, `OPPONENT`).

## Concept D — The glazier's bench

**1. Against the owner's direction.**
Card explanations and row labels are gone at rest. Residual headers remain: `Hand`, `Recruit` (`mid`, `first`), `Reserves in hand`, `If you lose`, `Fight`, `FIGHTING` (`fight`), `Why −13`, `Fight over`, `WHAT BEAT YOU` (`after`), plus the turn-1 prompt (`first`). Hover tooltips sit cleanly by cards and flip inward (`hover-board`, `hover-spell`). Keyword reminders stay in one fixed bottom-left shelf (`hover-shop`, `hover-board`) and vanish cleanly (`hover-spell`, `keywords-off`). The smoky lead frame is calm, Golden glows unmistakably, and round teal spell medallions read crisply; counting 4–6 bottom beads is slow and clashes with Charge pips. No next opponent's board is shown.

**2. Where the eye goes.**
On `mid`: first glaring pale-yellow `End turn` bottom right, second bright gold coins and portrait, third your board. Backwards for buying: `End turn` dominates with 20 gold unspent while shop and table share the same flat beige value.

**3. Defects.**
- `mid`, `fight`, `after`, `cards` (Wing Commander, Weasel bottom): white Charge pips stack directly over white Tier beads (`••••` over `•••••`), fusing into one cluster.
- `mid` (bottom right, shop, board): glaring `End turn` hijacks focus; shop and board lack lighting separation.
- `mid`, `fight`, `after` (stat stones, `fight` Reserves): two-digit stones feel tight, buff chevrons crowd rims, Reserve dots cramp beneath stones.
- `mid`, `fight`, `after` (between rows): violet Renown diamonds wedge tightly against neighboring tips (`fight` `4`/`7` crowd Gorger; `after` `3` overlaps Briar King).
- `fight`, `after`, `cards`: flat triangles read as confetti; `cards` omits 2× Tiers 2–5 and Golden; `16px` shelf/subtext runs small.

**4. Next round.**
1. Rebalance `mid` lighting: shop leads, board follows, `End turn` quiets while gold remains.
2. Replace countable tier beads so tiers read instantly without clashing with Charge pips.
3. Loosen stat-stone/Renown clearances and refine broken glass.

## Concept E — Glass in the forest

**1. Against the owner's direction.**
Card explanations and all zone headers (`Shop`, `Standings`, `Hand`, rows) are gone at rest—cleanest compliance. Cards keep small keyword marks; `after` stamps `Fell` on six dead cards beside the recap plaque; `first` has the turn-1 cue. Hover tooltips pin beside cards (`hover-shop`, `hover-board`) and flip inward (`hover-spell`); `extra1` shows March Hare's stats/history on lantern hover, never a board. Keyword reminders hold one fixed lower-left oak dock (`hover-shop`, `hover-board`) and hide cleanly (`hover-spell`, `keywords-off`). The metallic-came frame, Golden glow and spoked rondels cohere well, though Tiers 1–3 metals blur and Tier 5–6 beads spill outside.

**2. Where the eye goes.**
On `mid`: first the sunlit shop, second bright `Fight March Hare` (`20` Gold close behind), third your dimmed board. Nearly ideal—the only concept taming noisy art—though March Hare's lit portrait pulls slightly early with 20 gold left.

**3. Defects.**
- `mid` (Wing Commander), `fight` (Briar King, Weasel, Eighth Plague, Bodkin), `cards` (Tiers 5–6): top beads overflow the cap past the hexagon's roof.
- `mid`, `fight` (hand log): `Seek` floats off-centre with unclear 7-card capacity; in `fight`, `Home Guard`'s `11` overlaps `Hayloft Aunt`.
- `mid`, `first`, `fight`, `cards` (bottom point): tiny keyword circles crowd the bevel (`Home Guard`) and lose legibility.
- `mid` (stats, Upgrade): buffed stats (`15/11`, `11/17`) match base stats; `Upgrade`'s discount is missing.
- `hover-board`, `after`, `cards`: `hover-board` never tests an edge flip (no hover shows a pointer); `after` plaque crowds `☆7`; `cards` omits 2× Tiers 1, 2, 4, 5.

**4. Next round.**
1. Fix Tier 5–6 geometry and replace countable beads with an at-a-glance tier signal inside the frame.
2. Redesign the hand log so seven cards and reserves fit cleanly with clear capacity.
3. Quiet `Fight March Hare` while gold remains, distinguish buffed stats, and clean up keyword marks.

## 5. Ranking and Cross-Pollination

1. **E (1st):** Best adherence to the uncluttered vision and the only concept that actually tames bright card art into a readable visual hierarchy, with fixable geometry/hand bugs.
2. **A (2nd):** Cleanest overall craft, clearest tier legibility and sharpest hover/keyword-dock integration, held back by timid Golden/stat signals, missing fight reserves and gentle shop-vs-board contrast.
3. **D (3rd):** Strongest Golden frame, spell medallions and stat-change readability, but currently undermined by a backwards `mid` eye order (`End turn` first) and the Charge-vs-Tier dot collision.

**One idea from each that the others should borrow:**
- **From A:** Numeral tier seals (`1`–`6`) so tiers read instantaneously without counting dots or colliding with Charge pips.
- **From D:** Distinct visual states for buffed and damaged stats so a hundredth-game player reads stat deltas at rest without text.
- **From E:** Zone-based lighting and saturation hierarchy (lit shop over a subtly quieted board, with zero zone headers) to direct the eye across noisy art.
