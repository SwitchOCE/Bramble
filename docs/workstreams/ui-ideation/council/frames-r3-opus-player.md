**2. What round 3 lost.** The golden rim. Round 2's modelled gilt is now a thin, flat mustard line. The lead is consistent, but it reads more like a UI bevel than soldered came.

**3. At 112 px on teal.** Yes, it reads as a published board piece, near a Battlegrounds token: the art leads and the frame is quiet. All six tiers can be named at a glance by hue. Still close: 5 and 6 over warm art (the pigeon next to the fire badger), and 3 and 4 in greyscale. Golden is clear here only because it is the one warm rim. On yellow art it is not unmissable: the golden grasshopper and golden tier 2 blend into the thin gilt. Gold means only golden: yes.

**4. Craft at 300 px.** Tier 1's base is tiled slab. Tier 4's cyan diamonds look pasted on. Tier 5's leaf glass is soft beside tier 3's crisp leading. The disc faces have procedural-looking diagonal stripes. Glow, sheen and motion: not judged.

**5. Put it to the owner?** **Yes, with reservations.** What decides it: the unevenness between tiers is gone. It is one built system in which only the glass changes, and gold means golden.

**6. Still needed**

1. A heavier, modelled golden rim, like round 2's gilt and as wide as the lead, so it shows on yellow art.
2. Tier 1's base rebuilt as leaded grey glass, and the same glass on golden tier 1.
3. Tier 6 a clear step above tier 5 in glass mass or value.
