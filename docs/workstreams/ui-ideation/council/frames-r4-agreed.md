# Frame council, re-check of take M at round 4: what the council said

Put together by the orchestrator (an agent) from the three voting members' answers (`frames-r4-opus-player.md`, `frames-r4-opus-craft.md`, `frames-r4-neon.md`). The assembly is clerical: an item is "agreed" when at least two of the three voters raised it. Nothing here is the orchestrator's judgement. The owner had not reacted to round 4 when this was written; the owner's words outrank all of it.

## The owner's point: does the border blend?

Yes, three of three. "Lead and glass read as one border of about one width ... nothing steps where lead meets glass" (player). "One band of even width that turns from lead to glass and widens to a shallow V at the foot ... Round 3's jump from thin lead to a tall glass collar is gone" (craft). "Lead and glass blend as one continuous piece with nothing jarring" (neon).

"Flow" answers it better than "near", three of three: in flow the stat discs sit in the border; in near they float over the art.

## Is it good design?

- Player: "Yes. The first take that could sit beside a published frame without looking amateur."
- Neon: "Yes—at 112 px `flow` sits beside published board tokens without looking amateur."
- Craft: "Nearly. The outline and the balance of frame against art would hold up beside published frames ... Weakest: the materials, meaning the lead and the glass."

## What round 4 gained

One even outline and more art (all three). Stat discs ringed in the tier's glass, no longer flat chips (all three). Golden: "the best golden so far and unmissable even on yellow art" (player), "rich bevelled golden metal" (neon); craft disagrees: "still flat mustard".

## Agreed, still open

1. **The glass is a texture, not cut glass.** "A twisted-rope texture tiled along the edge: machine-made, not cut glass. Lead lines cross the strip at fixed steps" (player). "Looks like a texture clipped to the band ... instead of being cut into panes" (craft). "Airbrushed twisted rope, polygon-clipped mid-curve" (neon). All three ask for real cut panes, each edged in lead, fitted to the band.
2. **Tier reads more weakly than in round 3.** The glass is now a thin strip, and round 3's leaf and petal shapes are gone, so a tier is only a colour. Tiers 5 and 6 merge into warm art; in greyscale neighbouring tiers look alike. (all three) Their suggestions differ: a cut pattern per tier and a dark lead line between strip and picture (player); about a third more glass height (craft); tier-scaled pane work at the base (neon).
3. **The lead is still a smooth bevel.** No solder at the joints, no patina; "mathematically extruded". The golden rail should be the same shape in modelled gilt. (all three)
4. **Tier 1** still looks like marble, silver or rope, not pale clear glass, and golden tier 1 still pairs gold with silver. (all three)

One voter each: small glass spikes poke up above the discs (craft); the stat rings repeat a noisy mini-braid and want a cast collar (neon).

## Not judged

Glow, sheen and motion.
