# Frame council round 3: Opus, on craft

**1. The list**
1. Gold only golden: **done.** Ivory faces; tier 5 is now burnt orange, below the gilt in value.
2. One disc: **done.** Same size and place on every tier.
3. One upper came: **done** as one pewter came with a lit line on all six. It's crisp, but see 4.
4. Confusable tiers: **partly.** Tiers 5 and 6 now part in hue and value. In greyscale, 1 and 6 stand apart, but 2, 3 and 4 still differ only by a few px of side glass.
5. Sub-6 px bead and crest: **done.** Both are gone.
6. Tier 1: **partly.** The base is now leaded pale quarries. At 300 px they read as marble paving more than glass. Golden tier 1 still puts gilt on silver.
7. Tier 3 squeeze: **done.**
8. On the teal ground: **done.** The came holds even the darkest art (mantis, mole).
9. Spells: **done.** The ring is heavier and matches the cards.

**2. What round 3 lost.** The gilt went from bright gold to a muted brass-mustard. It still reads at 112 px, but less richly than round 2. The upper lead traded warmth for consistency: it's now a cool, even grey stroke, and the hand-painted glass below it makes it look drawn by a different hand. The join (rails part, leaf tip, knot) survived.

**3. At 112 px on teal.** Yes, it reads as a board piece from a published game: quiet, ordered, with art first. Hue tells the six tiers apart at a glance. The close pairs are now 2/3 by mass and 5/6 over warm sunset art, where the warm frame glass melts into the picture. Golden is unmissable on teal, because it is the only warm rim among the pewter ones, and gold now means only golden. Glow, sheen and motion are not judged.

**4. Craft at 300 px.**
- The upper came is a uniform vector stroke: same width, one flat highlight, no solder or patina. Beside the painted glass it looks machine-drawn.
- The disc faces have a streaky procedural marble, and the chip-segmented tier rings look like poker chips more than leaded roundels.
- Tier 4's cyan jewels and tier 1's grey slabs are the least glass-like pieces.

**5. Put it to the owner?** **Yes, with reservations.** What decides it: every agreed fix that the owner would see at board size is done, and none of round 2's strengths was lost. What remains shows at hover size.

**6. Still needed**
1. Give the upper came a cast-lead finish: slight width swell, a soft dull highlight, faint solder at the corners. It must stop reading as a UI stroke next to the glass.
2. Lead the disc rings as glass roundels in the tier's colour, at band saturation, and use a plain ivory face without the marbling.
3. Give tiers 2 to 4 a clearer step in glass height so the ladder holds in greyscale. Make the tier 1 base read as clear glass, not stone.
