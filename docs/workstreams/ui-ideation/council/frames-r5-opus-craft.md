# Frame council, round 5 re-check: visual craft (Opus)

**1. The owner's point.** Kept. At 112 px on teal the border is still one band of one width that turns from lead to glass and widens to a shallow V at the foot. Tiers 4 to 6 now run glass up the sides, but it tapers to a point, so nothing steps. One new jar: tier 1's near-white V is the brightest thing on the board. The width blends; the brightness doesn't.

**2. Gained and lost against round 4.**
- **Glass:** clearly gained. These are now real panes, each edged in lead and cut to fit the band: broad panes for tier 1, triangles for tier 2, facets for tier 3, diamonds for tier 4, a chain of lozenges for tier 5, jewels for tier 6.
- **Tiers:** gained. Each tier now has its own cut and its own glass height. In greyscale, tiers 1, 5 and 6 are distinct; tiers 2 to 4 are close at 112 px.
- **Golden:** about level. It reads clearly, but the rail is still a flat ochre copy of the lead's shape.
- **Lead:** no change.
- **Stat discs:** gained. They now have cut glass rings.
- **Frame against art:** level, apart from the side climb on the high tiers, which is a fair price for the tier signal.

**3. Good design?** Yes. It could now sit beside published frames.
- **Strongest:** one outline that carries a cut glass base, which gets richer with the tier.
- **Weakest:** the lead, still a smooth bevel drawn by code. Next weakest is tier 6, which is cut too fine. At 112 px it reads as red beadwork, not glass, and it blends into warm art.

**4. Craft at 300 px.**
- The rail has no solder knots where the band's lead lines meet it.
- The band's lead lines are thinner than the lead lines in the art, so frame and picture don't read as one window.
- Every pane carries the same painted diagonal streak, which looks generated.
- Thin glass slivers still poke up above the discs on tier 3.
- Tier 5's lozenges repeat at a fixed step.

**5. Ready?** Yes, subject to the owner's eye. What decides it: the glass is now cut panes. That was the last fault in the design itself. What remains is finish.

**6. Changes still needed**
1. **Lead finish.** Add solder knots at every joint, a slight hand-drawn wobble in width, and a dull patina. Make the band's lead lines as heavy as the art's. Give golden the same shape in modelled gilt.
2. **Tier 6, and to a lesser degree tier 5:** fewer, larger panes. Show rank through the shape of the panes, for example one jewel set at the V, not through how many pieces there are.
3. **Tier 1:** take the glass one step darker, to a pale clear glass with a slight tint, so the lowest tier isn't the loudest.

Glow, sheen and motion: not judged.
