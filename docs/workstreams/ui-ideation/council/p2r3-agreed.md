## One spacing rule (all three voters, nearly word for word)

- The gap across equals the gap down: about a quarter of the card's width (the player says 0.2 to 0.25, craft and neon 0.25). Across is measured between flat sides; down is measured tip to tip.
- Rows are offset by half a slot and never nested or interlocked.
- At least half a card's width between zones (shop to board, army to army), and about half a card between units and spells (neon). A lead line belongs in that gap, not through cards (craft). The shop and the board share one column pitch (craft).
- Best shown by E `mid` (player, neon) and by the board in A `mid` (craft, neon).

## For all three concepts
