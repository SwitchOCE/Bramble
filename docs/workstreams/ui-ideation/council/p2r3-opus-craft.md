## Rank

3. **D:** pale, empty panes, a board too small for its table, and nested rows.

## One spacing rule

Gap across = gap down = **¼ card width**. Measure across from flat edge to flat edge, and down from the bottom tip of a front card to the top tip of a back card. Offset the back row by half a pitch, but never nest it. Leave at least **½ card width** between zones, with a lead line in that gap and not through cards, and give the shop and the board the same column pitch. The render that shows it best is **A `mid`**, the board (36 / 36 on 140).
