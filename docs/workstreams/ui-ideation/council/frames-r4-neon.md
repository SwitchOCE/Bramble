1. **The owner's point.** At 112 px on the teal board, lead and glass blend as one continuous piece with nothing jarring: the outer rail holds steady weight while glass widens smoothly toward the foot. Round 4 clearly beats round 3's abrupt jump from wire sides to bulky flared corners. **Flow** beats **near**: flow seats stat discs on the lower corners, whereas near pinches the foot and leaves discs floating awkwardly over the art.

2. **Gained and lost vs round 3.** **Gained:** rich bevelled **golden** metal (unmissable even on yellow art); leaded tier-coloured **stat-disc rings** and clean ivory faces (replacing striped poker chips); calmer **art-to-border** balance without bulky corners; tier 4's cyan gems and tier 3's pinched shards gone. **Lost:** **ornament** and greyscale **tier** separation. One braided band replaces sculpted petals and the base motif, leaving tiers 1–2 nearly identical in greyscale, thinning board tier colour slightly, keeping tier 1 rope-like, and letting tiers 5–6 merge into warm art.

3. **Good design?** Yes—at 112 px `flow` sits beside published board tokens without looking amateur. **Strongest:** unified hexagonal proportions, modelled gold, and corner-seated leaded roundels. **Weakest:** one braided-rope pattern repeated across all tiers, lacking true leaded-pane geometry or base ornament.

4. **Craft at 300 px.** Glass reads as airbrushed twisted rope, polygon-clipped mid-curve along tapering spires (tiers 4–6) and pinched at the bottom seam. Lead and gilt rails look mathematically extruded, without solder joints or patina. Roundel rings repeat a noisy mini-braid without a cast collar; golden tier 1 pairs warm gilt with cool silver. Glow, sheen and motion are not judged from stills.

5. **Changes still needed.**
   1. Replace the braided strip with cut leaded panes fitted to each taper and foot (no clipped curves), clear cathedral glass on tier 1, and subtle tier-scaled base pane craft for greyscale clarity.
   2. Add cast-metal texture, patina, and soldered vertex joints to lead and gilt rails.
   3. Rebuild stat rings with bolder leaded tesserae in a soldered collar, plus a firmer inner keyline on tiers 5–6.
