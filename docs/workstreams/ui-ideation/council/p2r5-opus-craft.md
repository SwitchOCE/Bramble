All A views, the round 3 and 4 `mid` shots, the D and E sheets and today's frames (`assets/glass/frames/`) were checked at full size, with crops where needed. Motion, timing, hover feel and sound: not judged.

## 1. A's screen against the owner's words

- **Ground:** this is round 3's ground again: a dark teal lattice, a warm glow at the top and faint leaf sprigs. Nothing is added. Round 3's long curved lead lines across the board are gone, so it is a little quieter than round 3. That fits "just the vague suggestion".
- **Sizes and shop:** kept from round 4. Cards are 112×200, the shop is 5 units plus 2 spells with the controls in the left window, and the hand is one row. The side windows are narrower than in round 4 (about 320 px against 395). The owner didn't ask for that.
- **Spells:** round in the shop and in the hand.
- **Padding, board (`mid`):** 28 px across. On both diagonals it is also 28: for a 34 px slant at a 190.26 px row pitch, the gap normal to the slant works out to 28.0, and the crops agree.
- **Mismatch, `fight`:** the two front rows meet point to point across the midline. The gap from apex to apex is about 60 px (y 510 to 570 at x 890), against 28 everywhere else.
- **Mismatch, shop (`mid`):** the gap from the fifth unit to the first spell is about 58 px. The gap between the two spells is about 28. The shop row's centre is about 14 px left of the window's centre.

## 2. The frame, three takes (112 px)

- **A.** Quieter than today: yes. Mostly lead: yes. It keeps today's glass and stat discs in the bottom third. A higher tier is not more obvious at a glance: T4–T6 differ only in colour, plus a few gems. The six tiers can be told apart by colour, though cyan T3 and violet T4 are close. Golden is a thin brass line on the lead, so it reads as "tier 4 with a pale edge": not unmissable. It doesn't fight the art: the top lead nearly disappears.
- **D.** Quieter: yes. Mostly lead: yes. The bottom third is today's glass, unchanged. Higher tiers read more strongly: from T4 a coloured thread runs up the lead, and a coloured tier roundel sits at the top. The tiers can be told apart. Golden, with fully gilded lead, can't be missed. The coloured roundel adds a second spot of colour at the top, which is a light fight with the art.
- **E.** The quietest. Mostly lead: yes. But it redraws the bottom (short uprights and a thin V) instead of keeping "the current stuff". The tier ornament (petals and gems) is about 5 px at 112 and doesn't read, so the tiers rest on colour alone. Golden, with full gold glass, can't be missed. The stat discs poke out past the card's outline.

**Rank:**
1. **D:** keeps today's bottom third as the owner asked, adds a real high-tier cue, and its golden can't be missed.
2. **A:** the closest literal match, but its golden is weak and the high tiers don't read more strongly.
3. **E:** quiet, but it drops the current ornament, its tier steps vanish at 112 px, and its discs break the outline.

**Proportion:** a third. A quarter cuts through the stat discs (A's `extra1`). A half brings the busy side bars back.

## 3. Defects in A's screens

1. **Golden unit not unmissable:** `fight` and `after`, top centre (38/68). It has only a thin brass edge.
2. **Hover fills the card with a pale wash:** `hover-board` (right back card), `hover-shop` (shop 2), `fight` (both third-column cards). With art in place, that is a filter over the art. The art row on `cards` uses an outer glow only, so the two disagree.
3. **Shop row spacing:** `mid` and all recruit views. The unit-to-spell gap is about 58 px against 28, and the row sits about 14 px left of the window's centre.
4. **Marks too small:** all views with cards. The buff chevrons above the stats are about 8 px, and the charge pips on the T5 back card (and on shop 1 in `first`) sit on top of the bottom ornament.
5. **Narrower side window:** all views. Leaderboard names now wrap ("Old Mother Mousewife"), so the rows look uneven.
