### D. The glazier's bench

Labels and plaques as tinted glass with lead edges and depth, not cream paper. In fight and after, add clearance between the enemy front-row plaques and the central dividing lead strip (neon).

9. **mid (hand).** A hand layout that provably holds 7.
10. **fight (bottom left, stakes plaque).** Reserves drawn with real Attack and Health and names of at least 18 px. The kill threshold legible at 18 px.
11. **fight (side columns).** Fill or narrow the empty side columns (left about x 0 to 330, right about x 1600 to 1900).
12. **all views.** Menu and the Turn plate in one place.
13. **after.** Standings as at the end of the turn-9 fight. Keep the numbered threat links and the 10 + 3 damage breakdown (sol). Label starting values as starting values wherever the recap uses them.
