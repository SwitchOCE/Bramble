# Frame, take N: a lead window with a glass base

Units: card 560 × 1000; shapes in `make_guides.py`.

## 1. What it is for

It holds the art, tier and stats, separates neighbours, and steps back.
- **Magic:** a quiet, constant border; rarity is a small mark.
- **Hearthstone token:** the art dominates; golden swaps the stone for gold.
- **Runeterra:** all the ornament and stat gems sit at the bottom.
- **Real windows** (a Kempe window, a 13th-century grisaille panel): ornament gathers in a base under a plainer field, and one outer came runs round the panel.

## 2. Art to border

The window is 78% of the card (today's is 81%). The lead is about 11%, the glass about 11%, plus the roundels.

Compare Magic's art box, about 37%; the Hearthstone board portrait, about 70%; Runeterra, about 85%; the fox concept, about 75%.

Above the join the window is today's, so existing art fits. In the base, the window's corners rise from 811.6 to 795 and its point from 947.5 to 905. That covers about 4 px of ground at board size.

## 3. Ornament

Only in the base: the fleuron at the bottom point and the roundel rings. Never on the top point, the upper sides or the window; today's hanging top diamond goes. Above the join the lead is plain, dark brown-pewter like the lead in the art, with one soft highlight. At 112 px every piece is 8 px or more: big shapes, no filigree.

## 4. The join

An unbroken outer came runs round the whole card and ties the zones together. On each side the glass starts as a wedge tucked inside that came, pointed like a leaf, so it grows up out of the base. The join is an ordinary lead line, never a horizontal cut.

## 5. Tier ladder

- **Colour:** grey, green, blue, purple, yellow, red, in the owner's order.
- **Height:** the glass tip rises from 700 (tier 1) to 620 (tier 6), so the base is 30–38% of the card's height.
- **Count:** the tier's number of pale glass beads in the bottom chevrons. Pale reads on every colour and in greyscale.

The fleuron grows from a plain diamond to a crest with a faint glow; pieces grow from about 5 to 15.

## 6. Golden

All the lead turns to pale polished gilt: rim, upper band and base cames. Most of the frame changes, so golden reads at a glance. The glass and beads stay the same. Against tier 5's deep marigold, the gilt is a paler champagne gold with dark burnished edges. Each golden card is drawn from its normal card.

## 7. Stats, tier mark, spell

- **Stats:** blank ivory glass discs (radius 60, today's places) in a lead ring edged with the tier's glass. Gold stays for golden.
- **Tier mark:** the beads.
- **Spell:** round, with the same lead ring. Its lower third is the tier's glass with a small fleuron. No discs.
