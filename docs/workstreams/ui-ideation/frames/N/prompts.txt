What a guide is. Each guide is thin grey lines on black, on the same portrait 2:3 canvas you draw on. Draw the card so it lies exactly on the guide's lines, the same size and in the same place. Take nothing else from a guide: it has no style. Its lines mean:
- The outer six-sided line is the card's outer edge: a tall hexagon, pointed at the top and the bottom.
- The inner six-sided line is the inside edge of the frame. Everything inside it is the empty window.
- On each side, low down, a short slanting line runs from the outer edge in to the inner line. That is where the coloured glass begins. Above those two short lines the frame is lead only; below them it is stained glass.
- Two large circles at the lower corners are the two stat roundels.
- Small circles along the bottom are pale glass beads.
- A short curved line above the bottom point, where there is one, is the highest the ornament at the bottom point may reach into the window.

The design, for every unit card (items 1 to 12, 15, 16):
- One continuous outer lead came, an even rounded strip of dark lead, runs unbroken around the whole card edge.
- Upper part, above the two short slanting lines: the frame is plain lead and nothing else. A wide band of dark brown-grey cast lead, matte, with one soft pewter highlight along its rounded profile, like the lead in an old church window. No glass, no jewels, no ornament, no studs anywhere in this upper part, and nothing at the top point except the lead itself meeting in a clean point.
- Lower part, below the two short slanting lines: back-lit stained glass in the tier's colour, set in the same dark lead. On each side the glass begins as a slim wedge pointed at the top, tucked inside the outer came, like a leaf growing upward, and widens downward into the band that runs along the two bottom edges to the bottom point. Every piece of glass is outlined by a lead line of the same weight as the rest. The glass is textured, streaky, glowing from behind, with uneven pieces.
- At the bottom point: one simple symmetric glass ornament (the item says what), staying below the guide's curved line.
- The beads: small round beads of pale clear-white glass, each in a thin lead ring, exactly where the guide's small circles are, and no others.
- The roundels: on the two large circles, two round discs of plain smooth ivory glass, lit from behind, completely blank. Each disc sits in a lead ring with a thin outer ring of the tier's coloured glass. No numbers or marks on the discs.
- Everything is mirror-symmetric left to right.
- The window, inside the inner line, is empty: plain flat dark slate grey, one even colour, no picture, no texture, no glow.
- Outside the card: a plain flat dark charcoal background, no shadow, no glow except where the item asks for one.
- The look: big simple shapes, clean even lead lines, sharp edges, crisp at this size. Not fussy: the frame must still read when shown only 112 pixels wide, so no fine filigree and no tiny pieces.
