THE DESIGN, for every card item. A tall pointed hexagonal card border made of lead and stained glass, seen straight on, flat, lit from behind.
- The border is a lead came: two rounded metal rails side by side with a fine seam between them, dark warm grey-brown lead with a soft highlight along each rail. It runs unbroken all the way round the card.
- ABOVE the two join ticks the border is ONLY this plain lead: no glass, no colour, no ornament, no petals, no jewels, except one small round bead of the tier's glass set into the lead at the very top point.
- AT the join ticks the two rails part: the inner rail curves away from the outer rail in a smooth pointed leaf tip, closed by a small round blob of solder, and stained glass fills the space between the rails from there down. The glass grows out of the lead like a leaf from a stem. It must look made by a glazier, not cut off.
- BELOW the join, down both sides, around both roundels and along the two lower sloping edges, the border is the tier's stained glass held between the two rails, with dark lead lines between the pieces. At the bottom point a small crest of glass and lead rises into the window, never higher than the lower edge of the roundels.
- ROUNDELS: on the two circles, a smooth glowing cream-gold glass disc, ringed by small pieces of the tier's glass and the lead rails. The discs are BLANK: no numerals, no marks.
- THE WINDOW inside the border is empty: plain flat dark slate, with no picture in it. Nothing from the frame enters it except the bottom crest and the roundels.
- The lead is the same weight on every tier. Clean, even lead lines, sharp edges, drawn crisply. Stylised and hand-made, not photoreal, little fine detail.
- No text or lettering anywhere.
