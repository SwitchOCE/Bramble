# Take M: notes against the frame study's ten rules (round 1)

Study: `../study.md`. Renders: `/data/ui-ideation/frames/M/` (sheet, large, compare).

| Rule | Take M | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Art 70–80% | About 80%; the glass bulges into the window low down | Follows |
| 2. Lead ≥ 5 px at 120 | About 5 px measured, but drawn as thin striped rails, so it reads thinner | Round 2 fixes it |
| 3. Ornament in at most two places | Base, plus the top bead | Follows |
| 4. Rank by a glass area ≥ 8% | 5–7% of the card; hue reads at 112 px, greyscale doesn't | Partly; round 2 adds a height step |
| 5. One silhouette change at top tiers | None for tiers; golden adds the top leaf crest | Not followed: the outline is fixed. The crest grows inside it |
| 6. Lead bar across the whole card | Not used: a bar would cross the art | Deliberately not. The bar join was tried and reads worse |
| 7. Glass ends on a knot, never square | Leaf tip and solder knot | Follows; reads at 300 px, not at 112 |
| 8. Frame glass quieter than the art | As saturated as the art | Not followed. The owner wants higher tiers obvious; open |
| 9. No ornament piece under 6 px | Top bead about 2 px; glass pieces 2–4 px | Not followed. Hover detail only, not counted as a tier mark |
| 10. Golden changes the metal | Gilt came, glass kept, three-leaf crest | Follows; the strongest signal on the sheet |

Round 1 at 112 px: golden reads at once. Tier hue reads. The frame is far quieter than today's. Tiers 2–6 differ only by hue, and the upper lead blends into the art.

The bar join (t1-bar, t6-bar) is dropped: at 128 px it barely differs, and the leaf tip looks more made at 300 px.
