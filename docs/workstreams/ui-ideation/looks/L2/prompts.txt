CARD DESIGN (consistent across all 8 items):
- Every unit card is a tall hexagon (pointed top and bottom, roughly 0.56 wide to 1 tall).
- Border: ONE calm, even, continuous strip of chunky backlit coloured stained-glass panes running evenly all the way round the hexagon, neatly enclosed between a bold outer dark charcoal lead rim and a clean inner dark lead line, divided only by simple straight cross-lead joints. NO crawling vines, NO corner filigree, NO broken half-frames, NO tier numerals, NO tribe emblems protruding from the frame.
- Two clean round glass stat roundels sit neatly in the lower-left and lower-right angles of the hexagon: lower-left warm amber-gold glass disc with bold dark numeral (Attack), lower-right deep crimson-red glass disc with bold ivory numeral (Health).
- Six tier border strip colours:
  * Tier 1: frosted pearl / milky off-white glass in dark charcoal lead.
  * Tier 2: emerald-moss green glass in dark charcoal lead.
  * Tier 3: sapphire-cobalt blue glass in dark charcoal lead.
  * Tier 4: luminous royal amethyst purple glass in dark charcoal lead.
  * Tier 5: glowing deep burnt marigold-amber / sunset orange glass in dark charcoal lead, with a single bright faceted diamond glass pane at the top and bottom points (NOT metallic gold; the lead remains heavy dark charcoal).
  * Tier 6: incandescent ruby-crimson glass in dark charcoal lead, with a bright ruby faceted diamond pane at the top and bottom points.
- GOLDEN (tripled) unit cards: unmistakable in a fraction of a second! ALL dark charcoal lead on the card (outer rim, inner frame line, cross-joints, and the rims around both stat roundels) transforms into thick, sculpted, gleaming 24-karat burnished gold metalwork with bright specular highlights; a bold wrought-gold finial crown blooms symmetrically above the top apex (with a tiny matching gold trefoil at the bottom apex), visibly breaking the flush hexagonal silhouette; and a radiant warm sunlit white-gold halo with tiny rising gilt dust motes glows softly behind the golden card. The coloured glass tier strip remains luminous inside the gold framework.
- Spells are circular leaded stained-glass medallions (round, not hexagonal), with a dark lead rim and no Attack/Health roundels.
- Fighter art inside each card: European woodland animal warriors (badgers, foxes, mice, stags, owls, toads, pigeons, holly knights, moss oozes) shown full body, upright and poised, built from chunky stained glass pieces. Crucially, the background glass inside each card is softer, mistier, and lower-saturation frosted glass so both the vivid warrior figure and the coloured border strip read cleanly.
