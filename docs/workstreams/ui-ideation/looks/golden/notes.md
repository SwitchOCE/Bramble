# Golden light

- **Gilded lead** reads immediately on both cards at 112 px. The bright outline stays distinct from the yellow glass.
- **Gold lines in the glass** gets lost against yellow. On the dark card it reads as warmer artwork.
- **Light from behind** reads on both. The dark lead separates the yellow picture from the halo on the teal board.
- **A passing beam** is clearer on the dark card in the captured phase. The yellow card already looks sunlit, and the beam leaves no marker between passes.
- **Motes** are easy to miss on both. Specks over yellow can pass for reflections in the glass.
- **Still only** reads on both. The dark seams make the three amber panes legible even over yellow.

I’d choose backlight and pair it with the three panes for a persistent shape cue. Gilded lead and a passing beam also pair well because the rim carries the status between passes.

A game shader could use a dedicated lead mask, brighten individual panes without washing out their color, and keep bloom consistent as the board scales.

Timing and feel: not judged.
