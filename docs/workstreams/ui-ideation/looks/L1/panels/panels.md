# The auras panel and a heap's panel

Page: [panels.html](panels.html) (`?state=auras-few|auras-many|grid-many|heap-full|heap-empty|heap-from-auras`). Pictures: `/data/ui-ideation/looks/L1/panels/`.

## What the auras panel holds

What lasts and belongs to **you**, not to one unit: player counters (Tide, Hopper bonus), heaps (Compost), and slot buffs (Guard post, a slot's +2/+0), shown on a tiny four-over-three map. Left out: anything on a unit (Fury, Grudge, Renown, uses left, keywords), which is already on the card and its hover.

## The family

One leaded slate-glass pane, always in the same place: the right wing of the table, which is empty while buying. The board, the shop and the hand stay in view undimmed.

- **Auras: a list.** Medallion, name, one short line, and the number. A hexagon medallion is a heap: its row has a chevron and opens the heap in the same pane, with a back arrow. Six rows show; the rest scroll.
- **Opening.** With four or fewer counters there's no plate: a round medallion opens the list with its row marked, and a hexagon opens its heap. With more, the column shows four and "+8".
- **Heap.** Units four across at 68 × 121, newest first, copies stacked with "×4", golden ones gilt; an empty heap says "Empty". Smaller than in the hand: nothing here is dragged.
- **Closing.** Press ×, Esc, the medallion again, or anywhere outside. It opens with a short slide from the column. Not judged.

## Pick

**The list** over the grid (`grid-many`). The grid only repeats the column; a panel the player chose to open can afford one line of words.
