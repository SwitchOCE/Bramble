THE IDEA for every item: each card is a small leaded window. The whole screen is a dim room, and the cards are the brightest things in it, glowing as if lit from behind. Lighting does the work, not decoration.

THE CARD, unless an item says otherwise: a tall hexagon, about 0.56 wide to 1 tall, with a point at the top and a point at the bottom. Its border is ONE plain band of dark grey-black lead came, smooth and even, about as thick as a pencil line at the card's size, with no ornament, no coloured panes, no gems, no badges along it. Inside the border the whole card is the stained-glass picture. At the two lower corners sit two small round lead-rimmed roundels of pale glass, holding two short numerals: Attack on the left, Health on the right. Nothing else on the card: no name, no text, no tier mark.

THE CARD PICTURE: one animal-folk warrior from a European woodland (badger, mouse, pigeon, hedgehog, jay, toad and the like), full body, standing, holding one simple weapon, lean and serious, not cute. The figure is built of chunky, vivid, back-lit glass. The background behind it is the same kind of glass but DARKER and much less saturated (dusky blue-greys, deep greens, muted browns), in three or four broad calm pieces, so the figure stands out clearly. Every card has a different figure.

GOLDEN CARD: the same card, but its lead border and its two roundel rims are bright polished gold, with a hard sparkle of light on one edge of the border. Its glass glows warmer and noticeably brighter than the normal cards, and it casts a soft warm pool of its own colours on the surface beneath it. Nothing else on the screen is gold.

TIER LIGHT: in the shop, a card's tier is shown ONLY by the coloured light behind it, never on the card. Tier 1: plain cool grey daylight, faint. Tier 2: soft green. Tier 3: clear blue. Tier 4: violet purple. Tier 5: warm amber orange, strong. Tier 6: deep red, the strongest, with light rays spilling out. The higher the tier, the stronger and wider the light.

THE SCREEN, for the buy screens: wide 16:9, seen straight on, flat like a game screen, not a photo of a room. Painted, stylised, with big simple shapes and little fine detail, a hand-made feel, not photoreal. Regions are told apart by MATERIAL, each with a clear edge between them:
- Across the top third: THE SHOP, a wall of cool grey stone with a row of six or seven arched openings in it. In each opening stands one card, and behind it the opening is filled with the card's TIER LIGHT, which spills round the card and falls on the stone sill below it as a coloured pool.
- Across the middle: THE BOARD, a long dark oak table seen from slightly above, wide and calm, where the player's units stand in one row. A little of the shop's coloured light falls on its far edge.
- Along the bottom: THE HAND, a narrow dark leather ledge holding two or three smaller cards.
- Down the left side: a tall narrow lancet window of plain lead and dim glass, holding the player's portrait in a roundel, a gold coin counter, and two or three round glass buttons with simple icons.
- Down the right side: a matching lancet holding eight small round portrait roundels stacked in a column, each with a small number, and below them one large round glass button with an arrow icon.
The side lancets are dim; the shop's openings and the cards are the brightest things on screen.
