# Counters: opus

Names besides Compost and Tide are guesses.

**A Medallions.** A round glass emblem for each counter, its count on a cream badge at the lower right. Two to a row, up to six, with an auras button at the foot.
**B Plaques (recommended).** A full-width strip of coloured glass for each counter: emblem left, 33 px numeral right. A heap shows its last three units. The auras cap sits at the top.
**C Lead counter.** One counter big, up to three small. The rest are only in the auras panel.

**Pick B.** Large numerals, one size, lined up at one edge: one glance down reads them. It takes one to six counters and three digits with no change. Six strips stay calm on the diamond glass; A's twelve circles look busy. C hides counters and needs a rule for which one leads.

**Refresh count:** A's cream badge, in any design (cream for counts, gold for costs). B's inner "7" crowds the coin. C's beads fail past about five.

**Not solved:** seven or more counters (panel only), two-number counters (Hopper bonus), row order, and motion and hover (not judged). To paint: the plaque strips and the emblems.
