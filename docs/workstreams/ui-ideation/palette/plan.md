# Card art colour guide: stage 1 plan

**How the painter is told.** No palette rules: they flattened the pictures on 15 Nov. Instead, one sentence about light (the fighter stands in clear light; the place lies in shade or evening) plus a short "place colours" line of two or three named colours per tribe. Strength is carried by the light, hue by the named colours.

**Finding the level.** Two loud cards, `mouse/fencing-master` and `thorn/sweetbriar`, repainted at three strengths: softened (near today), in shade, hushed (near the "too washed out" middle). The old picture gives subject and pose only.

**Home colours to test** (proposals): badger moss green and bark brown; locust dusty rust and smoke plum; mouse dusk blue and dark timber; pigeon pale dove-grey and faded sage; thorn heather lilac and misty grey-blue; neutral warm stone and taupe. All avoid strong gold, amber, sun rays and saturated red or purple fields, which belong to golden and the tier lights.

**Palettes.** One repaint per remaining tribe and a neutral at S2, plus two new cards with no reference, to prove the wording works alone.

**Checks.** Full size, then seven at 130 px on dark oak beside today's seven, with a gilt border and each tier light drawn in code.
