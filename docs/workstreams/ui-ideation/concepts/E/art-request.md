# Art request: concept E, "Glass in the forest"

One picture: the whole-screen background for the recruit and fight screens.

- **Size:** 1920 x 1080 px, PNG.
- **Style:** the game's stained glass: flat panes of coloured glass, dark lead lines between them, no gradients inside a pane beyond the slight mottle of real glass. No text, no characters, no animals, no cards, no buttons, no frame or border.
- **What it shows:** a woodland clearing at dusk, seen from just above the ground, as one big leaded window. An idealised European wood: oak and beech trunks, roots, moss, ferns, bramble with a few blackberries, a ground path, fireflies as a few tiny bright panes.

## Where things sit over it (keep these areas calm)

Calm means large panes, low contrast, darker values, few lead lines. The cards and controls are lit glass drawn on top and must stand out.

## Light

Dusk going to night. Cool moonlight from the upper right, in blues and violets. Two or three soft diagonal shafts of pale gold light falling from the canopy onto the middle of the clearing floor. Warm amber only in small accents (fireflies, the far end of the path). Overall the picture should be clearly darker than the lit cards: aim for most panes at 15-35% brightness, so cards at full brightness read at once.

## Not wanted

A window frame, stone, tracery or arches (this concept has no window); a strong focal object in the centre; bright saturated panes behind the card areas; text.
