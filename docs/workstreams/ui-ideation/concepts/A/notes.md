# A · The great window, refit

The shop and the staggered 4+3 board live in the centre of a three-light window set in dark stone. Larger units carry the original art, stats and marks, with short captions in the lead below them and full rules in the left light.

## Play and glance

Buy into the hand or onto a free slot. Point at, focus or select a card to read its rules. End turn leads to the mirrored fight, then a knockout recap beside the opponent’s starting board. The prototype demonstrates inspection and transitions between recorded states.

- **Recruit · 27, up from 23.** One Game menu replaces three utility controls, and standings and row labels add two. Names and effects under shop and board cards add four.
- **Fight · 11, up from 9.** Add row labels and the phase badge. Eight appear in the opening still. Attacker/target, damage and deaths count as later events. Card details are reach in both phases.

## Look and reading

Amber and green panes, leaf tracery and lead-seamed controls hold the composition together. Read five offers, seven board roles and the next opponent without opening details.

Battlegrounds informed the single framed play surface, Once Upon a Galaxy the fixed inspector, and Glass Masquerade the bevels and backlit glass.
