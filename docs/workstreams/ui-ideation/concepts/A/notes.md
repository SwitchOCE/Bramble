The shop and staggered 4+3 board live in the centre of a three-light window set in dark stone. The units keep their glass art and stats, with short captions below, tribes on the shoulder in recruit, and full rules in the left light.

## Play and glance

Buy into the hand or onto a free slot. Point at, focus or select a card to read its rules. End turn leads to the mirrored fight and then a knockout recap beside the opponent’s surviving formation. The prototype demonstrates inspection and transitions between recorded states.
