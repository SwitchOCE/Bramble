# Concept B: Honeycomb

**Concept.** One continuous leaded stained-glass honeycomb of tall hexagons (`151.2×270 px`, exact `0.56` ratio) fills the window: lobby, shop, 4+3 board, hand/reserve, counters, and buttons are neighbouring clusters of the card's shape. Thin `5 px` cames join siblings, brass zigzag mullions divide clusters, and glowing seams mark active spatial links.

**Flow.**
- **Recruit (`mid`, `first`)**: Read the left lobby and top shop (Gold/Upgrade touch Shop 1; Refresh/Freeze touch Shop 5), drag units across the brass Market seam onto the 4+3 board or right into Hand/Reserve, then press the sole amber **End Turn** cell. Turn 1 hides unused counters and adds one removable drag overlay.
- **Fight (`fight`)**: Your board, counters, and Reserve stay fixed; the shop becomes the enemy 3+4 board across the crimson Battlefront seam, glowing seams show synergies and first strike, and End Turn becomes **Skip Fight**.
- **After (`after`)**: Surviving foes remain above with threat tags; a leaded window over your fallen board breaks down the loss (`−10` base + `−3` heavy defeat = `−13`) and three turning points beside **Play Again**.

**Glance tier.** Recruit: **23** items (same count as today, now showing all 8 players, card names, tribes, and cues). Fight: **9** items (+ **2** at end: autopsy, actions).

**Judging criteria.**
- **Look**: Backlit cathedral glass, diamond quarries, stone frame, and lead cames carry the craft onto every control—no generic pills.
- **Reading at a glance (route: more on the card)**: Leaded inscription bands put `18 px` names, `14 px` cues, and tribe gems on every card; the 8-player lobby stays permanently open.
