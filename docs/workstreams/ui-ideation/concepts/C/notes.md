# Concept C: The Rose Window

**Concept.** Bramble becomes one backlit Gothic woodland rose window: your seven-unit board blooms in the centre oculus, ringed by the five-unit shop arc, left spell alcove, bottom hand cradle, and all eight players as leaded rim roundels. Upright tall hexagons keep art unobstructed while adding a shoulder tribe crest and leaded banderole plaque below so the screen reads without hovering.

**Flow.** In Recruit (`mid`, `first`), read the shop arc and spell alcove, spend gold from upper-left tracery, place units on radial Front/Back arcs, and press the sole amber End Turn medallion. In Fight (`fight`), both boards share one rose across an equator, with Skip Fight. At End (`after`), surviving foes sit above a chronicle showing `10 + 3 = 13` math and deciding threats. Clicking any card opens Inspect Lens (`extra1`).
