# D. The glazier's bench

**Concept.** You are a glazier in a woodland workshop: glass for sale stands in the window, your panel lies on a light table. Names and rules are painted on tinted glass under each piece.

**Route:** more around the card; the face stays minimal, a label under it carries name and rules (shop) or name and keyword or a short cue (board, fight).

## Flow

Recruit: buy from window to table or hand; spend from the left lane; End turn (the one lit pane) names your opponent. A clicked piece's reading docks inside the table (`extra1`); `extra2` shows a full hand. Fight: both panels hang in the window; a plaque shows kills and what a loss costs. End: why −13, the three pieces that beat you, starting totals, standings. Menu and Turn stay bottom left, the one button bottom right.

## Glance tier

Recruit **23** (today 23): adds shop labels, board labels, player list; drops keyword marks, Past boards, Cards, cog (one menu). Fight **15** (today 9): adds name labels, stakes plaque, reserves, player list, change marks.

## The two judged things

1. Look: the commissioned scene; every control a leaded pane, every number glass.
2. Reading: the shop needs 0 hovers (was up to 12); all eight players always listed; every fighting unit named.

## Pass 2, round 1

*This pass replaces the label-based reading above.*

**At rest a card shows Attack, Health and its tier, nothing else.** Stats decide every buy and fight; tier says what it is worth and is quicker to read than art. Charge (pips) and Renown (a violet diamond on the slot) stay because they change what you do and cannot be inferred. Names, rules and keywords leave the screen; the room went to bigger cards (shop 262 px, board 270, fight 250) and space on the table.

**Eye order, mid-game:** 1 your panel, lit from below at the table's centre; 2 the offers in the window, matted down at rest; 3 Gold and End turn, the one warm pane. In fights only the blow being struck is lit; after, only the three that beat you.

**Frame:** one band of smoky glass in lead, no tier colour or ornament. Tier is 1–6 clear beads in a lead cartouche at the bottom point; golden turns the band gold. Spells are round medallions.

**Hover:** the card lifts to full light; a tooltip sits beside it and flips to the side with room. Keyword reminders go to one fixed shelf at the foot of the left lane, never by the cursor; a setting hides it (`keywords-off`).

**Reversed:** 20 (tier beads on the face), 23, 36, 38, 40, 42, 43, 50, 55, 73, 74.
