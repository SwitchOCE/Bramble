# Art request: concept D, "The glazier's bench"

One picture. **1920 x 1080 px**, PNG, in the game's stained-glass style (lead lines, flat glass panes, the card art's palette). Save to `/data/ui-ideation/concepts/D/art/background.png`.

## What it shows

A glazier's workshop built into the edge of an idealised European woodland, seen from the glazier's seat. Two parts, one picture:

- **Top 37% (y 0 to 400): the workshop's back wall is one wide leaded window** looking out into a sunlit beech-and-oak wood (ferns, a mossy bank, a glimpse of a toadstool ring and a far glade; no animals or people). The window's stone mullions and lead are part of the picture. The wood outside is soft and low-contrast, like light through thin glass: mostly pale greens and warm gold, no strong dark shapes. Glass for sale will stand in front of this window.
- **Bottom 63% (y 400 to 1080): the bench top, seen from above.** A dark oak bench whose middle is a **large light table**: a recessed sheet of milky white glass lit from beneath, glowing evenly. Around the light table, on the bench: the glazier's tools and stock at the edges only: coils of lead came, a glass cutter and grozing pliers, a soldering iron on a stand, horseshoe nails, a jar of brushes, a few offcuts of coloured glass, a sprig of bramble and some fallen leaves blown in from the window, a small oil lamp at the far right.

## Light

Two sources. Daylight from the window at the top, green-gold, falling forward onto the bench and catching the edges of the tools. A cool white glow rising from the light table in the middle. A small warm pool of lamplight at the far right. The edges of the picture fall into shadow (a soft vignette) so that the window and the light table are the two brightest areas on the screen.

## Not wanted

Parchment, paper scrolls or a wooden signboard UI; text or lettering of any kind; people or animals; a perspective view of the table (the bench is seen straight from above, the window straight from the front).
