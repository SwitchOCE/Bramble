# Game flow mock-ups

HTML mock-ups for the screens the glass mock-ups (`design/glass-mockups`) do not have: `start.html`, `result.html`, and `pause.html` (the pause panel, and `?panel=hint` for the fight's skip hint). Plain on purpose, made only of the glass mock-ups' own pieces. Ideation track 2; nothing here is decided.

- `recruit-play.html`: the recruit screen's play states (a Discover open, choosing a target, an ability button) on `recruit.html`'s default page; `?discover=1`, `?aim=unit` or `slot`, `?abil=1`. Compare: `recruit_discover.tscn` (`discover=1&abil=1`), `recruit_ability.tscn` (`abil=1`), `recruit_aim.tscn` (`aim=unit`) under `tests/screens/`.
- `stand-ins.html`: the stand-in looks with no glass mock-up, on the mock-ups' own screens. `?m=recruit`: keyword and counter pills, the golden rim, frozen ice, a dimmed button (its action not legal), the message line, a slot's buff under its unit, a counter with no emblem. `?m=trigger`: a unit's effect acting (glow, lifted 14 px), a destroy (a burst with no number). `?m=result`: the result over the transom and the damage on the loser's portrait. Compare: `recruit_stand_ins.tscn`, `fight_trigger.tscn`, `fight_result.tscn` under `tests/screens/`.
- `stage.css`, `stage.js`, `card.css` and `card.js` are copied unchanged from `docs/design/art-direction/mockups/` on `design/glass-mockups` at `8279442b`. `assets` links to the game's `assets/glass`, which holds the same pictures and fonts.
- `menus.js` and `menus.css` hold the shared helpers; the places are mirrored in `src/game/ui/`.
- Compare with the game: `python3 tools/glass/compare.py res://tests/game/start_compare.tscn docs/ideation-track-2/mockups/start.html --local` (also `result_compare`, `pause_compare`, and `hint_compare` with `--query panel=hint`). On 17 Nov all four were under 2 of 255 mean difference.
