# Game flow mock-ups

HTML mock-ups for the screens the glass mock-ups (`design/glass-mockups`) do not have: `start.html`, `result.html`, and `pause.html` (the pause panel, and `?panel=hint` for the fight's skip hint). Plain on purpose, made only of the glass mock-ups' own pieces. Ideation track 2; nothing here is decided.

- `recruit.html` and `fight.html`: STAND-IN copies of the owner's pages on `design/glass-mockups` (at `8279442b`). Their only difference is the plates: they load `plates.js`, which draws the player's counters as named plates in place of the emblem chips, because no emblem pictures exist for the tribes' counters (Compost, the Tide and the rest), so the game draws plates. They also read the same card data from `assets/card_data.json`. Run the same compares against the owner's own pages by dropping `--local`; on 17 Nov (screens pass 3) `recruit_mockup` gave 1.87 against the owner's page and 1.50 against the copy, `fight_start` 1.69 and 1.33. Compare: `recruit_mockup.tscn`, `recruit_spell_hover.tscn` (`lb=1&hover=s5`), `recruit_leaderboard_hover.tscn` (`lb=1&hover=b0`) against `recruit.html`; `fight_start.tscn` (`m=start`), `fight_mid.tscn` (`m=mid`), `fight_mid_leaderboard_hover.tscn` (`m=mid&lb=1&hover=o2`) against `fight.html`.
- `plates.js`: the player's counters as named plates by the portrait (polish.html's STAND-IN), in place of `stage.js`'s emblem chips, so every page here draws what the game draws.
- `polish.html`: the looks added after the first play-test (pills with numbers, green and red numbers, plates, the lifted hand card, the Discover's option text, a heap's hover). Compare: `recruit_polish.tscn` against `?m=heap` (the Compost plate's hover).
- `recruit-tribes.html`: a layout check drawn from the build's own data, not a mock-up: the recruit screen with real tribe cards, all five tribes. It draws `recruit_tribes.json`, which `tests/screens/recruit_tribes.gd` writes from the shipped content through `ViewAdapter` (its header says how to write it again); `units` links to `assets/units`. Compare: `recruit_tribes.tscn`.
- `recruit-play.html`: the recruit screen's play states (a Discover open with each option's text under it, choosing a target, an ability button) on `recruit.html`'s default page; `?discover=1`, `?aim=unit` or `slot`, `?abil=1`. Compare: `recruit_discover.tscn` (`discover=1&abil=1`), `recruit_ability.tscn` (`abil=1`), `recruit_ability_hover.tscn` (`abil=2`: screens pass 3's button with its cost, hovered), `recruit_aim.tscn` (`aim=unit`) under `tests/screens/`.
- `stand-ins.html`: the stand-in looks with no glass mock-up, on the mock-ups' own screens. `?m=recruit`: keyword and counter pills, the golden rim, frozen ice, a dimmed button (its action not legal), the message line, a slot's buff under its unit, the counter plates. `?m=trigger`: a unit's effect acting (glow, lifted 14 px), a destroy (a burst with no number), a prevented hit (a burst with 0). `?m=result`: the result over the transom and the damage on the loser's portrait. `?m=fight3` (screens pass 3): a used-up keyword's pill dimmed (screens pass 4 took out the board's "Outnumbered" and "Hoppers waiting" labels). Compare: `recruit_stand_ins.tscn`, `fight_trigger.tscn`, `fight_result.tscn`, `fight_moments.tscn` (`m=fight3`) under `tests/screens/`.
- `pause.html` shows the game's seed under the buttons (STAND-IN), for play-test bug reports.
- Settings cog (STAND-IN, 17 Nov; the owner: "we need a settings button in general ... Just a cog"): `stand-ins.html?m=cog` (the recruit page) and `?m=fightcog` (Fight3) draw a round `.btn` with a cog (`menus.js`, `cog()`) in the left light's arch above the turn, the same place on every game screen; it opens and closes the pause panel. `pause.html` gains the one setting under Quit, "Cards drop by" with Pointer and Card middle, the one on lit with recruit-play.html's lit rim; `?drop=middle` shows Card middle on. Compare (17 Nov, against the local pages): `tests/game/cog_compare.tscn` (`m=cog`) 1.60, `fightcog_compare.tscn` (`m=fightcog`) 1.38, `pause_compare.tscn` 0.87, `pause_middle_compare.tscn` (`drop=middle`) 0.78.
- `start.html` labels your portrait "You" (STAND-IN, screens pass 3).
- `stage.css`, `stage.js`, `card.css` and `card.js` are copied unchanged from `docs/design/art-direction/mockups/` on `design/glass-mockups` at `8279442b`. `assets` links to the game's `assets/glass`, which holds the same pictures and fonts.
- `menus.js` and `menus.css` hold the shared helpers; the places are mirrored in `src/game/ui/`.
