# Game flow mock-ups

HTML mock-ups for the screens the glass mock-ups (`design/glass-mockups`) do not have: `start.html`, `result.html`, and `pause.html` (the pause panel, and `?panel=hint` for the fight's skip hint). Plain on purpose, made only of the glass mock-ups' own pieces. Ideation track 2; nothing here is decided.

- `recruit.html` and `fight.html`: local copies of the glass mock-ups' pages (at `8279442b`), changed only to read `assets/card_data.json` and to load `plates.js`. Compare: `recruit_mockup.tscn`, `recruit_spell_hover.tscn` (`lb=1&hover=s5`), `recruit_leaderboard_hover.tscn` (`lb=1&hover=b0`) against `recruit.html`; `fight_start.tscn` (`m=start`), `fight_mid.tscn` (`m=mid`), `fight_mid_leaderboard_hover.tscn` (`m=mid&lb=1&hover=o2`) against `fight.html`.
- `plates.js`: the player's counters as named plates by the portrait (polish.html's STAND-IN), in place of `stage.js`'s emblem chips, so every page here draws what the game draws.
- `polish.html`: the looks added after the first play-test (pills with numbers, green and red numbers, plates, the lifted hand card, the Discover's option text, a heap's hover). Compare: `recruit_polish.tscn` against `?m=heap` (the Compost plate's hover).
- `recruit-tribes.html`: the recruit screen with real tribe cards, all five tribes. It draws `recruit_tribes.json`, which `tests/screens/recruit_tribes.gd` writes from the shipped content through `ViewAdapter` (its header says how to write it again); `units` links to `assets/units`. Compare: `recruit_tribes.tscn`.
- `recruit-play.html`: the recruit screen's play states (a Discover open with each option's text under it, choosing a target, an ability button) on `recruit.html`'s default page; `?discover=1`, `?aim=unit` or `slot`, `?abil=1`. Compare: `recruit_discover.tscn` (`discover=1&abil=1`), `recruit_ability.tscn` (`abil=1`), `recruit_aim.tscn` (`aim=unit`) under `tests/screens/`.
- `stand-ins.html`: the stand-in looks with no glass mock-up, on the mock-ups' own screens. `?m=recruit`: keyword and counter pills, the golden rim, frozen ice, a dimmed button (its action not legal), the message line, a slot's buff under its unit, the counter plates. `?m=trigger`: a unit's effect acting (glow, lifted 14 px), a destroy (a burst with no number), a prevented hit (a burst with 0). `?m=result`: the result over the transom and the damage on the loser's portrait. Compare: `recruit_stand_ins.tscn`, `fight_trigger.tscn`, `fight_result.tscn` under `tests/screens/`.
- `pause.html` shows the game's seed under the buttons (STAND-IN), for play-test bug reports.
- `stage.css`, `stage.js`, `card.css` and `card.js` are copied unchanged from `docs/design/art-direction/mockups/` on `design/glass-mockups` at `8279442b`. `assets` links to the game's `assets/glass`, which holds the same pictures and fonts.
- `menus.js` and `menus.css` hold the shared helpers; the places are mirrored in `src/game/ui/`.
- Compare with the game: `python3 tools/glass/compare.py res://tests/game/start_compare.tscn docs/ideation-track-2/mockups/start.html --local` (also `result_compare`, `pause_compare`, and `hint_compare` with `--query panel=hint`). On 17 Nov (screens pass 2) every compare listed here was under 2 of 255 mean difference: the four menus 0.64 to 1.89, the recruit and fight compares 1.33 to 1.99, `recruit_tribes` 1.72, `recruit_polish` 1.94.
