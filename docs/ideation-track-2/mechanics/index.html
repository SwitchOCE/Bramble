Pick a mechanic for each tribe

You asked to pick each tribe's mechanic yourself. Here are three ways each of the five tribes could play, each with one example card. Pick one per tribe, or “None of these”, and add a note if you like. The agents then design each tribe's 19 cards around your pick.

These are my ideas, not decided. Nobody has reviewed them. The names, keywords and numbers are first guesses, and any of them can change.

Card numbers use the rulebook's working scale, about double Battlegrounds. Each card has a size check against the tier guide. A “body” there means the stats of a plain unit of that tier.

On each card: the bold word is the keyword, and the small italic line under the text is the reminder you'd see on hover. “More” under each option has where it shines, a few variations, and what it looks like in other games.
