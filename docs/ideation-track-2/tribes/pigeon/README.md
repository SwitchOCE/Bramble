# Wood pigeons (The Regiment)

Ideation track 2. Nothing here is decided. The 19 units are in [units.json](units.json); what was tried and changed is in [LOG.md](LOG.md). Keywords shared with the other four tribes, and the rulings where tribes meet, are in [../SHARED-RULES.md](../SHARED-RULES.md); where this file and that one differ, that one wins.

The owner (16 Nov): "I think we should have a mix of trail *[read as Drill]* and medals, and I think maybe Pigeon should buff the slots rather than teach other pigeons and stuff. We're going to need some slot buffs stuff. We may need additional spice as well. I feel like this is a bit bland as is".

**The tribe in one line:** the post matters more than the soldier. Pigeons build up the slots of the board (Drill), and soldiers pass through them: summoned, stepping in from the hand, replaced by better ones. What a soldier carries with him is his Medals.

The spice, in the order a player meets it:
- **Reserve:** a unit in your hand steps into the fight when a slot opens.
- **Orders:** pay gold, the sergeant shouts, a slot changes.
- **A Guard post:** one slot that enemies must attack first, whoever stands in it.
- **Sorties:** flyers that go over the front row and hit the back.
- **Decorated veterans:** at 3 Medals a pigeon gets something new, and the Brigadier makes every Decorated pigeon attack twice.

It resembles Once Upon a Galaxy's slot buffs. Reserve has no close match in Battlegrounds.

## Slot buffs

Pigeons own these, and other tribes borrow the wording.

- **What it is:** a stat buff on a board slot (rulebook 8.5). Whatever unit stands there has it. It stays when the unit moves, is sold or dies.
- **Card wording:**
  - "Give this slot +2/+2."
  - "Give a slot +4/+4." The player picks one of their own 7 slots, empty or not.
  - "Give the slots in front of this +2/+2." A back-row unit has two slots in front of it (8.2.1); a front-row unit has none.
- **Reminder text,** the same on every card: *Slot buff: Stays on the slot. Any unit standing there has it.*
- **How long:** given in your turn, it is permanent. Given in a fight, it lasts the fight unless the card says "permanently". This is the same rule as buffs on units.
- **Who gets it:** any unit, from the moment it enters the slot: played, moved, summoned, returned, or stepping in from Reserve. It loses it the moment it leaves. A unit that leaves a buffed slot in a fight keeps its damage and can't drop below 1 Health from the loss.
- **It never travels with the unit.** An effect that copies a unit, or takes or keeps its stats (a copy from the Compost, a return, the Locusts' Eat, the Badgers' Sword-heir and Old Brock), uses the unit's own stats without the slot's. An effect that only reads Attack or Health in a fight reads the total.
- **Buffing a slot isn't a unit gaining stats,** so cards that watch for that don't trigger.
- **Multipliers:** two kinds, each applied once. The Regimental Sergeant Major doubles a buff when it is given. The Mice's Edgewright doubles the Attack slots give for one fight. One of each stack; two of the same kind don't, and the bigger applies (shared ruling 13, which changed this on 17 Nov).
- **On screen:** the buff is written on the slot's base, so an empty slot shows what it is worth. A unit shows its total.
- **A slot can hold other things too.** The Guard post is the first: the unit in that slot has Guard.

## Keywords

| Keyword | Reminder text |
|---|---|
| **Order (N):** *effect* | Once a turn, pay N gold to use this. |
| **Guard post** | The unit here has Guard. You have one Guard post. |
| **Reserve** | In a fight, this steps from your hand into a slot a friendly unit died in. Your Guard post first. |
| **Medal** | +2/+2 permanently. |
| **Decorated:** *effect* | Has 3 or more Medals. |
| **Stiff Upper Lip** | The first time damage would kill this each fight, it stays on 1 Health. |
| **Sortie** | Attacks the enemy back row while it has units. |
| **Cleave** | Also damages the units next to its target. |
| **Private** | A 2/2 Pigeon. |

Guard is shared by every tribe. Cleave is a Pigeon keyword only: the Badgers replaced theirs with Overrun, which is a different effect. "Joins the fight" means summoned, or stepping in from Reserve; a unit that returns after dying has not joined. A Sortie flies over a Guard in the front row; a Guard in the back row stops it.

## The four plans

Boards are for turn 9 at shop tier 5, written front row F1–F4, then back row B1–B3. Stats are shown as they stand in the fight. The boards assume things went well; they are for comparing the plans, not a promise.

### 1. Parade ground: full ranks, and the line never thins

- **The plan:** fill the rows with Pigeons so the Colour Sergeant drills every slot in them, then replace every pigeon that falls.
- **Key units:** Colour Sergeant (T3), Lieutenant (T4), Bugler (T4), Regimental Sergeant Major (T6). Territorial (T1) and Recruiting Sergeant (T2) are the replacements. Sentry starts it.
- **Why choose it:** every slot is good, so every arrival is good, and thirteen bodies fight where the enemy has seven. Nothing is lost when a body is sold for a better one.
- **Turn-9 board:** front slots +15/+15 (F2 has +8/+8 more from an early Sentry).
  - F: Colour Sergeant 19/24, Lieutenant 28/34, Grenadier Guard 19/26, Territorial 19/20.
  - B: Recruiting Sergeant, Bugler, Recruiting Sergeant. Hand: a Territorial and a golden Territorial.
  - The Lieutenant's row opens the fight with 15, 23, 15 and 15 damage to the enemies across from it. As the front falls, two Reserve units step in, then four Privates at 17/17. Six arrivals with the Bugler give every Pigeon +12/+12 by the end.
  - The other shape: a second Colour Sergeant in the back row in place of the Sergeants. Seven drilled slots, fewer bodies.
- **What beats it:** Overrun, Cleave and damage to all enemies, because the arrivals are small. A Sortie on the three officers at the back. Both rows must stay all Pigeons, so it can't take another tribe's best unit, and it grows in a straight line.

### 2. Hold the post: one slot, and a queue for it

- **The plan:** pile every buff onto one Guard post and feed bodies through it.
- **Key units:** Sentry (T1), Lance Corporal (T2), Padre (T3), Grenadier Guard (T3), Colour Ensign (T5). Territorial and Recruiting Sergeant are the queue. The Colonel (T6) makes the whole hand the queue.
- **Why choose it:** a slot's buff is used again by every unit that stands in it. It needs only one good slot, so the other six can hold anything, from any tribe.
- **Turn-9 board:** F2 is the Guard post at about +38/+48: a Sentry stood there early, then six Orders from the Lance Corporal behind it, the Grenadier's drill, and the Padre for the deaths there.
  - F: Colour Ensign 44/62 (its slot copies the post), Grenadier Guard 42/59 with Guard, Sentry (starting the next post), empty.
  - B: Lance Corporal, Recruiting Sergeant, Padre. Hand: two Territorials.
  - Enemies must attack F2. When the Grenadier falls, a Territorial steps in at 42/53 with Guard, then the second, then two Privates at about 42/52. Five bodies hold one slot, and the Padre makes it +1/+1 better each time, for good.
- **What beats it:** anything that doesn't have to attack the post: a Sortie or random damage that kills the Padre behind it, or "destroy" effects on the holder. Cleave reaches the plain units next to it. It is slow: before turn 6 the post is small.

### 3. Old soldiers: Medals and what they unlock

- **The plan:** keep the same pigeons on the board until they are Decorated, then make every Decorated pigeon attack twice.
- **Key units:** Old Campaigner (T2), Flying Ace (T3), Adjutant (T4), Brigadier (T5), Field Marshal (T6).
- **Why choose it:** Medals go with the unit, so the board can be rearranged freely, and they are earned in lost fights too. It hits twice as often as anything else in the tribe.
- **Turn-9 board:** 38 Medals. Five pigeons are Decorated and attack twice.
  - F: Brigadier 7/13, Flying Ace 29/27 with Cleave, Grenadier Guard 26/43 on the Guard post, Flying Ace 13/11.
  - B: Old Campaigner 24/27, Adjutant, Old Campaigner 20/23.
  - The Campaigners sit at the back to live through wins, which is worth 2 Medals each, and have Stiff Upper Lip. The Adjutant decorates F2 and F3.
- **What beats it:** selling a veteran loses its Medals, so the board is stuck with Tier 2 and 3 bodies, and bigger units out-trade them. A Sortie reaches the Campaigners. The Brigadier is a soft target in any row.

### 4. Air wing: over the line and into the back row

- **The plan:** Sortie units open the fight by attacking the enemy back row, and earn Medals for the kills.
- **Key units:** Flying Ace (T3), Squadron Leader (T4), Wing Commander (T5). The Adjutant makes the flyers bigger; the Brigadier makes Decorated ones attack twice.
- **Why choose it:** the enemy's engine is usually in the back row: officers, summoners, the unit that buffs the carry. This plan kills it before it does anything.
- **Turn-9 board:**
  - F: Flying Ace 19/17, Old Campaigner 28/27, Flying Ace 27/21, Flying Ace 15/13. The three Aces have Cleave.
  - B: Wing Commander 10/8, Squadron Leader (F2 and F3 have Sortie and +4 Attack), Adjutant.
  - At the start of the fight five units attack the enemy back row at once, three of them hitting all of it.
- **What beats it:** a Guard in the back row (one Grenadier Order does it), or a back row with nothing in it. A Badger board is not that: its Squires stand in the back row, and killing them is how to beat it. The flyers are thin and take the hits back, so a front row that survives the opening wins the trade.

## Units other tribes want

| Reach | Units |
|---|---|
| **Tribe-only** (names Pigeons): 5 | Colour Sergeant, Bugler, Brigadier, Wing Commander, Field Marshal |

- **Lance Corporal:** 1 gold a turn for +2/+2 on the two slots in front of him, behind any tribe's important units. Sell him and the buff stays.
- **Recruiting Sergeant:** two more bodies in the two front slots that matter, for any tribe. On a Badger board the Privates don't change the outnumbered count, which is fixed when the fight starts; the Sergeant himself is a counted unit.
- **Padre:** Locust and Mouse summoners and Briars that Root die in the same few slots over and over. Each death makes the slot better for the next.
- **Grenadier Guard:** a Guard post for a tribe with fragile attackers (Mice), or one whose units come back (Root, Molt): whatever lands in the post has Guard. In the back row it stops Sorties.
- **Squadron Leader:** sends any tribe's hardest hitters at the back row. Mice that Pile on then pile onto a back-row unit.
- **Adjutant:** +4/+4 a fight on any tribe's two front units, whatever happened to them.
- **Regimental Sergeant Major:** +4/+4 on every slot each turn for any board, and doubles a Mouse board's slot Attack as it is given. Their Edgewright doubles it again in the fight.
- **The Colonel:** up to 6 more units in the fight for any tribe. A lone Badger Knight with Knights in hand stays outnumbered: the count is fixed when the fight starts and units in hand aren't counted, so each Knight that steps in fights at the full count.

The payoffs name Pigeons; the units that build slots don't.

## Shared ground

- **Gives:** slot buffs, for anyone who stands in them. A Guard post. Back-row access (Sortie).
- **Takes:**
  - *Deaths and summons:* the Padre grows slots from deaths, and summoned Locusts and Mice arrive in buffed slots.
  - *Being outnumbered:* a board can leave drilled slots empty and let Privates, Reserve units, Hoppers or Compost copies fill them in the fight. The Badgers' count is taken before any of them arrive and never changes, so their Fury, Grudge and Relic Seeker pay on such a board all fight.
  - *Attack triggers:* the Wing Commander and Brigadier hand out extra attacks.
  - *Selling and the Compost:* slot buffs outlive the unit that gave them, so a Lance Corporal or Grenadier that has done its job is free fuel for a Briar heap.

## Tier check

A body is the median plain unit's stats at that tier: 9 / 12 / 14 / 18 / 20 / 25.

- **Tier 1:** a seed (Sentry, 4 stats a turn, about 0.4 of a body) and a tempo body (Territorial). Fits.
- **Tier 2:** a scaler (Old Campaigner, 4 to 8 stats a turn), an enabler (Recruiting Sergeant, two Privates a fight) and a buffer (Lance Corporal, 8 stats for 1 gold). **No economy unit, on purpose:** the tribe's saving is that slot buffs survive a sale, and Orders give gold somewhere to go. A pension unit was cut for space; see the log.
- **Tier 3:** Colour Sergeant gives 24 stats a turn in the front row against a target of about 11. Hot on purpose: it needs four Pigeons in the row and is Parade's engine. Padre is capped at 16 stats a turn, a little over. Grenadier Guard is under on stats (6 a turn); the Guard post is its value. Flying Ace is the combat engine.
- **Tier 4:** Lieutenant is about 2 bodies of damage when bought and nearly 4 by turn 9, against a target of 2, because it grows with the Drill; the dial is half the buff. Adjutant is 16 stats a turn against a target of 23, and it makes units Decorated. Bugler is about 6 bodies in a fight on a Parade board: hot on purpose, because the arrivals are small. The dial is +1/+1.
- **Tier 5:** Colour Ensign is about 4 bodies beside a big post and about 1 on a level board. Brigadier gives about five units a second attack, and Wing Commander gives about five units a free one: both above the target of 4 bodies, on purpose, as the direction cards.
- **Tier 6:** Regimental Sergeant Major is about 100 stats a turn on a Parade board (4 bodies, on target). The Colonel is 3 to 6 extra units. Field Marshal is about 150 stats at once on a 38-Medal board (6 bodies) and a saved hit for each Decorated pigeon.

## Rules the engine needs

1. **Slot buffs:** a stat layer on each of a player's 7 slots, added to the unit in the slot, as the "Slot buffs" section says. Permanent ones are on the recruit board; fight-only ones end with the fight (8.5.2).
2. **"Buffs you give to slots are doubled":** a hook on every slot buff a player's effects give (Regimental Sergeant Major). Two of him don't stack; the bigger applies. The Mice's Edgewright is a different effect (it doubles what slots give when read, for one fight) and does stack with him.
3. **Guard post:** one slot per player, a slot effect that gives its unit Guard (9.5.4). Setting a new one clears the old.
4. **Order (N):** once a turn for each unit on the board, pay N gold (11.4.5).
5. **Reserve:** units in hand with Reserve are copied into the fight off the board, in hand order. After each round of deaths, once death effects and returns are done, each slot a friendly unit died in this fight that is empty takes one Reserve unit: the Guard post first, then reading order. This happens before the end-of-fight check and before waiting summons land (the Locusts' Hoppers, the Briars' Elder Mother copies). Units in Reserve are not counted for Outnumbered or first attack. It isn't a summon. Attack order follows 9.4.8. A saved board that others fight carries its Reserve units.
6. **"Joins the fight":** one trigger for a unit summoned in a fight or stepping in from Reserve.
7. **Medals:** a count on the unit; each is a permanent +2/+2. Decorated is a count of 3 or more. A golden made by a triple adds the three counts.
8. **"After each fight":** a trigger on the recruit board once the fight is over, told which units fought, which were alive at the end, and who stood in front of whom.
9. **Permanent changes made in a fight:** the Padre's slot buff and the Flying Ace's Medal apply to the real board as well as the fight.
10. **Sortie:** the attacker's candidates are the enemy back row if it has units (settles 11.5.3 for this keyword). A Guard in the front row is ignored; a Guard in the back row applies.
11. **"The enemy across from it"** (Lieutenant): 8.2.6, falling back to the nearest enemy in that row, then any enemy.
12. **Stiff Upper Lip:** once a fight for each unit, damage that would kill leaves it on 1 Health. Destroy effects ignore it.
13. **Auras that give a keyword or attacks:** Squadron Leader (Sortie), Brigadier (attacks twice, 9.4.11), Field Marshal (Stiff Upper Lip, and Medals worth +4/+4), The Colonel (Reserve, to the hand).
14. **Private:** a 2/2 Pigeon token. The Recruiting Sergeant checks its two slots first thing at Start of fight, and after each round of deaths once Reserve units have stepped in and waiting summons have landed, and fills an empty one (8.4.6), 2 a fight.
15. **Copying a slot's buff** (Colour Ensign): for the fight, one slot takes another's permanent buff if it is bigger. It is not a buff given.
