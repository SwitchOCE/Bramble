# Sample: Bog Choir

## 1. Identity

**Core mechanic:** Moss: layers of +1/+1 that spread between your minions and survive being sold.

- **Spread:** Give a random friendly minion a layer of moss (+1/+1). Moss stays when the minion is sold to the shop.

You buy cheap oozes, coat them in moss, and sell them knowing the moss stays in the shop for later. The board snowballs slowly, then all at once.

## 3. Builds

- **Go wide:** Fill the board early and let the payoffs at Tier 4 scale everything at once.
- **One big carrier:** Pour everything into one minion and protect it with Taunts.

## 4. Risks and holes

- Tier 6 may be too swingy against Divine Shield.
- The early game leans on a single Tier 1.
