# Sample: Heron Wardens

## 1. Identity

**Core mechanic:** Wading: minions that can't be attacked until they attack.

- **Wade:** Can't be attacked until it attacks. After it attacks, it stops Wading.

Your herons sit out of reach until they strike. You decide each turn who wades and who stands in the open.

## 3. Builds

- **Go wide:** Fill the board early and let the payoffs at Tier 4 scale everything at once.
- **One big carrier:** Pour everything into one minion and protect it with Taunts.

## 4. Risks and holes

- Tier 6 may be too swingy against Divine Shield.
- The early game leans on a single Tier 1.
