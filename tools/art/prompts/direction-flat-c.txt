You are making character concept art trials for a woodland auto-battler game. Use your built-in image generation tool. For EACH item below, generate exactly one image, then copy the PNG into the current working folder under the given filename, overwriting any existing file. One item at a time. Do not write code or other files. No text, letters, logos, borders or watermarks in the images. Every design is original: do not depict or closely imitate any character or location from an existing game or film. Reply with just the saved filenames.

The game has six tribes. Each tribe lives in its own BIOME, and the art must make that obvious at a glance: every item names its biome, and the background, the light and the whole colour palette come from that biome. Two items from different biomes must never look like the same place or share the same palette.

Every item: tall portrait 4:5. ONE creature, FULL BODY from the top of the head to the feet, with the ground under it visible. The whole figure takes up about 65 percent of the image height, with clear space above and below. Three-quarter view. The camera is pulled well back: never a close-up, never a bust. Limbs are clear in the silhouette.

Mood: an old, quiet, wild place; the creature is graceful, watchful and a little dangerous. It is a fighter, not a mascot: lean, poised, serious. NOT cute: no big head, no baby proportions, no smiles. Eyes are small, simple glowing shapes. It carries one simple weapon that reads clearly in silhouette, and almost no other gear.

The character is designed silhouette-first: a few large, smooth, elegant shapes with long flowing curves and almost no interior detail. No individual fur strands, no individual feathers, no stitching, no small ornaments. It must be recognisable as what it is from the black silhouette alone.

STYLE for every item: the same luminous storybook mood, simplified for hand production. The CHARACTER is painted only in flat, hard-edged shapes: each shape one flat colour, with one flat shadow shape and one thin flat coloured rim-light line, no gradients or texture on the character at all. Use the character's full natural rich colours (russet, amber, cream, slate, moss and so on): it must NOT be a dark near-black silhouette, and it must be clearly lighter and more saturated than the background behind it. The BACKGROUND is soft: three layers of the biome in silhouette and one soft misty glow behind the character. Colourful, not monochrome. Far less detail than a finished game painting.
